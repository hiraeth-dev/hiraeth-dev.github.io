“Sorry for calling you in so early, Tsukamoto-senpai.”

“Ah, no. It’s fine, but…”

It was early the next morning. The piano club’s vice president, Aoi Tsukamoto—having found herself being summoned to the student council office—shrunk her shoulders uncomfortably as she looked toward Alisa.

Despite the fact that she had been used by Nonoa as well as… some unknown individual who’d become her pawn, the fact remained that—even if it had been to protect the piano club—Aoi had falsely accused Alisa and Ayano of wrongdoing. Now that the student assembly had been adjourned and her club’s continued existence had been provisionally decided, all that remained in her heart was an immense sense of guilt.

“Um, once again, I’m really sorry, Kujou–san. For almost framing you at that student assembly,” she said.

“About that. We’ll be considering the matter settled on the condition you cooperate in catching the person who incited you, Senpai,” Alisa replied after staring quietly at Aoi as she bowed deeply in apology. “I have my apologies too, Senpai. For cornering you in order to uncover the conspiracy.”

“Ah, no, that’s not… I was the one who threw the punch first…”

Her apology only made Aoi look even more ill at ease.

“Alright,” Masachika cut in between the two from the side, seeing that they were about to spiral into an endless exchange of sorrys. “We don’t have a lot of time; I’d like it if we got started right away… Could you look through this student registry and tell us if there’s anyone who looks like the female student that incited you, Tsukamoto-senpai?”

Faced with a thick stack that was the student registry as it was dropped onto the desk with an audible thud, containing the profile of every single high school student at Seirei Academy, Aoi visibly hesitated. She then timidly glanced up at Masachika.

“Um, like I said yesterday, I’ve only met her once…” she spoke. “I don’t remember her face <em>that</em> clearly… or rather, we had that deal that she’d join the piano club as long as I followed what she wanted me to do. But I guess it’s like that’s never happened now, huh…?”

Thinking about the mysterious female student who had never shown herself again since that one encounter, Aoi gave a wry smile, kicking herself over how foolish she had been to fall for it.

“Even so, you’re the only one who’s had contact with her at this point, aside from the eight other perps, Tsukamoto-senpai… So, even an ‘I guess this person kinda looks like her?’ is good enough. We’ll have to go through everyone one by one anyway,” Masachika explained.

“Oh, really…? I’ll take a look then, but… don’t get your hopes up too much, okay?”

Reiterating that caveat, Aoi picked up the student registry and began to carefully examine the photos present. Casting a sidelong glance at her as she did, Masachika looked past Aoi toward Sumire. Maybe due to the fact that it was early into the morning and her hair had been freshly styled, her curls looked livelier than ever.

“The disciplinary committee’s already started its own investigation under Sarashina-senpai’s command, amirite? Violet-senpai.”

“It’s Sumire, I’ll have you know!<a href="#TN1" id="Return1"><sup>[1]</sup></a>” Sumire delivered her expected comeback, before then answering with a sigh. “We’ve squeezed the eight perpetrators yesterday, and have a lead on who this mastermind of ours might be based on their testimonies. We’re splitting up to check on our top suspects as we speak.”

“Moving fast, aren’t ya…?”

Yuki and Ayano had also happened to cooperate in carrying out such an investigation as well. However, given the eight perpetrators were among the more radical of the former’s supporters, Masachika and Alisa hadn’t joined in the questioning, instead turning to Aoi for her cooperation and deciding to conduct their own independent investigation.

…Or at least, that was only half of the official excuse.

In reality, their real reason for acting independently was to identify the mysterious female student before anyone else. And then, in exchange for not exposing her, to demand that she never again cooperated with Nonoa.

<em>We’d be opening a can of worms if we did expose her, and run the risk of it coming out that Nonoa’s the real mastermind…</em> Masachika thought. <em>But oh well, I guess it’s still questionable that she’d leave any crucial evidence in the hands of someone acting as her pawn.</em>

Even so, for him—someone who didn’t want Nonoa’s betrayal to come to light—neither reporting the girl nor allowing anyone else to seize her was desirable.

It was for the same reason that he would have preferred to avoid any outside interference in this own little independent investigation of theirs… But, given he couldn’t explain their true motives at all, the moment Sumire said, quote unquote, “I’d like to cross-reference Tsukamoto-san’s info with that of the eight perpetrators,” he couldn’t say no at all.

That being said, the moment he realized that she would be tagging along, he had at least taken some precautions.

“By the way… why’s Yuusho-san here?” Sumire asked with a tilt of her head, glancing toward her cousin whom Masachika had summoned as that very “precaution.”

“Why, you ask…? Why, as the piano club president, isn’t it only natural that I’d want to catch the person who’d directed all that malice at us, Sumire-neesan?” said cousin in question responded, casually brushing up his bangs as he did.

“Yuusho-san… Were you always this virtuous?”

That was right: Sumire coming meant that Yuusho would too.

They could naturally steer things into splitting up the work by calling him in, after all. That is, having Sumire do her whole investigation with Yuusho. With that, Masachika and Alisa would be free to move independently.

And so, after about thirty minutes later, Aoi—having quickly gone through all the photos—looked up with little confidence.

“I’ve picked out the ones that seem likely for now, but…”

“Thank you very much. How many are there?” Masachika asked.

“Twelve in all, hm?” Alisa—who had relentlessly snapped photos of the selected suspects with her smartphone—answered his question while checking the images.

“Which ones?”

“Ah… <em>These</em> people,” Sumire uttered in her usual refined tone, placing a hand thoughtfully against her mouth as she peered at Alisa’s phone. “<em>Hrm…</em> nine of them are those we’ve already singled out on our end.”

“Oh, then that means our mastermind’s probably just one person rather than several. Ain’t that right?”

“That is indeed correct. The fact that our testimonies were so consistent too…”

“Could you point out which nine those are, just to be safe?”

“Yes. With pleasure.”

With the exchanging of information being completed, Masachika made a proposal in as casual of a tone he could manage.

“Well then,” he started. “Us showing up in too large a group would probably get them all suspicious; how ’bout we split into two?”

“That sounds good. Ah, Kiryuuin-senpai. Will you be going with someone from the disciplinary committee…?” Alisa promptly asked, with Sumire shaking her head from side to side.

“Not really? I’ve already shared all the info with Onee-sama and the rest at the committee; there’s really no particular need to regroup.”

“In that case, Kiryuuin-senpai, is it alright to ask you to go together with Kiryuiin…? Yuusho-san, I mean.”

“Yes, that would be best, wouldn’t it?” Sumire agreed. “Here we go now, okay, Yuusho-san?”

Masachika mentally threw a fist pump at her easy acceptance.

…Only for Yuusho to make an unexpected move right at that moment.

Brushing up his bangs with a faint smile, and for reasons only known to him, he held his hand out toward Aoi.

“Right then, would you help out with our investigation? Vice Prez,” he asked.

“Huh? No way in hell.”

He was rejected right on the spot.

Casting an exasperated look at her cousin, who stood frozen with the corners of his mouth twitching, Sumire briskly turned on her heel.

“Come on, we’ll have to question a few people before morning homeroom starts.”

“But Vice Prez has actually met the person in question; isn’t there just no way to figure out who our culprit is without her…?”

“You can at least tell who’s hiding what by their reactions, can you not?”

“<em>You</em> might be able to, Sumire-neesan, but for us normal people, unless it’s really obvious—”

“Stop complaining. Here we go now, okay?” Sumire cut Yuusho off sternly.

“Yes.”

She then ushered him out of the student council room.

“No, seriously, why’d he think he’d get an okay?” Masachika muttered under his breath as she did.

“Who knows? Maybe he assumed girls would just nod along if he’s the one inviting them.”

“…You’re pretty harsh on him, aren’t ya, Tsukamoto-senpai?”

“’Course I am, aren’t I? There’s <em>nothing</em> ’bout him to like.”

“<em>Ahaha</em>…”

Thinking about Aoi’s situation, Masachika couldn’t help but feel that she was understandable. Still, hearing it being stated so bluntly like that made even him feel a twinge of sympathy for Yuusho…

“Oh well, anyway… Twelve people, huh? Some of them might not be in today, so it’s not like we can check on everyone in a single day too…”

“We’ll just have to do it one by one over a few days then, won’t we…? As for now, maybe we’ll start with the second-years since we’d be more likely to find her among them?”

“Hmm<em>~</em>. You <em>can</em> fake your grade by swapping what ribbon you’re wearing, so we can’t count on it much… Shouldn’t we start by tacklin’ the smaller groups first?”

“Well I s’pose that’s true, right? Then, should we start with the first years? Is that fine with you as well, Tsukamoto-senpai?”

“Yeah, I guess that’s fine…”

And so, following such a brief exchange, Masachika and Alisa, together with Aoi, set about questioning their suspects one by one.

⋆⋅☆⋅⋆

“Um, sorry to bother you so suddenly. Could we have a moment?”

“Yes? You mean me—oh my, Kujou-san?”

“Yes. Um, I just wanted to ask… Does anyone who’s good at makeup come to mind?”

“Someone who’s good at makeup…?”

The trio were at their very first person: a first-year girl in Class 1-A who happened to be in the hallway.

Though she looked utterly bewildered by Alisa’s sudden question, it did have a purpose.

According to Aoi’s testimony, the mysterious female student had worn full makeup, something rare at a school where only makeup light enough to not draw a teacher’s attention was the norm. She had likely worn it as part of her disguise, but it was hard to imagine that she would have entrusted actually applying it to someone else. Following that reasoning, such logic only meant that said mysterious girl was skilled at makeup… and since “We suspect you’re the culprit” was definitely something the three could not bring themselves to say face-to-face, Alisa’s question simply fell under the pretense of an informal inquiry.

<em>Still, she should show some sorta reaction upon seeing Tsukamoto-senpai if she really is out culprit here</em>, Masachika thought as he carefully observed the girl’s reaction from behind.

However, even after noticing Aoi and him, the girl showed no particular suspicious recoil at all. After seriously chewing over the sudden question from strangers that she had barely—really barely—ever met, she named three people.

“Understood. Thank you very much,” Alisa said.

“You’re welcome…” the girl replied. “Are you maybe interested in makeup, Kujou-san? I feel like you wouldn’t need any at all with how beautiful you are.”

“Ah, no, this isn’t for me, but… in any case, thank you.”

“Oh, not at all. Glad if I could be of help.”

Leaving it at that, the girl bowed and walked away with a gentle smile.

“…She’s a really nice person, huh?” Masachika couldn’t help but comment.

“She was,” Alisa agreed. “So… what do <em>you</em> think, Tsukamoto-senpai?”

At her question, Aoi slowly shook her head from side to side.

“…I don’t think it’s her. The way she spoke, her voice, everything was completely different… Besides, the female student I met was a little slimmer too. At least I felt like she was.”

“Then it’s probably not her, hm…?” Masachika mused. “I mean, she didn’t get weird when she saw us either, right? Like, averting her eyes for example, or letting her gaze wander too.”

“So what about the three people she mentioned, Masachika-kun? The ones she said were good at makeup?”

“I’ve written their name down for now; guess we’ll check their photos again later, huh? We’ve still got eleven more people to go through right now, after all…”

Just as Masachika was starting to feel gloomy about how long this was going to take, the truth was that—spoiler alert—they wouldn’t need to question all eleven suspects.

The thing is, Aoi had initially picked out her suspects based solely on portrait photos. However, once they actually saw said suspects in person, it became painfully evident that a lot of them had figures clearly different from the mysterious girl they were after, or had faces that now looked different from what they had looked like in their photos. For example, some had become more plump, while others had acne bad enough that makeup couldn’t hide, and so on…

Only three out of the twelve suspects that Aoi had singled out were first years. Of the remaining two, one was dismissed without even needing to approach them. And so, by the time they’d finished checking all the first years, they still had fifteen minutes before morning homeroom began.

“That wrapped up faster than I expected… Right, next up’s the third-years, judging by the numbers, but…” Masachika muttered, trailing off at the end.

“They are, right? Should we do them during lunch break?”

“No, everyone leaves their classrooms once that starts; it’d be hard to tell where anyone is… I’d like to use the breaks in between too. Not for every single class, obviously, but…”

Glancing hesitantly at Alisa and Aoi and receiving both their nods, the trio agreed to continue their investigation during the ten-minute break between the second and third periods.

“In that case, it might be better to go with the second-years next, considering walking time and all. They’re kinda our main suspects anyway,” Masachika suggested.

“That’s true, isn’t it?” Alisa agreed

“I’m fine with that too,” Aoi said in tow. “Or rather, shouldn’t we go <em>now</em> in that case? We’ve still got around thirteen minutes.”

With Masachika and Alisa nodding at her suggestion, the three of them hurried over to the floor where the second-year classrooms were gathered.

And then…

“Oh my~? Alya-chan? Kuze-kun? What are you two doing here~?” a voice called out to them.

It belonged to none other than Maria, whom they ran into by chance on the stairs.

“Morning, Masha-san. Uh, we’re just looking for someone regarding yesterday’s student assembly, that’s all,” Masachika said only that before trying to leave…

But unfortunately for him, there was no escaping her.

“Looking for someone?” Maria asked. “Could it be they’re a second-year?”

“Yeah, well…”

“Alriiiight, I might be able to help~. Who’re you looking for, by the way?”

“Eh?”

Caught off guard by her unexpected offer, Masachika reflexively looked to Alisa, who in turn showed Maria her phone, albeit looking a little flustered herself.

“Ohhh, that’s Miume-chan, right?” Maria nodded once after looking at the photo taken from the student registry. “That’s perfect. She’s my friend; want me to introduce you guys to her?”

“Eh, really?” Alisa murmured, her eyes widening in surprise.

Masachika shared her shock, though there was no reason to accept her help if that was true.

“No, rather than introducing us to her… it’d be fine if we just watch you talking to her from behind, Masha-san,” he told her.

“Eh? That’s all you need?”

“Yes. We just wanna see her face and hear her voice.”

“Hmm<em>~</em>? I don’t really get it, but okay. Alrighty, follow me, ’kay?”

Exchanging glances with Alisa and Aoi, Masachika then followed Maria as instructed, who casually stepped into another class’s classroom before calling out to the girl they were looking for as she was chatting with her classmates.

“G’morniiiin<em>’</em> Miume-chan<em>~♪</em>”

“Hm? Ohhh<em>~</em>, Masha! G’morniiiin<em>’</em>. What’s up this early?”

The two then naturally clasped their hands together while exchanging extremely friendly hellos. It was only a moment after that the girl caught sight of the three standing behind Maria, blinking several times.

“Oh, and these three?” she asked. “Your li’l sister, her partner, and…?”

“Ah, don’t worry about them, okay~? They’re just here to observe.”

“What d’you mean observe…?” the girl smiled wryly, deciding not to dwell on it too much, seemingly used to hearing Maria say odd things.

After a brief moment of light chit-chat, Maria flashed her trademark bright, friendly smile.

“Thanks for the chat~,” she chimed. “Talking with you after so long’s made me suuuper happy, Miume-chan♪”

“Oh, reaaally? Thanks.”

“<em>Mm</em>, c’ya later~” Maria waved with both hands, receiving a wave back from the girl with a smile in response.

The moment they stepped out the hallway, she then glanced toward Masachika.

“Was that okay?” she asked with a slight tilt of her head.

“No, it was more than enough,” he replied. “What do you think, Tsukamoto-senpai?”

“I don’t think it’s her,” Aoi slowly shook her head with a troubled expression in response. “It was hard to tell since her hair’s covering her jawline in the photo, but it didn’t look as prominent as the one <em>that</em> girl had.”

“That so…? Right, on to the next one then.”

“There’s still more~? Who is it?” Maria perked up.

“Uhhh, this girl…” Alisa said, with Maria nodding yet again as she showed her her phone for the second time today.

“Ohhh, she’s my friend too. This way.”

“Eeeeh?”

“No way.”

Leaving the trio right in shock, Maria drifted to yet another neighboring classroom, glanced around the room again, and asked a nearby student a question before quickly coming back out.

“Sorryyyy<em>~</em>, looks like she’s not here yet.”

“Ah, no, that’s totally fine…”

“I’ll introduce you guys to her later, ’kay? So, who else?”

“Um, this girl…”

“<em>Mm</em>, she’s my friend too~” Maria nodded yet again as she saw the phone Alisa held out.

“Hol’ on, Masha-san, don’t you have way too many friends!?”

“<em>Do</em> Iiii~? Well, I mean, I guess I do make a point of eating lunch in the cafeteria with someone I don’t know at all once a week; maybe that’s it?”

“What an actual living embodiment of communication skills…!!!” Masachika couldn’t help but cry out, shuddering at her absurd social prowess.

Alisa, apparently unaware of this side to her sister as well, stared at her with eyes wide open.

However… the real shock was yet to come.

They showed her the fourth photo.

“Hmm<em>~</em>, I don’t know who she iiiis~” Maria mused.

<em>Well of course, right?</em> the three pondered the same thought, feeling oddly relieved.

And yet… without even a shred of hesitation, Maria charged right up to what was supposedly a complete stranger, and after about only two or three minutes of conversation, they had already warmed up to each other.

“Is she a communication monster, or what…?”

“…”

“Your older sister’s really amazing, isn’t she?”

With the three half-forgetting their original purpose, unable to do anything but marvel at the sight as they watched from behind, Maria continued to chat away cheerfully with a girl she’d literally just exchanged “Nice to meet you”s with a few minutes ago.

Thanks to Maria’s overwhelmingly reliable reinforcement, Masachika and the others would manage to make contact with all five of the second-year suspects during the morning and lunch break. The problem? None of the now-eight people they’d met so far had turned out to be the person they were looking for.

“That leaves us four third-years…” Masachika uttered. “We should be able to just get through them during the afternoon break at this pace, I think.”

“And if we don’t find her then?”

“Oh well, guess we’ll hold a strategy meeting then…”

With a vague sense of unease hanging over them, the four headed for the third-year classrooms as the afternoon break rolled around. Here too, Maria’s knack for having a wide network of friends was put to full use, with the group heading straight toward one of the girls who was said to be her friend from the remaining four.

“Woooow<em>~</em>. Long time no seeee, Takatsudo-senpaiii~” Maria beamed.

“Huh? Masha-chan? Oh, and your li’l sis, and…” the girl who Maria called out to responded, trailing off as she let her gaze drift to the three behind her. “Ah,” she slipped from her lips, “could it be… you’re looking for that mastermind everyone’s talking about?”

“!?”

“Ah, so it <em>really</em> is that,” she then smiled, seeing Alisa react instantly.

Seeing it as something they couldn’t just ignore, Masachika took a few paces forward to stand beside Maria.

“Excuse me for butting in. I’m Kuze, the student council’s general affairs officer,” he introduced himself. “Has something like that… already turned into a rumor by any chance?”

“Ehhh. Yeah, I mean, gossip about yesterday’s student assembly has already practically spread all over a while ago. I’ve also heard the disciplinary committee and Suou-san and her partner’s been looking for the mastermind…”

“Is that… so?”

It looked like the other side was no longer bothering to hide their objective…

Or maybe they simply couldn’t anymore.

<em>I mean, it’s only natural that someone’s oughtta catch on if they’re dragging the perps around and questioning people all over the place</em>, Masachika reflected. <em>No, maybe…</em>

It might be that Yuki was deliberately conducting her own investigation openly to prove her own innocence instead. It sounded like the more likely possibility now that he thought of it.

“Huh? Wait, does that mean… I’m being suspected out too?” the girl asked, pointing to her own face with mild amusement.

“I apologize. We’ve actually been going around and questioning students who look like this mastermind in question,” Masachika answered honestly, having watched his senior show no sign of being offended.

“Whoa, seriously?” the girl asked, still laughing and scratching her head without a shred of negative emotion. “Ehhh<em>~</em>, what do I do? Do I give an alibi, or somethin’?”

Faced with her demeanor, Masachika glanced over to Aoi, who in turn hesitated slightly before giving a small shake of her head.

<em>Not her, hm…? Oh well, she’d have to be one hell of an actress if she really were the culprit and could still act like this, wouldn’t she?</em>

With that in mind, Masachika decided to take the opportunity to ask this refreshingly good-natured senior of his a few more questions.

“There’s something I’d like to ask,” he started. “Regarding yesterday’s student assembly, what sorta rumors have been specifically going around?”

“Eh? What sorta rumors…? Uhhh, like, about how there had been a few students who’d tried to tamper with the votes to pin some kinda fraud on some student council officers? And that there’s this mastermind who had incited them, so now there’s a search going on to find them… Something like that?”

“Could you tell me about it a little more?”

“A little more? Uhhhh… Ah, right, right. That the students who got caught were allegedly targeting Kujou-san over there to get Suou-san elected?” the girl said, glancing toward Alisa. “I heard something along those lines too, I think?”

“…And what did you think when you heard that?”

“What did I think? I mean, well, enthusiastic supporters do go overboard sometimes, don’t they now?”

“And that there’s also some rumors that the person who incited said supporters was apparently actually Yuki Suou herself, right?” Masachika probed.

“Ehhh? No waaaay, no way. People are saying that?” the girl asked, bursting out laughing at the utter conspiracy that he’d voiced, something that hadn’t yet even become a rumor. “I don’t know her <em>that</em> well or anything, but Suou-san’s not the type to pull something that shady, amirite? Like, she wouldn’t be teaming up with the disciplinary committee if she <em>really</em> were the mastermind, right? I mean, <em>you</em>’re her former partner; shouldn’t you know that better than anyone else?”

“…Well, I guess that’s true, huh?” Masachika replied with a small chuckle, just as the girl then turned toward Alisa.

“Welp, sounds like you’ve got a lot going on, but… keep going at it, ’kay?” she said. “I’m personally rooting for ya, Kujou-san.”

“Eh? Th-thank you very much… But why?”

“Hm? It’s just… I can tell you’re really giving your all. Sparkin’ off with <em>that</em> Suou-san alone’s already impressive, y’know? Oh well, I’m graduating next April so I won’t be able to vote though.”

“…No, thank you very much. That really encourages me”

“<em>Mm</em>, keep goin’ at iiit~. Gah, I just realized I ended up wrapping the whole convo on my own here. Was that okay?” the senior commented, going out of her way to be considerate despite having no obligation to humor them in the first place.

“No, it was more than enough,” Masachika said, smiling as he bowed his head honestly. “Thank you very much for sharing all that valuable info.”

“Thank you for your time,” Alisa parroted his gratitude.

“Thank you so much, Takatsudo-senpai. Let’s talk again sometime, okaaay<em>~</em>?” Maria chimed.

“Yeah, c’ya.”

Having bid farewell to the senior who lightly waved, Masachika and the rest gave one final bow as they left Class 3-D’s classroom.

“She’s a… really great senior, hm?” Masachika commented. “We got to hear a lot of things from her too.”

“Right?” Alisa nodded.

“Wasn’t sheeee<em>~</em>? I love Takatsudo-senpai too♡” Maria carefreely declared, something Masachika couldn’t help but let his cheeks relax into a smile over.

<em>“Oh!”</em> her face then suddenly seemed to convey as she started to glance around restlessly, before then beckoning Masachika over with a subtle motion of her fingers.

“What’s up?” he asked.

“Ah, come this way for a sec…”

“?”

Following her gesture, Masachika moved a short distance away, allowing Maria to stretch up lightly as she tiptoed and leaned in close.

<em>“When I say ‘love,’ I don’t mean the same kinda love I have for you, you know, Saa-kun?”</em> she whispered into his ear in a sneaky hush.

“!?”

<em>“My love for you is a speeecial kind of loooove. ’Kay?”</em>

Hearing such brain-melting words in her version of what was a serious tone, Masachika couldn’t stop the blood from rushing toward his head.

He thought he was about to die.

“Hey, what’re you two doing?” Alisa called out just then, furrowing her brows.

Casting a suspicious look at her sister who’d smoothly stepped back, she then shot a sharp glance toward Masachika, who was already steadily turning red.

“What? What did you do?”

“No, it’s nothing…”

The shock to his brain was far too intense for him to come up with a good excuse on the spot.

“Mmm<em>~</em>, nothing that big of a deal,” Maria stepped in instead, wearing her usual easygoing smile as she spoke casually. “I was just saying that no matter how nice of a senior she is, he shouldn’t go falling for her, you know? Takatsudo-senpai’s already got a boyfriend, after all.”

“The heck? That’s not something you need to whisper about in the first place,” Alisa remarked.

“Reeeally<em>~</em>? I was just thinking that… maybe it’d be better to not point out a boy’s love too loudly, you knooow<em>~</em>?”

“Please don’t call it ‘love…’ I didn’t mean that she was a really great senior in <em>that</em> way or anything…”

“And you…” Alisa then honed in on Masachika again, looking at him with a look of half exasperation, half seriousness as he fanned his face with his hands. “What’s with your face being all red like that…?”

“See! Talking about who they like and stuff’s embarrassing for boys!” Maria smugly declared with a puffed out chest.

“…Is that really so?”

“…I mean, it’s not exactly you want the opposite sex to bring up at all, yeah,” Masachika tacked on.

<em>“</em>Hmm<em>~?</em>” Alisa hummed, giving off the vibe of being somewhat satisfied with their explanations.

It was only then did Masachika notice Aoi watching them on with an indescribable expression, seeming to have caught on to something. Clearing his throat lightly, he turned toward her.

“Pardon me for that. So, what d’you think about that senior back there, Tsukamoto-senpai?” he asked.

Returning a look as if she was tempted to comment on what had just happened for a few seconds, Aoi then closed her eyes once before scoffing lightly and giving him a troubled expression.

“She’s not it… I think,” she answered. “Her face and figure’s similar, but her overall vibe’s completely different…”

“That so? Oh well, I also felt she probably wasn’t the one from how she talked.”

“Yeah…”

With that, Masachka then turned to Alisa.

“That was great, ain’t it? Senpai back there’s rooting for ya,” he said with a slightly teasing smile.

“Yes, well… I guess,” Alisa, still wearing a faintly sharp expression, replied, looking diagonally upward in response to his half-joking remark. “Somehow hearing someone you don’t know at all… No, <em>precisely</em> hearing someone you don’t know at all cheer you on makes you want to try harder, doesn’t it?”

Her candid reaction made Masachika’s eyes widen for a moment.

“…That’s right,” he uttered as he returned to a soft smile, before then turning right back to Aoi and continuing. “Alright, that leaves us three people… We’ll be counting on you a bit longer, Tsukamoto-senpai.”

“Ah, yeah. Roger…”

Buoyed by their senior’s support, the group energetically resumed their investigation.

However, in the end…

“No way man… I can’t believe the last three would be completely out of the question without even having to call out to them,” Masachika couldn’t help but mutter as they reached an anticlimactic conclusion.

“Sorry, I guess…”

“No, it’s not your fault, Tsukamoto-senpai.”

Though the letdown admittedly felt especially sharp due to how fired up they had gotten, nothing could be done if Aoi could only declare “That’s not her” the moment she laid eyes on their last three suspects. What was more… Masachika had been easily able to see why as she watched from beside her… Specifically, one had been too tall, another was pretty tanned, and the last was rather… on the whole, overweight.

In the end, after checking their initial twelve suspects, there hadn’t been even a single student who had made Aoi go “This is her!”, nor anyone whose reactions seemed suspicious to Masachika. At this point, the only silver lining they had came from the fact that neither Sumire nor Yuki had succeeded in finding and catching the actual culprit, which also suggested that the investigation on their respective ends had run into hiccups too.

“I’m really… sorry…” Aoi apologized once again.

“No, no, it can’t be helped,” Masachika dismissed the thought. “I already knew from the start that trying to find someone you’ve basically only met once from pure memory’s not something that’d go well.”

Those were his honest feelings. Or rather, to be entirely frank, Masachika had already been seventy percent sure that they wouldn’t find her anyway.

“What do we do…? Should we go over the photos again in the student council office again after school?” Alisa suggested.

“Hmm… No, let’s go check out that drama club thing that came up when we questioned that first-year.”

<em>“Maybe someone from the drama club if it’s makeup?”</em> the very first girl they had questioned had told them, in response to Alisa’s original question.

Though her way of asking most likely didn’t come off as particularly serious, the girl’s answer had been incredibly convincing and had stuck with Masachika up to this point.

“I know the drama club’s president; I was thinkin’ of asking if they could give us a li’l time before they start practicing…” he explained.

“…All right. Please do.”

“Can you come along for this too, Tsukamoto-senpai?”

“Ah, yeah. That’s fine.”

“Thanks a lot.”

“Should I come toooo<em>~</em>?” Maria perked up.

“Ah, uh, either way’s fine, Masha-san…”

“<em>Hmph</em>, are you leaving me out?”

“Huh!? N-no, that’s not it… Well then, care to come with us?”

“<em>Mhm!</em>” Maria exclaimed, instantly flipping her pout to a beaming smile in response to whatever it was that had made her so happy, all the while Masachika exchanged a wry smile with Alisa.

And so, after laying the groundwork with the drama club’s president, the four of them visited the auditorium where its members were gathering after school.

“Alright, gather up!” the president boomed.

“““““Yes!!”””””

Responding to their call, around twenty club members hurriedly formed a circle and stood at attention, their hands clasped behind their backs.

As the four watched the unexpected scene—one that resembled that of an athletics team—the president glanced their way and spoke.

“Before we start practice, we’ve got a few visitors today,” they declared.

“““““?”””””

As the club members reacted in silent yet visible suspicion, Masachika and the rest stepped up onto the stage at the club president’s “By all means.”

““““Excuse us,”””” the four of them voiced out, converging the collective gaze of the club members…

<em>Hm!?</em>

And at that instant, Masachika saw it.

Specifically, one of the female members quickly turning her face away as she noticed them. Could we please have a moment of your time?” he asked, starting with that.

“Not a problem. But only for five minutes,” the club president replied.

“Thank you very much; that’s plenty.”

Having exchanged brief glances with Alisa and Aoi, Masachika could tell that they had seen the same thing.

Oh, Maria? She didn’t notice a single thing, so let’s leave it at that.

<em>That reaction the moment she saw us… Could it be she didn’t want Tsukamoto-senpai to see her face? That was blatantly suspicious either way.</em>

Without waiting for any signal, they began to walk toward said female club member in question, with Masachika questioning Aoi with his eyes and receiving her nod with a stern expression in return.

<em>Her figure’s not far off either… Could it be? Could it actually be her?</em>

As he kept his nerves taut in an attempt to now show them, Alisa spoke to the girl.

“Um, excuse me,” she said.

“Y-yes!”

Her response came in an oddly high voice, as if deliberately off-key. Accompanied by a face stubbornly refusing to look their way.

“Um…?” Alisa called out again.

But this time, the girl slowly turned toward her, her eyes lowered.

Her face was plain and pale, far from the “glossy eye-catching beauty” that Aoi had described.

<em>No, if anything, ain’t a face like this easier to transform with makeup…?</em> Masachika thought, still keeping his guard up as he stared at the girl’s eyes.

And as they locked onto Alisa…

“Ah, what an amazing face!” the girl blurted out, nearly collapsing on the spot as her knees gave way before she scuttled backward in a flustered retreat.

All Masachika and Alisa could do was just stare, dumbfounded.

“U-uhhh?” the latter uttered.

“Mmm!” came the girl’s aroused moan. “Your voice’s so pretty! Crap, it’s too powerful!”

Speaking so rapidly as her impressions spilled uncontrollably from her mouth, the girl then clung to another club member as if she were trying to use them as a shield. It was at this point that Masachika understood why she had turned her face away. His gaze turned lukewarm at the realization. Looking around, he saw the other drama club members watching the scene unfold with similar expressions.

“Ummm… And you are…?” Alisa asked, confused as she still tried to ask for her name.

In response, the female club member’s eyes went wide, her face crumpling on the spot.

“A faaaaaaan<em>~~~</em>!!!”

“Wha…?”

“Yeeeeeeeees<em>~~~</em>! Sorryyy! You were just way too cool in yesterday’s student assembly; I was moooooved<em>~</em>~~!!!”

“Th-thank… you?”

“Uwaaaaaah<em>~~~</em>! Your face’s amaziiiing<em>~~~</em>! It’s too beautifuuul~~~!!!” the girl continued to wail her overflowing emotions with tears welling in her eyes, now practically collapsing as her knees gave out.

At a loss, Alisa turned toward Masachika with a helpless look. Yet, being given that look only troubled him as well.

“…Why don’t you just shake her hand for now, or something?” he suggested.

“Come on, I’m not a celebrity or anyth—”

“Is that okaaaay!?”

“Oh, so she <em>really</em> does wanna do that…”

Having suddenly regained the strength in her legs, the girl sprang upright. Albeit recoiling slightly in reaction, Alisa hesitated her right hand. The girl clasped it with both of hers in return, practically holding it up over her head in reverence as she snapped her face up with overwhelming emotion.

“Thank you very muuuuch! I’m cheering for you! I’ll definitely vote for you next yeaaar!!!”

“Th-thank you… very much…”

<em>“How ’bout it?”</em> Masachika quietly whispered to Aoi behind the overwhelmed Alisa. “Anyone else who fits the bill?”

<em>“</em>Uhhh…<em>”</em>

After slowly scanning the drama club members’ faces, Aoi furrowed her brows.

<em>“Sorry,”</em> came her reply, still hushed in a whisper. <em>“I was thinking that maybe that club member would look different with makeup on… but everyone starts to look that way when I think that…”</em>

<em>“</em>Yeeeah…<em>”</em>

In the end, the only thing the group had ended up learning from paying a visit to the drama club was that Alisa had, without realizing it, gained an enthusiastic fan. In addition, the way she had handled her response to said fan had seemed to have raised her likability within the drama club as a whole as well.

⋆⋅☆⋅⋆

“<em>Haaah…</em> It was all a swing and miss in the end, huuuh?”

Having checked in with the drama club, Masachika and the rest decided to part ways with Aoi for the day, with the remaining three heading straight to the student council office for a break.

Following yesterday’s student assembly, and having been worn out from running all over the place investigating today as well, Masachika collapsed onto the sofa and stretched deeply.

“Nnngh…! Aaah…”

Enjoying the pleasant sensation of his muscles being pulled tight throughout his body, he went limp for a while as he checked his phone.

“No word from the guys over at the disciplinary committee either… Looks like they haven’t caught anyone on their end too.”

“Seems so, hm?”

“Welp, things sure are tough, huh…? <em>Hup</em>,” Masachika grunted as he threw his momentum into swinging himself upright on the sofa. “Thanks so much for today, Masha-san. You helped us a ton…”

“<em>Mm~mm</em>,” Maria dismissed the thought. “Not at all~. Ah, I’ll make us some tea, <em>’kay~</em>?”

Having been unbelievably reliable throughout the whole day, she smiled cheerfully without a hint of concern as she started to prepare the tea. Watching that big-hearted attitude of this senior of his, Masachika quietly found his respect growing for her… Maria, holding the tin of loose tea leaves, then suddenly seemed to realize something.

“By the way, what were you guys investigating today?”

“Gah!” Masachika exclaimed, letting out a sharp breath at a question that came far, far too late, with the respect he’d just built for her vanishing straight into thin air.

“Masha… You were doing all that without knowing…?” Alisa asked in exasperation.

“Ehhh<em>~</em>? But it’s ’cause no one explained it to meee~”

“That… might’ve been the case, but…”

<em>Surely you’d have asked at some point…</em> she almost said that thought out loud, pressing a finger to her temple as her eyebrow twitched before standing up with a weary sigh.

“I’m going to quickly get something sweet,” Alisa then announced. “Please explain it to her, Masachika-kun.”

“Ehhh…?”

“Ah, Alya-chan,” Maria called out to her. “Can you get something for ol’ Onee-chan too, pleeeease~? I want pudding.”

“…Yeah, yeah.”

Having casually dumped all the explaining onto Masachika, Alisa left the student council office. As she did, Maria—still making tea—tilted her head toward him.

“So then, what were you investigating?”

“No, well… how do I put this? Please just stay the way you are, Masha-san.”

“Reeeally~? Maybe I will if it’s you saying so, I gueeess<em>~</em>”

Masachika, for some reason, just couldn’t bring himself to explain things like masterminds and conspiracies to this senior of his. It wasn’t something that particularly bothered Maria either, having nodded without showing any concern.

“Ah, I accidentally poured a cup for Alya-chan too. <em>Umm</em>, should I just put a lid on it?”

“Why not just make it again later?” Masachika suggested. “I’ll take two of ’em if that makes things easier too?”

“Really? Alright, I’ll take you up on that then,” Maria said, bringing over a tray carrying a teapot and three teacups on it. “Okay, here you are.”

“Thank you very much.”

Taking the teacup from her, Masachika felt a belated twang of guilt for having her make tea for him as if it were second nature.

“I’m sorry, I guess… Even after you helped with the investigation and all.”

“Eh? Ah, but it’s not something to worry about? I did it because I wanted to.”

“No, I’ll definitely repay you for this,” Masachika asserted firmly.

He then brought the tea close to his lips… only for Maria to sit down to his right and lean in close.

<em>Hm? Ain’t she kinda close?</em>

No sooner had the thought crossed his mind than Maria pressed herself against him, for reasons known only to her, leaning toward his right ear.

<em>“So? Is it good?”</em> she whispered as if sharing a secret.

“Huh? Yeah, it’s good, but… what’s with the whispering?”

<em>“Hmm~? I wonder if you’ve got sensitive ears, Saa-kun.”</em>

“No, it’s not like they’re sensitive… <em>Anyone</em> would react like this, wouldn’t they?”

Despite having shivered slightly and edged to the left as her breath tickled his right ear, Maria leaned in just as much, pressing their bodies against each other even closer.

“Noooo, no, no, the heck are you doing?” Masachika squirmed. “What kinda game is this?”

<em>“Hmm~, a whispering one?”</em>

“What even is that!?”

Doing his utmost to divert his attention from the overwhelming presence pressed against his right arm, Masachika set his teacup back on the table for the time being.

Even so, Maria’s whispering continued unabated.

<em>“</em>Nufufu<em>, this whispering game… kind of feels like we’re doing something naughty, doesn’t it?”</em>

“I think this is already plenty naughty as is thouuuughh<em>~~</em>!?” Masachika squirmed as her breath continued to tickle his ear.

<em>“</em>Fufu<em>, you’re so funny, Saa-kun.”</em>

“<em>Eek!</em>”

Having felt a small laugh and a puff of breath blown straight into his ear, sending a shiver coursing right through him, Masachika tried to stand up, unable to take it anymore.

But at that very moment…

“Awww. Geez, Saa-kun, you’re so cuuute~♡” Maria sang, jumping at him from the side and hugging him tight, throwing him off balance and sending him toppling to the left.

“Hey!?”

“Kyaaa<em>!</em>” she cried out as they fell.

“W-whoa!?”

Though a momentary sensation of floating made him freeze in fear, Masachika was only thankfully pushed down onto the sofa, his head landing right by the armrest, unharmed in the end.

“That was close…”

“<em>Ehehe</em>, my bad,” Maria teased, sticking out her tongue as she apologized while draped over Masachika, before once again raising a hand beside her mouth before leaning in toward his ear.

“No, why’re we still doing this whole whispering game at a time like this!?” he exclaimed.

<em>“Because you’re cute, Saa-kun?”</em>

“Eeek<em>!</em> You’re giving me shivers!” Masachika exclaimed once again, shaking his head vigorously as a shudder ran down his back.

Pouting her lips at his reaction, Maria then suddenly broke into a smile as if she’d thought of something.

“Riiight,” she hummed. “You said you’d repay me earlier, right?”

“Huh? Ah, yes… Wait, don’t tell me…”

Looking up as a shiver ran down his spine, for an entirely different reason than before, Masachika met Maria’s mischievous yet seductive smile.

“Alrighty. Instead of thanking me, you’re gonna have to endure this… until Alya-chan comes back, ’kay?”

“I’ll die, you know!?”

“<em>’</em>Kaaaay. Here we go!” she beamed, her smile carrying an unusual hint of allure as she slowly drew closer… her hot breath brushed against his earlobe. <em>“Let’s start, ’kay?”</em>

“<em>Eep</em>!”

Scrunching up his neck as he curled in on himself, Masachika desperately tried to endure the ticklish sensation that felt as if his spine was being licked.

<em>“</em>Ufufu<em>, are you trembling? How cute♡”</em>

<em>Nuuooooghh! What’s with this live ASMRRRR~~~!?</em>

It was an utterly and completely different level than any ASMR audio he’d once listened to with earphones. He could physically feel her breath, after all. But more than that, her body pressed against his right side emanated a strong heat, texture, and scent.

The physical presence of the woman named Maria assaulted him with a violently dense intensity.

<em>“Hey, doesn’t whispering like this with just the two of us make you remember the old days?”</em>

<em>Uoooooh! Now’s the time, Alya-saaaaan!</em>

Wishing more fervently than ever for his partner’s arrival, Masachika shut his eyes and clenched his teeth as he continued to endure Maria’s assault.

Squirming endlessly as his caressed ears, trembling ear drums, and a sweet, pleasant voice all tickled his brain, he endured it with everything he had.

【I looove you, Saa-kun~♡】

⋆⋅☆⋅⋆

A dozen or so minutes later.

“I’m… back…?” Alisa announced her return with confusion.

“Ah, welcome back, Alya-chan♪”

“……”

Having returned to the student council office, what she now saw was Masachika, slumped over as if his soul had been sucked out through his ears, and Maria, who—in a stark contrast and for some reason—brimmed with life.
