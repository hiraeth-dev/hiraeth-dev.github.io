“Yeaaah… This pretty much doesn’t feel right, huh…?”

It was currently after the closing ceremony. Masachika, who had finished helping set up the venue for Yuki’s Christmas party, found himself alone. He stood in front of a mirror in the men’s bathroom, his lips pressed into a thin line.

He wore a suit that he’d bought in the spring for formal occasions. It wasn’t something most ordinary folk would exactly call cheap, but the fact that it was still off-the-rack as opposed to being custom-made most likely qualified it as such by this school’s standards.

Trying to at the very least properly put his hair together, Masachika awkwardly worked hair wax through his stubborn strands, something he never really got used to using. However… no matter how hard he tried, it just wouldn’t stay the way he wanted.

“It had turned out a li’l better when I tried it at home, though…” he muttered.

Scrunching up his face, he racked his brain for what he had done differently, only to realize that he had practiced right after taking a bath.

<em>Huh? Wait, no way… does how clean your hair is actually make that much of a difference?</em>

The unpleasant thought slowly sent his back breaking out in a cold sweat.

<em>No, but, I used to do it just fine for piano recitals and stuff when I was a kid…</em>

Relying on hazy memories from long ago, Masachika continued to fiddle with his hair.

<em>Huh? How did I do it back then again? I’m... pretty sure I did it like this…</em>

Yet, just as you might have thought, it still wouldn’t work out. Perhaps simply washing all the wax out and starting over was the smart choice here.

But still, even if he did want to wash his hair, doing it at school was a little… you know? Besides, there wasn’t much time left in the first place.

<em>Hol’ on, no way… Isn’t this pretty… no,</em> seriously <em>bad?</em>

A chill ran down his spine as panic overtook him, his heart pounding right into overdrive.

<em>Aaargh, shit. First the closing ceremony and now this… My lack of prep’s too much! Seriously, the hell am I doing!?</em>

Ever since hearing Alisa’s campaign promise, Masachika had powered through to this very day on an endless motivation bought on by this so-called “invincible mode” he had fallen into. It was thanks to it that he felt that everything up until yesterday had gone smoothly, whether it was his studies, student council work, or even practice with the wind ensemble club. Yet now, when it came down to the day that actually mattered, the very tunnel vision that had kept him moving—making every step forward feel like undeniable proof that he couldn’t stumble—had finally spectacularly come back to bite him.

<em>Aaargh, geez!!!</em>

Clenching a fistful of his hair in a cry of how things simply would not go his way, Masachika tightened his grip until the pain gave him something else to focus on beside the disappointment he felt toward himself.

“Honestly, what on earth are you doing, Kuze?” a voice forced its way through the noise in his head.

It belonged to none other than Yuusho. He had, at some point, waltzed right in, leaning against the restroom wall with his arms folded.

“…Why the hell are <em>you</em> even here, man?” Masachika caught his reflection in the mirror, his eyes as flat as a week-old cup of coffee.

“Why, you say? I was just escorting Sumire-neesan; I’m simply here to do my own business, okay?”

“Yeah, sure.”

“That aside…” Yuusho shrugged, a smirk tugging at his mouth, “you two sure have made quiiite the grand campaign promise, hm?”

The derisive edge in his voice matched the mockery in his eyes.

“Do you both <em>seriously</em> believe you can make it happen?”

Masachika didn’t so much as blink. “It ain’t about whether we can. We <em>will</em>.”

“…Hmmm?”

His firm declaration was met with a hum so ambiguous that it made it impossible to tell whether Yuusho bought it or not.

“Oh well, that’s another matter in itself…” Yuusho made a languid sweep of his hand, squinting as he moved his eyes up to Masachika’s head. “What’s with that hair of yours? Surely a gentleman ought to be able to style his own hair, don’t you think? And yet…”

Stopping to scrutinize Masachika’s hair disaster once more, he dramatically raised an eyebrow.

“……”

It was a gesture so pompous that Masachika would have normally fired back at without a moment of hesitation. But with no room to argue this time, he simply let the silence answer for him.

Seeing his quick and yielding surrender, Yuusho gave an exaggerated shake of his head, pushing himself off the wall with an exasperated sigh.

“Oh dear, oh dear. I can’t very well let a man who’s supposed to be my rival show up to a party with hair like <em>that</em>, can I?”

<em>Since when did I become your rival?</em> The question sprung in Masachika;’s mind. <em>And you’re the one with weird hair.</em>

Still, realizing what Yuusho was about to do, he swallowed his jabs, letting them die behind his teeth.

“Here, lower your head a little.”

Doing as he was told, Mascahika bent his knees slightly, allowing Yuusho to shovel a small amount of hair wax with his fingers before running them through his hair, closely observing the reflection in the mirror. He worked the wax in with confidence, giving volume to his hair and shaping it with remarkable ease.

“Hmm… Oh well, we ideally should’ve started with blowing your hair dry but… I suppose this does it for now, huh?”

Though he still didn’t seem all that satisfied, Yuusho finally took his hands to his pockets. It only barely passed by his own standards, but it looked noticeably better than whatever Masahika had been able to conjure up at home.

“…Thanks man. I’ll say that honestly this time, Kiryuuin.”

“Is that so? Well in that case, make sure you can do it yourself next time, okay?”

“…Yeah. Seriously, thank you.”

Thanking Yuusho once more and washing his hands, Masachika hurried toward Class 1-B.

“Phew… Thank God. She’s not here yet.”

Sliding open the classroom’s door, he let out a sigh of relief, his shoulders finally loosening after observing that the partner that he’d been expecting had yet to arrive. He glanced at the clock. 3:40 p.m.

<em>The party starts at 4:30 while doors open at… wait, it was 3:30, wasn’t it? I should prolly head over soon… No, wait, Alya and I aren’t even part of the event staff; we don’t gotta be there early…</em>

With that thought in mind, Masachika looked out over the school grounds, still unable to quite settle down. Elegantly dressed students arrived one after another beyond the front gate in the distance.

<em>Oooh… Just what you’d expect from a school full of rich kids. Everyone’s reaaaally going all out here, huh…?</em>

Looking down at his off-the-rack suit, Masachika once again felt as if he’d wandered off into another world. Still, there was no point in crying over spilt milk. He had to, at the very least, carry himself with confidence. And just as he recalled the etiquette and bearing drilled into him from his days back in the Suou household…

Buzz.

Vibration rattled against his leg.

“Hm?”

It stopped after about two more pulses. Fishing out his phone from his pocket, Masachika made out a single message from Alisa.

Come pick me up.

“Come pick you up…? What, from the student council office?”

All the girls on the student council—including Alisa—were all preparing for the party in the student council room. That naturally and obviously included changing. It made Masachika stand frozen on the spot, hesitating for a moment over whether it was really okay to go there just because she’d asked him to.

“I mean… surely everyone’s done by now if she’s telling me to come, no?” he murmured, his words sounding more like an argument he was making to himself. “But like, why does she need <em>me</em> to pick her up…?”

Cocking his head in confusion, he left the classroom and made his way over to the student council office.

Arriving in no time, he knocked on the door.

“Yes,” a voice answered from the inside. He swung the door open.

“Alya? Did something hap… pen…?”

The question withered away the instant he laid his eyes on the girl standing inside.

“Ah,” Alisa looked up at him with a smile. “Thank you, Masachika-kun.”

“……”

She was dressed in an evening gown, and her hair had been pulled back into a braided updo. She looked even more mature than usual, helped along by the light touch of makeup she wore. More than anything… she was so stunningly beautiful that it robbed him of his words.

<em>Huh…? She… feels a little… different, somehow?</em>

The impression that struck Masachika felt completely different from when he had seen her in a dress at the cultural festival’s after party. And it surely wasn’t because of her hairstyle or makeup…

It was instead the difference in the radiance that shone from within Alisa herself.

<em>Specifically that… sort of fragile… or rather vulnerable side to her…? It’s as if it’s gone…</em>

Her ethereal, almost celestial aura that usually seemed to bloom around her had completely dissipated, a dignified grace that blossomed from confidence and pride unfurling in its place. It was as though a fairy had descended to earth and became human… And it was precisely because of that reason that her beauty felt even all the more detached from reality.

“Masachika-kun?” The very fairy Masahika had been dazing over broke the spell he had fallen under.

“Ah, no, uh…” he stumbled over his words. “Ah, um… it’s just, you look absolutely beautiful.”

“O-oh? Thank you… Um, Masachika-kun, you look great too, you know?”

Perhaps realizing that his unusually flustered demeanor meant that he was truly speaking from the heart, Alisa bashfully averted her gaze. Her sudden shyness brought a faint smile to his lips, easing the butterflies that had been fluttering in his stomach.

“<em>Haaah</em>…” he let a sigh of relief escape. “Thanks.”

Taking a moment to clear his throat, he repeated the question he’d asked earlier.

“Uhhh, did something happen? You said to come pick you up…”

“Eh? Ah, about that…”

Twirling her hair around her finger, Alisa bashfully averted her gaze once more.

【I just… wanted you to be the first to see me…】 she murmured under her breath.

<em>Gah!</em>

Those unbearably endearing words, spoken in Russian as they slipped from the lips of a girl so beautiful she seemed beyond this world nearly made Masachika choke. Even so, he somehow managed to hold his composure by a thread, delivering his customary response.

“What was that?”

“I said it’s a gentleman’s duty to pick up and escort a lady, is it not?”

“Ah, that’s right, isn’t it?”

Hearing the very word “gentleman” spoken from Alisa’s lips, Masachika quietly gathered himself, drawing upon every bit of etiquette the Suou household had drilled into him as a kid as he gracefully offered her his arm.

“Shall we?”

“Y-yes.”

Maybe a little intimidated by his smooth, unexpectedly polished movements, Alisa timidly reached her arm out…

Only to, for some reason, pull it back.

“Hm? Alya?” Masachika cast a puzzled glance her way.

She gave no response and lowered her eyes, dodging his gaze as her thoughts seemed to drift somewhere far away.

“Today is… Christmas Eve, isn’t it…?”

Her words were hesitant and her voice shook, each syllable catching in her throat.

Masachika could only blink once. “Yeah.”

He cocked his head to its side, searching her face as to why she was bringing something so obvious up now, while the very girl stood before him and held her silence.

One second turned to two, and then to several.

Then, all at once, she raised her head, holding him firmly in an oddly unwavering gaze.

“Step outside for a bit!”

“Huh? Wha—?”

“Just do it!”

Without warning, she shoved him out of the room and slammed the door shut, leaving Masachika standing there with his mouth hanging open.

“U-uhhh…?”

<em>“Wait there! Whatever happens, don’t open the door until I call you! No matter who comes!</em>

“Uh… okay.”

Had he somehow rubbed her the wrong way? The thought crossed his mind for a moment, but he quickly dismissed it. It didn’t seem to be the case.

Still, despite not having the foggiest idea what was going on, the lady here had assigned him the role of doorman, so he obediently followed her orders.

A little over ten minutes later…

<em>“Y-you’re okay to come in now.”</em>

“Oka—”

<em>“Ah! There’s no one else, right!?”</em>

“There isn’t, but…”

<em>“A-alright… Come in.”</em>

Hearing the stiff tension beneath her words from the other side of the door, Masachika cautiously reached for the handle.

“W-what’s up with you…?”

Slowly, he opened the door. Alisa stood on the other side.

Merely laying eyes upon her robbed him of his words again, the impact of her beauty not diminishing by the slightest even though it was already his second time seeing her like this…

Only now, the dress she wore had changed completely.

“!?!?”

It was now as inky as the night sky, its dark surface broken by corsages of blue roses that seemed to bloom like fireworks crackling across the horizon. The obsidian fabric contrasted her pristine white skin that shimmered in the light, boldly exposed like freshly fallen snow beneath the winter sky…

Or rather, her boobs!!! More importantly, those were her boobs!!!

<em>They’re huuuge! Gah, where did her bra even go!?</em>

Barely forcing those thoughts right back down his throat, Masachika hurriedly reached behind himself and slammed the door shut. Yet, even as he did, he simply could not tear his eyes away from you know what: those magnificent peaks that boldly boinged outward and the alluring valley that formed between them.

<em>No, no, calm down, Masachika. Alya’s not that busty compared to Masha-san or whatnot if we’re being objective here. It’s just her shoulders and waist are unbelievably slender that it makes her look larger than she really is. Calm down, they’re not</em> that <em>big if you look at it…</em>

Still standing there frozen on the spot, Masachika continued to let his eyes wander.

<em>Who am I kidding, they’re freaking huge. She’s, like, basically straight out of a manga or something, ain’t she?</em>

Even after an attempt to analyze the situation as rationally as possible, the sight before him was so mind-boggling that he was at a complete loss for what to do.

Lightly tugging at the hem of her skirt, Alisa shot a teasing smirk his way as she struck a coquettish pose.

“!!!?”

Her gleaming white leg gradually came into view… and then her left as well. A suuuper surprise.

<em>No, seriously, where did her bra really go!?</em>

Unable to find where their straps were supposed to be, Masachika’s ears filled with a scream he refused to let escape.

It was strange. The dress objectively covered far more than a swimsuit ever would, yet the crucial areas a normal swimsuit would have served to keep protected felt as fragile and bare as armor merely made of paper. It could only be described as an imbalance that dangled his brain on the edge of bugging out.

Alisa’s grin curved upwards even more.

【Your present is… M… E…♡】

“……”

Her dress, though dark as a moonless ocean, gleamed with a bewitching sheen as it traced along her waist as it arched sharply like a bow. It emphasized the curves brought about by her figure sharply, as if it existed solely to frame and accentuate her supreme beauty. Just catching a few glimpses of the patches of her pure white flesh it left out exposed felt illegal and so… alluringly sensual that it threatened to take Masachika’s breath away.

Such a sight, combined with the sheer devastation of her sweetly whispered woo in Russian, was more than enough to leave him beyond flushed like a rose caught beneath the summer sun. His mind simply froze.

“……”

“……”

“……”

Just how long did the two of them remain staring at each other like that?

Before she knew it, Alisa, too, glowed with a faint flush that gradually stretched from her collarbone toward her chest…

<em>Ah, she’s got blood flowing through her. Wooow, so she really is a living human after all, huuuh?</em>

While Masachika coped with an absurd comment that drifted through a corner of his mind, Alisa—her fair skin now stained a soft cherry pink—spun around on her heel.

“I’m going to die.”

Her tone was frighteningly serious, so no-nonsense that it practically punched Masachika right out of his trance, making him jump right on the spot.

“H-hey! Wait a sec!” he quickly yanked her hand, stopping her as she started toward the window. “Don’t go dyin’ dressed like that!!!”

“Dressed like that.” Words that perhaps made Alisa suddenly aware of what she was wearing. Frantically covering her chest, she shot a sharp glare Masachika’s way, a complete reversal from just moments ago.

“D-don’t stare at me like that!”

“You’re asking the impossible…” Masachika let out a long sigh to what was quite simply an utterly unreasonable complaint.

<em>Or rather, you’re not even really hiding them properly anyway… And honestly, trying to actually makes it feel more ero… Ahem.</em>

To begin with, the dress made her side-boob situation basically a complete disaster if she was concerned with keeping herself covered.

<em>So, like, even if you try to cover it like that…</em>

Wisely keeping that observation to himself, Masachika pressed a finger to his temple.

“Uhhh, so…” he flicked his wrist toward her. “What’s the whole deal with this here?”

“Th-the handicrafts club, um, basically forced it… on me…”

Masachika pinched the bridge of his nose. “Those girls again…”

Hearing the details from Alisa, it all began once the members of the handicrafts club had heard that the members of the student council would be attending the Christmas party, apparently pleading every female member to accept one of their dresses to the point of worship.

<em>So the reason why it fits her so perfectly is ’cause they’d already gotten her measurements for that whole crossdressing thing earlier, huh?</em>

“Huh?” Masachika put the pieces together. “So that means they got to Masha-san and Sarashina-senpai too?”

“…Theirs are there, okay?”

Following Alisa’s gaze, Masachika spotted four garment bags lined up on the sofa. He only needed a quick glance to see that they looked full. As expected, everyone else had decided to go with their own dresses.

<em>I mean, that figures, yeah?</em> Masachika looked Alisa up and down. <em>Alya’s one looking like this means Masha-san’s would’ve probably been even more insane, so…</em>

“Masachika-kun?” Alisa fixed him with an icy stare.

“Y-yes! What is it?”

“I-I told you to quit staring at me!”

Raising her left hand, she firmly swatted his face to the side, drawing a harsh, involuntary grunt from deep in his throat.

“Guh!”

Palm still pushed on his cheek, she forced him toward the door.

Fearing his neck might snap if things continued like this, Masachika whipped his head to the side before he could meet her eyes again.

Only after he stumbled away did Alisa finally pull her hand back.

“I-I figured I’d try it on since they made it for me, but… Geez! As I thought, I’m changing back! I’m changing back, so get out!”

“…Okaaay.”

With Alisa somehow having the patience to provide an excuse, Masachika left the room in spite of his heart lightly tugging at him to turn back.

Now standing with his back against the door outside, he let out a long, deep sigh.

<em>I mean, I’m somehow… relieved that Alya’s still Alya, I guess…</em>

The way Alisa held her shoulders during the closing ceremony had already suggested the bearing of a student council president. And then there was the way she stood in front of him just moments ago, so breathtakingly beautiful and draped in pure elegance that using just words to describe her charm felt insulting. Witnessing her in both of those ways had made Masachika feel as though she had somehow moved to a place far beyond his reach…

Yet in the end, it had apparently never been like that at all.

<em>No, but… maybe it’s only a matter of time.</em>

The vigor in Alisa’s eyes flashed across his mind as he relived watching her speech from the wings of the stage just a few hours earlier. She had been pressed by an immense pressure, facing the expectations of the audience that had been pushed to its absolute limit after his own introduction. And yet, even when blinded under such a spotlight, she had spoken of her ideals boldly, not hinting at even the slightest bit of hesitation all the way to the end.

<em>There’s no way I… hell, not even Yuki… could’ve pulled something like that.</em>

It was a conviction that Masachika genuinely swore by. It was all he was sure about at this moment: he could never measure up to her.

He had to admit it. Alisa’s rate of growth, the speed at which she moved forward, had far surpassed anything that he could have ever imagined. It was a pace that was, without a doubt, faster than… even his own.

“……”

Just how much longer would he be able to stay by her side, you may ask? Experience, connections, communication, they were all things that Masachika had a slight edge over. They were reasons as to why she still needed him as someone who could support her.

However… little by little, Alisa was reaching a point where she no longer needed him even for that. And that was what today’s closing ceremony had made crystal clear.

<em>Oh well, all I can do is keep hanging on, huh?</em> Masachika reassured himself, as if cutting off his thoughts.

He had already decided to stop hanging his head over his own inadequacies, realizing that dwelling on them only kept him standing still. Even if it meant that the day eventually came when she left behind…

Because until then, all he could do was keep trailing her footsteps with everything he had. No matter the consequences.

Clack. The sound of the latch clicking open sent a jolt through Masachika as the door swung open from behind him.

“…Sorry to keep you waiting,” Alisa peeked through.

“…Yeah.”

She had changed back into her original evening gown, fidgeting as she awkwardly failed to meet his gaze…

With the knot in his stomach finally unravelling, yet a quiet sting of disappointment taking its place, Masachika once again offered her his arm.

“Well then, shall we?”

“…Yes.”

Nodding, Alisa properly entwined her arm around his at last. The two of them slowly began to walk.

Waltzing through the hallway and descending a flight of stairs, they exited through the door at the end of the corridor, heading toward the gymnasium where the party was being held. Despite there still being some time before it officially began, the lively atmosphere overflowing from the venue suggested that quite a crowd had already gathered.

Inhaling the scene from afar, Masachika found himself stopped in his tracks before he even realized…

“Hm?” Alisa drifted her eyes up to him. “What’s wrong?”

“…Nothing.”

His denial came almost reflexively, but the reason was obvious. His familiar sense of self-deprecation: an inferiority complex that bore its fangs once again upon the realization they were about to draw the eyes around them as he entered with the girl by his side.

<em>I’m not worthy of being by her side as her partner.</em>

<em>We simply don’t match no matter how you look at it.</em>

These were the thoughts that kept him rooted to the spot.

<em>No, I mean… that reaaally is a given by just looking at us, huh…? Haaah, it’s time like these I really wish I was still in that whole invincible mode.</em>

Completely unaware of the mockery that clung onto him like a curse he couldn’t shake, Alisa—who had been looking at him with a confused look tinged with concern—flashed him a mischievous smile.

“Could it be… you didn’t want to show me in my dress to anyone else?”

A question so overly full of herself that it would’ve been easy to laugh off had it come from any other person.

Masachika could only offer a wry, helpless smile. “…Yeah, I guess so. I s’pose you’re just so beautiful that I can’t help but want to keep you all to myself, Alya.”

Alisa jolted backward, her entire body tensing in sudden shock.

“…Really?” she squeaked, catching herself far too late. “Well… I suppose I don’t mind… if that’s the case? But even so…”

Unable to bring herself to finish her words, she brushed her hair back in an attempt to hide her embarrassment, all the while Masachika could only furrow his brows, his expression clouded with confusion as he tried to trace where her words were leading to.

Shooting a few sideway glances toward him, Alisa spoke again.

“If what you want is to dance together alone, like at the cultural festival’s after party… then I’m fine with that too, you know? I’m the one being escorted, after all. I’ll go along with your lead.”

Her words, declared so boldly, conveyed her absolute trust and willingness to fall right into his arms… It instantly blew away all the sense of inferiority that had wrapped its thorns around Masachika’s heart.

<em>Yeah, she’s right, huh…? I can’t afford to be tormented by this stupid inferiority complex if she’s saying something like this. I really ain’t no man if I can’t hold my head high here, am I?</em>

Feeling a surge of omnipotence coursing through his veins, Masachika felt his invincible mode reactivate once again.

“Thanks for having that much faith in me,” he shot her a fearless grin. “…But honestly, if anything, I actually feel like showing you off to everyone right now.”

“Oh my, is that so?” Alisa’s lips curved up into a provocative smirk. “Well then, let’s hold our heads high and give everyone something to look at, shall we?”

Even the sheer insolence in her tone was ridiculous. Masachika could only chuckle, throwing his hands up in defeat.

“Alrighty, shall we go, my princess?”

“Yes. Let’s, shall we?”
