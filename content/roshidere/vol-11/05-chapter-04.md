“Yes.”

It was currently the final day of the three-day weekend following the turbulent student assembly and the furious hunt for the mastermind. Ayano, who’d been ordered to wait in everyday clothes inside of Yuki’s room at the Kuze residence, stepped outside in response to Yuki’s call. Walking down the hallway, she then opened the door to the living room… and froze.

“Ayano! Great work on the spy operation!” Yuki chimed.

“Good job. Make sure to rest well today,” Masachika added.

Her two masters welcomed her with smiles. They were both dressed in… none other than a butler and maid uniform respectively.

“?????”

As mentioned earlier, Ayano was, at this very moment and having been instructed by Yuki, in an everyday outfit: a stark contrast to Yuki, who was presently dressed as a maid, and Masachika, who was dressed like a butler.

<em>W-what… what sort of situation is this exactly?</em> Ayano asked herself, still maintaining her polite tone despite her confusion.

Of course, her beloved masters were capable of wearing even servant uniforms with elegant perfection, and yet, saying it suited the both of them was something that Ayano herself wasn’t sure was appropriate.

But even before that, what was more pressing was that, with the present situation, it was almost as if… their usual positions had been reversed, and that she had become the esteemed young lady of the house.

“…??”

Faced with an utterly baffling situation, Ayano swept her gaze, looking for Kyoutarou, the head of the household, as if she were seeking help… However, he was nowhere to be found. Only then did she recall that he had gone off to the Suou household earlier at noon.

“You know what day it is today, Ayano?” Yuki asked, forcing her frozen brain to move and figure out what holiday today was.

“…Today is November 23rd: Labor Thanksgiving Day<a href="#TN1" id="Return1"><sup>[1]</sup></a>, is it not?” Ayano answered, albeit after a short deliberation.

“Right! In other words, it’s the day to give our thanks for our servant’s loyal service, y’know!?”

“So with that in mind… we were thinking about doing our best to take care of you today, Ayano,” Masachika added. “Here, I even fried up some of your favorite donuts.”

“Go on, go on. Sit, sit.”

Urged by the both of them, Ayano stiffly sat down in the chair. In front of her was a deep plate, piled high with Masachika’s homemade donuts.

“Alright, here you are,” Yuki said, setting down a freshly brewed cup of tea.

“Itadakimasu…”

Still unable to fully process what was happening, Ayano brought the teacup to her lips as told.

“<em>Ghk</em>!? Hot!” she winced, having taken a sip of the tea without thinking, immediately pulling her lips away from the intense heat.

“Ah! Why didn’t you blow on it!? Onii-chan, ice!”

“R-right!” Masachika said, frantically getting a glass of cold water filled with ice and handing it to her.

“Th-thank you very much, Masachika-sama…”

With the tip of her tongue throbbing and stinging, Ayano immediately took a mouthful of cold water, along with a single ice cube, pressing it against where her tongue had been burned.

“<em>Geez</em>, Ayano, you’ve got to be more careful, you know? And also, no using ‘-sama’ today, ’kay?” Yuki whined, earning a silent questioning look from Ayano, who continued to press the ice against the inside of her lips with her tongue.

In response, Yuki raised her index finger sharply.

“Today, you’re calling us Yuki-chan and Chika-kun, just like in the old days!” she declared

“!?”

“Ah, and no polite speech either, ’kay?”

“!?”

Startled, Ayano nearly choked on her ice, instinctively about to spit it out before barely managing to stop herself at the last second.

“W-whoa, you okay? Did you choke?” Yuki asked, gently patting Ayano’s back with the palm of her hand, seeing her puff out her cheeks as her eyes darted around in a dazed confusion.

Somehow managing to avoid the rudeness of spitting out what had been in her mouth, Ayano used the time it took for the ice to melt to steady her thoughts.

“Uhhh, am I to take that as an orde—”

“No polite speech.”

“<em>Auuhh</em>…”

Despite being told that, the polite speech that had been so ingrained into her body wouldn’t come off so easily. With no other choice, Ayano reluctantly turned her gaze to the donuts in front of her, putting off her reply.

“Itadakimasu…” she murmured, bringing her hands together before carrying a donut to her mouth.

Its crisp texture, unique to freshly fried food, immediately hit her first, followed by the warmth of its fluffy dough melting on her tongue, spreading a gentle sweetness throughout her mouth.

“<em>Fwaaah</em>…”

Without thinking, a blissful, ecstatic sigh slipped from the back of her throat.

It was simple, yet not overly sweet, and with a light texture that never grew tiresome. Truly exquisite. All the more so because it’d been made by one of her deeply respected and loved masters.

“<em>*Munch</em>…<em>*</em>”

“I-it is truly very delicious, Masachika-sa—”

“How’s the tea? Is it good?”

“Yes,” Ayano almost said, stopping herself halfway. “<em>Yeah</em>. It’s good.”

“Glad to hear that.”

“Alright then, can you lift your hips and turn a li’l to the right~?” she asked. “Yep, yep. Now then, since you’ve been worn out from all your daily work, Ayano… no, m’lady Ayano-sama, we’ll give you a massage.

<em>Way too much</em>, she was about to say, unable to as she gasped at the sight of Yuki suddenly kneeling at her feet.

“Ooooh~, our client sure is stiff down here, riiight~?” Yuki chimed.

“Really?” Masachika asked. “Her shoulders aren’t really, though…”

“Nope. Just wanted to say it.”

“The heck’s that s’posed to mean?”

“U-um, this is… honestly already… enough…”

“T-that’s not what I meant. Doing such a thing like this is…”

“It’s fine, it’s fine. Look, relaaaax<em>~</em>.”

“<em>*Munch, munch, munch, munch, munch, munch, munch</em>.<em>*</em>”

“<em>Fwahaha</em>, you’re so cute, Ayano! Your munching face’s the cutest thing! I seriously want to watch it forever!”

“Then I shall continue eating until my stomach nearly bursts!”

“<em>Nnh…</em> It feels good… It feels good… I think?”

“Haha. Oh well, it’s probably hard to tell at first, huh?”

Different from how her grandmother washed her hair, the sensation of her scalp being tugged upward in firm movements left her unsure with how she was supposed to react…

“Alrightyyy, I’ll give you a foot massage on this side, ’kaaay<em>~</em>? …<em>Hyah!</em>” Yuki exclaimed, mimicking the sound of a martial artist doing a karate chop.

“……”

“Huh? Did that not work? How ’bout this one?” she asked, moving onto Ayano’s other foot.

“……”

“N-no way… Are you just… super healthy…!?”

Nope, it was working: Ayano was just enduring it all like usual. Pain on this level was nothing but a mere reward if it came from one of her masters.

Still, setting all that aside, the situation of being attended to by the both of them brought on a sensation deep within her stomach that could only be described as part anxiety and part fear…

“Ah…! Hngh!” Ayano gasped as she squirmed in reaction.

“How’s that? Starting to feel all good?”

“Y-yes.”

“Your polite speech’s back, Ayano.”

“Y-yeah…”

What was more, facing the pressure of not being able to use her normal polite speech only made that sensation build even more and more.

“Alright, next up’s her back,” Masachika chimed.

“’Kaaaay,” Yuki agreed. “Alrighty then, m’lady. Let’s get you on the bed, okaaaay~?”

With Yuki calling her “m’lady” once again, something inside of her finally snapped.

“Eee<em>—</em>”

““?””

“Eeeeeeeeeeeeeek!!!”

“Woooah!?”

“What’s wrong!?”

“Eeeeeeeeeeeeeek!!! Eeeeeeeeeeeeeek!!!”

““Did Ayano break!?”” the siblings yelled in unison.

Clutching her head as she let out bizarre cries, Ayano rolled around on the floor before curling up into a small ball face-down, breathing in ragged breaths.

“…Please ride me.”

Her gaze remained fixed on the floor as she practically squeezed these words out.

“Huh?”

“Come again?”

“I beg you… Please… ride me…!”

“R-ride you…?” Masachika murmured, bewildered.

“Eh? You mean like this?”

Complying with Ayano’s request, Yuki settled onto Ayano’s back, allowing her to timidly feel the pressure of her hips.

“<em>Ahh</em>…<em>!</em>”

That weight on her back. The pain in her arms and legs. Such sensations came together to wash away all the guilt and tension that had built up throughout her entire body, dissolving them with a soft, fizzing release.

<em>At this moment, Yuki-sama is… nothing but a servant, so I… who has become her chair, am therefore even lower…</em> Ayano thought, using such wild logic to cope with the present situation. <em>It is like I’m furniture. No, maybe livestock? Heh… Heheh.</em>

“Eh? What’s up with this girl laughing like that…? That’s scary,” Yuki remarked.

“Is she… kicking down her self-respect in order to stay mentally stable…?”

“Shouldn’t we be just calling it self-deprecation at this point…? Oh weeeeell, might as well just go ahead and fondle her ass since we’re here,” Yuki slyly said, taking full advantage of the situation at hand. “Ohhh~, ohhh~, now ain’t that quite a nice ass, huh?”

“Stop with the casual sexual harassment already,” Masachika grumbled, whacking his sister right in the head.

“Ow… Anyway, banning her in everyday clothes and the whole ‘no polite speech’ thing looks to be the right choice for now, right? For her mental stability, I mean.”

“You’re the one who had the bright idea to change how she spoke in the first place…”

It was now after that exchange.

With Masachika and Yuki having changed into their casual home clothes, Ayano had at long last finally returned to her usual state of mind.

The three of them now sat at the table facing one another, with Masachika having lowered his head with a serious expression.

“Well, um… we somehow kinda ended up fooling about, so… sorry, okay?” he apologized. “For putting you through all that stress.”

“Eh…? N-no, that isn’t—”

“No, hear me out.”

“……”

Reacting to his solemn tone, and realizing that he was now talking about more than just what had gone down earlier, Ayano swallowed the words that had instinctively risen to her lips.

“I had given you a painful role to play, and ended up doing something that betrayed your devotion on top of it. I’m truly, very sorry,” Masachika admitted, apologizing with another deep bow before relaxing his expression slightly as he continued. “And also, I’m… honestly grateful that you worried so much about me to the point where you had even hurt your heart for my sake. Thank you.”

Hearing his sincere words, his honest appreciation for all that she had done behind the scenes, Ayano felt a gentle warmth spreading from deep within her chest. Her eyes unexpectedly grew hot out of nowhere, and she clenched her hands tightly beneath the table, blinking over and over as she steadied her throat.

“…Such praise is far too kind for me,” she said, taking a deep breath to suppress the swell of such immense emotion before offering her own words in order to keep her master from worrying. “And… I do not consider what you did as an act of betrayal. Certainly, I had been surprised at the time, but… I understand that it was something unavoidable as opposing candidates.”

“…Gotcha.”

With Masachika nodding in visible relief, Ayano let out a quiet breath of her own, the tension releasing from her shoulders.

“And let me thank you too, okay?” Yuki chimed in, shifting the focus to her. “You really saved us this time, Ayano… Had you not been there, Nonoa-san might’ve completely crashed the election for me and Onii-chan… We might’ve really lost the very stage to clash our conflicting motivations before we even got the chance… So, really, thank you. I’m truly happy that you’ve stayed as my partner, Ayano.”

The moment Ayano heard those words, the dam that she had been barely holding back inside finally burst all at once. Her vision was swallowed by tears, and droplets soon began to stream down her cheeks one after another.

“Th-thank you… very much… <em>*hic*</em>!”

Squeezing out only those words from her trembling chest, Ayano lowered her head and let her tears fall.

With the sharp scraping sounds of chairs being pulled back from right in front of her, she then felt the warmth of her beloved masters against her back and her head.

<em>Ah…</em>

In truth, Ayano had been anxious all along.

Though despite thinking that Nonoa was plotting something when she had faced her on that very emergency stairwell, she had still, in that moment, instinctively leapt straight into her grasp.

What was Nonoa trying to do? Would it harm her two masters? She had no certainty about it all.

<em>Could my very actions themselves be a betrayal against my masters?</em>

<em>Would they be disappointed in me if I confessed that I was deceiving someone like this?</em>

It had been doubts like these that had swirled endlessly in her mind up until this point.

But now… all of that anxiety had finally been swept away. Such a relief, combined with the reassurance that she had truly been of help to the both of them, now caused all that pent-up anxiety and fear she had been shouldering to spill out as tears.

“Thank… goodness… Oh thank goodness…” Ayano uttered, continuing to sob quietly as she felt Yuki’s arms as they gently embraced her and Masachika’s hand as it softly stroked her back.

A peaceful silence then passed for a while… As the last of her tears finally stopped and she began to calm down, an intense wave of embarrassment then suddenly washed over her entire body.

“U-um… um…”

Murmuring in a voice as faint as a mosquito’s buzz, Ayano lightly shifted her body, with her two masters letting go and returning to their own seats across from her, understanding her intent. Feeling a small trace of loneliness at that, Ayano then stood up with the scrape of her chair.

“U-um, please excuse me… I will go wash my face…!” she said, bowing hurriedly as she did before immediately dashing to the bathroom.

Taking the opportunity to fix her hair as well, she returned to the living room after gathering her composure.

“Thank you… for waiting…”

Still unable to look at the two of them out of lingering shame, Ayano returned to her seat.

“So, Ayano… What d’you wanna do?” Yuki asked gently.

“Eh…?”

“Well, I’ve been doing some self reflecting after everything that’s happened this time around, y’know…?” she confessed. “I heard you apparently told Nonoa-san that all you want is for me and Onii-chan to get along, and that got me thinking that maybe those really were your true feelings, or something… Or that you possibly didn’t want to go up against Onii-chan in the elections too, I guess~? Things like that… The more I thought about it, the more I’ve realized that I’ve never actually properly asked what <em>you</em> want, Ayano.”

Pausing to scratch her head, Yuki then gave a wry smile.

“So, I’ll ask you again, okay?” she asked again once more. “Ayano, what do <em>you</em> want to do? What do you want <em>us</em> to do?”

Looking over, Ayano saw that Masachika was waiting for her answer as well, wearing a gentle expression just like Yuki’s.

<em>As for myself…</em> she thought, almost confessing the desire hidden within the depths of her heart… only to swallow it at the very last moment.

A wish so stained with such a selfish desire that it was something, as a servant, must never be said.

So… instead, Ayano voiced a different wish.

“I… would like to play cards.”

“…Cards?”

“Yes, like in the old days… I would like to play cards together. The three of us.”

Turning to each other at her answer, Yuki and Masachika both gave slightly awkward smiles.

<em>I understand that</em>, Ayano thought.

She knew that this was not the answer her masters had truly wanted her to give.

“…Alright, as I requested, let’s play cards now, shall weee~?” she said, intentionally speaking in an out-of-character tone with that in mind. “We decided today’s a day we do everything ol’ Ayano wants to do, riiight~?

“That’s right, huh?” Masachika agreed. “Now, where were these cards again…?”

Despite her two masters most likely—no… without a shadow of a doubt—noticing that she had dodged the question, they still chose to go along with it as if nothing had happened.

<em>I’m sorry… And thank you very much</em>, she apologized in her heart to those endlessly kind masters.

“Ah, found them,” Masachika called out from his room. “These are the ones I used for practicing magic and like tricks, though…”

“Oh, well let’s use those then, yeah? There aren’t any trick cards mixed in them, right?” Yuki asked.

“There aren’t, there aren’t. Uhhh, we’ve got the three of us… so is poker fine?”

“Yes, that’s not a problem,” said Ayano.

“Alrighty, we’ll need chips, hm…? Oh well, I guess chocolate’s fine, huh?”

“So the rules are… we take turns being the dealer, and have five cards per hand. You get one draw before we start betting. Sound good?”

“Yes.”

“Fufuuu<em>~</em>, I’ve actually found this interesting app the other day, ya knooow~?”

“…Would that even make it a mind game at that point?”

“Yeah, yeah, just try installing the app first, okay? Both of you,” Yuki said, nonchalantly dismissing Masachika’s valid point.

Following her suggestion, both Masachika and Ayano downloaded the app and got through its initial quick setup. The app itself apparently worked by placing your index finger on the screen while you spoke, with it judging whether your statement was a lie or the truth based on your pulse and voice. The more likely it was that you were lying, the more the needle swung to the right… That was how it basically worked.

“After each player finishes their draw and before we start betting, everyone gets to ask one question to anyone else, starting from the dealer and going clockwise…” Yuki explained. “Said person being asked must then answer while using the app. So? Think that sounds pretty interesting?”

“I… suppose so?” Ayano answered, despite still not grasping the special rules as she did.

No, or rather, she did understand the rules themselves.

<em>But what sort of strategies would they lead to?</em>

She couldn’t imagine how the game would unfold at all from it.

On the other hand, however, Masachika looked to have already read that far ahead, slowly nodding with a thoughtful expression.

“Sounds good. Let’s do it that way, yeah?”

With that, the three of them placed their phones on the table, and the game began.

The first match.

“Yuki, is your hand stronger than a two pair?”

<em>Uhhh…? Was that… a lie? Or the truth?</em> Ayano thought, struggling to keep up with the ambiguous result, just as Yuki turned toward her.

“How ’bout your hand, Ayano? Is it stronger than a two pair?” she asked.

At the very least, she understood that an honest “No” here was almost certainly a loss.

At the mixed expressions of exasperation and fond amusement from her two masters, Ayano slightly shrank her shoulders.

“Now then, it’s your turn, Ayano. Ask away.”

<em>Uhhh, Yuki-sama’s hand is most likely a two pair or better… and since Masachika had asked whether it had been, does that mean he has a two pair too?</em> she contemplated. <em>Did Yuki-sama also take that the same way? “A royal flush.”

The needle on her phone swung sharply to around ninety on a scale of a hundred.

“Ah…” Ayano muttered, realizing her mistake.

Why hadn’t she just asked the same kind of question as the other two? Wouldn’t asking something so specific, like she had, be brushed off so easily with an answer that was obviously a lie?

Still, realizing that she had completely wasted her question, Ayano could only shrink her neck in embarrassment.

“Now then, we’ll move on to betting, alright?”

“I will fold.”

“Call,” yet Masachika proclaimed the instant after.

<em>Masachika-sama is accepting her challenge…!? It can’t be, could his earlier question have been a trap, and that his hand is actually stronger than a two pair? Or maybe so?</em>

Yuki, an utter fish.

“What do <em>you</em> want us to do, Ayano?”

A question from her master, filled with nothing but absolute care.

<em>There’s nothing else I want from the two of you</em>, she almost said on instinct… only for the lie detector in her hand to catch her attention.

<em>But… I absolutely mustn’t say it.</em>

<em>I absolutely…</em>

“Like this. Forever… I want the three of us to stay close and be together. Forever, and ever…!”

“Yes.”

“Yeah.”

“Alright! Now’s the final showdown! There ain’t no point in making wishy washy li’l bets to the end! Let’s go! All in!” she declared, shoving all her chocolate forward with a dramatic clatter.

“Hey, hey, you’re actually betting it all? What was the point in all of the matches ’til now…” Masachika retorted, giving a wry smile as he pushed all his chocolates forward as well.

In response, Ayano, too, pushed forward her chocolates—far fewer in number than the other two—in front of her, and all bets were set.

“Alright then… reveal your hands! Showdown!”

At Yuki’s call, the three of them then turned over their cards…

“Wha—!? A straight flush!?” she exclaimed, dramatically clutching her head.

“Hey, hey, seriously?” Masachika added, letting out a wry laugh.

All the while, Ayano shrank her shoulders in embarrassment. Yuki, immediately breaking into a wide grin, then swept all the chocolates on the table toward her with one fell swoop and began to clap.

“Congrats, Ayano. As expected of today’s star.”

“Congrats.”

“Thank you… very much…”

Receiving both their applause, Ayano smiled shyly and shrunk her shoulders even more.

“Now then,” Masachika said, clapping his hands together once as if to reset the mood. “What do we do next? Wanna play another game? Call it winner’s privilege: you can choose whatever you like, y’know?”

“Uhhh, let’s see, hm…?” Ayano murmured. “Then, how about The Game of Life, perhaps?”

“The Game of Life, huuuh<em>~</em>? Do you have it, Yuki?”

“<em>Ahh</em>, not sure ’bout that. Oh well, I’ve probably got some video game version somewhere if it comes to it.”

With the two of them heading toward Yuki’s room as they talked, Ayano, too, rose from her chair.

And yet…

“Ah, you’re good, Ayano,” Masachika called out to her. “<em>We’ll</em> look for it.”

“Mm-hmm, mm-hmm,” Yuki agreed. “Ah, actually, sorry, but could you put the cards away?”

“Ah… Certainly.”

Settling back into her chair at their words once again… Ayano let her gaze drift down to her phone, where the lie detector app was still running.

In that instant…

<em>“You wanna go back to those days. Those days when it was just the three of you: a peaceful and happy time alone with those feelings. You never wanted some intruder barging in.”</em>

The words that Nonoa had once said resurfaced in her mind. Talking a careful glance toward the door leading to the hallway, Ayano then placed her index finger against her phone.

“I will be fine as long as the three of us get along,” she murmured. “Even if… I am no longer the second most important to each of them…”

Finishing with her words, she then stared silently at the needle on the screen.

“Hmm~? You say somethin’, Ayanooo<em>~</em>?” Yuki called out to her.

“No, it was nothing of any importance.”

Answering her call, Ayano then quietly switched her phone off.
