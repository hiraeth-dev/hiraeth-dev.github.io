Yo. Thanks for reading our fan translation of Roshidere Volume 11! We hope you enjoyed it. Feel free to take the time to rate the series on <a href="https://www.novelupdates.com/series/the-neighboring-aarya-san-who-sometimes-acts-affectionate-and-murmuring-in-russian/">Novel Updates</a> if you did!

I’d like to express my gratitude to Flaze and gorg for proofreading, Indi for helping us with typesetting the cover and colored illustrations, and to Iviera for coloring the chapter illustrations.

First of all, I want to apologize for how long I took with this volume. As I had explained on many occasions with different chapter releases, I have been really, <em>really</em>, <strong><em>really</em></strong>, busy over the past year. I am sorry for the delay, and hope that the <a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1-oSejdWuY2X_SWy8mR6YsUI9CWtuhXxc?usp=drive_link">effort</a> and <a href="https://glucosetl.xyz/about-us">quality</a> we put into our projects can make up for it.

Anyway, I kinda found this Volume pretty mid I guess? It was mostly just filler and felt more transitional to set up V12. I am honestly also getting pretty tired of Sun<sup>3</sup>’s mediocre prose and narration. Those of you who have read this series in its original Japanese might have noticed how he often suffers from very bland and direct writing—such as only telling and never showing—to the point where most of the time it’s as if he’s writing a manga/anime script as opposed to an actual novel.

Sure, it has been fun for me to rewrite/rephrase his narration with my own creative writing—such as making it more show and tell, adding more descriptive flair, or enhancing the imagery to convey the emotions of characters in a more impactful manner—especially with this volume’s epilogue; I went way beyond than what I usually already do. But at this point, it’s honestly gotten way too mundane for me.

One thing I will say, however, is that—perhaps because Sun<sup>3</sup>’s writing style is more akin to an anime/manga script than an actual novel—the dialogue in Roshidere is very colorful and vibrant. From trying to accurately convey their tones and the way they speak, to dissecting and carrying over all the nuances in the words and expressions they use—all different between each character, and even within individual characters depending on the context or setting—translating it all has been very enjoyable for me. I will miss it.

Which leads me onto my next point. Unlike Sun<sup>3</sup>, this will be the last time I address you all at the end of a Roshidere volume.

That’s right, I am dropping the series that had roped me into this stupid hobby of mine. This has actually been a decision I had made all the way back in December of 2025, stemming from the realization that my increasingly busy life due to my own personal goals and ambitions was becoming more and more incompatible with fan translating two ongoing series.

That being said, I am glad to say that I will be passing the torch on to our affiliates, <a href="https://secreftls.com/">Secre Translations</a>. They will be picking up Roshidere from V12 and onwards, so make sure to join their <a href="https://discord.gg/BeRTmj45v8">Discord</a> for further updates and news. Some of you might already know them, but to those who don’t, I assure you this series will be in good hands. I’ve known Secre for a pretty long time now, and we often discuss and help each other with our respective fan translation projects.

Volume 12 is supposed to release sometime around October, so here’s hoping it will be a banger. Again, I won’t be catching you guys there, so please anticipate it and any other future fan translated volumes from <a href="https://secreftls.com/">Secre Translations</a>. And to all you shitty MTL spam groups looking at this as an opportunity to snipe, do know I am more than prepared to make another <a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1-oSejdWuY2X_SWy8mR6YsUI9CWtuhXxc?usp=drive_link">document</a> and petition for your removal from Novel Updates (and thus basically the entire English speaking Japanese light novel community) like I had done so successfully before.

Anyway, I will still be fan translating <a href="https://glucosetl.xyz/translations/gimai-seikatsu">Gimai Seikatsu</a> for the indefinite future, so feel free to catch any future updates on our <a href="https://glucosetl.xyz/">website</a> and our <a href="https://discord.com/invite/GAMsRtrztP">Discord</a>! I’ve gotta continue locking in to finish V16 and hop on V17 (please Ghost, slow down 😭).

As always, please support the author by buying the <a href="https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975347857-alya-sometimes-hides-her-feelings-in-russian-vol-1">official translation</a>!

If you haven’t already, please take the time to read <a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1-oSejdWuY2X_SWy8mR6YsUI9CWtuhXxc?usp=drive_link">this document</a> to stay informed about the differences between a proper translation and MTL spam/slop (or ones done by an ESL speaker).
