“Ngh,” Alisa grimaced, letting a strange voice out from the back of her throat as she shot Yuki a reproachful look, deciding that she was being teased. “There’s no way a greeting like that exists.”

“Ehhh? We really <em>do</em> that sorta thing though…” Yuki refuted while sitting with her head tilted in confusion.

“Listen here…” Alisa grumbled, raising her voice tinged with a hint of irritation.

But just then… the door to the student council office swung open, and in came Masachika.

“Good work guuuys,” he sang as casually waltzed into the room.

And in the blink of an eye, Yuki set off dashing.

“To be precise: like this!!!” she shouted boldly, ripping off her refined ladylike façade as she rushed up to her brother.

Springing off the ground with a boing, she wrapped both her arms and legs around him, latching onto him completely.

“And then like this!!!”

Booming once again, this time in a deep, rough voice, Yuki rubbed her cheek against him over and over, fawning over him with a “Yaaaay<em>~</em>♪, Onii-chan’s heeere~♡,” immediately switching to a sweet tone.

And then finally, she unleashed her finishing touch.

“What’s more: this!!!”

Declaring that once more, Yuki grabbed Masachika by the shoulders to lift her own body up before locking her legs right around his chest, squeezing his head tightly against her.

“Welcome baaaack<em>~</em>♪, Onii-chaaaan<em>~</em>♡”

Still speaking in that extremely sugary voice, she rubbed her cheek against the top of his head in a series of quick, circular motions, before then glancing back over his shoulder at Alisa.

“Alright, wanna give it a go?” she asked, giving her a confident thumbs up.

“There’s no way I’d wanna, would I!?” Alisa snapped back instinctively, all the while Masachika—who had barely even stepped a foot into the room before being ambushed—muffled a strained voice from within Yuki’s chest.

<em>“…Whaf ish this?”</em>

“Hmm<em>~</em>?” Yuki hummed at her brother’s squashed voice, sliding down his body, burying her face into his neck while still clinging onto his torso. “I was just thinkin’ I’d teach Alya-san how <em>our</em> hugs work. Huggy huggy.”

“Don’t bite me.”

With his little sister still latched onto him, Masachika smacked the back of her head with an audible thwack.

<em>Eh, “bite”…?</em> Alisa chewed over his words, having noticed Masachika’s wince as Yuki sank her teeth into him. <em>Wait, this feels like déjà vu…</em>

Masachika being bitten, and on the neck at that. Such a combination sent her thoughts down memory lane as she furrowed her brow deeply… Recalling it all, she remembered how she had noticed the bite marks on Masachika’s neck from back when she had visited the Kuze household during the summer break.

<em>Ah!</em> she gasped as the realization hit her. <em>So</em> that <em>was this sort of thing…</em>

It was something that Masachika had simply brushed off as a result of fooling around with pro-wrestling moves at the time… But still, judging from the scene unfolding in front of her now, this kind of dynamic looked to be an everyday occurrence.

“……”

The sight of someone else throwing her whole entire body onto Masachika, the man she loved, pressing her lips against his neck. And worse still, the fact that the girl in front of her was doing it as a mere casual greeting.

【But he’s <em>my</em> partner…】 Alisa muttered quietly in Russian, pushed on by a small flame that seemed to flicker to life deep within her chest.

As if sensing that stir of emotion, Yuki turned her head while still clinging to Masachika.

“Ooooh, Alya-san’s jealousy sure is heavy,” she mocked, giving an exaggerated shiver before turning back to her brother and tacking on a lame pun. “Guess that makes you a <em>heavy</em>sitter.”

“Quit making it sound like I’m some sorta weird babysitter.”

“But you <em>are</em> a real babysitter, y’knooow!?”

“I don’t need a baby <em>this</em> big.”

“Waaah<em>,</em> waaaaaahhh<em>~</em>” Yuki wailed, flailing around as she made baby-like cries while still clinging to her brother.

“D’you have like no sense of shame?” Masachika jested in exasperation.

With the whole scene leaving Alisa completely dumbfounded, Yuki set her hands on Masachika’s shoulders in the next moment, stretching her body backward into a deep arch.

“Whoa, careful…!”

With Masachika frantically leaning back in an effort to fight for his balance, she ended up staring straight at Alisa from a mildly mortifying upside down position.

“So yeah, it’s basically just <em>this</em> all day every day at our place. Think ya can handle that?”

“…Impossible,” Alisa answered in an instant, her voice devoid of any life.

“Let your guard down there, didn’tchaaaa?” Yuki grinned, letting her long black hair hang down toward the floor. “’Cause Alya-san, you never thought I’d show this side of myself at school, eh?”

With the nail having been hit on the head, Alisa could only catch her breath in surprise.

“Ya fool! I’m <em>always</em> lookin’ for any chance to get all lovey-dovey with Onii-chan!” Yuki declared with a triumphant smirk. “Didja really think I’d hold back around you just ’cause my true nature’s out, Alya-san!? What a shame! If ya can’t cut yourself into this sorta vibe, then there’s no room for you at all as long as I’m around!!!”

Her words were so rough that it actually made Alisa stagger a step back, flinching in shock to the type of provocation you’d only hear from a stereotypical yankee.

Snorting lightly in response, Yuki then raised herself back up, clinging onto Masachika again as she rested her shoulder on his chin.

“So yeah, I guess Onii-chan’s gotta marry Ayano at this point, hmmm~?”

“Eh!?” Alisa gasped lightly in response to Ayano being suddenly thrown into the mix.

“Wait, give me a sec…” Masachika let out a worn out voice on the other hand, seemingly not having the bandwidth to react. “You’re hitting me with <em>way</em> too much info here; it’s actually freaking me out. Seriously…”

“Just marry Ayano alreadyyy,” Yuki continued without a pause, ignoring the fact that she was completely overwhelming him. “Let the three of us live happily together forever, okay?”

Sharpening his expression in an instant, Masachika glanced toward the door behind him… Specifically, toward Ayano on the other side.

“<em>Fufufu</em>…” Yuki chuckled. “It sure sounds fun, y’know?”

Her softly whispered words left Masachika momentarily silent…

And yet, after a brief moment, he let out a small chuckle of his own, gently placing a hand on her back.

“…Yeah, maybe,” he agreed.

“<em>Mhm</em>.”

“……”

Before Alisa knew it, a warm little world had formed right in front of her, one that left her out entirely, and one where Masachika’s wife had been decided on without her.

In that instant… something snapped within her.

“I’ll do it,” Alisa declared.

“Hm?”

“Oh really?”

Marching straight toward the siblings who stared at her with equally puzzled expressions, Alisa grabbed Yuki by the scruff of her neck, practically yanking her off as she placed her hand firmly on Masachika’s shoulders with an intense gaze.

“Wh-whoa, what’s gotten into you?”

“I said I’ll do it…” Alisa gruffed. “<em>That</em>. That was how greetings are at the Suou family, wasn’t it?”

‘“No, no, no, that was obviously a lie!” Masachika exclaimed, frantically shaking his head with the realization of what she was about to attempt. “There’s no way you’d pull it off even if it wasn’t anyway, you know!? You’d fall, Alya!”

“What, are you saying I’m heavy!?”

“I mean, you’re heavier and bigger compared to Yuki, no!?”

“How rude!”

“But it’s true… I mean, sure, I <em>might</em> be able to manage it if I’m allowed to hold you by your legs here, but that’ll basically just make it—”

“The station bento position<a href="#TN1" id="Return1"><sup>[1]</sup></a>, hm? I get it,” Yuki interjected.

“Stop it!!!”

“Station bento…?” Alisa questioned. “What’s that supposed to mean?”

“Well, that’s…” Masachika stammered.

“What? Say it clearly already.”

“I <em>can’t</em> say it outright; it’s why it’s slang, y’know!?”

The two continued to bicker noisily, going back and forth. A moment in which Yuki could only watch from the sidelines.

<em>“As I thought, you</em> do <em>like him, huh?”</em> she murmured quietly under her breath.

Her voice, amused yet tinged with a small sense of loneliness, never reached the arguing duo.
