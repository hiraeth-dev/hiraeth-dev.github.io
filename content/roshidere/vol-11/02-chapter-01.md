“Oh my, oh my… What a most distinguished gathering we have here,” Nonoa called out in a voice tinged with unmistakable mockery, greeting Masachika, Alisa, Yuki, and Yuusho as they stepped into the classroom at night.

Her expression was clearly one of delight, her eyes sparkling with glee as she faced the five of them wearing grim looks.

Normally, seeing a beautiful girl with such a look in her eyes would make just about anyone feel a fond warmth… Yet, when it came to Nonoa, it was an entirely different story.

“““““!”””””

What the five felt upon seeing her instead was a skin-prickling chill, a feeling that anyone who actually looked into her brown eyes at this moment would be forced to share.

True, they were vividly shining with life. And yet, because of that, her pupils stood out all the more starkly, as if they’d been gouged out and filled with a flat, uniform black, making it all the more eerie. It was akin to confronting an unknown creature disguised in the skin of a human… That kind of indescribably unsettling feeling.

“<em>Phew</em>… Told you, didn’t I, Kuze? That you should seriously think twice of making <em>that</em> your ally,” Yuusho sneered as he shook his head theatrically, whether it was his usual bravado or simply a quick attempt to recover his composure.

“……”

Masachika said nothing in response. Or perhaps, he couldn’t.

“That’s <em>my</em> line, y’know~?” Nonoa, the very person who’d just been dismissed so bluntly as “that,” reacted instead. “What’re you doing over there acting like one of the gang, Junyuushou-chan<a href="#TN1" id="Return1"><sup>[1]</sup></a>~?”

“Not saying I’m their ally or anything. It’s just it looked like you were dragging the piano club through something shady, weren’t you now? So I’m merely just cooperating temporarily to crush that li’l scheme of yours.”

“<em>I</em> was the one who brought him in,” Alisa stepped forward as she said that.

Nonoa could only widen her eyes in brief surprise in response, before bursting out into laughter while looking as if she were enjoying herself from the bottom of her heart.

“<em>Ohoho~</em>?” That’s how it is, hmm~? Welp, guess Kuzecchi wouldn’t willingly try to team up with Yuusho anyway~,” she speculated while shooting a sidelong glance at Masachika, whose mouth twisted in its corner as she hit the mark dead on, before resuming her analysis with a matter-of-fact tone. “So basically, knowing that someone was settin’ things up, Alissa here still went ahead and attended the student assembly all on her own, without a care in the world, ’cause she had Yuushou’s table-flip as insurance. You prepared a way to forcibly shut down the whole thing in advance, and purposefully dove headfirst into the trap to figure out who was plotting what.”

Laying out her deductions one after another as if they were mere idle musings, Nonoa then shifted her gaze to Alisa, who in turn wore a stern expression.

“Then, when Vice-Prez-san started screaming all about fraud, you more or less grasped the whole setup… and deliberately called for a revote to pinpoint the culprit. That’s bold, riiiight~?” she stated with a cheerful smile playing at her lips, clapping her hands as if to praise Alisa’s valiant effort.

<em>*Clap clap, clap, clap, clap.*</em>

The sound of her applause echoed, somehow chilling in the silent classroom.

An empty applause that no one else joined in on… As it came to its end, Nonoa kept her hands clasped together with a soft thud, letting her smile drop away in an instant before raising one eyebrow and lightly spreading both her arms.

“So,” she called out. “What’s this?”

This being the current situation: pressed by the five of them with Alisa at the forefront.

<em>“I’ve got absolutely no idea what’s the cause of all this. I don’t understand why you’re all here doing this to me.”</em>

That was the attitude she projected, an attitude so brazen that it seemed nothing but insolent to Masachika and the others. Even Alisa and Yuki couldn’t help but furrow their brows in response.

“Nonoa-san, you appeared to have known that something would happen even before it was decided that a student assembly was to be convened, and you hinted as much to Ayano,” the latter stated, being the first to speak as she maintained her refined ladylike smile. “Why was that?”

“Well look, I’ve got loads of acquaintances, right? Kiiinda heard some rumors from them, like there was some shaaaady stuff goin’ on to target Alissa~. I was actually originally planning to warn Kuzecchi and her ’bout it, y’know~? …But, you see, I’d already said I’d side with Ayanono, didn’t Iiii~? Welp… was never expecting her to pass all that info to you and Alissa, though.”

Nonoa had simply pretended to play out the role of a well-meaning informant. It was a smooth explanation lacking any hesitation, spun with such a carefree smile, and sounded so natural that anyone who didn’t know her true nature would instinctively have the thought, “Oh, I-I see how it is,” spring into mind as they easily found themselves accepting it outright.

However…

“““““……”””””

There was, of course, not a single person present who truly bought that story. None at all, and yet… there also wasn’t any concrete proof to decisively brand it as a lie either.

Following the body and baggage inspection of those who’d attended the student assembly earlier, eight perpetrators involved in manipulating the voting results had already been apprehended. Yet, during such an inspection, Nonoa had been cleared. No testimony linking her to any crime could be obtained from those caught as well. The only thing the eight of them—alongside Aoi, the piano club’s vice president—had said unanimously was that they had been egged on by an unfamiliar female student. A student whose physique and appearance didn’t match Nonoa’s at all… What was more, Ayano herself had not been able to obtain any incriminating statement of admission from Nonoa up until this point either. Despite never realizing that she was only pretending to be compliant while plotting otherwise, Nonoa had maneuvered with such care to ensure that she would never be allowed to be pinned down by her own words.

“I mean, it sounded like Ayanono wanted Kuzecchi and you to get along, right, Yukki?” Nonoa nonchalantly explained. “So, just to make sure that neither of you would’ve gotten caught up in any plot targeting Alissa by any chance, I went out of my way to make sure that the two of you didn’t participate in the student assembly.”

“Then what was with pulling the strings to get Ayano and Alya-san to handle the votes?” Yuki pressed further.

“There’s nothing really deep ’bout it, though? If Alissa was being targeted, then Ayanono—who knew that much—might as well stay with her. Being close together means she’d notice right away if something actually happened, right? So, in the event that Alissa actually ends up in a pinch, wouldn’t the question of whether to help her or not be up to Ayanono? Something like that?”

“In that case, wasn’t there no need to designate them as vote counters?”

“Welp, maybe you’re right… But counting the votes means you’d always be up on the stage, and therefore be able to see the whole venue, right?” Nonoa reasoned. “On the flip side, given it makes you so clearly visible from where the audience is sitting, it’d be harder for the culprits to make their move. It was simply a placement I thought worked the best both offensively and defensively. I <em>did</em> at least try to be considerate in my own way, y’know~?”

“……”

Smoothly delivered excuses with a slack, easy smile still on her face. It was a moment where Yuki could only maintain her refined smile, with lips sealed shut, in response to. She had probably judged that continuing to press the issue head-on like this would be like pushing water uphill. Masachika, too, thought that Nonoa’s excuses, though impressively bold, were flawless. So flawless, in fact, that they looped back to bordering on suspicion.

Then, maybe because she had sensed that Yuki’s offensive had now stalled, Nonoa let her expression drain away as she turned her gaze toward Ayano.

“By the way, when did you start leaking all <em>this</em>, Ayanono?” she questioned, leaning her upper body sharply to her left. “I was helping you out because you said you wanted Kuzecchi and Yukki to get along, you know~? Was that all a lie then?”

“…I had already received warnings from Yuki-sama and Masachika-sama to be cautious around you, Nonoa-san,” Ayano opened her mouth to explain after slowly blinking in response.

She spoke with a calm and careful tone, as if she were organizing her own thoughts.

“That you are exceptional when it comes to manipulating others, a genius when it comes to controlling hearts… And that you have been able to carry out numerous covert schemes until now with such talent. That is precisely why… that day, on the stairs, when you closed your distance with me…” she continued, recalling back to the time on that emergency staircase when Nonoa had pressed her with such eloquent words, urging her into self-disclosure despite them being almost complete strangers back then, “…I became wary. I had suspected that you might have some sort of plot at play when you had approached me for that reason… It was precisely why I decided to deliberately show an opening myself to observe your reaction, Nonoa-san.”

Nonoa could only narrow her eyes slightly at her words. Deliberately revealing a weakness to slip into another person’s inner circle? That was exactly the method Nonoa herself had used on Masachika prior to approaching Ayano.

<em>So I ended up being caught by the very method I used myself, huh…?</em> she lamented. <em>That was pretty careless of me, wasn’t iiit~?</em>

It was a realization that induced a strange amusement inside of her, one that made her want to applaud even as she let a wry smile creep across her face. Even so, she shook her head side to side with an aggrieved look.

“That hurts, y’know…? To think that both Kuzecchi and Yukki would look at me like that. And you too, Ayanono. All I did was listen to you sincerely while giving advice,” Nonoa spoke as if she were the victim, spitting out words that grated on the nerves.

“Do you really think such an excuse will pass?” Yuki asked.

“I don’t, y’know? But if it’s the truth, there’s nothing that could be done, could it?”

Even to her question, one that carried a subtle heightened pressure, Nonoa simply replied with a calm, feigned air of innocence.

At this, Alisa finally opened her mouth with a frustrated huff, unable to hold back any longer as she—

“!”

Yet even faster than that, Masachika stepped forward to grab her hand, stopping her from speaking. Seeing his partner turn to him in surprise, Masachika signaled with his eyes and a quick flick at her palm that he would handle this himself.

Adding to this, he directed his gaze toward Ayano’s skirt pocket, causing Alisa to fall silent with an albeit startled expression.

<em>Good, good… Looks like she’s calmed down.</em>

Relieved that he’d managed to stop Alisa from saying something rash, Masachika let out a quiet sigh of relief.

Facing Nonoa here was unexpected even for Masachika. Because of that, he hadn’t had the privilege of discussing with Alisa beforehand to coordinate how they should handle the present debacle… Still, that didn’t change the fact that they had already strategized how to handle such general situations like this before.

<em>As I thought, making sure we’re aligned in our course of action ahead of time really does matter. Right, everything’s gone according to plan up to this point. From here on, it’s all about whether I can steer this convo to the exact point we’re heading for. That’s where it counts.</em>

It was for that reason that he had to be careful with every single word from here on.

<em>The whole reason we’ve been able to crush Nonoa’s scheme and corner her this far has been entirely because of Alya. She’s got Kiryuuin on our side and caught the other perpetrators, and we’ve gotten this far thanks to that… But it’s all ’bout the battle of the words from here on out, and Alya’s got practically no experience with this kinda verbal deception. Taking on Nonoa and whoever’s out there like that would be too much for her.</em>

So from here on, Masachika wanted her to leave it to him, pouring all that intent into his gaze as he stared straight into her eyes. With that, Alisa lowered her gaze slightly before nodding and stepping half a pace back.

<em>“Do your best,”</em> came his partner’s words, brimming with nothing but trust, flowing into him through the contact of their hands.

Drawing courage from that, Masachika switched his mindset into battle mode once more.

<em>Alright… This is where it all counts, me,</em> he addressed himself, steeling his resolve as he fixed his gaze on Nonoa, something she seemed to sense as she turned her eyes toward him as well, her lips curving into an amused grin.

“So, what about it, Kuzecchi?” A question tinged with provocation then came from those lips. “Will you believe me?””

“……”

Meeting Nonoa’s probing stare with a stern one of his own, Masachika scratched at the back of his neck with his left hand.

“Yeah, I believe you,” he answered. “Oh well, can’t be helped if that’s how it is.”

His words prompted Ayano to widen her eyes as she looked toward him. Meanwhile, Nonoa’s smile deepened in satisfaction.

Her question was simply her just extending a hand toward Masachika, after all. A hand that he had firmly taken.

<em>Welp, of course you had no choice but to do that, did you now, Kuzecchi~?</em> Nonoa thought, full of certainty after hearing Masachika’s reply.

She was now sure that this conversation was, at this very moment, being recorded, most likely by Ayano. And that Masachika had no intention of cutting Nonoa loose.

<em>Why of course, right~? Because if you won’t stand a chance in the election if you cut me off, would you~?</em>

Yes, Nonoa understood. She understood that in this situation, right here and now, Masachika and Alisa had no choice but to protect her.

Her popularity was extraordinarily high within this school, after all. If her betrayal were to come to light, Masachika and Alisa would completely lose the supporters who stood behind her. On top of that, they had boldly declared to the entire student body that they would be working together as fellow student council officers if they were to be elected back during the closing ceremony of the first semester. An internal strife erupting less than half a year later would inevitably mean that people would say that they lacked the ability to bring others together.

<em>Even if, hypothetically, all the evidence of my betrayal were to come out, proving that I was entirely at fault beyond doubt, we’d still get a “Looks like they’re fighting themselves anyway, so let’s just play it safe and vote for Suou-san” type of situation. If it’ll end up being that way, then Kuzecchi’s best bet is to cover up the very fact of betrayal itself.</em>

In short, in this place and moment, Masachika and Alisa could not be Nonoa’s enemies. Yet, the instant she became certain of that, Nonoa felt her excitement rapidly drain away.

<em>Ahhh… That’s boring, right?</em>

It was essentially all just a dead rubber from here on out. All that would unfold would just be Yuki and Yuusho’s persistence in trying to squeeze some sort of definite statement out of her, while Masachika would keep them in check. It basically just required Nonoa to be careful to not slip up, a casual situation where she could just dodge and weave obstacles nonchalantly. Having read that far ahead, her interest waned, and she turned her eyes to the wall clock in the classroom.

Yet, however…

“But to begin with, you’re fundamentally lazy: you’ve got no reason to go through all that trouble to backstab us,” Masachika conjectured. “The only scenario that I can think of is that Sayaka’s still actually aiming to be president and told you to set us up.”

At his words, Nonoa froze for an instant before turning her gaze back.

There, she saw Masachika’s mouth curved into a loose, casual smile. “Oh well, it probably ain’t like that, though…” it seemed to say, yet his eyes fostered a keen, icy light. It was something that Nonoa understood with complete clarity: a clear threat.

<em>Haaah~, so that’s how you’re comin’ at me.</em>

In other words, Nonoa committing any further acts of betrayal aimed at causing harm to the Masachika-Alisa pair would only result in Sayaka being made to shoulder the responsibility. That was essentially what Masachika was saying. It was, without any question, a threat with the full understanding of Nonoa’s weak spot.

<em>So you’re still laying down the law while protecting me, huh?</em> Nonoa lamented. <em>Maybe it’s still too early to let my guard down, after all?</em>

Revising her assessment that Masachika was no longer an enemy, Nonoa shifted her thoughts back into gear.

<em>But y’know…? That threat doesn’t really exist, Kuzecchi. The oh-so-good-natured Kuzecchi pinning the stigma of being a traitor on an innocent Sayacchi? You wouldn’t be able to do that, would you?</em> she mused, giving a dispassionate analysis of the situation with a cool head.

And yet, as if he’d seen straight through her thoughts, Masachika turned toward Yuusho.

“Hey, Kiryuuin,” he called out. “You think so too, don’t ya?”

“…Yeah, you’re right. Miyamae denying it here would just surely mean she’s protecting Taniyama. After all, she did take the blame to shield her at that debate during the first semester as well, amirite?” Yuusho answered, giving a theatrically exaggerated shrug, making Nonoa narrow her eyes slightly.

<em>Ah… Yeah, Yuushou’s definitely happy to slap that kinda stigma onto Sayacchi if it’s him we’re talking about. Even if Kuzecchi tries to stop him…</em>

She understood now. This really did work… as a threat.

<em>No way… To think that Yuushou would end up playing the joker here…</em>

He was already a loser whose status within the school had fallen as far as it could, shunned by every member of the current student council. Nonoa had been keeping an eye on his movements, given that he’s the head of the very piano club that had formed the basis for her whole scheme to start with, but in all honesty, she hadn’t been particularly wary of him at all.

<em>And yet, to think your plans have been crushed by ol’ loser-chan over here, going all the way to set your fate in stone, hm…?</em> Yuusho conveyed as much with a provocative jerk of his chin, as if he’d read her thoughts.

It was the type of gesture that should have been irritating beyond measure, and yet… it felt amusing to Nonoa. It was as though the excitement that had once cooled was flaring back up with a sudden whoosh.

<em>Ahhhh… How nice,</em> she thought.<em>That’s right, guess I should play along here?</em>

Letting that inner amusement rise to her cheeks, Nonoa flashed a grin so bright that it almost seemed fake.

“Of coooourse, isn’t there just noooo way I’d betray Kuzecchi and Alissa?” she mused. “I mean, suuure~, I might’ve if Sayacchi had told me to, but… she would never ask for such a thing, would she?”

“Welp, that’s true. I naturally believe that too,” Masachika answered, returning an equally dubious-looking cheerful smile as he continued to scratch the back of his neck with his left hand.

Fully aware that neither was saying what they truly meant, the two exchanged friendly grins all the same.

“But it’s truuue~. It’s true I sided with Ayanono instead of giving you two the warnings I should have; I swear I’ll make up for it sometime soon, okaaaay~?”

“That so? That’d be a great help. Just make sure to report back to us and communicate properly when the time comes, alright?”

The two continued to maintain their outwardly amicable demeanors as they kept up with the quiet sparring beneath the surface… At least, until Yuki suddenly jumped in between them.

“Nonoa-san,” she called out. “Hand over who incited both the piano club’s vice president and the actual perpetrators, and we promise we won’t go after you any further. What do you say?”

Her proposal, casually tossed out, caught Nonoa completely off guard. A question posed in the form of an offer, one where showing even an ounce of hesitation would mean defeat.

“Hmm<em>~</em>? Sorry, but I don’t really get what you’re talkin’ about here, Yukki,” yet she replied without even the slightest of pauses.

“……”

Once again unable to extract a straight answer, Yuki lightly narrowed her gaze as she gave off a subtle air of frustration, as if she were clicking her tongue inwardly.

And then, at that moment…

“H-how…?”

A voice perked up, unexpected not only for Yuki and Nonoa… but for Masachika as well.

“?”

“H-how!? How! How can you be so calm about this!?” cried a thin, squeezed out voice, belonging to none other than Ayano as she stood diagonally behind Yuki.

“I-it’s fine if it’s <em>m-me</em>. But Alisa and… Masachika-sama… th-they are, to you, Nonoa-san, f-friends, are they not!?” Ayano continued to shout with trembling lips, clenching her skirt tightly as the astonished gazes of everyone present turned toward her. “A-and yet you… b-betrayed th-those two, you… you—!”

Her words seemed to spill from the deepest settings of her heart before her head could even catch up. Her throat, her chest, her fists, her entire body shook violently as she desperately forced out her words with a tongue that wouldn’t cooperate. It was a raw display of passion from Ayano that even Masachika and Yuki rarely ever saw. She looked as though she might hyperventilate at any moment…

So as Yuki turned back, she pulled her into a tight embrace.

“Thank you, Ayano. It’s okay now. It’s really all okay now.”

“Y-Yuki… sama…”

As she gently stroked Ayano’s head, the tension gradually drained from her stiff body, and her breathing started to settle little by little. In front of her, Masachika and Alisa stepped forward, positioning themselves to block Nonoa from her view.

“Thanks, Ayano.”

“Thank you, Ayano-san. For getting angry on our behalf.”

With the former gently patting her head just as his sister had, and the latter giving a gentle, considerate smile, Ayano finally slowly took a deep breath as she lowered her gaze.

“…Masachika-kun, Alya-san,” Yuki said. “We will be heading home a little ahead of you two.”

“Yeah… Take care of Ayano.”

“Yes. Then, to you too, Kiryuuin-san,” she then addressed Yuusho, leaving only those words behind before exiting the classroom while holding Ayano close.

After watching their retreating figures, Masachika turned to Yuusho.

“You really saved us today, Kiryuuin. Thank you,” he expressed his attitude, bowing toward him.

“Oh…? Y-yeah…”

“But sorry, could you head home for now?” Masachika continued, unfazed by Yuusho, who had blinked repeatedly with a surprised expression on his face, clearly caught off guard. “I want to talk with Nonoa, just Alya and me.”

“Ah, <em>mm</em>…”

And with that, Yuusho also left the classroom, albeit looking unusually shaken as he nodded with an uncharacteristic obedience.

So, with only the three of them remaining, Masachika and Alisa faced Nonoa once again.

“So then, why did you end up backstabbing us?” the former asked her bluntly, discarding his manufactured, plastered-on smile.

“What’s gotten into you all a sudden, Kuzecchi?” Yet Nonoa merely deflected his question with the mere tilt of her head, her bright smile unchanged. “You said you’d believe me.”

“…Is that right? So you <em>really</em> have no intention of admitting it, hm?” Masachika spoke, inwardly clicking his tongue when faced with Nonoa as she continued her transparent act, unwilling to let her guard down or show a single opening.

<em>Tsk. So you ain’t gonna slip up that easily, huh…?</em> Masachika mused, scratching his head roughly with his right hand and dropping his gaze downward before continuing.

“Hey, Nonoa. Who was the female student that tried to set Alya up?”

“Geeez~, I told you. I don’t get what you’re talking about.”

“Oh, you don’t get what I’m talking about? You’re really serious?”

“<em>Mm</em>, not a clue~” Nonoa answered in an offhand tone, spreading both hands lightly as she did… only for Masachika to let out a small chuckle.

“Yeah, figures,” he commented. “Yuki did ask you something similar earlier, yet you kept feigning your ignorance of it, after all.”

“Right, right~,” Nonoa agreed, recalling how Yuki had pressed her. “I really wish you’d cut it out alrea—”

“There we go, knew you’d slip up eventually.”

Nonoa’s smile vanished in an instant.

“You don’t get what I’m talking about?” Masachika continued, staring straight at Nonoa, who had, in turn, gone utterly blank-faced in an instant. “What a strange thing to say. Y’know, wouldn’t you think that the first person that should’ve come to mind with the description, ‘the female student that tried to set Alya up,’ be the girl who’d confronted her from the audience back during the student assembly?”

In other words, one of the perpetrators who had manipulated the colors of the balls they had used to cast their vote. The very girl who was the first to denounce Alisa. Perhaps recalling her existence, Nonoa’s eyes widened just slightly.

“If you truly knew nothing, then you would’ve answered my question with something like, say, I don’t know, ‘She was Yuki’s classmate,’ or, ‘I don’t know who she is.’ And yet, when you heard my words, ‘the female student that tried to set Alya up,’ who you instantly thought of was the female student who incited those eight perpetrators and the piano club’s vice president. Someone that you, who definitely really knows nothing here, shouldn’t even know about. That’s what you told me, right? That you, quote-unquote, don’t get what I’m talking about, hm?”

Saying that much, Masachika then showed Nonoa the still-recording voice recorder he’d pulled out from his pocket, causing her eyes to shoot open even wider…

“Well, you see,” she spoke, letting out a soft chuckle. “Yuki <em>had</em> mentioned that there was such a person, didn’t she?”

“That’s absolutely true. But what she specifically asked was <em>who</em> incited them. She never once mentioned a female student.”

“You say that, but it’s still within the realm of worldplay, if anything… Isn’t that a li’l too weak as proof of betrayal?”

“It’d be real nice if that argument works on Sayaka too,” Masachika retorted wittily, causing the cracks to finally spread across Nonoa’s plastic smile as a hint of madness peeked through from within.

“…How interesting. You really are an interesting one, aren’t you, Kuzzechiii~? You’re about the only person who can corner me head-on like this.”

Her eyes gleamed brightly as she stared ahead, and she donned an expression that belonged to someone itching to lick their lips at any moment. It was a presence that would engraft an unconditional sense of danger to any ordinary person.

“What… are you?” Alya finally raised her voice as she faced such a sight. “What is it… that you want?”

It was a question that had slipped out almost involuntarily, prompting Nonoa to turn toward her this time, her eerie smile still firmly in place.

“Hmm~? What am I, huuuh…? To be honest, I don’t really understand myself all that well either<a href="#TN2" id="Return2"><sup>[2]</sup></a>… Ah, but maybe I’ve figured it out just a little today,” she murmured, before tearing her mouth into a wide grin as she threw both her arms open and looked up toward the ceiling.

“I want to witness the unexpected!”

Clenching both her hands tightly, as though she was grasping for something, Nonoa let out a sick laugh.

“Things that go beyond my expectations! Things that overturn them! All for me! That’s what I want to see!”<a href="#TN3" id="Return3"><sup>[3]</sup></a>

It was the first time Masachika had witnessed such a raw outpouring of pure desire from Nonoa. It was something so animalistic, a craving that was so unfiltered. Something that he could only frown at.

“So for that, you’d betray a friend or trick an innocent classmate. And you wouldn’t care, huh?”

“…Well, I never expected you to understand,” Nonoa responded, her head snapping back forward as her feverish madness cooled in an instant. “I don’t even understand why I feel this way myself.”

“I can’t say <em>I</em> can understand it too, but… I don’t think that’s a reason to deny it either,” a voice, which sounded as though it cast even herself aside, then responded to her remark.

It belonged to none other than Alisa.

“Just like how I always want to aim higher, you want to see the unexpected. That’s all there is to it, isn’t it?” she continued, looking straight at Nonoa all the while Masachika—and she as well—stared at her with eyes wide. “I’ve come to learn that all kinds of people live all kinds of lives ever since I enrolled in this school. And that there’s just no such superiority or inferiority among said ways of living either.”

Her words, backed by an undeniable sense of conviction, carried tremendous weight. A weight strong enough to naturally convince anyone listening that they were not spoken lightly. That they were not just mere words for show.

“Every way of living has its own merits and… there are just things that only someone living a specific life can see. So, regarding things I don’t understand, I’ve decided not to use their differences from myself as reasons to deny them. It’s what I’ve decided on. That’s why… I won’t deny you either. If that’s what gives you a reason to live then… I’ll accept it for what it is.”

A response that was neither affirmation nor rejection: acceptance. Something that Nonoa could only stare intently at Alisa over…

“How nice…” she murmured, breaking into a wide grin. “I think I might kinda like you, Alissa.”

“H-huh?”

“Mm-hmm, mm-hmm, that thought sounds gooood<em>~</em>. You were like that back at the student assembly earlier too, that was really great. I kinda wanna kiss you.”

“<em>Uehh</em>!? No, that’s…!” Alisa protested, instinctively taking a step back while Masachika shifted sideways to shield her.

Watching the both of them with clear amusement, Nonoa then spun around and turned her body toward the rear classroom door.

“Oh well, anyway… as long as you keep showing me things that exceed my expectations, I’ll proooperly cooperate with you two, ’kaaaay~?” she teased. “I’ll start stirrin’ things up if I get bored, though.”

“And we’ll make sure to give a response worthy of it if it comes to that.”

“Do as you liiike~. Oh, and go ahead if you wanna let Sayacchi hear this whole convo. Feel free~”

And with those parting words, maintaining her cheerful attitude to the very end, Nonoa left the classroom.

“Well that was something…” Masachika uttered after letting out a long breath and making sure she was gone, turning to Alisa. “Nice work. You okay?”

“Yes, thanks for your hard work too… But, ‘you okay’?” Alisa asked, confused with his sudden question.

“No, um… Seeing her true self was like that was a first for you, right? I was just wondering if it shook you up.”

Faced with Masachika’s concern, Alisa fell into serious thought.

“…I was surprised, that’s true,” she slowly opened her mouth after a short while, carefully picking her words as she went. “But as I said earlier, I don’t intend on denying who Nonoa-san is. If anything, I think that her showing her true nature—or rather… the core of who she is?—was a good thing.

A response that was not one of bravado, nor indulgence toward a friend, but a conclusion she’d reached after proper consideration. Something that Masachika felt a genuine respect for Alisa over.

“It’s amazing… that you’re able to think that way,” he sang his praise.

“W-well, I suppose part of it is because it makes it feel like I wasn’t really betrayed.”

“Ahhh, gotcha.”

True enough, the plot against them itself had been thwarted partway through, and Nonoa hadn’t <em>directly</em> done anything to Alisa herself. Maybe that was precisely why Alisa could still view Nonoa somewhat objectively.

“Well, besides that…? It was nothing compared to who Yuki-san truly is,” she commented.

“…Yeah, right~?” Masachika replied, only being able to stare off into the distance.

Thinking of his little sister—someone who was even closer to Alisa than Nonoa ever was, and someone whose gap between her public façade and true self surpassed that of Nonoa’s—Masachika cleared his throat lightly before pulling himself together.

“Oh well, glad you weren’t too shaken… It’s getting late anyway; let’s head home for today.”

“Yes… Okay.”

Then, without either of them taking the lead, the two began to walk, leaving the school building and strolling side by side as they headed home along the dark night road.

“So, what are we going to do? About that recording from earlier?” Alisa asked.

“Hmm… Let’s put it on hold for now. There’s no telling how Nonoa’s gonna react if Sayaka actually finds out about her backstabbing us and tries to stop her. Worst case scenario, she might just throw caution to the wind and openly turn against us… Relying on Sayaka has gotta be a last resort. We’ll keep it as some sorta deterrent against Nonoa for now.”

“I see… I suppose it’s fine if you say so.”

“…<em>Mm</em>,” Masachika nodded slightly, exhaling as he released all the tension that he had been holding onto.

And then, all at once, a deep fatigue washed over him.

<em>Yeah, I really was on edge during that convo, wasn’t I…? No, sure, that’s part of it, but this is…</em>

What he was feeling wasn’t just mental fatigue, but instead a murky, heavy feeling in his chest… Emotional fatigue.

<em>Ah… That’s it.</em>

It was from the outburst of raw emotion Ayano had shown earlier. The lingering image of his usually expressionless childhood friend like that weighed heavy on Masachika’s heart.

<em>As for why she blew up back there… I’m partly to blame if you think ’bout it calmly, aren’t I?</em>

At the very least, it was something that could have been avoided had Masachika not taken Nonoa’s side, instead joining Yuki in seriously condemning her. Ayano would have easily stayed as she always did if he’d done that, behind Masachika and Yuki as usual…

<em>No, it was gonna happen sooner or later… huh? She’d been bottling it all up ever since she started spying on Nonoa, after all…</em>

Even so, the fact remained that, despite Ayano putting such a burden on her heart to spy and warn Masachika and Alisa of danger, Masachika had still ended up repaying her kindness with what could only be described as an act of ingratitude.

<em>We’d used her info to dodge Nonoa’s scheme, and yet when it actually came time to confront her, I ended up taking “her side” to stop it…</em> Masachika lamented. <em>That must’ve definitely looked like nothing but outright betrayal from Ayano’s perspective…</em>

Still, Masachika had no regrets about how he had handled himself back then. With the upcoming election in mind, he believed he had handled the situation in the most optimal way possible, given the situation at hand as well. Still… it was also true that such relentlessly rational and calculating actions had caused Ayano to break down into such a fit.

<em>Ugh… This makes me kinda… hate myself.</em>

It couldn’t be helped, after all. They were opposing candidates. It was something Masachika understood on the logical level. He really did. And yet… right now, even making such attempts to justify it all made him feel even more unbearably detestable.

“Thanks, okay? For thinking things through deeply,” yet words of thanks came from beside him suddenly.

“Huh?”

They had caught him completely off guard, halting his thoughts. Turning to his side, he saw Alisa, still facing forward as she continued to speak slowly.

“If that was just me alone back there… I really might have just confronted Nonoa-san without thinking that far ahead, leaving nothing behind but distrust and friction before ending it all and breaking things off.”

“Yeah… Oh well, that straightforwardness of yours is one of your good points though, Alya.”

It was a straightforwardness that was always dazzling to Masachika… And yet, right now, that admiration hurted a little.

“But even so, I’m sure that <em>that</em> alone would’ve just let Nonoa-san continue with all that nonchalant dodging, and she wouldn’t have let us hear her true feelings,” Alisa opined. “The reason she showed us what she was really thinking was because <em>you</em> drew it out of the course of that conversation… So, thank you,” she then turned back to thank him, having walked a few steps ahead.

Masachika widened his eyes slightly in response. Meeting such a gaze head-on, Alisa could only widen hers as well.

“…What’s wrong?” she asked.

“Huh…?”

“You somehow… look like you’re in pain…”

“…Ah,” Masachika murmured, tugging the corner of his mouth up in a wry smile in response to her question. “It’s about Ayano… A li’l bit.”

“Ah…”

“As I thought… she really isn’t cut out for elections like this. Wait, I guess I’m the whole dang reason why she’s involved in all this in the first place, though…”

And part of why she made that face this time too…

“Masachika-kun…”

“No, my bad. It was already too late for regrets the moment I became an opposing candidate to her. I thought I was prepared for something like this… or at least I <em>should</em>’ve been, but seeing something like that…? Ahhh yeah, it still gets to me.”

After saying that much, he shook his head from side to side, as if forcing himself to change gears.

“But… yeah. Being acknowledged by you, Alya… It makes me feel like maybe I wasn’t in the wrong after all. As for Ayano… I’ll make sure to properly apologize and make it up to her next time.

“……”

“Don’t make that face now,” Masachika said with a small smirk. “We’ll win this election no matter what happens. That resolve hasn’t changed. Ain’t that right?”

“…Yes, that’s right.”

Seeing Alisa still looking troubled as she gave her nod, Masachika stepped alongside her and deliberately put on a brighter tone.

“Anyway, man, her acting was rock-solid… I’ve gotta hand it to her; her tongue just kept going.”

“Eh? Ah… Nonoa-san?”

“Yep. She was so flawless I almost wanted to laugh.”

“But even then, you went toe-to-toe with <em>that</em>, and… you won in the end, didn’t you?”

“Yeeeah<em>~</em> …Welp, it was a pretty narrow victory, though.”

“But even so, you outplayed her… I have to admit I honestly respect you.”

“Yeah… <em>Mm</em>… thanks.”

【And you were really cool, too…】

“<em>Ggh!</em>” Masachika nearly choked, feeling a hot gaze on his cheek right after Alisa’s murmured Russian whisper.

Or rather, a strange sound <em>had</em> slipped from the back of his throat.

“Anyway, I’m surprised,” he then quickly changed the subject, hastily trying to cover his reaction up. “I’d never thought you’d call for a revote just to expose the plot against you.”

“Eh…? Ohhh. We still couldn’t catch the mastermind behind it all because of that, though. Ayano-san’s spying was exposed to Nonoa-san too…”

“No, that actually worked out in our favor too. Had Ayano kept that up, there was a chance that Yuki could’ve gotten hold of more concrete proof of Nonoa’s betrayal.”

“Ah, I suppose you could look at it that way too.”

To begin with, Masachika and Co. had been waiting near the empty classroom after the student assembly because of a realization he had come to while listening with Yuki to Alisa and Yuusho’s account of what had happened. “Huh? Don’t Alya’s actions look unnatural from Nonoa’s point of view?” he had thought, and with that, they had gone along with Ayano when she was summoned by Nonoa. So, though it probably felt like Alisa had done something completely unnecessary and extra from her own point of view, from Masachika’s perspective, it was nothing short of a great move.

“Besides,” he continued, “Catching the perpetrators was huge. Though, yeah, it would’ve been ideal if we’d caught the female student who’d egged them on too…”

“Yeah… I wonder who she is?”

“No clue, but we’d really be neutralizing one of Nonoa’s important pawns if we can identify her. Same goes for Yuki and Ayano too. That girl would be a crucial witness for them: someone with a high chance of being able to connect the whole thing to Nonoa. Securing her’s the next decisive battle, I guess…”

“Understood,” Alisa gave a firm nod as she fixed her expression, setting her sights on the next objective.

Nodding back once, Masachika then turned his gaze toward the empty night sky.

<em>That female student’s almost definitely cooperating with Nonoa while knowing her true nature… Just who is she?</em>

⋆⋅☆⋅⋆

Right around that very moment, the very female student in question, Miyabi Shikumagawa, faced Nonoa in a room of the school building that had long been completely deserted.

“So, the plan fizzled out and our eight perpetrators were caught in the end, hm…? How unfortunate,” she bemoaned, lowering her brows in genuine sympathy despite being the person who had taken part in orchestrating the whole thing. “So, now what? The plan failed, and yet here you are looking like you’re having fun. Why’s that, my majesty?” she then moved on to questioning Nonoa.

Unlike her usual listless expression, Nonoa currently wore an oddly cheerful look.

“Hmm<em>~</em>? I wonder whyyy~?” she spoke without even turning toward Miyabi. “Perhaps it’s ’cause I got to see Alissa and Ayanono’s unexpected sides; maybe that’s iiiit~?”

“<em>Haaah…</em>”

“Then there’s Kuzecchi and Yuushou who totally went with moves I toootally didn’t expect, riiiight~? I’ve never had my plans completely foiled like this before, so maybe I just ended up enjoyin’ it.”

“I see; don’t get that at all,” Miyabi could only say so with an exasperated yet somewhat lonely tone, only to continue with her questioning. “So then, what’re we gonna do from here?”

“Hmm<em>~</em>, I guess I’ll have to cozy up to Kuzecchi and Alissa for a whiiile~? I’ve gotta regain the trust I’ve lost from this whole thing, after all.”

“That so…?”

“Shouldn’t you maybe lie low for a bit too, Miyabi~? Yukki and Kuzecchi’s definitely gonna try and pin you down.”

“Yeah. Oh well… I was in disguise. I think I’ll be fine.”

“I think so too, y’knooow<em>~</em>?” Nonoa agreed. “But, complacency’s the enemy of progress. Alright, good work! Dismissed!”

“Hold it,” Miyabi stopped her flat with a reproachful stare, just as she tried to leave after clapping her hands once together. “You haven’t paid me back, you know?”

“Ahhh<em>~</em>…”

“You promised me no NGs<a href="#TN4" id="Return4"><sup>[4]</sup></a>; you told me that, right?” Miyabi continued to demand her reward with a serious expression, prompting Nonoa to curl her lips into a sinister smile, one that seemed to mock her desperation as she reached toward the ribbon tie at her neck.

“Can’t be helped, huh…? Sure?”

And with that, the red ribbon tie slipped free with a soft swoosh, falling into the space in between the two of them.
