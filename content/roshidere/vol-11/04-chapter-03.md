A day which, by chance, all the student council members, along with Sumire, Yuusho, and Aoi—a large ten people in total—had gathered in the student council office, the atmosphere heavy with deadlock.

Amidst such a tense atmosphere, Touya—who hadn’t participated in the actual investigation himself, but had come to check on things as the person responsible for the student assembly—was the first to break the silence.

“<em>Haaah</em>,” he sighed. “…So, no one was able to find anyone who fit the bill in the end. That’s what you guys are saying?”

Considering the level of security in this school, the chance of it being a complete outsider was low. But still, that didn’t mean it was entirely impossible for a student from the school’s junior high to come into the high school building, change into a high school uniform, and pretend to be a second-year. That was something they all had considered before the investigation had truly begun, but expanding the search to include the junior high would simply just increase the number of suspects they had to check in with by far too much, so that line of inquiry had been ruled out.

<em>I mean, it’s not like we really intended to seriously hunt down the perp to begin with anyway…</em>

But more than that, for Masachika, making sure that no one else captured her was more important. In fact, given that both Yuki and the disciplinary committee had still yet to get to her, he actually even thought that this was the perfect point to just call off the whole investigation altogether.

<em>And it’s probably the same for Yuki too</em>, he contemplated.

Yuki had originally wanted to catch the mysterious girl because the already existing circumstantial evidence had put her in a position where she herself was being suspected of being the mastermind. But now, with the investigation having progressed to the point where she realized that there were far fewer people suspecting her than she had initially anticipated, she didn’t really have much reason left to desperately pursue who it truly was.

<em>So that leaves the disciplinary committee led by Sarashina-senpai, huh…?</em> Masachika deduced. <em>Now then, I wonder just how we’re gonna get them to call off</em> their <em>investigation?</em>

In truth, a failure shared amongst everyone involved in this whole investigation. This world was full of people who’d spread baseless rumors and treat someone like a criminal just because the police had come to question them, after all.
