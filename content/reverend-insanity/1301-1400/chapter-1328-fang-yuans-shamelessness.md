<p>Chi Shang felt he was humiliated.</p>
<p>Yes, humiliated!</p>
<p>At present, in the entire Southern Border Gu Immortal world, who did not know of Wu Yi Hai&#x27;s identity? Who was not aware he cultivated transformation path? But now, Fang Yuan did not use his primary cultivation to trouble Chi Shang, but instead picked Chi Shang&#x27;s most proficient domain.</p>
<p>This was like saying: I don&#x27;t need to use transformation path to trouble you, because you simply can&#x27;t compare to me!</p>
<p>The disdain overflowed in this challenge.</p>
<p>He could not take it!</p>
<p>This could not be endured!</p>
<p>Chi Shang&#x27;s eyes were wide open as he pinched Fang Yuan&#x27;s information path Gu worm and mumbled: &quot;Let me see what problem you brought out.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Eh? This is quite something...&quot;</p>
<p>Soon, a trace of solemness appeared on Chi Shang&#x27;s face.</p>
<p>As far as he was concerned, Fang Yuan&#x27;s question was quite challenging.</p>
<p>But soon, Chi Shang settled himself: &quot;No wonder he is confident of using formation path to harass me. This problem is slightly difficult.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;He made this problem? That should not be possible! His main cultivation is transformation path, it should be a Wu clan formation path Gu Immortal behind him who created this problem. Hmph, with his identity as Wu Yong&#x27;s brother, how could Wu clan&#x27;s Gu Immortals not help him?&quot;</p>
<p>Although Wu clan was not specialized in formation path, as long as it was a super force, they would have some involvement in formation path.</p>
<p>Because super forces needed to guard their resource points, and had a large scale demand for Gu formations.</p>
<p>&quot;But even if a Wu clan Gu Immortal helped you, so what? In terms of the clan&#x27;s strength, my Chi clan is indeed slightly inferior. But if just comparing formation path attainment, my Chi clan is undoubtedly in first place in Southern Border!&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Shang felt intense pride towards his clan.</p>
<p>He gathered all his senses and dived into Fang Yuan&#x27;s problem.</p>
<p>The more he pondered, the more he lost himself.</p>
<p>With his analysis, he had divided the problem into dozens of segments.</p>
<p>&quot;Normal methods cannot be used to solve the issue of water and fire opposing each other.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The whole Gu formation demands a circular formation, a square formation can be discarded...&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;How to solve this difficulty?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Hiss... this is slightly difficult.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;But this can&#x27;t defeat me!&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Shang&#x27;s fighting spirit soared high like he had been injected with adrenaline, he inwardly told himself that he had to teach Fang Yuan a fierce lesson!</p>
<p>Let him know that he, Chi Shang, could not be humiliated!</p>
<p>&quot;Lord, the evening banquet that Luo clan&#x27;s Gu Immortals invited you to is going to start soon. We should be on our way.&quot; At this time, a rank six Gu Immortal of Chi clan, Chi Liu, reminded Chi Shang from outside the door.</p>
<p>&quot;Not going, not going!&quot; Chi Shang did not even raise his head, he refused while continuing to deduce the problems in his mind.</p>
<p>&quot;But, we already agreed...&quot; Chi Liu hesitated.</p>
<p>&quot;I said I am not going. Leave.&quot; Chi Shang impatiently replied.</p>
<p>Chi Liu could only helplessly leave.</p>
<p>Chi Shang took in a deep breath, looking at the formation path problem Fang Yuan sent him, his eyes brightened as he mumbled: &quot;What good is there in a banquet? The important thing is to defeat him! The faster I can solve this problem, the better it is. You, you dare to humiliate me!&quot;</p>
<p>The night passed like this.</p>
<p>Chi Shang had not slept the whole time, his eyes were bloodshot, his hair was in a mess and he was extremely tired.</p>
<p>With a Gu Immortal&#x27;s physique, it did not matter if they stayed up all night. But Chi Shang was rather exhausted, he had not rested even a moment during this night, and had put in all his effort to deduce and plan, taxing both his mind and energy.</p>
<p>&quot;This problem is indeed slightly difficult. But... I have already solved it. Hahaha, you dare to mess with me! I will let you taste my awesomeness!&quot;</p>
<p>Despite being exhausted, Chi Shang was extremely excited and laughed heartily.</p>
<p>He could not wait to see how Fang Yuan would respond.</p>
<p>&quot;I will go right now and enjoy your expression!&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Shang walked to the entrance when he suddenly stopped.</p>
<p>&quot;Hmm? No! I am standing against Wu Yi Hai right now, we are like generals, how could I take the initiative to visit him? My reputation will be lost! I need to send a subordinate...&quot;</p>
<p>Soon after, Chi clan rank six Gu Immortal Chi Liu arrived in Chi Shang&#x27;s study.</p>
<p>&quot;Little Liu, send this to Wu Yi Hai. Haha, I want to teach him a lesson!&quot; Chi Shang instructed.</p>
<p>Chi Liu quickly agreed.</p>
<p>His cultivation level was lower than Chi Shang, and he knew Chi Shang was the hope of Chi clan in formation path. Chi clan&#x27;s first supreme elder, Chi Qu You, had said a few times in the past that Chi Shang would inherit his legacy.</p>
<p>The significance of these words was huge.</p>
<p>Chi Qu You was a formation path great grandmaster, him acknowledging Chi Shang meant Chi Shang was very likely to become a formation path great grandmaster as well.</p>
<p>Chi Shang also did not let down the clan.</p>
<p>He had displayed his talents and aptitude in formation path since a young age, and with the clan&#x27;s resources being poured into him, he cultivated hard all year round and did not slack.</p>
<p>After receiving Chi clan&#x27;s nurturing, his current formation path attainment was not to be underestimated.</p>
<p>&quot;Hmm... relaxing.&quot; Fang Yuan slowly woke up and stretched out his arms.</p>
<p>Although his soul had the protection of guts Gu, it would become agitated when used too much.</p>
<p>Guts Gu was not able to resolve this, but sleep could restore the soul&#x27;s calmness.</p>
<p>After Fang Yuan had a night&#x27;s sleep, his exhausted state was swept away, and his mind was vigorous again.</p>
<p>Chi Shang&#x27;s information path mortal Gu had already been delivered while he was asleep.</p>
<p>When Fang Yuan glanced at it, a look of pleasant surprise flashed past his eyes: &quot;Oh? He indeed solved it!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;This speed is quite fast.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan sighed as he then immersed himself into the information and started studying it.</p>
<p>His eyes shined repeatedly as he gained great enlightenment from Chi Shang&#x27;s solution.</p>
<p>&quot;So it was like this!&quot; Fang Yuan rubbed his chin, this was a completely new thought process and it opened a new window to him.</p>
<p>&quot;According to this thought process, this answer is still not suitable for my entire Gu formation, I need to make slight adjustments in some areas.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan inwardly pondered.</p>
<p>He did not give the whole Gu formation to Chi Shang, and only took out parts of it for Chi Shang to solve.</p>
<p>Chi Shang&#x27;s answers had no errors, but there would still be deviations when the entire Gu formation was arranged.</p>
<p>However, Fang Yuan&#x27;s formation path attainment level was already at quasi-grandmaster, and using the foundation of these answers, he fully could make some adjustments.</p>
<p>Chi Shang was a grandmaster, and Fang Yuan a quasi-grandmaster, there was not a huge difference between the two. If the difference was too high, it would be impossible to modify the formation.</p>
<p>It was just like how Fang Yuan currently saw this super Gu formation that covered the dream realm, it was still a mystery to him, he could not understand a thing.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan made huge gains by receiving the answer to the issue that was troubling him.</p>
<p>&quot;Indeed worthy of being Southern Border&#x27;s Chi clan.&quot; He mumbled, praising Chi Shang&#x27;s formation path attainment.</p>
<p>With this problem solved, Fang Yuan could continue his plan to modify the Gu formation.</p>
<p>His mind was calm and serene, as he began deducing once again.</p>
<p>A few days passed like this.</p>
<p>Chi Shang was very anxious.</p>
<p>After he sent the answer, he had been waiting for Fang Yuan&#x27;s reply. But, even after some days, he had not received a response, he did not even feel he could taste his food and drinks properly.</p>
<p>&quot;What is Wu Yi Hai playing at?&quot; Chi Shang mumbled.</p>
<p>&quot;Chi Shang, what did you say?&quot; In the banquet, a Luo clan Gu Immortal asked in confusion.</p>
<p>Chi Shang slowly came to his senses, only then realizing he was still in the banquet.</p>
<p>He had refused a banquet invitation from Luo clan before, but after solving Fang Yuan&#x27;s problem, he came to attend one.</p>
<p>He had to attend it, otherwise, was it not the same as not giving face to Luo clan?</p>
<p>Gu Immortal Chi Gui, who was sitting at the side, quickly mediated: &quot;Forgive us, Chi Shang has always been like this. His mind often wanders, sometimes he even forgets to eat when he is thinking of a formation path problem. He would stand still all of a sudden, and it can happen even while he is walking.&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Gui had rank seven cultivation, and was the leader of the Chi clansmen stationed here.</p>
<p>The Luo clan Gu Immortal laughed loudly: &quot;I have heard about Chi Shang&#x27;s behavior. He is indeed a formation path genius. Sigh, if my Luo clan could produce one or two geniuses with just twenty or thirty percent of Chi Shang&#x27;s talents, I would be content.&quot;</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Fang Yuan&#x27;s brows were tightly furrowed.</p>
<p>He ran into another problem.</p>
<p>&quot;It seems modifying this Gu formation is already beyond my limits.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan had encountered a new problem, and this one was much more difficult than the previous one.</p>
<p>He could still make an attempt for the previous one, but this time, he did not even think to attempt it as his ideas were too unreliable.</p>
<p>&quot;Since that is the case, let&#x27;s hand it to Chi Shang.&quot; Fang Yuan smiled.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s here, it&#x27;s here.&quot; When Chi Shang returned to Chi clan&#x27;s station, he soon got Fang Yuan&#x27;s information path mortal Gu.</p>
<p>His eyes brightened in excitement.</p>
<p>&quot;Do you think it was easy for me!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Even I was getting impatient from waiting.&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Shang sneered while feeling proud. Right now, he was like a winner picking up his battle spoils.</p>
<p>However, after his consciousness entered and he learned of the contents in the information path mortal Gu, his eyes opened wide in anger.</p>
<p>&quot;What is Wu Yi Hai saying!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;He is actually acting so shamelessly?!&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Shang immediately cursed.</p>
<p>In the letter, Fang Yuan did not mention a single word about the previous challenge letter, instead, he casually admitted Chi Shang had some ability. Naturally, it could also be Chi Shang getting lucky.</p>
<p>&quot;If you can solve this problem, I will admit you have the qualifications to pursue Fairy Si Liu.&quot; This was what was written in the letter.</p>
<p>At the mention of Qiao Si Liu, Chi Shang was even more unable to endure it.</p>
<p>&quot;Are my qualifications up to you to decide?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Wu Yi Hai, I misjudged you. You shameless guy!&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Shang felt like Fang Yuan was standing in front of him, sneering in disdain and looking down on him.</p>
<p>He suddenly had a strong impulse to rip apart Fang Yuan!</p>
<p>&quot;Still mentioning going on a sightseeing tour with Si Liu. Even I have only traveled once with Si Liu. Damn this guy...&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Shang paced around the study, gritting his teeth in resentment.</p>
<p>He almost crushed the information path mortal Gu in his hand.</p>
<p>But he thought for a moment and withstood this impulse.</p>
<p>&quot;If I crush this Gu, wouldn&#x27;t it be satisfying Wu Yi Hai&#x27;s wish?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Hmph, he was not able to create difficulties for me, he is definitely afraid of me now, so he is intentionally saying this.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Right! Me giving up is probably what he wants to see.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I can&#x27;t let that happen, I will continue to win and make him completely admit his defeat.</p>
<p>Make him fully realize my strength!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Of course, to prevent him from acting shamelessly again, I must make this matter public, the more people that know of it, the better.&quot;</p>
<p>Chi Shang learned from his mistake.</p>
<p>The origin of this matter was a dispute about a girl, Chi Shang had also found it slightly embarrassing, but now, he was willing to throw caution to the wind against Fang Yuan!</p>
