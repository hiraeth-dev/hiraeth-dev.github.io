<p>The most shocked person was Star Constellation Immortal Venerable.</p>
<p>She had penetrated incredibly deeply into Crazed Demon Cave and Limitless&#x27; formation.</p>
<p>Even after countless investigations, she did not notice that Limitless&#x27; revival method was in this Crazed Demon Cave.</p>
<p>Giant Sun Immortal Venerable had the same investigation results as her.</p>
<p>Precisely so, the two venerables fought so intensely earlier.</p>
<p>But the truth was in front of them, Limitless Demon Venerable had actually managed to revive.</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t tell me, when the final piece of truthful floating ice was successfully carved, Limitless Demon Venerable revived and attained eternal life in one step?&quot; Fang Yuan was also shaken.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan could also sense acutely that the current Limitless Demon Venerable&#x27;s aura far surpassed the other two venerables.</p>
<p>Giant Sun Immortal Venerable and Star Constellation Immortal Venerable could feel this difference even more deeply.</p>
<p>Facing Limitless Demon Venerable, they seemed to be up against towering mountains and vast oceans.</p>
<p>&quot;This is not in line with historical records, did Limitless Demon Venerable really surpass the venerables and attain eternal life?&quot; Giant Sun Immortal Venerable&#x27;s eyes flickered with golden light.</p>
<p>&quot;His current condition is extremely strange, his aura is so strong that even master cannot compare to him.&quot; Star Constellation Immortal Venerable was shocked and suspicious.</p>
<p>Giant Sun Immortal Venerable and Star Constellation Immortal Venerable looked at each other and moved at the same time, attacking Limitless Demon Venerable with fierce methods.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable smiled lightly, chains flew around in a spiral, easily resolving the methods of the two venerables.</p>
<p>The two venerables were shocked internally but their fighting spirit soared, they launched even stronger killer moves in response.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable only defended instead of attacking, even after a few rounds of barraging, they did not manage to harm a hair of Limitless Demon Venerable.</p>
<p>Limitless let out a soft sound as he waved his hands, at once, space rippled, Giant Sun Immortal Venerable and Star Constellation Immortal Venerable retreated immediately.</p>
<p>Paradise Earth and Fang Yuan did not dare to linger either, they quickly dodged.</p>
<p>At once, around a thousand steps of Limitless Demon Venerable, other than Not Immortal and Secret Schemer, there was nobody else.</p>
<p>Giant Sun had a grim expression.</p>
<p>Star Constellation had a deep gaze that flickered with starlight, she seemed to be making deductions with her full strength.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable smiled: &quot;I do not wish to be enemies with all of you. The fruit of eternal life has been obtained after a million years, but I have not truly tested it yet.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;How do you want to test it?&quot; Giant Sun Immortal Venerable asked.</p>
<p>&quot;Watch.&quot; Limitless Demon Venerable turned around slightly.</p>
<p>All the immortals stared fixedly.</p>
<p>At the original spot that Limitless Demon Venerable was in, a tiny hole appeared.</p>
<p>Through this hole, the Gu Immortals could see a vast sea of — Chaos!</p>
<p>It seemed like black, yet it was not black. It seemed like white, yet it was not white.</p>
<p>If one said it was light, it would be light. If one said it was darkness, it would be darkness.</p>
<p>Almost no descriptive word in the world could accurately describe it.</p>
<p>Because chaos was just chaos after all.</p>
<p>Chaos was the greatest calamity!</p>
<p>&quot;You lunatic, you actually broke the world boundary again, aren&#x27;t you afraid that the entire world will be destroyed?&quot; Giant Sun Immortal Venerable called out.</p>
<p>Star Constellation Immortal Venerable also added in with a solemn tone: &quot;Limitless, I know about your aspiration. But if you intend to go ahead with this and destroy the five regions and two heavens, even if I have to sacrifice my life alongside Heavenly Court, we will exterminate you!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Both of you, calm down. The hole is not big, I merely want to test the result of this eternal life. Us venerables are invincible in the world, yet we are plagued by tribulations that originate from this chaos.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Of the venerables here, who has not gotten injured from the chaos? Who has not suffered an ordeal because of it?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Crazed Demon Cave has been active for a million years, there is one most clear indicator if we want to know if the eternal life obtained is successful.&quot;</p>
<p>Saying this, Limitless Demon Venerable extended his arm into the hole.</p>
<p>The other venerables and the various Gu Immortals held their breaths subconsciously, staring at him fixedly.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable did not activate any killer move, he was using his own body to do this experiment.</p>
<p>He was simply too daring!</p>
<p>Even Giant Sun Immortal Venerable and Star Constellation Immortal Venerable would never attempt this.</p>
<p>Even though they could last for some time in the chaos, that was the result of them using their full strength to defend their bodies.</p>
<p>If they acted like Limitless Demon Venerable, who did not put up any defense, Giant Sun Immortal Venerable and Star Constellation Immortal Venerable would be completely obliterated with just a slight touch of the chaos! Unless they managed to cut off the body part in time.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable placed his arm in simply like this, after a while, he pulled back his arm.</p>
<p>His arm was pitch-black, it seemed like charcoal created after a tree was completely burnt.</p>
<p>But when he clenched his arm, the &#x27;black charcoal&#x27; on the surface instantly vanished into thin air, exposing a slightly smaller arm.</p>
<p>Giant Sun Immortal Venerable&#x27;s mouth was wide open as he said dejectedly: &quot;Is this... the true realm of eternal life?!&quot;</p>
<p>Star Constellation Immortal Venerable sigh to herself: &quot;I understand why I did not deduce it now. When you created Crazed Demon Cave, you had constantly been making use of the chaos outside the world. You lunatic!&quot;</p>
<p>Back then, Thieving Heaven Demon Venerable nearly destroyed the world, the reason was that he created a huge hole.</p>
<p>And at the deepest part of Limitless&#x27; formation, he broke the world boundary and created a tiny hole.</p>
<p>If there was a commotion in the Crazed Demon formation and this tiny hole became larger, it would be trouble. The entire Gu world might be destroyed as a result.</p>
<p>Star Constellation Immortal Venerable called Limitless a lunatic, she was not wrong at all.</p>
<p>For the last million years, the world had been on the brink of destruction. And the reason for this was Limitless Demon Venerable.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable looked at his arm and sighed softly, replying Star Constellation Immortal Venerable: &quot;In my previous life, I had already reached the end of the road. Even when I asked the Heavenly Dao, there was no possibility of eternal life. I was pitifully restrained by fate Gu, after searching long and hard, through laborious planning, I designed this Crazed Demon Cave. I simply had no other choice but to use this method.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;But to play safe, I involved Heavenly Court in this.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;With you guys around, even if a problem occurred in the Crazed Demon formation and the hole got larger, you would do your best to fix it.&quot;</p>
<p>Hearing this, how could Star Constellation Immortal Venerable&#x27;s expression possibly be amicable?</p>
<p>She realized now that the reason why Limitless Demon Venerable drew her into the gamble was not just because he needed her wisdom path attainment or Heavenly Court&#x27;s strength to derive new heaven path research results. The deeper intention was to bring in Heavenly Court&#x27;s involvement as an assurance.</p>
<p>If Crazed Demon Cave became a problem, Heavenly Court would have to clean up after Limitless Demon Venerable.</p>
<p>But because of Heavenly Court&#x27;s perspective, they would spare no expense in repairing it, they would be willing to do so in fact!</p>
<p>&quot;But...&quot; Limitless Demon Venerable added: &quot;This is still not at the level of eternal life. Don&#x27;t you think so too, Paradise Earth?&quot;</p>
<p>Paradise Earth nodded slowly and expressionlessly.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan had some sort of realization, the true realm of eternal life would be complete indestructibility against the chaos. Limitless Demon Venerable had clearly not reached that stage yet.</p>
<p>&quot;Just a bit more, I am lacking by just a bit.&quot; Limitless Demon Venerable continued to shake his head, sighing deeply.</p>
<p>He turned his gaze towards Demon Immortal Qi Jue: &quot;Qi Jue, Qi Jue, can you lend me a hand?&quot;</p>
<p>Demon Immortal Qi Jue snorted coldly: &quot;Why must I help you?&quot;</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable took out practice Gu, smiling: &quot;This Immortal Gu was prepared for you to begin with, Qi Jue, for this moment, I have already waited for you for over a million years.&quot;</p>
<p>Demon Immortal Qi Jue laughed loudly until tears flowed out: &quot;So my revival was really part of your schemes. Hahaha, too laughable, simply too laughable. This is so disappointing, so disappointing!&quot;</p>
<p>He was not mocking Limitless Demon Venerable, he was laughing at himself.</p>
<p>He had always felt resentful and unresigned after losing to Limitless Demon Venerable.</p>
<p>After revival, he had always wanted to take revenge.</p>
<p>But at this moment, he finally understood the huge gap between him and Limitless Demon Venerable.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable was quiet as he watched Demon Immortal Qi Jue cry and laugh, afterwards, he tossed practice Gu to the latter.</p>
<p>Demon Immortal Qi Jue had a complex expression at this moment.</p>
<p>He recalled the scene of the deepest memory he had.</p>
<p>&quot;Limitless, you want to become a venerable, but did you ask me? With me around, you can dream on about becoming a venerable!&quot; Demon Immortal Qi Jue sneak attacked Limitless Demon Venerable and succeeded.</p>
<p>Because he knew that once Limitless became a venerable, he would no longer have a chance of winning.</p>
<p>This battle lasted nine days and nights.</p>
<p>Thereafter, Demon Immortal Qi Jue lost.</p>
<p>His eyes were blood red as he called out to Limitless Demon Venerable: &quot;You have passed the tribulation long ago and became a venerable already. Why did you seal your own cultivation level to fight to the death with me?&quot;</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable was bathed in blood, he had struggled at death&#x27;s doors several times during the intense battle.</p>
<p>He smiled lightly: &quot;Because you are my Dao Guardian, only when I am at the brink of death can I understand the true relationship between a venerable and their Dao Guardian. And the truth has been proven, my guess was not wrong after all. I have already comprehended the secret behind the Dao Guardian.&quot;</p>
<p>Demon Immortal Qi Jue stared with wide eyes: &quot;To prove such a guess, you even disregarded your own life? Even though you had already become a venerable, even though you are already invincible in the world?!&quot;</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable replied plainly: &quot;Even though I became a venerable, this is merely another cultivation level. Compared to this, I would prefer to pursue the Great Dao.&quot; ...</p>
<p>&quot;Limitless, oh Limitless. Even a million years cannot change your nature. You are still that Limitless I know!&quot; Demon Immortal Qi Jue pointed at Demon Immortal Qi Jue and shouted.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable smiled: &quot;Like how I am still Limitless, you are also still that Demon Immortal Qi Jue, right?&quot;</p>
<p>Demon Immortal Qi Jue gritted his teeth, he seemed to have become too helpless to rage, he grabbed practice Gu and flew towards Limitless Demon Venerable: &quot;You are right, I am still me. Then let&#x27;s do it!&quot;</p>
<p>Bright light flashed in Limitless Demon Venerable&#x27;s eyes: &quot;Good! You are my Dao Guardian, our destinies are intertwined, according to my deduction, only with the help of the Dao Guardian can a venerable go one step further, to break through the realm of venerable and attain eternal life!&quot;</p>
<p>Demon Immortal Qi Jue gritted his teeth: &quot;Then let me see what is true eternity!&quot;</p>
<p>Saying so, he activated practice Gu.</p>
<p>The light of practice Gu enveloped Limitless Demon Venerable&#x27;s entire body.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable&#x27;s body expanded rapidly, he went from a normal person&#x27;s size to a ten foot giant.</p>
<p>In contrast, Demon Immortal Qi Jue became weaker and weaker, his aura fell to rock bottom, he had no choice but to stop using practice Gu.</p>
<p>As for Limitless Demon Venerable&#x27;s aura, it was completely concealed, no different from an ordinary mortal.</p>
<p>But anyone could tell that the current Limitless Demon Venerable had become powerful to a point which nobody could fathom. Because even against the investigative killer moves of the various immortals, he was no different than a common man.</p>
<p>The immortals could not see through him!</p>
<p>Giant Sun Immortal Venerable, Star Constellation Immortal Venerable, Paradise Earth, and the others did not move, they watched in anticipation.</p>
<p>Even if they wanted to stop him, it would be too difficult.</p>
<p>Limitless Demon Venerable took in a deep breath before stretching his arm into the hole of the world boundary again.</p>
<p>A moment later, he pulled back his arm, it was completely intact!</p>
