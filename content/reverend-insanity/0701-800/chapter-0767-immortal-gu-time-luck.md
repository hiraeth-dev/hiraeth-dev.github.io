<p>This Gu&#x27;s name was Time Luck, it was a luck path and time path Immortal Gu, it consumed immortal essence and time to gain a boost in one&#x27;s luck.</p>
<p>Fairy Li Shan was very reluctant, she tried to explain to him: &quot;Fang Yuan, if you really want to choose this Gu, I have some words for you. <p>&quot;Oh? So that&#x27;s it...&quot; Fang Yuan&#x27;s showed some interest in his gaze.</p>
<p>When Gu Immortals cultivate, they gained no lifespan during the process.</p>
<p>In this world, ordinary people had approximately a hundred years of lifespan. They would die of old age when they exceeded that.</p>
<p>When Gu Masters became immortals, this time limit would not change. Only by using lifespan Gu could they increase their lifespan without leaving behind any backlash. Other than that, there were methods like turning into a zombie, treeman, or the yin-yang life extension method, possession, or to sleep for most of the time to reduce their expenditure, but these were all suboptimal methods that went against nature to extend their lifespan.</p>
<p>Because of this, Gu Immortals treasured their lifespan more than Gu Masters.</p>
<p>When Qin Bai Sheng held this auction, almost all of the heroes and experts in Northern Plains&#x27; Gu Immortal world showed up. But even so, no lifespan Gu were sold.</p>
<p>In contrast, in auctions held by mortal Gu Masters, there were lifespan Gu sold occasionally.</p>
<p>This was understandable.</p>
<p>When Gu Masters become immortals, they naturally would understand the difficulty of cultivating, it was not easy to become a Gu Immortal, they would even more treasure this feeling of being above mortal concerns, having such a high status and standard of living. As a result, they treasured their lives, often roaming the five regions. Unless there was a need, they likely would not live inside their grotto-heaven or blessed land.</p>
<p>Because the time flow in the grotto-heaven or blessed land was faster, a Gu Immortal&#x27;s limited lifespan would be expended even more quickly than if they were in the five regions.</p>
<p>Mortal Gu Masters did not know how precious lifespan was, they would often use some forbidden techniques that expended their lifespan, to raise their battle strength. The higher the rank one reaches, the broader their horizons, the more they treasured their life, and they would try all sorts of ways to extend their lifespan. For example, like First Gen Gu Yue, and Century Boy.</p>
<p>Such situations, in regards to Gu Immortals, were much more severe. This was like the contrast of rich and poor people. Ordinary people were busy trying to earn a living, they disregarded their health. Rich people however, treasured their lives and paid great attention to sustaining their health.</p>
<p>At the end of the day, it was not that rich people were superior to poor people, it was because of their circumstances and statuses that they could act and think differently.</p>
<p>Fairy Li Shan was obviously a Gu Immortal. Time luck Immortal Gu expended immortal essence and lifespan to boost the user&#x27;s luck. Since she took it, she naturally had a way to make up for this flaw.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan was a shrewd person, he immediately guessed that Fairy Li Shan had such a crucial method.</p>
