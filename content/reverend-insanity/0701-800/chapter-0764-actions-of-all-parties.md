<p>Heavenly Lord Bai Zu became the final winner of the intense bidding war, obtaining the complete recipe for Red Fiend Divine Boat. And the beneficiary of the back-door deal, Fang Yuan, also successfully obtained unravel mystery Immortal Gu.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan was very satisfied with this transaction.</p>
<p>Heavenly Lord Bai Zu became the focal point of everyone&#x27;s attention, and apart from a few related people, no one knew unravel mystery Immortal Gu had landed in Fang Yuan&#x27;s hands.</p>
<p>Red Fiend Divine Boat&#x27;s recipe could be considered as an unexpected final climactic moment of the auction.</p>
<p>Time passed, four hours later, this auction which included various outstanding people finally ended.</p>
<p>&quot;There is still no reply from Fairy Li Shan&#x27;s end?&quot; Fang Yuan secretly asked Tai Bai Yun Sheng.</p>
<p>Tai Bai Yun Sheng quickly sent his message: &quot;Nothing until now. Fairy Li Shan actually missed such a grand event, it seems there is definitely some issue on her end. Do we return through the previous route?&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan and Tai Bai Yun Sheng came to Qin Bai Sheng&#x27;s blessed land through the hole earth Gu established between Qin Bai Sheng and Fairy Li Shan.</p>
<p>Now, the situation on Fairy Li Shan&#x27;s end was unclear, and rashly using hole earth Gu to return was undoubtedly an unwise move.</p>
<p>&quot;No, we will first leave along with the others, then use Fixed Immortal Travel. We need to be careful, though, there will definitely be turmoil soon.&quot; Fang Yuan reminded.</p>
<p>Tai Bai Yun Sheng nodded with a serious expression.</p>
<p>The auction might have ended, but there were sure to be many who were not satisfied with the results. With the end of the auction, the previous agreement signed by all parties would expire.</p>
<p>Without the restriction, people&#x27;s hearts and minds would be moved by wealth; robbery, extortion and ambushes would be the next event.</p>
<p>Most of the Gu Immortals had the same thoughts as Fang Yuan and Tai Bai Yun Sheng. Thus, they left the blessed land in a hurry and full of caution.</p>
<p>&quot;We must get that guy! He actually dared to sell the Red Fiend Divine Boat recipe openly, our Zombie Alliance&#x27;s face had been lost completely!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;We could let go if it was only our face, but the key thing is that the leader had repeatedly reminded us of the importance of this task before she left. But now, we have screwed up.</p>
<p>When the leader comes back and hears the news, who knows how she will punish us!&quot;</p>
<p>Yin Liu Gong and Dragon Commander Ye Cha discussed as they looked at their target hurriedly leaving.</p>
<p>In the sky above crimson reed marsh.</p>
<p>&quot;Heavenly Lord, I wonder if you are interested in selling the Red Fiend Divine Boat recipe to my Yao tribe?&quot; Yao Huang stayed behind and persuaded Heavenly Lord Bai Zu.</p>
<p>Heavenly Lord Bai Zu chuckled: &quot;Lord Yao Huang is jesting, how can such a treasure be sold easily? I won&#x27;t conceal it from you, I have a lot of descendants and they are filling up my grotto-heaven. Recently, I have been planning to establish a tribe, perhaps we can have many opportunities to collaborate with Yao tribe.&quot;</p>
<p>The leaders of various forces in the surroundings had changes in their expressions as they heard this.</p>
<p>Heavenly Lord Bai Zu was a rank eight powerhouse. A tribe he established, with his support, was certain to thrive.</p>
<p>Not only this, with the support of a rank eight Gu Immortal, even if the new tribe&#x27;s foundation was lacking, it would still be a super force!</p>
<p>Northern Plains was Giant Sun Immortal Venerable&#x27;s homeland, the place where he rose up and established a hereditary monarchy. For a long time up to now, the super forces in Northern Plains were almost all of Huang Jin bloodline.</p>
<p>Heavenly Lord Bai Zu was trying to poke his nose into this by establishing a super force, which no doubt was going to create an enormous impact on those Huang Jin descendants.</p>
<p>At the same time, with the lone cultivator, Heavenly Lord Bai Zu, becoming the first supreme elder of a righteous path force, it would have huge impacts on both the demonic path and lone cultivator camps.</p>
<p>Yao Huang&#x27;s smile became even brighter: &quot;Since Heavenly Lord has such an intention, who could stop you in this huge world? It is very difficult for us old fellows to gather together, no time is better than the present, how about we have a chat today?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Haha, this is also what I wanted to say.&quot; Heavenly Lord Bai Zu laughed heartily.</p>
<p>Yao Huang opened up his Immortal Gu House, Medicine Cottage, and invited all the Gu Immortals present to join.</p>
<p>Most of the super forces entered in the end, including Five Elements Grandmaster, who represented the benefits of lone cultivators.</p>
<p>However, Old Ancestor Xue Hu refused.</p>
<p>In this auction, Old Ancestor Xue Hu had invested a lot to buy Ma Hong Yun, as well as precious contents of the essence of the luck path true inheritance.</p>
<p>What he most wanted to do now was to use Ma Hong Yun as the primary material in refining Fortune Rivalling Heaven and use its effects on himself.</p>
<p>The person representing Prince Feng Xian&#x27;s will — Feng Jiu Ge, who had disguised himself as Swordmaster You Lan, also refused this gathering and left on his own.</p>
<p>In a hidden area among the reeds, he quietly met with the Central Continent Gu Immortals.</p>
<p>&quot;I have sent you the message regarding the auction.&quot; Feng Jiu Ge looked at everyone with a smile.</p>
<p>&quot;Lord Jiu Ge made a really good move, such courage, I am in admiration.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;With Zhao Lian Yun, we can search her soul and find many precious clues regarding the main culprit of the Imperial Court incident!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I still have to thank lord for trading a suitable Immortal Gu for me.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What should our actions be next?&quot;</p>
<p>The Central Continent Gu Immortals talked one after the other, clearly treating Feng Jiu Ge as their leader.</p>
<p>Feng Jiu Ge smiled. In this trip, he first used his tyrannical strength to subdue everyone, and now he had taken the risk to enter the tiger&#x27;s den and trade Immortal Gu for them. Through Feng Jiu Ge&#x27;s methods and charisma, a stick in one hand and a carrot in the other, he was finally able to lead this group of experts!</p>
<p>Of course, Feng Jiu Ge knew that behind this harmonious atmosphere, everyone still had their own thoughts.</p>
<p>It was only that all the experts were astute, now that they were in Northern Plains with a heavy mission on their shoulders, even if they had any hidden thoughts, they suppressed it deep within themselves for the time being, choosing to sincerely cooperate.</p>
<p>To Feng Jiu Ge, this was enough.</p>
<p>&quot;Everyone, although we obtained Zhao Lian Yun, the clues available to verify the true culprit are probably not enough. If there were enough clues, Northern Plains&#x27; Gu Immortals would have already discovered the truth and the perpetrator would have nowhere to hide.</p>
<p>However, I found someone who is suspicious.&quot; Feng Jiu Ge said.</p>
<p>Everyone&#x27;s spirits rose at hearing this information.</p>
<p>Feng Jiu Ge continued: &quot;But this person has hidden themselves too deeply, I never was able to find out their true identity, to find this person, we must start from the host of this auction.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Lord is talking about Qin Bai Sheng?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;He is a famous expert of Northern Plains, a rank seven metal path Gu Immortal.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I also know of this person, he is a lone cultivator. Creating such an achievement today with just his own strength is truly rarely seen.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Qin Bai Sheng not only has outstanding strength, he also has many favorable social connections. If we are going against him, we need to use all our strength to strike as quickly as possible. If we let him escape and ask for assistance from his allies, the situation will turn for the worse.&quot;</p>
<p>Everyone was discussing vigorously.</p>
<p>They were the representatives of the ten great ancient sects of Central Continent, powerful and with lot of methods, but they were fighting in enemy territory. Once their identities were exposed, it was likely that Northern Plains&#x27; Gu Immortals would band together to attack them.</p>
<p>Fortunately, they still had Prince Feng Xian to aid them secretly.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmph, what is there to not dare? So what if you are from Chanyu tribe? No matter how powerful the immortal killer move winged blade is, can it save you now? <p>&quot;He Lang Zi, you have hidden your cards well. You actually have an informant in the auction! Didn&#x27;t you call yourself a lone wolf, moving alone and disdaining interaction with other Gu Immortals? Hmph, if you have the guts, then place a stake against me in battle. <p>It was because of this immortal killer move, immemorial flame axe, that he had the confidence to participate in this auction as Chanyu tribe&#x27;s representative.</p>
<p>But who could have thought that he had still greatly underestimated human desire, and also overestimated Chanyu tribe&#x27;s stature.</p>
