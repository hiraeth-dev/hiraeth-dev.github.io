<p>&quot;Junior, take this move!&quot; Liu Hui shouted, a powerful aura erupting from his whole body.</p>
<p>He raised his arms above his head, a grand radiance rose up from his body and rapidly condensed into a crescent moon in the air.</p>
<p>The crescent moon was thin and long, it was pale white but covered in a grey halo.</p>
<p>&quot;Good! Elder Liu Hui finally got the chance to use his move, grey rock moon!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;This move is a well known method of Liu Hui.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s right. Those that are struck by this move will start solidifying before finally turning into stone. Even their immortal aperture will also be contaminated, turning rocky, it is a truly troublesome move!&quot;</p>
<p>Inside Golden Dawn Hall, righteous path Gu Immortals were discussing with smiles on their faces.</p>
<p>On the demonic path&#x27;s side, Xue Wu Hen was only illuminated by the grey moonlight for several breaths of time before his expression changed greatly.</p>
<p>He immediately felt the power of this move, and did not dare to be rash as he quickly used an immortal killer move.</p>
<p>Strong cold aura coiled around his whole body, ice and frost attaching to his body. The grey moonlight turned the ice and frost to stone, but the ice and frost regenerated themselves, the grey moonlight was unable to penetrate through them and reach Xue Wu Hen&#x27;s body.</p>
<p>&quot;What? He actually used an offensive killer move on his own body?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Amazing! Xue Wu Hen handled such a complicated thing with ease, he is quite clever! For the moment, he has blocked Old Liu Hui&#x27;s signature trump card!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Go, beat him!&quot;</p>
<p>The immortals in Chu Du&#x27;s side cheered and applauded loudly.</p>
<p>By contrast, the relaxed mood inside Golden Dawn Hall had now completely dissipated, and a bad feeling rose up in everyone&#x27;s hearts.</p>
<p>&quot;We are not going to lose to Xue Wu Hen again, right?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Even Elder Liu Hui cannot hold him down?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Xue Wu Hen is an unknown character, while Elder Liu Hui is a renowned rank six expert!&quot;</p>
<p>As the immortals talked, Xue Wu Hen wore the heavy layer of stone and frost, and charged towards Elder Liu Hui.</p>
<p>&quot;Old geezer, you are so old, what are you doing not resting at home? Just die!&quot;</p>
<p>Xue Wu Hen pushed both his hands forward, instantly a wave of snow and frost rose up in the air.</p>
<p>The righteous path Gu Immortal, Elder Liu Hui, had to maintain the grey crescent moon in the sky, his hands were raised as if he was supporting the sky, he could not move and was directly submerged by this wave of snow.</p>
<p>Xue Wu Hen won!</p>
<p>Liu Hui died!</p>
<p>Everyone was surprised, they had not expected such an outcome.</p>
<p>&quot;Before this battle, Xue Wu Hen had already won two consecutive battles, he was injured and not in his best condition.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;In the previous two battles, he battled against righteous path juniors, and could not kill the opponent. But now in this third battle, he killed Liu Hui.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The grand Liu Hui actually died at the hands of a nameless demonic path minor character!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Heavens, this is unbelievable.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;This person might only have rank six cultivation, but he truly has rank seven battle strength. Where did this guy come from?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I can&#x27;t help but think of Old Ancestor Xue Hu, this Xue Wu Hen is similarly of ice and snow path, he truly resembles Old Ancestor Xue Hu when he was young.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;He is still far from being qualified to compare to Old Ancestor Xue Hu. But three victories in three consecutive battles, his name will spread throughout the whole Gu Immortal world.&quot;</p>
<p>Whether it be the righteous path or Chu Du&#x27;s side, they were both discussing Xue Wu Hen.</p>
<p>&quot;Xiao Jin, don&#x27;t put your loss to heart. This is not your fault, but the opponent being too strong. He actually killed Liu Hui! Sigh.&quot; Ye Lui Hui Hong consoled the young man beside him.</p>
<p>Ye Lui Xiao Jin felt his right arm.</p>
<p>His right arm was currently still in a frozen state, his whole arm had already turned black and blue from the cold, the intense chill was even affecting his bones and blood.</p>
<p>Ye Lui Xiao Jin felt such pain that after his defeat, he returned to the hall and had his head lowered the whole time.</p>
<p>As for Ye Lui Hui Hong&#x27;s words, he did not seem to have heard them.</p>
<p>Ye Lui Hui Hong saw this, and sighed inwardly: &quot;You are still young. But this setback will be beneficial to you. This shame will make you try harder, who can say what will happen in the future? Maybe your future achievements will be above them. The main thing is to not lose the confidence in yourself.&quot;</p>
<p>As he thought of this, Ye Lui Hui Hong gazed at Xue Wu Hen, who was standing alone in the air, and then at Nian Er Ping Zhi who was unable to stay still.</p>
<p>&quot;Can I go fight now?&quot; Nian Er Ping Zhi impatiently transmitted his voice to his senior, Nian Er Yi Fang.</p>
<p>However, Nian Er Yi Fang shook his head: &quot;This person has won three battles, there are many injuries on him and his state is not at its peak. If you kill him, it would be nothing extraordinary, but if you can&#x27;t kill him, it would instead weaken your prestige. Moreover, the other side is not foolish, why would they let Xue Wu Hen continue fighting?&quot;</p>
<p>Sure enough, the next moment, Chu Du spoke, withdrawing Xue Wu Hen from the battle.</p>
<p>Nian Er Ping Zhi snorted: &quot;Even if he does not battle, I will go up. He won three battles, I will win at least six battles!&quot;</p>
<p>Nian Er Ping Zhi rose up and bowed to Gong Wan Ting: &quot;I request to go to battle!&quot;</p>
<p>Gong Wan Ting&#x27;s frown relaxed a little.</p>
<p>The righteous path, which should have been victorious, was instead defeated thrice by a nameless character from Chu Du&#x27;s side.</p>
<p>This was not only a heavy blow to the righteous path Gu Immortals, it harmed their reputation and was also damaging to Gong Wan Ting&#x27;s prestige.</p>
<p>Gong Wan Ting knew Nian Er Ping Zhi was Nian Er tribe&#x27;s current generation Sword Child, she naturally had no reason to not permit him.</p>
<p>Nian Er Ping Zhi took the stage, and shouted: &quot;Who wants to die?&quot;</p>
<p>The rising morale of Chu Du&#x27;s side immediately stifled.</p>
<p>&quot;Careful, don&#x27;t be fooled by this kid&#x27;s rank six cultivation, he can kill rank sevens.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;He made outstanding battle achievements in the battle of Iron Eagle blessed land, he is also extremely savage, killing multiple Gu Immortals.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Even Old Monster Corpse Poison could not do anything about him.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;He is the current inheritor of Sword Saint Nian Er&#x27;s true inheritance, he naturally is not ordinary.&quot;</p>
<p>Chu Du&#x27;s side was silent.</p>
<p>Facing Nian Er Ping Zhi&#x27;s challenge, there was actually no one who dared to accept for a period of time.</p>
<p>&quot;Youngsters at the moment, each of them truly are fiercer than the other.&quot; Chu Du stroked his forehead.</p>
<p>&quot;Big brother Chu, let me go handle him.&quot; Xue Wu Hen requested.</p>
<p>How could Chu Du permit it.</p>
<p>He already saw Xue Wu Hen&#x27;s weak state, not only were there injuries on his body, many areas of his immortal aperture had even turned into rocks, causing losses to many resources, as a result of Elder Liu Hui&#x27;s immortal killer move.</p>
<p>&quot;Relax, I have already made arrangements to deal with Ping Zhi.&quot; Chu Du smiled, assuring Xue Wu Hen.</p>
<p>&quot;Granny Yin, I will hand this battle to you.&quot; Chu Du spoke to a black robed Gu Immortal behind him.</p>
<p>This Gu Immortal with a hunched back was holding a walking stick, and had been silent the whole time.</p>
<p>Hearing Chu Du&#x27;s words, terrifyingly bony hands stretched out of the wide sleeves and lifted up the hood, revealing an old, crooked face.</p>
<p>&quot;Hehehe, let this old woman love you tenderly.&quot; Granny Yin flew to the battle, and stared at the young Nian Er Ping Zhi with her turbid yellow eyes, which flickered with dangerous light.</p>
<p>&quot;What, she is the rank seven demonic Gu Immortal Granny Yin.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Chu Du is shameless, our side&#x27;s Nian Er Ping Zhi is only a rank six Gu Immortal, they are actually sending a higher rank expert famous in Northern Plains!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;This won&#x27;t do, call back Nian Er Ping Zhi.&quot; Nian Er Yi Fang was also very anxious.</p>
<p>Nian Er Ping Zhi was a future star of Nian Er tribe who was heavily nurtured, and Nian Er Yi Fang was in charge of his safety in this trip. If he died here, Nian Er Yi Fang would have greatly neglected his duty.</p>
<p>However, Nian Er Ping Zhi&#x27;s fighting intent burned even stronger when facing a strong opponent.</p>
<p>He howled towards the sky, and disregarding the crazed voice transmission of Nian Er Yi Fang, he turned into an incomparably sharp sword light that charged towards Granny Yin!</p>
<p>Snowy Mountain, first peak.</p>
