<p>Several days after the copper dragonfish was put up for sale.</p>
<p>Treasure yellow heaven went into a commotion.</p>
<p>&quot;What dragonfish is this?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Copper dragonfish? A brand new species?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I heard the seller saying that the copper dragonfish has a better effect compared to normal dragonfish.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Haha, this will be quite a show.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Indeed, I thought that this seller was defeated by You Chan. But to think that he waited so long just for this powerful counterattack!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;We are all going to benefit from the appearance of this new copper dragonfish species. I can deduce that this seller has a food path true inheritance.&quot; Some Gu Immortals were jealous and envious, especially those experts.</p>
<p>The feeding of Immortal Gu was a problem that troubled not just Fang Yuan alone.</p>
<p>Food path true inheritances were something that most Gu Immortal experts wanted, they just did not have the fortuitous encounters.</p>
<p>However, treasure yellow heaven was a safe place that even Heavenly Court was without options against, these Gu Immortal experts went to approach Fang Yuan&#x27;s will instead.</p>
<p>They directly talked about the food path true inheritance, they expressed their strong intentions to buy it.</p>
<p>But Fang Yuan ignored all of them.</p>
<p>He had so many inheritances in his hands, if he sold them, it would cause a huge commotion in the market, he would earn a ton of profits.</p>
<p>But that was fueling his enemies.</p>
<p>In the following great era, when the five regions chaotic war starts, anyone could become Fang Yuan&#x27;s enemy. In fact, Heavenly Court was already an irreconcilable enemy!</p>
<p>&quot;Find him, find out the identity of this seller.&quot; After being rejected by Fang Yuan, some did not give up.</p>
<p>Some rank eight Gu Immortals decided to collect Fang Yuan&#x27;s intel and try to deduce themselves, or hire wisdom path Gu Immortals to help.</p>
<p>An innocent man attracts greed because of his wealth.</p>
<p>Those who were troubled by the feeding of Immortal Gu were usually the ones who had more Immortal Gu in their possession.</p>
<p>And these people who had lots of Immortal Gu were basically the stronger ones among their peers. Food path true inheritances were not just the solution to this problem.</p>
<p>There was also the dragonfish business!</p>
<p>With the appearance of the copper dragonfish, Gu Immortals saw how much potential this business had as a result of the food path true inheritance!</p>
<p>But they were bound to fail.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan&#x27;s wisdom path defense method made even one of the three wisdom path expert in Eastern Sea, Hua An, fail his deductions. Heavenly Court and heaven&#x27;s will were also unable to deal with him.</p>
<p>In the past, Fang Yuan had gone into hiding because he needed to avoid deductions, but now, he no longer cared about this problem.</p>
<p>An ordinary copper dragonfish was held in You Chan&#x27;s hands as it struggled from time to time.</p>
<p>You Chan&#x27;s hands were trembling slightly, her expression was unsightly.</p>
<p>This tiny insignificant copper dragonfish was a huge threat in You Chan&#x27;s eyes. This was far more terrifying and severe than Fang Yuan lowering his prices.</p>
<p>&quot;To think that the enemy is able to retaliate like this. Impressive, truly impressive, my worry was completely correct.&quot;</p>
<p>After a long while, You Chan calmed down and sighed repeatedly.</p>
<p>The appearance of the copper dragonfish was detrimental to her dragonfish business, it was a fundamental threat.</p>
<p>The severity was extremely high for You Chan, she was in great danger!</p>
<p>In fact, not just her dragonfish business, dragonfish businesses in the entire world was challenged by this copper dragonfish!</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ve already tested it several times, the copper dragonfish is indeed better than ordinary dragonfish, the reason is that its body has a higher proportion of food path dao marks.</p>
<p>What should I do?&quot;</p>
<p>You Chan bit her lips, she had a solemn expression.</p>
<p>She had a tough personality, but after accepting the facts, she felt an intense indignant feeling in her heart.</p>
<p>&quot;I cultivated so hard for countless years, how can I admit defeat so easily?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The dragonfish business is my main source of revenue, if it goes under, my cultivation will be in huge danger.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I cannot lose like this!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Right, I, You Chan, am not a weak woman, how can I admit defeat so easily?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Copper dragonfish, since he can nurture it, why can&#x27;t I?&quot;</p>
<p>You Chan clenched her fists, bright light shone in her eyes as fighting spirit burst out of her.</p>
<p>She wanted to compete, she wanted to try and create her own copper dragonfish.</p>
<p>That was the only way she could contest this mysterious enemy, otherwise, ordinary dragonfish would not allow her to even be in the competition.</p>
<p>You Chan was not blindly confident, she had her own assurance in food path.</p>
<p>She had once obtained a food path inheritance, but it was not as authentic as Fang Yuan&#x27;s.</p>
<p>Even though food path was barely circulated, it was not cultivated by only the creator himself.</p>
<p>Precisely because You Chan had the food path inheritance, she chose the dragonfish business as her main source of income.</p>
<p>In fact, she had been trying to modify the dragonfish&#x27;s quality too, but she had not succeeded yet.</p>
<p>&quot;But now, the modified dragonfish is right in front of me.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I had no progress earlier because my food path attainment was too low. Now that I have this copper dragonfish as reference, I will be able to reverse deduce it, I will rather kill myself if I can&#x27;t even do that.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Hehe, you might not realize it, but your copper dragonfish will become my fortuitous encounter instead!&quot;</p>
<p>You Chan was full of vigor, she researched the copper dragonfish thoroughly, for the following period of time, she was like a crazed researcher, she worked so hard she even forgot to eat her meals.</p>
<p>On the other side, Fang Yuan was also working hard, he continued to deduce the solution to Spring Autumn Cicada&#x27;s problem.</p>
<p>He moved forward with difficulty like a person who had just healed from illness trying to climb a steep mountain with his weak body.</p>
<p>It was filled with difficulty.</p>
<p>But no matter how difficult it was, Fang Yuan&#x27;s ambitions could not be doused.</p>

<p>In this process, Fang Yuan gained a deeper understanding of how powerful the light of wisdom truly was.</p>

<p>Bathing in the light of wisdom, Fang Yuan had unlimited inspiration, and these inspirations led him towards the proper answer.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan&#x27;s wisdom path grandmaster attainment level was also barely holding him together.</p>
