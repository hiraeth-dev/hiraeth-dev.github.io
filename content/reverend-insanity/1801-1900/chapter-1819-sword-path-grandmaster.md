<p>Immortal killer move — Golden Thread Sword!</p>
<p>Fang Yuan shouted loudly as he used the killer move.</p>
<p>Instantly, countless golden threads burst out of his body, stabbing towards his surroundings like a porcupine, piercing everything in their path.</p>
<p>Crack crack crack...</p>
<p>The three Gu Immortals around him were pale and shocked, they wanted to dodge but it was too late, their bodies and souls were completely penetrated by countless golden threads.</p>
<p>Plop, plop, plop, the three Gu Immortals fell onto the ground, lifeless.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan breathed roughly, this was a tough battle, after finally killing the enemy, he was extremely tired.</p>
<p>The world he was in started to vanish until it was completely gone, only Fang Yuan&#x27;s desolate soul was left inside the sovereign immortal aperture&#x27;s Mini Green Heaven with heavy injuries.</p>
<p>&quot;I finally passed the final scene.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;It was really difficult.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;If I did not use unravel dream at the most crucial moment, to find that tiny loophole, I would have failed for sure!&quot;</p>
<p>After Fang Yuan&#x27;s soul returned to his body, he still felt some emotions from earlier.</p>
<p>This was a sword path dream realm.</p>
<p>Inside this dream realm, Fang Yuan&#x27;s strength was low, especially in the final scene, every Gu Immortal enemy was stronger than him.</p>
<p>He had to keep trying and making use of the conflicts between these enemies to disrupt them, creating chances for himself so that he could remain standing.</p>
<p>Eventually, after a hundred tries and incredible luck, he managed to kill the last three Gu Immortal enemies.</p>
<p>&quot;But I passed after all.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Not bad, my sword path attainment level has reached grandmaster.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan inspected himself, he became quite happy.</p>
<p>He had quite a number of sword path Immortal Gu, he only lacked sword path attainment level.</p>
<p>This weakness was finally removed, in the future, his sword path battle strength would also rise greatly.</p>
<p>After resting, Fang Yuan recovered fully and continued exploring dream realms.</p>
<p>&quot;This is likely a human path dream realm.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan&#x27;s desolate soul arrived in the next dream realm as he looked with anticipation.</p>
<p>Extorting Southern Border&#x27;s righteous path was a correct choice. This gave Fang Yuan a large number of dream realms to explore.</p>
<p>The value of these dream realms were extremely high to him.</p>
<p>Because Fang Yuan did not lack resources, Immortal Gu, killer moves, or Immortal Gu recipes, he only lacked attainment level and actual experience.</p>
<p>His desolate soul entered the dream realm.</p>
<p>The world that he was seeing changed immediately.</p>
<p>This was a battlefield.</p>
<p>It was filled with smoke and dust, shouting and killing were all around him.</p>
<p>&quot;Clan leader, be careful!&quot; Fang Yuan had just taken a look when someone shouted at him.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan sensed something amiss, he quickly dodged.</p>
<p>However, his body could not move as fast as his thoughts, an intense pain assaulted him.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan fell onto the ground, looking at the hole on his chest, only half his heart was left.</p>
<p>&quot;This...&quot; Fang Yuan was speechless, he shouted loudly: &quot;Where is the healing Gu Master?&quot;</p>
<p>But no answer came.</p>
<p>The world started to spin, it turned dark rapidly.</p>
<p>At the next moment, Fang Yuan&#x27;s desolate soul suffered heavy injuries as he was pushed out of the dream realm.</p>
<p>Using guts Gu, Fang Yuan&#x27;s desolate soul recovered quickly, after resting, he entered the dream realm again.</p>
<p>&quot;Dodge!&quot; Fang Yuan entered and immediately dodged.</p>
<p>&quot;Clan leader, be careful!&quot; The Gu Master beside him shouted.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan&#x27;s movements suddenly froze as he suffered a fatal hit again.</p>
<p>The moment he fell to the ground, he realized: &quot;The Gu Master who called out to me earlier is a spy. He used an information path method that pretends to alert me but is actually a mortal killer move that restrains my actions!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;There are no healing Gu Masters around me, with this spy around, I can only try to pass this using my own strength.&quot;</p>
<p>In the dream realm, Fang Yuan&#x27;s strength was greatly restricted, he could only use the abilities of the character within the dream.</p>
<p>Originally, Fang Yuan did not want to do this, because he had too little time to react, he could not check the Gu worms that he had.</p>
<p>Before he inspected them, he would definitely get killed.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan could only accept his loss and try a third time.</p>
<p>Plop!</p>
<p>He died again.</p>
<p>Back to reality, Fang Yuan was troubled: &quot;How can this be? I can&#x27;t check my own aperture?</p>
<p>How can I do anything without even knowing what Gu worms I have?&quot;</p>
<p>The dream realm restricted him, he could not inspect what Gu worms he had.</p>
<p>Guess? That was unrealistic.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan tried for a fourth time.</p>
<p>Different from the previous two explorations, Fang Yuan used unravel dream this time.</p>
<p>&quot;Clan leader, be careful!&quot; The Gu Master beside him shouted.</p>
<p>Plop!</p>
<p>Fang Yuan suffered a fatal blow, he fell on the ground.</p>
<p>&quot;Clan leader!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Impossible, lord clan leader has justice Gu on him, how could he be defeated so easily?!&quot;</p>
<p>The traitor shouted beside him.</p>
<p>After the exploration failed, Fang Yuan&#x27;s desolate soul was thrown out of the dream realm again.</p>
<p>&quot;Immortal killer move unravel dream only gave me one hint.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The traitor spoke an extra sentence, pointing out that I have justice Gu.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Then let&#x27;s use it.&quot;</p>
<p>During Fang Yuan&#x27;s fifth exploration, at the crucial moment, he used justice Gu.</p>
<p>&quot;Clan leader, be careful!&quot; The traitor screamed.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan&#x27;s dodging movement froze again.</p>
<p>Justice Gu!</p>
<p>Piercing white light burst out of Fang Yuan&#x27;s body, his speed went back to normal as he dodged the fatal blow.</p>
<p>&quot;Huff! I succeeded.&quot; Fang Yuan rolled on the battlefield as he quickly looked around, assessing the area.</p>
<p>This was a village.</p>
<p>Everywhere, there was rubble and corpses lying on the ground.</p>
<p>&quot;The conflict between two clans? This is already at the step of exterminating a clan.&quot;</p>
<p>But Fang Yuan found that he was not the victim, he was the invader, his side had the absolute advantage.</p>
<p>&quot;This is a mortal level fight and I am the clan leader, by just leading these Gu Masters, we can win.&quot; Fang Yuan had a calm and composed mood.</p>
<p>&quot;But is the condition for clearing this dream realm winning the battle? Let&#x27;s try.&quot;</p>
<p>After that, Fang Yuan was joyful to find out that his aperture could be inspected again.</p>
<p>The dream realm character had rank five cultivation level and many mortal Gu, there were five to six rank four Gu, and one rank five Gu which was justice Gu.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan immediately understood as he smiled: &quot;With these Gu worms, I can settle this myself.&quot;</p>
<p>Be it leading the troops or fighting personally to obtain victory, it was extremely easy for Fang Yuan.</p>
<p>&quot;Lord clan leader!&quot; At this time, the traitor appeared, showing a concerned expression.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan smiled coldly: &quot;I will kill this traitor first!&quot;</p>
<p>He attacked immediately, killing the traitor with one killer move.</p>
<p>&quot;Clan leader, what are you doing?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The vice clan leader is dead, the clan leader killed him!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I was right after all, Chen clan is trying to take down our Shan clan.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Rebel! Rebel! Let&#x27;s take revenge for Lord Shan!&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan was shocked, he killed the traitor but half of the Gu Masters started to rebel and attack.</p>
<p>The scene fell into chaos, a three-way battle started.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan noticed that this was bad: &quot;I have rank five justice Gu, it can give me great strength. With my strength, I can still win and kill all opposition. But... this traitor should not have been dealt with this casually.&quot;</p>
<p>As expected, even though Fang Yuan won, there were only a few Gu Masters left standing on the battlefield.</p>
<p>Corpses and broken body parts were everywhere, blood flowed like a river.</p>
<p>The world dissipated as Fang Yuan&#x27;s desolate soul was thrown out again.</p>
<p>But this time, he did not suffer much injuries.</p>
<p>This result made Fang Yuan gain a deeper guess: &quot;This means that victory is indeed the condition to pass the first scene. However, the type of victory is crucial. If the losses are too high, I will not be able to pass it.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan entered the dream realm again with newfound knowledge.</p>
<p>This time, he tolerated the existence of the traitor, he went as the vanguard and used justice Gu along with many killer moves to slaughter the enemies, obtaining a perfect victory!</p>
<p>&quot;Lord clan leader is so courageous!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;We have won, we are victorious!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Nie clan has finally been exterminated. Hahaha.&quot;</p>
<p>Amidst a wave of fun and laughter, the dream realm entered the second scene.</p>
<p>Within the hall, bright lights were shining. There were delicious food and wine on the table, dozens of Gu Masters were gathered here as they enjoyed a luxurious feast.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan found that he was sitting beside the main seat, towards his left was Chen clan leader, who was sitting at the main seat.</p>
<p>&quot;What is going on?&quot; Fang Yuan was shocked: &quot;In the previous scene, I was in the role of Chen clan leader, but this time, I actually became the traitor vice clan leader?&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan was seeing this for the first time, he did not know whether to laugh or cry.</p>
<p>However, he soon thought: &quot;No wonder I could not kill the traitor in the first scene. Because he is the next character that I should portray, now then, how do I pass this?&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan looked at the situation with cold eyes.</p>
<p>Chen clan leader drank a huge mouthful of wine as he placed down the empty wine cup, sighing: &quot;We finally won! Brother, to speak the truth, I have finally relaxed only now. When I heard that Nie clan was planning to destroy the mountain, I could not sleep at all. They were unable to compete with the alliance of our two clans, so they created such a vicious plan, it was truly evil. Thankfully, heaven is not blind, they got what they deserved, these people died because of just deserts.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan did not know what to say.</p>
<p>This Chen clan leader was ignorant, he had not realized the ill intentions of this vice clan leader.</p>
<p>But it was not surprising.</p>
<p>During the first scene, even though the vice clan leader used an information path method, he disguised it as a warning of concern, there were no traces left behind, it was truly hard to notice.</p>
<p>Fang Yuan had a sharp sense because he was a Gu Immortal, he realized the vice clan leader&#x27;s vicious scheme.</p>
<p>When he tried to kill the traitor in the first scene, a rebellion occurred.</p>
<p>&quot;Wait, even if the vice clan leader&#x27;s members see him getting killed, they would not act so united, right?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;There has to be some people who are hesitant during the chaos.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;But these people rebelled in unity, after the vice clan leader was killed, they acted together and took revenge on the Chen clan members without holding back.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan suddenly had a flash of inspiration, he understood: &quot;So that means that the vice clan leader&#x27;s intentions to harm Chen clan leader is not his own plan, it is the collective plan of the entire Shan clan.&quot;</p>
<p>Fang Yuan thought of this as Chen clan leader&#x27;s expression suddenly changed, he held his stomach as he stood up, taking several steps back: &quot;This wine... is poisoned!&quot;</p>
