<p>Even though he used all his strength to shout, Meng Qiu Zhen&#x27;s voice was still faint.</p>
<p>&quot;Who is asking for help?&quot; A mortal girl pushed aside the brambles and came to the place.</p>
<p>&quot;Here, look below...&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen spoke, his voice so faint that it was like a whisper.</p>
<p>The mortal girl did not hear him, mumbling: &quot;Did I hear wrongly? Sigh, it is getting late, I need to quickly gather mushrooms and sell them in Tu clan village. Eh? There are so many mushrooms here!&quot;</p>
<p>The mortal girl saw Meng Qiu Zhen.</p>
<p>But right now, Meng Qiu Zhen&#x27;s body was already covered with mushrooms.</p>
<p>The mortal girl squatted and started picking the mushrooms on Meng Qiu Zhen&#x27;s body.</p>
<p>Every mushroom she picked, the cause of Meng Qiu Zhen&#x27;s falling vitality would decrease by one.</p>
<p>His illness was alleviated rapidly and his strength slowly recovered.</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s right, there are still two days before my illness truly breaks out. For now at least, my life should be safe!&quot;</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen quickly realized.</p>
<p>He then spoke: &quot;Girl, the mushrooms on my body are delicious, it doesn&#x27;t matter if you pluck them all.&quot;</p>
<p>The mortal girl jumped from shock.</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t be afraid, I am also a person. It was me who asked for help earlier, thank you for saving me, kind girl.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;But I did not do anything.&quot;</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen gave a forced smile: &quot;Pluck all the mushrooms from my body and that will be saving me. These mushrooms are harmful to us mushroommen, but they are harmless and even beneficial to other races.&quot;</p>
<p>The girl was shocked before smiling: &quot;So you are a mushroomman, I have heard rumors about you guys since I was a child, the rumors said you were gentle and never harmed people. I had never seen a living mushroomman before I met you.&quot;</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen quickly said: &quot;Pick the mushrooms quickly, I can&#x27;t last much longer.&quot;</p>
<p>The girl&#x27;s hands were nimble, she rapidly picked the mushrooms from Meng Qiu Zhen&#x27;s body.</p>
<p>He was out of the hopeless situation and rescued from the brink of failure.</p>
<p>&quot;There is still one last mushroom!&quot; The girl said seriously.</p>
<p>&quot;Wait.&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen&#x27;s expression changed, &quot;You don&#x27;t need to pick that mushroom — ahhh!&quot;</p>
<p>The girl blinked her pure eyes and her face reddened slightly: &quot;I can&#x27;t pull it out, does it hurt?&quot;</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen was pale as he took a sharp breath: &quot;Don&#x27;t pull it.&quot;</p>
<p>But the girl shook her head, saying stubbornly: &quot;That won&#x27;t do, mushroomman big brother, you said it earlier, I need to pick all the mushrooms from your body for you to be cured.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t, don&#x27;t, don&#x27;t... ahhh!&quot;</p>
<p>The girl pulled up her sleeves, showing an extremely serious and cute expression: &quot;Hmph, I don&#x27;t believe I can&#x27;t pull it out!&quot;</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen: &quot;Ahhughhh—!&quot;</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Amidst the miserable cries of Meng Qiu Zhen, the second stage was finally passed.</p>
<p>His sight changed from a chaotic whirlpool of colors to gradually clearing up.</p>
<p>This was a bustling and busy market with people walking everywhere.</p>
<p>&quot;This is Tu clan village?&quot; Behind Meng Qiu Zhen, the mushroomman Huang Xiao Mi, who was covered tightly, said in a low voice.</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen&#x27;s mind shook slightly.</p>
<p>In the previous two stages, he had played the role of Huang Xiao Mi, but in this third stage, his identity was actually changed.</p>
<p>This situation was not common.</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen quickly inspected himself.</p>
<p>&quot;Huh, I became that human girl.&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen sighed inwardly.</p>
<p>Huang Xiao Mi&#x27;s strength was barely enough, and now, Meng Qiu Zhen was a mortal, a young girl at that.</p>
<p>The change in identity caused Meng Qiu Zhen to feel some doubts.</p>
<p>What was the main mission in this Paradise Earth dream realm?</p>
<p>In the previous two stages, he had Huang Xiao Mi&#x27;s identity and had estimated the dream realm&#x27;s main mission was to save the mushroomman tribe by curing the plague.</p>
<p>But now that his identity changed, Meng Qiu Zhen immediately felt his speculation wavering intensely.</p>
<p>&quot;No matter what, since Huang Xiao Mi followed the human girl into Tu clan village, I should still try to help Huang Xiao Mi to find the mushroomman witch.&quot;</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen decided to continue following the previous trail.</p>
<p>&quot;Fang Tu, you came? Quick, the position beside me is very good, take it quickly. The evening market is not like the morning market, it only lasts for a short period of time.&quot; A middle-aged uncle waved at Meng Qiu Zhen.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you, uncle.&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen thought for a bit and placed her basket filled with mushrooms at the space beside the uncle.</p>
<p>Huang Xiao Mi silently helped Meng Qiu Zhen set up the mushrooms.</p>
<p>The middle-aged uncle took a glance and exclaimed: &quot;These mushrooms are really fresh.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Naturally.&quot; Huang Xiao Mi said proudly.</p>
<p>The middle-aged uncle&#x27;s gaze moved towards him: &quot;This is?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;This is my distant relative, he came to help me.&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen quickly replied.</p>
<p>The middle-aged uncle did not ask anymore, the evening market had already started, crowds of people were moving around and everyone was busy.</p>
<p>Some were busy buying, some were busy selling.</p>
<p>The traded goods were all mortal materials like mushrooms, vegetables, bean cakes, and so on. This was a mortal market and had daily necessities. There were no transactions of Gu worms, unless a merchant caravan passed through Tu clan village.</p>
<p>After about five minutes, the crowd began to grow sparse.</p>
<p>At this time, sounds of disturbance came from ahead.</p>
<p>&quot;Step aside, step aside!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Move aside, can&#x27;t you see Lord Green Beast is here?&quot;</p>
<p>A young man shouted, five to six people were pushing through the narrow pavement.</p>
<p>Everywhere they went, pedestrians made way in fear and nervousness, while some even showed disdain and hatred inwardly.</p>
<p>&quot;Fang Tu, hide quickly. Don&#x27;t let that &#x27;beast&#x27; see you.&quot; The middle-aged uncle quickly reminded.</p>
<p>&quot;Ah?&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen was dazed.</p>
<p>&quot;Lady Fang Tu!&quot; At the next moment, the leader of the group, the bald youngster, shouted.</p>
<p>His eyes let out a sneaky light as he noticed Meng Qiu Zhen from far away.</p>
<p>&quot;What&#x27;s going on?&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen narrowed his eyes as he saw the bald youngster walking towards him with quick steps.</p>
<p>The bald youngster had thick brows and large eyes but had not even a single hair on his head; instead, there was a tattoo of a green beast on his head.</p>
<p>The bald youngster rubbed his green beast tattoo and smiled widely: &quot;Lady Fang Tu, you are selling mushrooms again. I want to buy them.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Oh?&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen was inwardly vigilant while asking, &quot;How much do you want to buy?&quot;</p>
<p>The bald youngster raised a finger: &quot;I want to buy one kilogram. Help me pick the big and good ones.&quot;</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen began to pick the mushrooms.</p>
<p>&quot;This is good, hmm, that one is also good, I want them.&quot; The bald youngster nicknamed &#x27;Green Beast&#x27; pointed here and there.</p>
<p>&quot;Eh, I told you, I want this one and that one!&quot; The bald youngster said.</p>
<p>&quot;Which ones do you want?&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen frowned, sensing that the young man seemed to be toying with him.</p>
<p>The bald youngster grinned: &quot;Forget it, Sister Fang, let me help you, we will choose together.&quot;</p>
<p>The bald youngster acted immediately.</p>
<p>&quot;Soft, so soft.&quot; He chose several mushrooms and said.</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen replied: &quot;These are all fresh, I picked them just today.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Smooth, so smooth.&quot; The bald youngster praised again.</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen slowly looked up and said with a frown: &quot;You are touching my hand!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Ah, sorry, sorry.&quot; The bald youngster laughed.</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen opened his eyes wide: &quot;You are still touching?!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Accident, it was an accident!&quot;</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen was speechless, he was actually being molested!</p>
<p>&quot;Pfft, shameless guy, actually molesting an innocent girl in broad daylight!&quot; Huang Xiao Mi could not endure it anymore, he shouted.</p>
<p>&quot;Who are you? Why are you standing beside my Lady Fang!&quot; The bald youngster was immediately provoked and stared at Huang Xiao Mi.</p>
<p>The two started arguing, sparks seemed to be flying in the air.</p>
<p>&quot;This is the limit of dream butterfly Immortal Gu.&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen sighed inwardly.</p>
<p>Dream butterfly Immortal Gu could inspect the details of the next event of the dream realm, but it could not inspect what the owner of Dream Butterfly would encounter.</p>
<p>Because the changes in the dream realm would occur when the Gu cultivator entered and interacted with the dream realm.</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen had already inspected the bald youngster and this Tu clan village&#x27;s market in the previous stage. But he was unable to know what he would encounter specifically in advance.</p>
<p>The two argued in front of Meng Qiu Zhen, their argument got increasingly heated to the point of nearly exchanging blows.</p>
<p>Meng Qiu Zhen was just about to persuade them to stop when a flash of inspiration hit him, and he kept quiet.</p>
<p>&quot;Ugh, I am getting angry! Brat, you are looking for a beating!!&quot; The bald youngster suddenly rubbed his head and attacked.</p>
<p>&quot;Hmph, who is afraid of you!&quot; Huang Xiao Mi did not show weakness, attacking immediately.</p>
<p>The two started fighting.</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t fight, don&#x27;t fight.&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen called out while retreating. He was in a mortal body now, and had no ability to interfere in a Gu Master&#x27;s battle.</p>
<p>But fortunately, be it Huang Xiao Mi or the bald youngster, they both cared about Meng Qiu Zhen.</p>
<p>When Meng Qiu Zhen retreated to a safe distance, they started using Gu cultivator methods.</p>
<p>After several rounds, Huang Xiao Mi was clearly at a disadvantage and was not the bald youngster&#x27;s match.</p>
<p>&quot;This ruffian is really strong!&quot; Meng Qiu Zhen was slightly surprised. He was well aware of Huang Xiao Mi&#x27;s strength, after he acted as the latter in the first and second stage of the dream realm.</p>
<p>But he did not expect the bald youngster to be so strong, he had already suppressed Huang Xiao Mi.</p>
<p>&quot;Wait a second, you are not a human, you are a variant human.&quot; The bald youngster suddenly called out.</p>
<p>In the heat of the battle, Huang Xiao Mi&#x27;s identity was exposed.</p>
<p>&quot;Amazing, you spy, what are you trying to do by entering my Tu clan village? You definitely have bad intentions! Hahaha, I am going to make a great contribution!&quot;</p>
<p>The bald youngster continued to shout: &quot;Attack him together, he is a mushroomman, we don&#x27;t have to uphold the moral of humanity against such a person!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Despicable! If you are a man, fight me alone!&quot; Huang Xiao Mi was heavily outnumbered and captured, but he was still shouting indignantly.</p>
<p>&quot;Hahaha. I will be Tu clan&#x27;s hero by catching you!&quot; The bald youngster laughed, kicking the bound Huang Xiao Mi, &quot;Just lie still, you variant human.&quot;</p>
<p>After saying this, the bald youngster ran towards Meng Qiu Zhen and asked in concern:</p>
<p>&quot;Sister Fang, are you alright? You definitely had no idea or were forced by this mushroomman. Haha, how will you thank me now?&quot;</p>
