<p><strong>I</strong> KEPT THE SHOCK I felt from seeing Zenith and Claire from showing on my face…I think. I wasn&#x27;t sure I could win this standoff or that everything would turn out okay. The only thing I could control was me, and I&#x27;d do what I could. It only took a second to run a mental simulation of how to get Zenith out.</p>
<p>I couldn&#x27;t use a teleportation circle in front of this many people, but I had a good idea of the capabilities of the Temple Knights. I didn&#x27;t know how strong the Temple Knights lined up behind the pope were, but if the Blessed Child were telling the truth, they wouldn&#x27;t be stronger than the Keepers of Anastasia.</p>
<p>I could get Zenith. Just knowing that, I&#x27;d as good as achieved one of my goals. I&#x27;d get Zenith and Cliff, then get Aisha, and Geese. Then we&#x27;d get the hell out. I was worried Aisha and Geese were being held somewhere, but I could find out if and where from one of these guys.</p>
<p>With that plan in mind, I escorted the Blessed Child to her chair and stood beside her. I kept tight hold of her arms.</p>
<p>Before I sat down in the seat next to hers, I said, &quot;I&#x27;m so glad you&#x27;re all here. It&#x27;ll make everything go more quickly.&quot;</p>
<p>I was perfectly calm—the words rolled easily off my tongue. It&#x27;d been a while since I felt like myself.</p>
<p>&quot;I believe this is the first time a number of us have met,&quot; I went on. &quot;I represent the Dragon God Orsted, and I came here to deepen his bonds of friendship with the Millis Church.&quot;</p>
<p>The title of Dragon God sent a ripple of unease shivering around the table. No one here had met Orsted in person, and I seriously doubted any of them knew about his objectives. What we were up against. Possibly some of them hadn&#x27;t even heard of the Seven Great Powers. But everyone knew the title Dragon God. It was usually found alongside another title: &quot;Demon God.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Owing to unfortunate circumstances,&quot; I went on, &quot;I currently hold the Blessed Child&#x27;s life in my hands.&quot;</p>
<p>I pointed at her and concentrated my magic to make a lighter-sized flame at my fingertip. The tension in the room mounted.</p>
<p>&quot;I can&#x27;t tell you how much I regret that it had to come to this. To stoop to taking hostages is to bring dishonor upon the name of a superlative uber being like Sir Orsted. Alas, it was a necessary measure in order to facilitate these negotiations—and to guarantee my own safety and that of my subordinates. I hope you all understand.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;A superlative…uber…?&quot;</p>
<p>My tongue ran away with me there. I wasn&#x27;t trying to be funny, promise.</p>
<p>I coughed, then continued. &quot;Why,&quot; I said, looking around the table, &quot;was this attempt made upon my life? Why was I forced to bring shame upon my master&#x27;s name?&quot; My eyes came to rest on Claire. She was frowning. &quot;Would anyone here care to explain? If no explanation is forthcoming, I, along with the Dragon God Orsted and all his followers, will have no choice but to move to open hostilities with the Millis Church.&quot;</p>
<p>This wasn&#x27;t an empty threat. If the Man-God had the top members of the Millis Church in his back pocket, then it was a potential development I had to consider.</p>
<p>The room stayed silent. Not one person took the bait. No cries of &quot;Bring it on, then!&quot;</p>
