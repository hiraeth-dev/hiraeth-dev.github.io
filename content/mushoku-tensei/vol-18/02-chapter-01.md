<p>&quot;Yes. All right. Thank you… Yeah.&quot; Angie jerkily bobbed her head up and down, her face devoid of emotion. Alas, the irregular events of a few moments prior were still burned vividly into her mind.</p>
<p>&quot;Well then, take care.&quot; Rudeus spun on his heel and started to leave, only to pause as if he&#x27;d just remembered something. He glanced over his shoulder at her. &quot;Oh, that reminds me. Miss Angie, you said you would return the favor, yes?&quot;</p>
<p>Another chill ran down her spine. A favor? Yes. Now that she thought about it, he had saved her life. If he wanted her body, she couldn&#x27;t very well refuse him. It didn&#x27;t matter how instinctively disgusted she felt by him, she wasn&#x27;t such an ingrate that she would say no.</p>
<p>&quot;Erm, uh… Well, I&#x27;m afraid I really can&#x27;t give you my underwear…&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No, I don&#x27;t need your underwear. There&#x27;s something I want you to do for me.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;S-something you want me to do?&quot;</p>
<p>Oh crap. She just knew he would ask her to do something totally debauched. The color drained from her face as she mentally prepared herself for it.</p>
<p>Rudeus saw her reaction and scratched the back of his head. &quot;Guess it&#x27;s only natural she&#x27;d be disgusted,&quot; he muttered as he slipped something out of his backpack. He held out a picture book and a figurine. &quot;Miss Angie, if you happen to have a child someday, I&#x27;d like you to read this picture book to them. Tell them that the Superd Tribe aren&#x27;t a bunch of devils.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Huh? Supe-what? What are you talking about?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The Superd Tribe.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The Superd Tribe…&quot; Angie echoed his words, completely thrown by this request out of the left field. Her eyes were wide as saucers.</p>
<p>&quot;There is a glossary at the end of the book to help with learning how to read the letters, so you can use that as part of your child&#x27;s education, too. I do hope you&#x27;ll share this with them.&quot;</p>
<p>He then departed, leaving Angie stood there dumbfounded, a picture book and a figure with green hair in her hands. Just looking at the likeness of the Superd Tribe was terrifying. The figure itself was eerily intricate and the colors painted on it made it look so realistic she wondered if it might suddenly spring to life. This little statuette was a perfect replica of a terrifying demon. She felt the urge to dispose of it immediately, but she remembered that Rudeus had saved her life, and stopped herself.</p>
<p>&quot;Um…&quot;</p>
<p>The Superd Tribe, huh? She had never met one of them before, but she had heard of them. They were a group of people often referred to as devils. When she was younger, her parents would often warn her that if she did anything bad, the Superds would come to carry her off and devour her. And yet Rudeus was trying to spread the story that they weren&#x27;t awful people after all.</p>
<p><em>Why would he do such a thing?</em></p>
<p>Unable to decipher his motivations, Angie wasn&#x27;t quite sure what to do. She pressed her finger against the figure&#x27;s head.</p>
<p>&quot;Ah!&quot; She gasped as the hair came off. Now all that was left behind was what looked like a bald warrior wielding a spear, cradled safely in her hand. &quot;Pfft.&quot; Angie burst into laughter. She still had no idea what to make of this, but her savior had made a request of her that she planned to honor.</p>
<p>Several years after that, Dochil—whom Angie had cured with her tonic—proposed to her and she became the second wife of their new village leader. He was a hard worker, but a rather boring man. The only upside was that he wasn&#x27;t a pervert. Angie was grateful for that at least. When her child was born, she did as she was told and read the picture book to them as she raised them.</p>
<p>Eventually, that picture book&#x27;s story would become widespread throughout the village. The nearby region would come to recognize the Superd Tribe as a clan of bald warriors of justice. That, however, is a story for another time.</p>
