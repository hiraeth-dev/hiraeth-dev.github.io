<p><strong>M</strong>y name is Rudeus Greyrat, and I&#x27;m a pretty boy who just celebrated my eleventh birthday a few days ago. As a skilled magician, I&#x27;ve gained notoriety for my ability to use magic without chanting spells and my unique way of mixing the different elements.</p>
<p>One year ago, I was caught in a magical disaster and teleported to the Demon Continent. My hometown was on the exact opposite side of the map in the Asura Kingdom&#x27;s Fittoa Region, which meant I had to travel halfway across the world to get back.</p>
<p>I became an adventurer to earn money as I started out on the long voyage home. In the past year, I&#x27;ve successfully cut my way across the Demon Continent.</p>

<p>It was a city with a flavor uniquely its own, different from the others I had seen before. Beyond the harbor, across from the city, the sea spread out vast and wide. When was the last time I had seen the ocean? Probably back when I attended a middle school on the coast.</p>
<p>This world was different from ours, but the sea was not. It was the same blue, had the same sound of crashing waves, and birds that looked just like seagulls. There were even sailboats. It was the first time I&#x27;d ever set my eyes on one. I saw them occasionally in movies, but seeing the real thing—made of wood with its cloth sails unfurled, gliding through the water—made my heart pound as if I were still a young boy.</p>
<p>There must be some mechanism in this world that allowed a boat to sail against a headwind. Actually, considering the world I was in, maybe they propelled themselves forward using wind magic.</p>
