<p>How was I supposed to respond to that? I hadn&#x27;t seen this coming. Should I tell him it was luck? Well, maybe not luck. I <em>had</em> gone to see Randolph of my own volition.</p>
<p>I considered telling him <em>You can&#x27;t call</em> yourself <em>the most powerful king ever,</em> but ditched that idea. There was something I needed to ask him.</p>
<p>&quot;I have one question. Are you a disciple of the Man-God?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, I am. I am indebted to Him. He helped me to escape the clutches of Death God Raxos and showed me the way to Hell on the Divine Continent. I&#x27;ve only survived this long thanks to Him… But then I left, and look where it got me. I suppose it was fate.&quot;</p>
<p>Vita shrank smaller and smaller. When we first got to this room, he&#x27;d been person-sized, but now he was only as big as a fist.</p>
<p>&quot;Let me tell you one last thing, Rudeus,&quot; he said. I waited. &quot;The Man-God is awful, but there are many like me who will put their faith in Him simply because He saved them.&quot; Vita was now the size of a fingertip. Meanwhile, the skeleton crumbled into dust and blew away.</p>
<p>&quot;Wait! The other disciples…!&quot; I shouted, but my awareness faded.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>My eyes opened. I felt wide awake. I remembered all of it—the dreams and the conversation from the room at the end.</p>
<p>&quot;Ugh.&quot; I was seized by a sharp pain in my stomach and felt the urge to puke. &quot;Bleargh…&quot; I moaned, bending over on all fours as I vomited up a sticky fluid. It was blue. The blue fluid oozed over the ground, mixed with stomach juices and last night&#x27;s dinner.</p>
<p>Was this…Abyssal King Vita&#x27;s corpse?</p>
<p>Just then, I felt a strange sensation in my left hand. I removed my gauntlet and the Death God&#x27;s ring fell to the ground in shattered pieces. It sank into my puke with a squelching sound.</p>
<p>The ring had broken. I guess that confirmed Vita&#x27;s story. By entering my body of his own accord, Vita had committed suicide via the Death</p>
<p>God&#x27;s ring. Poor guy.</p>
<p>Was this a bad call on Vita&#x27;s part, really? If he&#x27;d taken control of me, the Man-God would have as good as won. There would have been nothing I could do to stop it…</p>
<p>It was a coincidence—or maybe I ought to call it fate—that had stopped him. Raxos&#x27;s bone ring hadn&#x27;t only been good for making Kishirika talk after all. Randolph himself might not have known the ring&#x27;s real power, either.</p>
<p>&quot;Oh, right,&quot; I said, looking around. &quot;What about Ruijerd?&quot; I was inside a building. This floor, these walls, this layout… I knew this place. It was Ruijerd&#x27;s house.</p>
<p>Considering what had happened, maybe Ruijerd had carried me here after Vita jumped from him to me? It was light outside. How many hours had passed? I decided cleaning up the puke could wait until after I&#x27;d found him.</p>
<p>&quot;Ruijerd?&quot; I called, but the master of the house didn&#x27;t reply. Maybe he was out. Or maybe there was another reason. For the time being, I&#x27;d survey my surroundings. I needed to see what was going on.</p>
<p>I sat up. Right away, I found Ruijerd. He was lying on the ground on the other side of the hearth.</p>
<p>&quot;Rui—&quot; I began, then broke off, speechless. Ruijerd&#x27;s face was gray and he was wheezing, shivering violently as he clutched at himself.</p>
<p>Oh, this was <em>bad</em>.</p>
<p>It reminded me of something he&#x27;d said. <em>If Abyssal King Vita dies, his offshoots die as well. The village will be engulfed by the plague again.</em></p>
<p>So Ruijerd was in this state because…</p>
<p>&quot;The…the plague…&quot;</p>
<p>Abyssal King Vita hadn&#x27;t just died quietly. Yeah, what he&#x27;d done had been more than an inadvertent suicide… It was a suicide bombing.</p>
