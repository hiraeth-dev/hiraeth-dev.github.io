<p><strong>T</strong>HE RANOA UNIVERSITY OF MAGIC. The world&#x27;s greatest magic school, occupying a vast plot of land and sponsored by three separate countries as well as the Magicians&#x27; Guild. The current principal was one of the higher-ups at the Magicians&#x27; Guild, the Wind King-tier magician Georg. The student body was over ten thousand strong, with numerous professors in the university&#x27;s employ. Despite the name &quot;Magic University,&quot; you could actually learn a variety of different things there.</p>
<p>Students were welcomed from all races, including demons, who were still deeply ostracized by the Millis faith, or beastmen, who tended to be isolationists. They even accepted human royalty who&#x27;d been driven from their country due to power conflicts, or noble children who were born cursed. There were no sky folk or sea folk enrolled, but if you had mana and could cast magic, you were free to apply, whatever your checkered history. This policy had invited some opposition, from what I heard, but only the Asura Kingdom could conceivably oppose the united might of the alliance and Magicians&#x27; Guild, and it had invested no small amount of money into the Magicians&#x27; Guild itself.</p>
<p>Incidentally, a certain sect within the Holy Country of Millis—the Temple Knights, as they were called—had positioned themselves as standing in direct opposition to the university and everything it represented. However, given they were on the other side of the world, it seemed they didn&#x27;t care enough to start a war over it.</p>
<p>The enrollment period for students was seven years. You could take a year abroad twice, for a maximum of nine years of study. If you became a researcher affiliated with the Magicians&#x27; Guild, you could continue using the university&#x27;s equipment after graduation.</p>
<p>The school had a massive five-story dorm, but staying there was optional. Those who had a house in the city commuted from home. In general, however, most students lived in the dorm. A room was prepared for me, a simple space about twenty-tatami-mats wide, with a bunk bed. There was also a table and chair. Two students typically shared a single room, but special students lived alone. I could request a roommate if I wanted, but I decided against it. I hadn&#x27;t come here to make friends.</p>
<p>Apparently, you could also pay to be moved to an exclusive room for noblemen, which was more spacious and secure. Not something I needed, I was sure. I wasn&#x27;t being targeted by assassins for the moment.</p>
<p>The bathroom was in the hallway. Surprisingly, it was flushable. Granted, it wasn&#x27;t like you could just pull a lever and whoosh! There was a water jug next to it and you had to pour water out of it for a manual flush, which would send the crap all the way down to the sewers. Of course, those like me were encouraged to use water magic to wash it down. On that note, the task of filling the water jug fell to the person on duty, but as a special student, I was exempt.</p>
<p>Uniforms were also provided. Men were given a suit while the women were given what resembled a blazer and skirt. Apparently, the specific uniforms had only been introduced this year.</p>
<p>Honestly, I found the designs quite cute. There had to be gym shorts for exercise wear, right? Or so you&#x27;d think, but unfortunately, it was just robes. The school didn&#x27;t provide those, and didn&#x27;t specify any restrictions or preferences. Students who didn&#x27;t already have their own robes probably just bought whatever they wanted. I had the robe I&#x27;d been wearing for a while now, so I didn&#x27;t need to buy another.</p>
<p>&quot;Well, does it look good on me?&quot;</p>
<p>Elinalise, in her new school outfit, was currently modeling for me. The way her hair was shaped in lustrous rolls made the robe she was wearing look more like cosplay, but the uniform actually suited her well. Although that, too, looked like cosplay to me because I knew her true character.</p>
<p>&quot;If you roll up the skirt and make it a little shorter, you might have an easier time catching men. Make sure it&#x27;s just enough that they can almost see your panties.&quot;</p>
<p>Elinalise looked at me like I was a genius. &quot;But isn&#x27;t that going to be a little cold?&quot; she asked.</p>
<p>&quot;Put on thigh-high tights and you should be fine, right?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I see. I should&#x27;ve expected as much from you, Rudeus. You&#x27;re a genius.&quot; Elinalise followed my advice and folded up her skirt like a high school girl. Then she rolled up the waist until you could almost glimpse her fancy underwear.</p>
<p>Hmm… yeah, sexy panties like that just don&#x27;t suit a uniform, I decided.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>We headed to the opening ceremony, which was apparently a thing at this school. The year&#x27;s new intake was assembled in the cold courtyard. There was a girl looking bored by herself, while another boy listened intently to the principal&#x27;s speech. Acquaintances were loosely gathered and some were even chatting idly. No one was lined up in an orderly fashion. If this were a Japanese school, the civic guidance teacher would no doubt be screaming their head off. The principal stood before our motley bunch, atop a podium constructed of magic resistant brick, giving his speech.</p>
<p>&quot;Ladies and gentlemen, many moons have passed since those known as magicians were considered inferior to swordsmen. It is true that the styles of swordplay created by the Sword Gods are supreme. However! Magic is just as peerless! Swordsmanship, after all, is nothing more than a tool with which to kill. Magic is different. Magic has a future! We will retake what we have lost, and combine it with current styles of incantation to bring forth a new—&quot;</p>
<p>I stood quietly beside Elinalise. The principal&#x27;s sermon felt just as long in this world as it had in my old one, but this one was more tolerable. Perhaps because his speech was overflowing with a passion for magic!</p>
<p>…Nope, that wasn&#x27;t it. It was because of how hilarious it was watching him frantically try to restrain the wig on his head.</p>
<p>Elinalise was surveying the area, evaluating the men she saw. She looked like she was struggling to decide who to start with first.</p>
<p>&quot;That is all. Ladies and gentlemen, the path of magic extends before you!&quot;</p>
<p>Jenius ended with words that made him sound like some protector of freedom and justice. There was no singing of the school anthem. There wasn&#x27;t even a school anthem to begin with, despite the fact that the country had its own song.</p>
<p>&quot;And now, a few words for the new students from the Student Council President.&quot;</p>
<p>At the Vice Principal&#x27;s words, three people, a girl and two boys, took to the stage. Standing at the forefront was a young girl with beautiful golden hair in long, silky tresses with braids woven in. Her clothes—a brand new school uniform—were the same as mine, but even the way she walked brimmed with grace. Completely unlike the joke of a young lady beside me. Granted, while Elinalise&#x27;s actions lacked grace, they had their own charm.</p>
<p>&quot;My, my, isn&#x27;t that the kid you made cry not so long ago?&quot; Elinalise mused.</p>
<p>At her words, I looked to the two boys walking behind the girl. One of them had white hair and sunglasses—Fitz. He kept his guard up, surveying their surroundings as they mounted the stage. And for what it was worth, I didn&#x27;t think he&#x27;d cried when I beat him.</p>
<p>The other boy was someone I didn&#x27;t know. He seemed a bit older than me. With his brown hair slicked back, he had a frivolous look about him, a sword hanging from his side. He didn&#x27;t look like a magician, and judging by the way he carried himself, he was probably a swordsman. The only other noteworthy thing about him was his good looks.</p>
<p>Incidentally, according to my research, sharply defined features, which I considered handsome, were popular in the countries of the Central Continent. That aside, this guy kind of looked like Paul. On a similar note, I was often told I didn&#x27;t look half bad, except for when I smiled. Elinalise was the only one who ever told me I had a stunning, manly smile. Since no one complimented it, the only smiling I did anymore was fake.</p>
<p>As the three of them took to the stage, the young crowd around us erupted in murmurs.</p>
<p>&quot;Isn&#x27;t that Princess Ariel…&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Then that one over there must be Silent Fitz!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Aaah, it&#x27;s Lord Luke!&quot;</p>
<p>They were famous, judging by the squeals. Luke was probably the Paul-lookalike. He got shrill cheers from the girls and raised his hand to wave back. Tch, and he&#x27;s got a name like some male adult film star.</p>
<p>&quot;My, my, that&#x27;s a nice man.&quot; It seemed Elinalise wasn&#x27;t a good judge of character, either.</p>
<p>&quot;Silence! Princess Ariel has something to say!&quot; At (presumably) Luke&#x27;s order, the clamor fell into silence. Quite impressive given that he hadn&#x27;t used a mic. &quot;Go ahead, Princess Ariel.&quot;</p>
<p>She waited for things to quiet before coming to the front of the stage. &quot;My name is Ariel Anemoi Asura. I am the Second Princess of the Asura Kingdom, and the Student Council President of the University of Magic!&quot;</p>
<p>Her voice rang out amidst the silence. My heart trembled as I heard the sound of her voice. This was probably what people called charisma. It wasn&#x27;t just that her voice was loud, and clear something about it was pleasant to listen to.</p>
<p>&quot;You have all gathered here from around the world. Many of you have ideas unlike ours about what constitutes normalcy. However, here at this University, we maintain a sense of order that differs from your places of origin.&quot;</p>
<p>The rest of her speech was primarily about school rules, and boiled down to the fact that even if the rules here differed from those of your homeland, you still needed to abide by them. But there was something about her words that sunk deep into your soul and remained there. We need to obey the rules, I thought, and not because I&#x27;d been Japanese in my previous life. I felt compelled to do so because she was the one saying so.</p>
<p>&quot;Now then, I hope you all have an enjoyable time as students.&quot; Ariel wrapped up her speech with that final line and descended from the stage.</p>
<p>It was at that moment that I suddenly caught Fitz&#x27;s gaze. I shouldn&#x27;t have been able to tell he was staring at me through his sunglasses, but I was certain because of how strong his gaze was.</p>
<p>This is bad. I better hurry up and buy that cake.</p>
<p>Once the ceremony was over, I parted ways with Elinalise and headed for my designated classroom. There was homeroom once a month and I had to participate. From what I&#x27;d heard, there were only six special students, myself included. Apparently, they were an assortment of eccentric, troubled individuals. Jenius, the vice principal, had even said, &quot;Please, please be careful not to get into any fights.&quot; Not that he needed to; I had no intention of causing a stir. No matter what anyone said to me, I&#x27;d just bow my head and let it roll off my back.</p>
<p>I headed to the tail end of three buildings, to the innermost classroom on the third floor. Midway there, I found a line drawn across the floor with the words, Beyond This Point is the Special Students&#x27; Classroom. Almost like they were segregating us, even though the special students were supposed to be allowed to wander the school grounds freely. No, perhaps it was the opposite. Special students tended to be arrogant and cause problems, so this was a measure to keep the general admission students from approaching them.</p>
<p>As I mulled over this, I reached the classroom. There was a plate above the door that read Special Students&#x27; Room.</p>
<p>&quot;Pardon the intrusion,&quot; I called out quietly as I pried the door open and crept inside. The classroom was a familiar sight. There was a brand-new chalkboard, something like a lectern and a teacher&#x27;s desk. Wooden desks lined the room. The windows were firmly shut, but the room was bright. In contrast to the vastness of the room, there were only four people sitting at the desks.</p>
<p>In the front row, there was a boy who was reading and taking notes. The most striking thing about him was the way his dark brown hair hid his eyes. He glanced briefly in my direction, before losing interest immediately and returning to his book. Further in and closest to the windows sat two girls, both of whom were beastfolk. One was chewing at a stringy piece of meat on the bone. A dog-type. Her eyes regarded me suspiciously. The other, a cat-type, had her legs resting on the desk and both hands folded behind her head as she leaned back, glaring my way.</p>
<p>Seeing them reminded me of the two young girls I&#x27;d met back in the Doldia village. What were their names again? They were both good kids. In comparison, these two looked a bit ill-mannered. Reminded me of those fashion-obsessed teen girls from back home.</p>
<p>And then there was the last guy—a man I&#x27;d seen somewhere before. He had a long face with round glasses, the kind of guy who might have gotten nicknamed Spock back in the day. He spent a few moments gaping at me, then stood up and screeched with his mouth still wide open.</p>
<p>I immediately unleashed my Eye of Foresight.</p>
<p>&quot;M-maaaaster!!&quot; He sent his desk flying as if it were a mere obstacle in his path. He was like a snowplow in the way he crashed through all the other desks between us. One by one they went flying as he plunged forward. Yes, plunged—he was steamrolling toward me!</p>
<p>&quot;Stone cannon!&quot; I&#x27;d smack him before he reached me.</p>
<p>&quot;Maaaaster!&quot;</p>
<p>He took my stone cannon right to the face and it hit him with a loud crack, but he didn&#x27;t even stagger a bit. That cannon had enough power to knock an adult man out, but it had absolutely no effect on this guy? Impossible. Was this really the power of a Blessed Child?!</p>
<p>He grabbed me by my waist and tried to hoist me to the ceiling.</p>
<p>&quot;Whoa, whoa, hold it in, hold it in! Release the tension from your shoulders, relax, calm down! Knock it off!&quot;</p>
<p>His arms had enough power to send me flying into the ceiling, but fortunately, he just lifted me up.</p>
<p>&quot;Master! Have you forgotten me? It is I, Zanoba!&quot; Zanoba was grinning from ear to ear as he carefully wrapped his arms around me in a hug.</p>
<p><em>What&#x27;s with that introduction? Are you supposed to be the wife of a certain Mr. Isono?</em></p>
<p>&quot;Yes, I remember. My dear pupil, please release me, this is terrifying.&quot;</p>
<p>Before me stood the Third Prince of the Shirone Kingdom, Zanoba Shirone. It seemed when Zanoba was sent away under the pretense of studying abroad, he&#x27;d been sent off to the Ranoa University of Magic.</p>
<p>Under normal circumstances, a Blessed Child who couldn&#x27;t control their power would be treated like a Cursed Child. However, the Magicians&#x27; Guild had a department that studied curses and blessings, and Blessed Children made excellent specimens. And so, Zanoba was allowed to enroll at the University as a special student in exchange for allowing himself to be studied. A timely offer, given that he&#x27;d recently acquired an interest in magic.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ve been aiming to be just like you, Master. I&#x27;ve been diligently practicing my earth magic every day,&quot; my dedicated pupil declared.</p>
<p>&quot;Have you? I&#x27;m glad to see Your Majesty is doing so well. Once things have calmed down, let&#x27;s make a figurine together.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes!&quot; He smiled and nodded.</p>
<p>This was nice. It brought back memories of my underclassmen in junior high, who&#x27;d latched onto me the same way when I bragged about having built my computer myself.</p>
<p>&quot;Also, while we&#x27;re here at school, you&#x27;re an upperclassman. What year are you right now?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Second year. Ha ha, please don&#x27;t refer to me as &#x27;Your Majesty&#x27; or as an upperclassman. You can just call me Zanoba. You are my master, after all.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Zanoba, then.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, Master.&quot;</p>
<p>A sudden loud smack interrupted our pleasant conversation. I instinctively looked in its direction. The beast girl who&#x27;d had both feet on her desk had slammed one to the floor. The other still remained on the desk, which meant her skirt was spread right open so I could see a certain something. &quot;I don&#x27;t like this, mew.&quot;</p>
<p>She said &#x27;mew&#x27;! That was something I associated with the Doldia tribe. And Eris&#x27;s… no, let&#x27;s not go down that road.</p>
<p>&quot;Hey, Zanoba, what are you and that new kid rattling on and on and on about?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Mistress Linia, this is the person I spoke about before, my master.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s not what I&#x27;m asking about, mew!&quot; The cat-eared girl irritably slammed the heel of her other foot against the table. &quot;Hey, Zanoba, don&#x27;t mess around, okay! You know what I&#x27;m talkin&#x27; about, right, mew? You know, don&#x27;tcha, huh?!&quot;</p>
<p>His face went stiff.</p>
<p>What was going on? Was he actually being bullied? Zanoba was supposed to be pretty strong, but this could be a question of social hierarchy. Brute strength didn&#x27;t necessarily put you at the top.</p>
<p>&quot;If ya do, then bring him over here.&quot; She made a beckoning motion in my direction.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m sorry, Master.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No, it&#x27;s fine.&quot; I approached the cat-eared girl as I was told.</p>
<p>A cat-eared girl and a dog-eared girl. Their piercing gazes would have made my legs tremble in the past, but they didn&#x27;t feel scary at all now. Their glares needed a little bit more… well you know, right? They needed some murderous intent in there, right? That was how truly scary people—like Ruijerd—glared.</p>
<p>&quot;Greetings. A pleasure to meet you, I&#x27;m Rudeus Greyrat. I&#x27;ll be in your care starting today. I&#x27;ll be careful not to butt into anything. I hope we&#x27;ll get along well.&quot; I bent in a swooping, Japanese-style bow. Where people like these were concerned, it was best to behave modestly and to do my utmost not to get involved.</p>
<p>Linia purred out a laugh. &quot;Straightforward, eh? Not bad at all, mew. I&#x27;m Linia Dedoldia, a fifth year, mew. Though you might not know it just by looking at me, I&#x27;m actually the daughter of Gyes, the Warrior Chief of the Great Forest&#x27;s Doldia Village. At some point I&#x27;ll inherit the position of Village Chief, so you&#x27;d best start servin&#x27; me now, mew!&quot;</p>
<p>So she was really of the Doldia Tribe. And Gyes&#x27;s daughter, to boot. Come to think of it, they did say his oldest daughter had been sent off to another country to study. So this was it? Man, that sure brought back memories.</p>
<p>I gushed: &quot;Oh, truly? Mister Gyes took good care of me when I visited the Doldia Village before! Ah, I&#x27;m so touched! To think I&#x27;d be able to meet the daughter of the man who looked after me at a place like this! Oh, that means you must be Mister Gustav&#x27;s granddaughter as well, right? Mister Gustav was good to me, too. He even let me stay at his house during the rainy season!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;O-oh really? That so? So you&#x27;re one of Grandpa&#x27;s acquaintances…&quot;</p>
<p>In contrast to her earlier spiel, spitting out word after word like a machine gun, Linia was left staring at me dumbfounded. Not that it really mattered, but the force with which she&#x27;d kicked the table made a certain article of clothing super visible. Aqua blue, huh?</p>
<p>Beside her the girl who was gnawing away at the meat bone twitched her nose and pulled a face. &quot;It stinks.&quot;</p>
<p>That was rude. She was referring to me, right? Still, I didn&#x27;t let my face betray my emotions, but gracefully turned to the dog girl and bowed. &quot;Pardon me. Might I ask your name as well?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Pursena. I&#x27;m basically the same as Linia.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Miss Pursena, what a lovely name! A pleasure to meet you!&quot;</p>
<p>She pinched her nose and turned her face away. &quot;Fuck.&quot;</p>
<p>That last word was meant as an insult, I assumed—though when girls like her talked that way, it actually turned older men on.</p>
<p>Regardless, this had been a successful preemptive attack. At least, I wanted to believe my efforts had been good enough to avoid getting caught up in something outrageous later on.</p>

<p>Zanoba had a conflicted look on his face as he watched me interact with the two of them. Once we stepped away, he spoke in a hushed voice. &quot;Master, why are you acting so submissive toward them?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;My dear pupil, it is important to avoid unnecessary conflicts.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You really think so…? Well, since you&#x27;re the one saying that, I&#x27;ll keep my mouth shut.&quot; He looked vexed even as he nodded his head.</p>
<p>I had no idea what he&#x27;d gone through, but if it looked like he was being bullied in the future, I&#x27;d be sure to shield him from it. Bullying was a no-no. An absolute no-no.</p>
<p>As I was preoccupied with that resolution, someone called out from behind me. &quot;Hey.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, what is it?&quot;</p>
<p>I looked back and the boy from the front of the row was standing there. &quot;You. You said your name was Rudeus, right?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, my name is Rudeus Greyrat. A pleasure to make your acquaintance.&quot;</p>
<p>He looked taken aback when I bowed my head. &quot;Cliff Grimor. I&#x27;m a genius magician.&quot;</p>
<p>A genius magician, eh? Incredible. But was he seriously going to call himself a genius? Didn&#x27;t he feel embarrassed about doing that at all?</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m a second year, but I&#x27;ve already acquired Advanced-tier ranking in all the offensive magics. I&#x27;m also advanced in healing, detoxification and divine magic, too. I&#x27;m still a beginner at barriers, but I&#x27;ll soon be Intermediate-tier. There aren&#x27;t any decent teachers at this school.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s amazing,&quot; I praised him sincerely. It made sense now why he called himself a genius. What did it take to become an advanced user of all seven types of magic in only two years? I could still only use intermediate healing magic and basic detoxification magic.</p>
<p>So this was the special student class. I&#x27;d known there would always be someone better than me out there, but this just drove it home. Probably the only reason my own self-esteem didn&#x27;t take a nose dive was because I&#x27;d was Saint-tier with water magic.</p>
<p>&quot;It took me two years just to become Advanced-tier in four types of offensive magic. You really are incredible.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Tch, don&#x27;t get carried away.&quot;</p>
<p>I just honestly meant to praise him, but he clicked his tongue and turned grumpy. He glared at me with such force he might as well have grabbed me by the collar of my shirt. Although I was a little bit taller than him, so he had to look up at me slightly. &quot;You can wield a sword as well as use magic, can&#x27;t you?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, well, I&#x27;m not very good at it though.&quot; I was technically Intermediate-tier in the Sword God Style. I basically remembered nothing of Water God Style. Part of my bodybuilding regime included swinging a wooden sword, but that wasn&#x27;t swordsmanship that was useable in combat.</p>
<p>To be honest, no matter how much time passed, I still couldn&#x27;t master what came as easily as breathing to other swordfighters such as Eris and Ruijerd. So I&#x27;d semi-given up on the path of the sword. I never even used it once while living as an adventurer. Even so…</p>
<p>&quot;Who told you that? That I could wield a sword.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;…Miss Eris.&quot;</p>
<p>That shook me. Had he met Eris within the last two years? No way…she wasn&#x27;t here at the University?!</p>
<p>&quot;She&#x27;s at this school, too?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What? Of course she isn&#x27;t,&quot; he retorted curtly.</p>
<p>He just glared at me without answering. Was that a bad question? Ah, don&#x27;t tell me he was one of the people she&#x27;d punched a long time ago? I&#x27;m sorry, I really am, I apologize on her behalf, I thought inwardly.</p>
<p>&quot;Uhh… did she say anything else about me?&quot;</p>
<p>He glowered with such force it could have had its own sound effect. After glaring me up and down, he finally said, &quot;Hmph. She said you were small.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;R-really? She said I&#x27;m small?&quot; Like down there?</p>
<p>I felt like I was going to cry. So it really was the sex that drove her away from me. If only I&#x27;d been bigger, then… Come to think of it, I&#x27;d gotten a similar vibe from the way Sara looked at me, too. Her face had said, &quot;Oh wow, you&#x27;re smaller than I thought you&#x27;d be.&quot;</p>
<p>No, she was mistaken! It only looked small because it wasn&#x27;t reacting! Once it was energized and standing at attention, it had the ferocity of a lion!</p>
<p>&quot;W-well, it&#x27;s been two years since we parted ways, and I&#x27;ve grown since then,&quot; I stammered.</p>
<p>&quot;What? You and Miss Eris parted ways?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Hm?&quot; I got the feeling that we weren&#x27;t quite on the same page. A sense of unease arose in me. But before I could confirm that unease…</p>
<p>&quot;Hm, well, whatever. You don&#x27;t suit Miss Eris regardless!&quot;</p>
<p>Those words were like daggers to the heart. Cliff huffed air out of his nose and returned to his seat. I&#x27;d have to keep an eye on this one.</p>
<p>The teacher arrived soon after, I introduced myself, and after a short conversation, homeroom was over. Though we were missing one person.</p>
<p>When I tried asking Zanoba, he just shook his head. &quot;Master Silent is exempt from the monthly homeroom.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;And why&#x27;s that?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Good question, but I haven&#x27;t an answer for it.&quot;</p>
<p>The last person was apparently someone named Silent. Don&#x27;t tell they&#x27;re a person who can&#x27;t use shadow magic?!</p>
<p>&quot;I guess they must be pretty incredible, huh?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;They&#x27;re well-known. They influence the Academy at every opportunity, or so I hear. They&#x27;ve increased the items on the school menu, created magical implements… these uniforms were also one of Master Silent&#x27;s suggestions. Rumor has it they were recommended by one of the Seven Great Powers, so they&#x27;re getting special treatment.&quot;</p>
<p>The image that popped up in my head was that of a mad scientist with a white coat and bottlecap glasses, carrying flasks of green goop in their hands. Someone who was intelligent and delivered successful results, but was otherwise a sad excuse for a human being.</p>
<p>&quot;They usually shut themself in their private research room, but they do emerge if they have reason to, so I&#x27;m sure you&#x27;ll eventually meet them,&quot; Zanoba said. He also mentioned that Silent was a thirdyear student. If I saw them, I&#x27;d be sure to show them the proper respect.</p>
<p>And just like that, I was absorbed into the ranks of the special students.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>Once homeroom was over, Zanoba and the others went off to their classes.
