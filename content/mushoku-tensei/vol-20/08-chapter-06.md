<p><strong>S</strong>YLPHIE WAS ONCE AGAIN pregnant. This would be the second child for her. And it happened just before I was going to set out. Once, this might have made me clutch my head and agonize over what to do. Now? This was my fourth time hearing a baby was on the way just as I was set to embark somewhere. It wasn&#x27;t nothing, but Sylphie and the baby didn&#x27;t take up all my thoughts.</p>
<p>If anything, I was elated. What should we name the baby? Would they be a boy this time, or a girl? Lucie, you&#x27;re getting a new baby brother or sister! Are you ready to be the big sis again? <p>&quot;Madam Sylphie is… Wh-what shall we do?!&quot;</p>
<p>Lilia was bewildered, her normally placid face now drawn and uncertain.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m the only one who can attend to Madam Zenith… But Madam Sylphie is the only one who can attend to the house, and she&#x27;s now with child… If the unthinkable happened, then…&quot;</p>
<p>We&#x27;d struck a deal that Lilia would come to Millis and attend to Zenith while Sylphie focused on maintaining the household. But now: pregnant. It wasn&#x27;t the end of the world. Roxy was capable of doing any chore that needed doing, and we could always hire outside help to tide things over. I was tempted to leave it at that, but even I was worried about leaving a pregnant woman alone for months at a time.</p>
<p>Lilia couldn&#x27;t decide. Should she head out with Zenith, or should she stay to look after Sylphie? It was hard not to be shaken by the sight of Lilia herself actually shaken by something. Maybe it would be best to tone down my goofy celebration.</p>
<p>When I decided to serve Orsted, I was fully prepared for the possibility that I&#x27;d have to leave a pregnant wife behind for the sake of my work. But now it dawned on me that I&#x27;d committed to that sacrifice on the assumption that Lilia and Aisha would be there for my wives when I couldn&#x27;t be.</p>

<p>…Children were accidents waiting to happen no matter what I did. If I started imagining hypothetical disasters, I&#x27;d never stop.</p>
<p>But there were real problems on the horizon. The first big one: when Sylphie told me that she&#x27;d probably hit her limit given her race&#x27;s biology, I took that as a personal challenge. Didn&#x27;t even think about family planning. Of course I&#x27;d never make babies for the fun of it! How dare you? I&#x27;d always wanted a second child. But maybe the five-year gap since Lucie was born without another baby in sight made me consider that Sylphie really had hit her limit, and maybe I did get a little lazy about using protection…</p>
<p>I&#x27;d chosen a hectic time to get my wife pregnant, and now I was leaving her alone. A repeat of history. Why did I only seem to have children right before running off on long journeys? Perhaps it was the Man-God&#x27;s curse.</p>
<p>There was the option of delaying my departure to the Holy Country of Millis. <p>Be it Sylphie or be it Cliff, my thoughts went straight to the worst-case scenario. If either had been an emergency, my choice would be made for me, but no such luck.
