<p><strong>T</strong>HE RECEPTION was a success. We didn&#x27;t seal our promise with a kiss or exchange rings, but spent the whole time eating, drinking, chatting, and having a good time. I appreciated the ease and informality of it all.</p>
<p>People broke into groups of two or three when it was time to go home. The first to bid us goodbye were Linia and Pursena. Perhaps it was considered good manners among the beastfolk not to linger too long?</p>
<p>&quot;Well, mew… Enjoy yourself, Boss.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Now you really <em>are</em> the boss of the school. I&#x27;m looking forward to next semester.&quot;</p>
<p>After saying that, the two of them began trudging home through the snow.</p>
<p>The second to leave was Nanahoshi, with whom Luke had randomly struck up a conversation. Most of it was him trying to hit on her, though he wasn&#x27;t being <em>completely</em> transparent about it. He made a concerted effort to talk about food and clothes, topics it seemed Nanahoshi might be interested in. He was good at sounding interested in a topic the other person cared about, even though he was a bit out of his element here. Still, it was educational. Not that I intended to make use of such knowledge.</p>
<p>Nanahoshi, on the other hand, made no attempt to disguise how clearly bothered she was by him. She looked at him in annoyance; she sighed in annoyance. In the end she ran to the bathroom just to escape him. When she re-emerged, she came straight over to where I was, looking agitated. &quot;It&#x27;s about time for me to leave. That one&#x27;s annoying me.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;All right, then. I&#x27;m sure you&#x27;re exhausted. Thank you for coming today,&quot; I said.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll be counting on your help again tomorrow. And one more thing.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What&#x27;s that?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Sometime in the future, can I use your bath?&quot;</p>
<p>Apparently, she&#x27;d gotten a look at our bathroom while on the way to the restroom. As a fellow Japanese person, she probably missed baths. Her name <em>was</em> Shizuka after all. &quot;Sure. But you might have Nobita peeking in at you—&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Forget I said anything.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No, I&#x27;m just joking, honestly. You can come any time.&quot;</p>
<p>Nanahoshi nodded and started to leave. The sun hadn&#x27;t set yet, but I wondered if she would be safe walking home alone, even though she&#x27;d come here herself, and had magic items for her own protection.</p>
<p>&quot;Escort Master Silent back to her residence, please.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, Princess.&quot;</p>
<p>While I was hesitating over what to do, the Princess moved to send one of her attendants as an escort. I should&#x27;ve expected it of someone with such charisma. However, Nanahoshi stubbornly refused the offer and went home by herself.</p>
<p>Next were Zanoba and Julie, then Badigadi. Badigadi, Zanoba and Ariel had all shared drinks while happily chatting amongst themselves. Since I knew of Badi&#x27;s affinity for alcohol, I&#x27;d prepared an appropriate amount just to be sure. But it apparently hadn&#x27;t been enough. Before I knew it, two of the three wine casks I&#x27;d stashed in the basement were empty. I debated sending out for more, but before I could, Zanoba got hammered.</p>
<p>&quot;Bwahaha! You sure are weak for a &#x27;Blessed Child&#x27;,&quot; Badigadi chortled.</p>
<p>&quot;Ha ha ha…urgh, I&#x27;m ashamed. It seems I got carried away.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Master, are you okay?&quot; Tiny Julie was trying to support Zanoba as he stumbled.</p>
<p>&quot;Hee hee hee. Maybe you should rest in one of the rooms here?&quot; Ariel suggested. She hadn&#x27;t drunk that much herself—keeping her wits about her was probably part of her training as a highborn lady. Everything she did was poised elegantly, from the way she tipped her cup to the way she laughed. She was probably a bit tipsy, but the faint blush the alcohol had given her just made her more charming.</p>
<p>&quot;No. As a pupil, and a proud member of the Shirone Noble family, it already brings me shame to be so utterly inebriated in my own master&#x27;s house. I&#x27;ll take my leave while I can still walk.&quot;</p>
<p>Zanoba came to say his farewells to me. Personally, I&#x27;d have been fine with him staying in our guest room… Well, whatever he wanted to do.</p>
<p>&quot;Suppose I should be off, too. Princess of Asura, stay well!&quot; said Badigadi.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, your highness. I hope you will stay in good health as well.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Bwahaha! I don&#x27;t get sick or injured!&quot;</p>
<p>And so, both Zanoba and Badigadi took their leave. Huh. I&#x27;d thought for sure they would be the last ones to leave.</p>
<p>The reception drew to a close as Ariel and her group prepared to depart. While they were doing that, I decided to check on Elinalise. I went up to the second floor and peeked into the guest room.</p>
<p>I was greeted by an exciting display—no no, not the sexual kind. Just Elinalise using Cliff&#x27;s lap as a pillow. Apparently, he was done</p>
<p>comforting her, and they&#x27;d moved on to the lovey-dovey bits. I felt kind of envious. I&#x27;d have to do the same with Sylphie later.</p>

<p>&quot;Thanks, Rudy.&quot; Sylphie smiled softly before heading inside.</p>
<p>Cliff and I headed down the stairs together. He looked anxious. &quot;Are those two going to be okay?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;If they aren&#x27;t, we just have to be there for them afterward,&quot; I replied as we made our way to the ground floor.</p>
<p>When we arrived, Ariel and her lot had just finished their preparations to depart. The two attendants were helping Ariel slip her coat on. When she saw me, she dipped her chin. &quot;Lord Rudeus. Thank you for today.&quot; The rest of her party likewise bowed deeply.</p>
<p>I bowed in return, Japanese-style, though I was pretty sure I wasn&#x27;t supposed to do that in this situation.</p>
<p>&quot;How is Sylphie doing?&quot; Ariel asked.</p>
<p>&quot;She&#x27;s talking to Elinalise right now.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I see. It was certainly a surprise to find out Sylphie had family here. I thought she&#x27;d lost them all.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Indeed. It really is a small world.&quot; Not to mention that Elinalise and Sylphie were as different as day and night. Mainly in terms of chastity.</p>
<p>&quot;In any case, this is the perfect opportunity. Lord Rudeus, may I have a moment of your time?&quot;</p>
<p>Her words hinted at an ulterior motive, but I nodded anyway.</p>
<p>&quot;Very well, then. Come this way.&quot; As she spoke, Ariel quickly cut across the room and moved into the hallway. From there she proceeded to the front entryway, opened the door and headed outside. As if it came as naturally as breathing to them, the other three tailed her. I followed suit.</p>
<p>Outside, the sun was beginning to set. Ariel stopped along the path where people had been walking and the snow had barely had a chance to pile up. She turned back to look at me.</p>
<p>&quot;Lord Rudeus. I realize it might be inappropriate of me to ask…&quot; A moment&#x27;s hesitation. &quot;Would you agree to a duel with Luke? No magic, just sword against sword.&quot;</p>
<p>A very sudden request. Unable to respond, I pursed my lips. Luke looked completely composed, his hand resting on the hilt of his sword. This was clearly not something Ariel had decided on the spur of the moment. &quot;Could you explain your reasoning, at least?&quot;</p>
<p>She just smiled softly. &quot;Just for fun.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;&#x27;For fun&#x27;?&quot; I said, and Luke unsheathed what was a very <em>real</em> sword. Considering it was double-edged, he wasn&#x27;t going to be hitting me with the blunt side. &quot;Can we use wooden swords, at least? I don&#x27;t even have a real sword.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t mind if you conjure one for yourself,&quot; she replied.</p>
<p>&quot;I thought you said no magic?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m fine with you using it to create a weapon.&quot;</p>
<p>Very well, then. I used my earth magic to create a stone blade. I made it thick and durable, which meant that it was heavy, too. I did practice my swings every day, so I could wield it just fine, but if it hit someone in the wrong spot and the worst came to pass, they could die. It wasn&#x27;t something you should hit someone with &quot;for fun.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t worry,&quot; Ariel said. &quot;This is something Luke requested.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t mind if you use your full power to beat him senseless.&quot;</p>
<p>Without my magic, I was just an average swordsman. There was no guarantee I <em>could</em> beat him senseless.</p>
<p>&quot;For reference, Luke is an intermediate in Sword God Style and a beginner in Water God Style. His sword is a magic item, made to cut through steel shields as easily as butter. His boots are the same as Sylphie&#x27;s, giving a boost to the wearer&#x27;s speed. His cloak can block heat, his gloves increase his strength, and beneath his uniform, he&#x27;s wearing swordproof clothing.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s incredible.&quot; So he was garbed head to toe in a dashing hero&#x27;s gear! Even selling my freshly renovated house wouldn&#x27;t get me enough money to pay for all of that. &quot;In other words, <em>I</em> might be the one getting beat senseless.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I doubt it. But if you do sense your life is in danger, feel free to use magic.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll just pray he doesn&#x27;t cut me in half before I get the chance.&quot;</p>
<p>Why had he proposed this in the first place? None of us would benefit from someone dying here.</p>
<p>&quot;Before we begin, I&#x27;d like you to tell me why we&#x27;re doing this. Have I done something to upset you?&quot; I asked.</p>
<p>&quot;No. It&#x27;s just for fun. Of course, you can refuse if you&#x27;d like.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Whether I accept or not, this troubles me. Even this stone sword is deadly enough that it could kill someone if it hit them in the wrong place.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Luke is prepared for that possibility.&quot;</p>
<p>Well, I wasn&#x27;t. I was newly married and I didn&#x27;t want to kill <em>or</em> be killed.</p>
<p>&quot;Please,&quot; Ariel said. Her voice was somber.</p>
<p>What was this match going to prove? Maybe it was some kind of Asura Kingdom tradition. I could easily picture old man Sauros</p>
<p>saying, &quot;If you want to take Eris as your wife, you must defeat me first!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;All right. Please be gentle with me, then.&quot; I was going to use my Demon Eye of Foresight, at least. I didn&#x27;t want anyone to die accidentally.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you for accepting our request.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, Master Cliff. If things start looking bad, please cast a healing spell immediately.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Y-yeah. I was already planning on that.&quot;</p>
<p>Stone sword in hand, I slowly took a stance of my own. We were about three steps apart. That meant one step and we could swing at each other. It was closer than the distance I normally set for myself in my simulations.</p>
<p>&quot;Now then, are you ready?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>By the time we finished cleaning, the sun had set and the sky had grown dark. I lit up the place and returned to the living area. When I took my spot on the three-seater couch, Sylphie plopped down beside me. I was suddenly exhausted from the day&#x27;s events.</p>

<p>&quot;Rudy, that tickles.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Because it&#x27;s a serious conversation.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Um, it&#x27;s not really <em>that</em> serious. I just, you know, my hair isn&#x27;t green anymore, right? Princess Ariel told me to be more feminine, but I still plan to wear pants at school, so I thought maybe I should at least let my hair grow out.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sylphie, thanks for all the work you did today.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you too, Rudy.&quot;</p>
<p>Since we were both exhausted, I decided we&#x27;d just hit the bath and then relax.</p>
