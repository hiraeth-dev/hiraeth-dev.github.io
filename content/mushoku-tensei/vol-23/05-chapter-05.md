<p>An enormous arch stretched over it, and the underside had been etched with cramped markings. These presumably formed part of a magic circle. It was a remarkably three-dimensional magic circle. The twodimensional name no longer fitted. It was more of a magical device or apparatus.</p>

<p>The inter-world teleportation system itself was functional. All that was left was to complete the arch, analyze these mana traces, and to slowly but surely move toward objects that were more physically like Nanahoshi.</p>
<p>These final stages were supposed to take around a month. Losing that much time while Geese was on the loose wasn&#x27;t ideal…but it was what it was. I thought of it as starting from scratch to make up for failing to make a firmer ally of Perugius before.</p>

<p>&quot;Also, this,&quot; I said, handing her one more letter, far thinner than the one before. &quot;Just in case years have passed in that world, and if you have nowhere to go and nobody to rely on…I wrote this letter to tell my old brothers to look after you. Even if it&#x27;s just for a little bit.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;…!&quot;</p>
<p>Nanahoshi accepted this letter with shaky hands.</p>
<p>&quot;But I couldn&#x27;t…&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Well, hey, I was nothing but a nuisance over there, so they might just refuse you outright…but you know.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You were a nuisance?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yep, a jobless leech.&quot;</p>
<p>Might as well hear it from me. If she did meet my brothers, they&#x27;d let her know.</p>
<p>Nanahoshi looked deep into my face as if she were inspecting it for signs I was a loser. &quot;Kinda hard to believe that.&quot;</p>
<p>Maybe that disbelief was proof of just how hard I&#x27;d worked. Wasn&#x27;t that a happy thought?</p>
<p>&quot;Well, if you&#x27;re offering, I&#x27;d be honored to accept,&quot; Nanahoshi said, cradling the letter to her chest and bowing her head. &quot;I can&#x27;t thank you enough for everything you&#x27;ve done.&quot;</p>
<p>Nanahoshi would be returning home tomorrow.</p>
<p>The experiments had gone perfectly. There wasn&#x27;t the slightest error in that magic circle. But even still, an anxiety coiled around my insides that I couldn&#x27;t shake.</p>
<p>We&#x27;d made every possible preparation, made every calculation we could. Nanahoshi seemed confident. Not a soul thought it would fail.</p>
<p>There was still one last cause for concern. One that I had no intention of spreading further by daring to name aloud. One that, in her heart, Nanahoshi surely knew herself. One that, if she did know, she also wouldn&#x27;t speak about. Maybe she already had it under control.</p>
<p>So, I left it at that.</p>
<p>&quot;Tomorrow…let&#x27;s get you home.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yeah.&quot;</p>
<p>If your convictions were strong enough, everything else is just details.</p>
