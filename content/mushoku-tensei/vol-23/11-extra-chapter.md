<p><strong>F</strong>OUND MYSELF in the Biegoya Region of the Demon Continent inside a certain town mayor&#x27;s estate. The scent of booze stunk up the air. The men in the room, all completely hammered, were half-naked. Not a stitch on the bastards from the waist up.</p>

<p>I&#x27;d been insinuating myself into his group lately, but I hadn&#x27;t got used to being around &#x27;em. My knees were still knocking.</p>

Yeah, I am!&quot; He offered only that brief response before peeling his gaze away. &quot;Get me another drink, I&#x27;ve taken a liking to your ale! A real vintage. Fwahaha!&quot;</p>
<p>This guy wasn&#x27;t interested in me. I knew a word that&#x27;d grab his attention, though. As soon as he heard it, I knew he&#x27;d sit up and listen.</p>

<p>Badigadi knew instantly what it was that rested in the depths of the ocean. Something he should have detested with the whole of his being. But if they were going to take on the Dragon God, never mind harbor any hope of defeating him, then it would be absolutely necessary.</p>
<p>&quot;Hmm… Fine. I shall do it!&quot; Badigadi spent only a moment waffling about the matter before agreeing.</p>

If the Man-God wanted him to retrieve that detestable thing, bring it back, and use it to defeat the Man-God&#x27;s enemies, then there was nothing to hesitate about.</p>
<p>&quot;And you have no other advice for me?&quot; The man asked.</p>
<p>&quot;Unfortunately, my vision counts as a kind of demon eye. I cannot see your future since you drank the Anti-Demon Eye elixir.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Aha, I see then! Good news for me! After all, life would be boring if you could see exactly where it ends! Fwahahaha!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Fwahahahaha!&quot;</p>
