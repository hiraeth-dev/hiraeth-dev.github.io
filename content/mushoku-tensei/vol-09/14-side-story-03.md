<p><strong>T</strong>HAT NIGHT, I dreamed about the past—about Rudy&#x27;s arrival at the University.</p>
<p>It was my third year at the Ranoa University of Magic. Linia and Pursena had settled down after their defeat, and <em>Princess Ariel</em> had secured the position of student council president. Those successes had attracted many new supporters to our camp; everything was going relatively smoothly. By this point, we&#x27;d recruited as many of the school&#x27;s influential students and faculty as we could. We decided to start luring powerful potential allies to the University of Magic, where we could then attempt to secure their cooperation. And as we worked on this, we stumbled across something totally unexpected.</p>
<p>Specifically, our research led us to a person known as &quot;Quagmire Rudeus.&quot; I knew immediately that it had to be Rudy. Quagmire was described as a young magician who&#x27;d quickly worked his way up to the A rank in the Adventurers&#x27; Guild. He&#x27;d only been in this region for a few years, but word of his exploits had already spread throughout the Magic Nations. His specialty was Earth magic. It was hard to judge how powerful he truly was from the rumors, but most said he could summon a bog without speaking a word.</p>
<p>The silent spellcasting was the detail that convinced me it had to be him. And in hindsight, he&#x27;d used mud-based magic the very first time we met, too. Rudy could use Saint-tier Water magic, so people tended to expect him to rely on that. But he&#x27;d favored using clever tricks like blasting himself around with shockwaves, or summoning bogs to slow down his opponents.</p>
<p>I explained to <em>Princess Ariel</em> that Quagmire Rudeus was almost certainly the boy who&#x27;d taught me magic—my old friend who&#x27;d been missing for many years.</p>
<p>&quot;Well, if he&#x27;s the genuine article, it would certainly be nice to have him on our side…&quot;</p>
<p>At the time, <em>Princess Ariel</em> was obviously skeptical about Rudy. That was understandable. The rumors going around about him sounded really fishy. They went something like this:</p>
<p>Rudeus Greyrat was born in Buena Village, located in the Fittoa Region of the Kingdom of Asura. While only three years old, he began studying under the King-tier Water mage Roxy Migurdia (although she was only Saint-tier at the time). At the age of five, he became a Saint-tier Water magician in his own right. At seven, he took a position as the tutor of Eris Boreas Greyrat, the daughter of the mayor of the Citadel of Roa; over the next few years, he transformed this wild, uncontrollable child into a respectable young lady. After this, he went missing in the Displacement Incident.</p>
<p>Back in the day, I probably wouldn&#x27;t have raised an eyebrow at any of this. But now, after the time I&#x27;d spent living in the Silver Palace and studying at the University of Magic… I had to admit, it sounded bizarre. Even fictional.</p>
<p>Of course, I knew that Rudy really <em>had</em> studied under Roxy, and that he respected her deeply. I&#x27;d never met Roxy myself, but I knew she&#x27;d spent some time in Buena Village. In fact, the wand I carried was something she&#x27;d given to Rudy. The part about him becoming a tutor at the age of seven made sense, too; that would&#x27;ve been right when he was shipped out of the village by his parents.</p>
<p>&quot;Trust me, Princess Ariel, this information is accurate. It&#x27;s definitely him.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Perhaps so. But I must say, in all honesty, that these rumors are rather difficult to believe.&quot;</p>
<p><em>Princess Ariel</em> and Luke hadn&#x27;t been convinced by my claims. They trusted that I wasn&#x27;t deliberately lying to them, but they didn&#x27;t fully buy the story either. I couldn&#x27;t blame them. I knew Rudy personally, and it sounded crazy even to me.</p>
<p>&quot;In any case, would such a remarkable magician really lend us his aid? Let alone one with ties to the Boreas Greyrats?&quot;</p>
<p>I still wasn&#x27;t that familiar with the tangled web of alliances and rivalries that defined the noble families of Asura. I&#x27;d only spent a year at court, and there was just too much to learn. But I did know a good deal about the various Greyrat branches. The Boreas family was loyal to the first prince. That made them our enemies. And if Rudy was working for them, there was a good chance he was our enemy too.</p>
<p>That said, it seemed fairly obvious that he&#x27;d cut ties with them some time ago. Otherwise, it wouldn&#x27;t make much sense for him to be wandering around the Northern Territories as an adventurer. &quot;I-I&#x27;m sure he&#x27;ll help us out if I ask him…&quot;</p>
<p>My voice wasn&#x27;t particularly confident. I couldn&#x27;t even convince myself that it was true.</p>
<p>Luke snorted in amusement. &quot;With a chest that flat, you&#x27;re not going to win over any Notos man.&quot;</p>
<p>I covered my chest with my arm and shot Luke an angry look. He was <em>always</em> like this. He never missed a chance to mock me about the flatness of my chest. According to him, women without &quot;proper breasts&quot; weren&#x27;t even women, which meant I was the definition of unattractive. I&#x27;m not sure what he wanted me to do about that. I had elf blood in my veins, and elves just aren&#x27;t voluptuous.</p>
<p>To be fair, Luke usually softened the blow in the end: &quot;I suppose that&#x27;s the reason that we&#x27;re friends, though.&quot;</p>
<p>It was nice to know that he thought of me as a friend. Still, the constant insults about my appearance weren&#x27;t exactly doing</p>
<p>wonders for my self-esteem. I knew my looks were unremarkable compared to <em>Princess Ariel&#x27;s</em>, but that was a really high bar to clear.</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s not even what I meant, Luke!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Well, what <em>did</em> you mean, then? Surely you don&#x27;t intend to reveal your true identity to him.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Huh? Oh… you&#x27;re right.&quot; I was supposed to be Silent Fitz now. I couldn&#x27;t go around blowing my cover… what now?</p>
<p>&quot;Still, I&#x27;m very happy for you, Sylphie,&quot; said <em>Princess Ariel</em> with a smile. &quot;It must be wonderful to find someone you&#x27;ve been looking for all this time.&quot;</p>
<p>Really, the princess was a kind person. Sometimes she could be very stern, and she did spend a lot of time cooking up all sorts of sneaky plots, but deep down she had a good heart. I knew that by now. But even so, I wasn&#x27;t expecting what came next.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll make a special exception. You can reveal your identity to this Rudeus.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Huh?&quot; <em>What? She&#x27;s willing to unmask Silent Fitz?</em> &quot;But Princess Ariel, our whole plan might fall apart…&quot;</p>
<p>I was conscious of the importance of the role I played in our overall strategy. Fitz was the living embodiment of <em>Princess Ariel&#x27;s</em> power. He was a man of mysterious origins and remarkable magical skills who stayed silently by her side, obeying her every order. It added greatly to her mystique, and made her seem much more intimidating.</p>
<p>Over the last few years, I&#x27;d come to realize that I was powerful enough to beat an average magician or swordsman handily. I suppose Rudy had trained me well. I wasn&#x27;t yet at the King- or Imperial-tier, let alone that of the Seven Great Powers, but I could probably hold my own against a Sword Saint. I was no match for the King-tier warriors some of the other contenders for the throne could call on, but right now I was the strongest weapon in the arsenal of</p>
<p><em>Princess Ariel&#x27;s</em> faction. There were a good number of people at the University who&#x27;d decided to support her because she&#x27;d won the loyalty of someone as strong as &quot;Fitz.&quot; If word got out that I was actually just a common girl from a backwoods village, those people might very well break away from our camp.</p>
<p>My abilities were still real either way, of course…but people don&#x27;t always think about these things too logically.</p>
<p>&quot;You&#x27;ve done a great deal for me already, Sylphie. Surely I owe you an emotional reunion with your friend, at the very least.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;But—&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Should that somehow result in all our plans falling to pieces, I&#x27;m willing to accept that outcome,&quot; Ariel interrupted, her voice firm and decisive. &quot;And in any case, if we&#x27;re going to cajole this young man to our side, who better to recruit him than his childhood friend?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;…Thank you, Princess Ariel.&quot; I hesitated for a moment, but ended up simply expressing my gratitude. The <em>princess</em> was obviously angling for some personal profit here as well, but that didn&#x27;t bother me at this point.</p>
<p>What was Rudy going to think when he saw me now, all grown up? I was already looking forward to it.</p>
<p>Our plan to lure Rudy to the University went very smoothly. We passed our information on him to the administration, lightly hinting that it might be a good idea to recruit him. Vice-Principal Jenius took care of the rest without any further nudging.</p>
<p>A few months later, the day I&#x27;d been waiting for finally arrived. I was in a practical skills class in the training hall when the viceprincipal walked in with someone right behind him. When I saw who it was, I very nearly shouted out in joy.</p>
<p><em>It&#x27;s Rudy! It&#x27;s really Rudy!</em></p>
<p>There was no doubt about it. His face looked a little gloomier than before, but it was definitely Rudy. There was no mistaking him.</p>
<p><em>Oh wow! He&#x27;s so handsome!</em></p>
<p>You could still see traces of the boy I&#x27;d known back home, but he&#x27;d done a lot of growing up. His movements were smooth and steady, too—it was obvious he&#x27;d done a lot of training. The robe he wore was slightly tattered, but that just added a nice hint of danger. You could tell he&#x27;d been through many battles in that thing. He carried his staff with practiced ease.</p>
<p>As he walked into the training hall, Rudy looked all around him, studying his surroundings carefully. It was something he&#x27;d been doing ever since we were kids. <em>I thought I was going to marry him back then, but maybe he&#x27;s out of my league…</em> The longer I stared at him, the hotter my body grew.</p>
<p>Seized by a sudden impulse, I took a sharp step forward, ready to run over calling out his name. &quot;Ru—&quot;</p>
<p>But just as I started to speak, I froze on the spot. A very beautiful woman had just stepped into the hall behind Rudy.</p>
<p><em>Wait… is that Rudy&#x27;s wife?</em></p>
<p>She was an elf, from the looks of things. Something about her kind of reminded me of my dad. Her face was elegant and dignified; she looked a bit like a queen, or maybe a wealthy noblewoman. And she was hanging <em>all over</em> Rudy. He seemed a little exasperated by her behavior, but he didn&#x27;t complain or push her away.</p>
<p><em>…Huh? Huh?</em></p>
<p>As I stood there watching, stunned and bewildered, I lost my chance to run over and greet him.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, I was called over to help administer Rudy&#x27;s &quot;entrance exam.&quot; I guess they wanted to verify that he really could cast spells silently.</p>
<p>By this point, I&#x27;d managed to calm down somewhat. It wasn&#x27;t strange that Rudy had a gorgeous woman in his life, given how handsome he&#x27;d become. That was what I kept telling myself. It didn&#x27;t matter if he was married now. It really didn&#x27;t. The two of us were just friends, after all. Why would it be a problem?</p>
<p>I&#x27;d have to congratulate him. Well, not right away, of course. First we could take some time to celebrate that we were both alive. Holding these thoughts firmly in my mind, I braced myself for my first conversation with Rudy in many years.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s a pleasure to meet you. My name is Rudeus Greyrat.&quot;</p>
<p>I froze in place.</p>
<p><em>A pleasure… to meet me?</em></p>
<p><em>Huh? Uh… what? No way. Hold on. Did he… forget about me?</em></p>
<p>&quot;Provided everything goes smoothly, I&#x27;ll be a first-year starting next semester. If you find me lacking in any way, I hope you&#x27;ll help guide and encourage me along.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Ah… huh?&quot;</p>
<p>After a few totally bewildered moments, I finally remembered I was wearing a large pair of sunglasses, had white hair now, and was dressed up like a boy. On top of all that, eight years had passed since the two of us were parted. I&#x27;d grown much taller over that time. It would be silly to expect him to recognize me immediately.</p>
<p>I&#x27;d gotten too far inside my own head. I&#x27;d recognized him, so I just assumed he&#x27;d recognize me. I hadn&#x27;t been thinking straight, plain and simple. All I had to do was take off my sunglasses and tell him who I really was. <em>Princess Ariel</em> had already given me permission. I couldn&#x27;t do it in public, but I could always call him somewhere private later.</p>
<p>But even as I was thinking this, another thought flashed through my mind. A very ugly one.</p>
<p><em>Rudy doesn&#x27;t remember me anymore, does he…?</em></p>
<p>Once I allowed myself to think this, my fate was sealed. My sunglasses weren&#x27;t going anywhere. What if I showed him my face, told him my name, and he <em>still</em> said &quot;Sorry, who are you again?&quot;</p>
<p>The thought of that was too painful to bear.</p>
<p>&quot;Oh, y-yes!&quot; I stuttered awkwardly. All the things I&#x27;d planned to tell Rudy were gone, scattered to the wind. I didn&#x27;t even know what I should say to him anymore. And before I had the chance to gather my thoughts, the exam got underway.</p>
<p>I lost our duel. Rudy totally crushed me.</p>
<p>He started by binding my magic with some spell I&#x27;d never seen before. As I stood there helpless, he fired off an unbelievably powerful Stone Cannon spell that grazed my cheek. He could have hit me if he wanted to, of course. He took it easy on me.</p>
<p>All the progress I&#x27;d made as a magician felt completely irrelevant. Rudy had moved far, far ahead of me.</p>
<p>&quot;H-how did you do that just now?&quot; It was the only thing I could even manage to ask him.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s called Disturb Magic. You don&#x27;t know of it?&quot;</p>
<p>I&#x27;d never even heard of it. It was probably some obscure secret spell passed down among the members of a specific tribe or something. I doubted else anyone in the University would recognize the name.</p>
<p><em>Rudy&#x27;s amazing…</em></p>
<p>I&#x27;d known that from the start, of course. But he&#x27;d <em>really</em> driven it home. I couldn&#x27;t help feeling a little awed by him. I&#x27;d worked my butt off for years to get where I was, but he&#x27;d somehow grown far more than I had.</p>
<p>As I stared at Rudy, he slowly bowed to me. &quot;Thank you, sir! For purposefully losing so I could look good in front of everyone else!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Huh?&quot;</p>
<p>Now I was even more confused than before. Rudy wasn&#x27;t making any sense at all. I hadn&#x27;t stood a chance against him, and he had to know that. What was he <em>talking</em> about? Totally nonplussed, I shook Rudy&#x27;s hand when he offered it to me. It didn&#x27;t feel like a magician&#x27;s hand—more like that of a swordsman. There were callouses on it where blisters had formed and broken. Rudy had probably spent more time with a sword in his hand than Luke, even. And he wasn&#x27;t even a swordsman.</p>
<p>Even in my state of confusion, I felt my heart beating harder than before. Rudy&#x27;s warmth was spreading through my hand, and it made me irrationally happy.</p>
<p>He kept right on saying things that made no sense, though. &quot;I&#x27;ll be sure to thank you properly later.&quot; What was he talking about? I really didn&#x27;t understand. Feeling myself begin to blush, I just nodded.</p>
<p>Once he left, I remembered that he hadn&#x27;t recognized me all over again. I had to take some time to cry.</p>
<p>A month later, I saw Rudy at the entrance ceremony. He looked even sharper than before, now that he was wearing our school uniform. When our eyes met, my heart skipped a beat.</p>
<p>Still, he&#x27;d enrolled here as a special student. There probably wasn&#x27;t much left for him to learn here, so I figured we wouldn&#x27;t run into each other very often. After the entrance exam, I&#x27;d discussed the matter with the others, and we&#x27;d decided that I shouldn&#x27;t approach Rudy too aggressively if he didn&#x27;t remember me. <em>Princess Ariel</em> and Luke said all sorts of things to justify their opinion on this,</p>
<p>but they mostly just seemed upset that Rudy had forgotten me. That cheered me a little. I could tell they cared about me as a friend.</p>
<p>Ultimately, the <em>princess</em> said she&#x27;d leave the matter up to me. It seemed we wouldn&#x27;t be recruiting him to our cause immediately, but we could always wage a slower campaign to win him over, as we had with many others. She added that &quot;Fitz,&quot; as a fellow silent spellcaster, was best suited to the job of recruiting him. In hindsight, I think she was well aware that I had feelings for Rudy.</p>
<p><em>How should I even start talking to him, though…?</em></p>
<p>For the rest of the day following the ceremony, I thought it over while sitting through classes with <em>Princess Ariel</em>. The princess was expected to be an exemplar for the other students, so she had to maintain superb grades. Sometimes it&#x27;s tough being on top.</p>
<p>They taught combined magic very differently here, for some reason. Rudy had supposedly learned it from his master Roxy, who&#x27;d studied at this school, so I&#x27;d expected the methods of instruction to be familiar. I felt like they made it really complicated. Still, I had Rudy&#x27;s teachings to fall back on, so I usually figured things out sooner or later. <em>Princess Ariel</em> and Luke, on the other hand, were struggling. I did my best to help, but when I tried to explain things the way Rudy had, it often just confused them more.</p>
<p>&quot;Fitz, could you go bring me anything that might help with the next class?&quot;</p>
<p>When my explanations weren&#x27;t enough, <em>Princess Ariel</em> often asked me to stop by the library to look for helpful reference material. The library here was its own building, and there wasn&#x27;t that much time before our next class. Still, I&#x27;d been visiting it for three years now, so I had a good idea where to find books on any particular subject. It only took me a moment to picture where I&#x27;d find the material she needed.</p>
<p>Once I made it over there, I moved quickly through the aisles, grabbing one book after another. At this rate, I&#x27;d be back in no time.</p>
<p>But then I saw a certain someone standing in front of a nearby bookshelf and let out a small gasp of surprise. &quot;Oh!&quot;</p>
<p>Rudy was in the library too. I&#x27;d been thinking about finding an excuse to go see him in the next few days, but now I&#x27;d bumped into him by sheer coincidence. <em>Wh-What do I say?!</em></p>
<p>Just as I was starting to panic, Rudy glanced over and noticed me. And a moment later, he bowed his head deeply.</p>
<p>&quot;I apologize for the other day. It was my shallow actions that caused you to lose face. I planned to bring you a box of sweets, but unfortunately, as a new student, I&#x27;ve been busy with so many things…&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Guh?! N-no, it&#x27;s fine, please don&#x27;t bow.&quot;</p>
<p>Apparently, Rudy was under the impression that I was upset with him. That was a surprise…but it did explain why he&#x27;d made up that nonsense after the exam. Come to think of it, he <em>had</em> kind of embarrassed me in public, hadn&#x27;t he? Yeah.</p>
<p>…Maybe that had something to do with why <em>Princess Ariel</em> had seemed so upset earlier, too. I&#x27;d gone in assuming that I didn&#x27;t stand a chance against Rudy, although I hadn&#x27;t expected him to beat me <em>that</em> soundly. But <em>Princess Ariel</em> and Luke were probably a bit unhappy that I&#x27;d lost at all.</p>
<p>That wasn&#x27;t important right now, though. I&#x27;d have to think about it later.</p>
<p>&quot;Rudy—um, I mean, Rudeus, was it? What are you doing here?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Just a bit of research.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Into what?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The Displacement Incident.&quot;</p>
<p>Those words brought me up short for a moment. Was there any chance he&#x27;d been thinking something along the same lines as me?</p>
<p>&quot;The Displacement Incident? Why?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I lived in Asura Kingdom&#x27;s Fittoa Region, and I was teleported to the Demon Continent after the incident.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The Demon Continent?!&quot;</p>
<p>Once again, I found myself too startled for words. I&#x27;d heard about the Demon Continent, of course. It was supposedly a terribly harsh place where every single monster was D-ranked or tougher. Some devoted swordsmen sometimes travelled there to train themselves, but most never returned. The people who landed there during the Displacement Incident were said to have no chance of survival. But Rudy had made his way back in one piece.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes. It took three years for me to return home. My family has all been found since, but there&#x27;s still one acquaintance of mine that&#x27;s missing. This seemed like a good opportunity to do a little research.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Is that why you came to this school?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s right.&quot;</p>
<p>That was incredible. Honestly incredible.</p>
<p>&quot;I see. You really are amazing, after all.&quot;</p>
<p>He&#x27;d spent three long years making it back from the Demon Continent. And then, instead of breathing a big sigh of relief, he went searching for other people, which was impressive in itself. But when the University reached out to him, he jumped at the invitation as a chance to learn more about the Incident. Talk about determination. If I&#x27;d been in his shoes, I would have collapsed in exhaustion the moment I made it back home and spent the next couple years hanging around a refugee camp.</p>
<p>&quot;And what, might I ask, are you doing here?&quot;</p>
<p>The question snapped me out of my reverie. I&#x27;d forgotten all about the reference books I was supposed to be bringing back to <em>Princess Ariel</em>. I wanted to keep talking to Rudy, honestly, but I couldn&#x27;t just leave her hanging. Class was going to start soon.</p>
<p>&quot;Oh yeah. I&#x27;m carrying some documents with me. I have to go now. I&#x27;ll see you again, Rudeus.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yeah, sure, see you.&quot;</p>
<p>As I turned away to check out the material I&#x27;d gathered, something suddenly occurred to me. This library was really big and had tons of books in it, but there were relatively few that had information relevant to the Teleportation Incident. Rudy might be brilliant, but tracking down the stuff he was looking for would probably take him a while.</p>
<p>&quot;Oh, right. You should read a book by Animus that&#x27;s about teleportation, called <em>An Exploratory Account of the Teleportation Labyrinth</em>. It&#x27;s creative nonfiction, but easy to read.&quot;</p>
<p>Just for starters, I recommended him a book that had helped me understand teleportation. It was simple enough that even a child could learn the basics from it. And it also mentioned some specific details that were often torn out of the more advanced books on the subject.</p>
<p>Feeling slightly pleased with myself, I left the library behind.</p>
<p>That evening, I was washing a load of underwear. <em>Princess Ariel&#x27;s</em> underwear, specifically.</p>
<p>There was a reason that this job had fallen to me. First of all, the <em>princess&#x27;</em> underwear was made of extremely expensive fabric. And the fact that they had been worn by an Asuran princess added considerably to their value. In other words, you could sell them for <em>quite</em> a lot of money on the black market. There&#x27;d been an incident not long after we enrolled here, actually. Some of her panties had</p>
<p>been stolen after we sent them off to be washed. Of the five that were washed, four disappeared; three of these were subsequently sold, and the male student responsible kept one for his own private purposes. Some of the more innocent girls in our dormitory had shrieked in disgust when this incident came to light. But for <em>Princess Ariel</em>—who&#x27;d grown up in the royal court of Asura—and myself, who&#x27;d served as her attendant for a brief time, it wasn&#x27;t really shocking. There had been <em>many</em> people in that place who did far more depraved things on a regular basis.</p>
<p>That didn&#x27;t mean the situation wasn&#x27;t unpleasant, though. Since then, doing the princess&#x27; laundry had become one of my official duties. She&#x27;d hesitated slightly to push the job on me, but I could wash my own clothes at the same time, so it wasn&#x27;t much of an inconvenience.</p>
<p>Incidentally, in order to disguise my gender, I now wore the exact same panties as the princess—only in a different color.</p>
<p>I finished up the day&#x27;s washing and headed to the balcony to put out the underwear to dry. The rest could wait, but we wanted these ready for tomorrow. But just as I was starting to hang them on the clothesline…</p>
<p>&quot;Huh?&quot;</p>
<p>I happened to glance down at the road below, and saw something that made me blink in surprise. There was a male student walking along the path, even though the sun had set.</p>
<p>The dormitory rules were very strict on this: men weren&#x27;t allowed to walk this way after dark. Nobody wanted their panties stolen, and although it wasn&#x27;t that time of year yet, there was also the mating season to consider. What was this boy thinking, coming here at this hour? Maybe he was just taking a shortcut back to his own dorm. But even if that was the case, he&#x27;d probably be</p>
<p>surrounded by the &quot;self-defense committee&quot; from the first floor soon enough.</p>
<p>Should I tip them off right now, actually? The first person who spotted a boy at this hour was supposed to let everyone else know. I wasn&#x27;t supposed to talk out loud if I could help it, though…</p>
<p><em>W-Wait a second, am I seeing things?</em></p>
<p>As the boy drew closer, I realized it was Rudy. <em>What&#x27;s he doing here?!</em></p>
<p>In my surprise, my hands slipped. The pair of panties I was holding fluttered downward through the air… right toward Rudy. The instant they flitted by his face, he caught them with a quick snap of his hand.</p>
<p><em>He&#x27;s so fast…!</em></p>
<p>He <em>never</em> let down his guard, did he? The sheer speed of that reaction told me something about what it took to make it across the Demon Continent alive.</p>
<p>After a few seconds, Rudy seemed to realize that what he was holding was a pair of underwear. He looked upward, spotted me, and lifted the panties as if to say &quot;you dropped this.&quot; It was a slow, casual gesture, very different from his reflexive movement earlier.</p>
<p><em>Oh, of course! He just enrolled today! He doesn&#x27;t know!</em></p>
