<p><strong>I</strong>T WAS EVENING, but there were still three people in the student conference room.</p>
<p>The first was a striking beauty who turned heads everywhere she went—Ariel Anemoi Asura. The second was a sharp-faced but handsome young knight who charmed women with ease—Luke Notos Greyrat.</p>
<p>&quot;…So, what was it you wanted to discuss?&quot;</p>
<p>Across the desk from them was a young man with white hair, wearing sunglasses and a male school uniform. His name was Fitz. He&#x27;d brought his hands to his belly and was fiddling with his fingers anxiously.</p>
<p>Ariel looked at him for a long moment. But he didn&#x27;t seem inclined to speak, so she continued. &quot;The other day, Rudeus ran into us while we were out shopping. He found &#x27;your&#x27; actions rather suspicious, Fitz.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;…&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Not long thereafter, he threw you to the floor in the library, after which you ran off, declaring yourself to be a man. Or so the rumors have it.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;…&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Rudeus probably believes that to be a lie. He did get the chance to touch your body at some length, from the sound of things.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;…&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;However, it seems he has no plans to tell anyone about your secret. He claims to be frightened of antagonizing me, but given his skills, that seems highly unlikely. I believe he&#x27;s simply attempting to do right by a friend. Quite admirable, really.&quot; Ariel shot Fitz a sharp look. &quot;The question at this point is…what are you going to do?&quot;</p>
<p>Fitz&#x27;s shoulders twitched at Ariel&#x27;s harsh tone, but he made no reply.</p>
<p>&quot;I do think it&#x27;s all right to take your time with this,&quot; Ariel continued. &quot;However, it&#x27;s now been half a year since you made any progress at all. Can you blame me for wanting to say something?&quot;</p>
<p>She waited for Fitz to reply. Thanks to the large sunglasses he wore, she couldn&#x27;t see the look in his eyes. But she recognized the way he was fiddling with his fingertips. It was a tell-tale sign that he was overwhelmed, something he only did when he couldn&#x27;t think of anything at all to say. If she let him keep going like this for much longer, he&#x27;d probably whimper something like I&#x27;m sorry or I need a little more time to try and postpone this conversation.</p>
<p>And so, Ariel pressed on. &quot;I have to say, I&#x27;ve gotten rather sick of watching you dither about like this.&quot;</p>
<p>This wasn&#x27;t actually true. Ariel enjoyed watching Fitz squirm. She was a little jealous of his feelings for Rudeus, but she certainly didn&#x27;t oppose them. However, Rudeus was spending less and less time with Fitz as a result of his new friendship with Silent. And Fitz was growing more melancholy by the day. That was rather painful for her to watch.</p>
<p>&quot;I think it&#x27;s about time you found the courage to tell him who you really are, Fitz… or rather, Sylphie.&quot;</p>
<p>Fitz pressed &quot;his&quot; lips together tightly and raised his head to look at Ariel. And a moment later, he took off his large pair of sunglasses.</p>
<p>The face underneath them was distinctly feminine. It would have been hard to mistake it for that of a boy, in fact.</p>
<p>It was the face of Sylphiette, Rudeus&#x27; childhood friend.</p>
<p>&quot;Princess Ariel, I…&quot; she began, seemingly ready to speak her mind at last… but then stopped almost immediately, looking like she might burst into tears.</p>
<p>This was enough to tell Ariel something. Something she&#x27;d suspected vaguely for a long time. &quot;Sylphie. This is going to be the third time I&#x27;ve asked you this, but… Right now, is there something that you want to do?&quot;</p>
<p>There was. Yet Sylphie shook her head. What she wanted was impossible, for two different reasons.</p>
<p>First of all, she was too afraid. She felt that Rudeus might have forgotten her entirely. Secondly, she cared too much about the friends in front of her. If she chose to pursue this new goal, it might mean parting ways with Ariel. That would mean betraying her and Luke—these friends who&#x27;d fought at her side, struggling to survive and realize their objective. Together, these doubts kept Sylphie silent.</p>
<p>But this time, Ariel didn&#x27;t take no for an answer. &quot;Sylphie… you&#x27;ve saved my life many times,&quot; she said, her voice soft and gentle. &quot;If you hadn&#x27;t fallen from the sky in the gardens of the Silver Palace, I would have died on the spot. It was you who protected me from the assassins who came for me when I slept, as well. And you fought for me, desperately outnumbered, at the Red Wyrm&#x27;s Upper Jaw. You&#x27;ve helped me countless times in the last few years.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;But I owed you that, Princess Ariel… and more. When I was teleported into the palace, I didn&#x27;t have the first idea what was going on. If you hadn&#x27;t helped me—&quot;</p>
<p>Ariel shook her head slowly. &quot;Any debt you owed me was more than repaid when we fled the Kingdom. Since then, we&#x27;ve been on equal footing. I&#x27;ve simply been manipulating you into serving me.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You aren&#x27;t manipulating me!&quot; Sylphie shouted, her eyes wide. &quot;I want to help you, because we&#x27;re friends!&quot;</p>
<p>In response, Ariel smiled in satisfaction and nodded slightly. &quot;I&#x27;m sure that&#x27;s true, yes. And for the very same reason, I&#x27;d like to help you now. Because we&#x27;re friends, aren&#x27;t we?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What…?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I know you, Sylphie. You&#x27;re probably holding back for my sake, aren&#x27;t you? But you aren&#x27;t my servant, you&#x27;re my friend. There&#x27;s no need for you to put my goals first and ignore your own. If there&#x27;s something else you want to do, then leave me and prioritize it.&quot;</p>
<p>Ariel&#x27;s kind words were enough to shake Sylphie&#x27;s resolve. But even as her heart wavered, she managed to squeak out an objection. &quot;But that would mean…betraying you.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;It most certainly would not,&quot; Ariel replied firmly. &quot;In fact, were I to hold you back, I would be betraying you.&quot;</p>
<p>This claim might not have passed scrutiny if they were still in the Kingdom of Asura. There, Ariel was a princess, and Sylphie merely the daughter of some nameless village hunter. She had earned herself the title of Guardian Mage, true, but they were still far from equal in rank. However, this was the Kingdom of Ranoa, and Ariel was essentially in exile. Because of that, her words had the ring of truth.</p>
<p>If she&#x27;d said something similar to Luke, he would no doubt have objected strongly. He took great pride in his role as Ariel&#x27;s retainer, and would have begged her to give him orders, and to use him as she saw fit.</p>
<p>Sylphie, on the other hand, hadn&#x27;t sworn an oath of undying allegiance to Ariel. But she considered the princess a woman worthy of her service. She respected her so strongly that she would have sacrificed herself obediently if Ariel ordered her to do so.</p>
<p>She couldn&#x27;t quite manage to express these thoughts in this moment, though. Mainly because Ariel was speaking to her with such great kindness.</p>
<p>&quot;Tell me, Sylphie. Do you intend to make a traitor of me, after all that I&#x27;ve done for you?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What? No!&quot; Surprised by Ariel&#x27;s manipulative words, Sylphie looked up with wide eyes. The princess met her gaze with a stern expression. Sylphie found herself wanting to avert her eyes, but managed to resist the impulse. She couldn&#x27;t help swallowing loudly, though.</p>
<p>&quot;Show some courage and speak your mind. What do you want to do right now?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Well… I…&quot; Sylphie pinched her lips together and squeezed her hands into fists.</p>
<p>She knew what she wanted to do. All she needed now was the courage to put her feelings into words. At some point, she&#x27;d lost that courage altogether. But now, as her good friend waited patiently, she managed to find it once again. &quot;I want to… be with Rudy.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Well done.&quot; Ariel smiled at her friend. For once, it wasn&#x27;t artificial. This was her genuine smile—one she used only rarely. &quot;I&#x27;m glad you finally came out and said it. Pursue your own goals first, Sylphie. You can always come back to help me out once you&#x27;re ready.&quot;</p>
<p>There was kindness in Luke&#x27;s eyes as well. &quot;She&#x27;s right. Deal with your personal affairs before worrying about ours.&quot;</p>
<p>He had somewhat conflicted feelings about this situation, in truth. But he was happy that his friend had finally voiced her real thoughts, and he wanted to trust Ariel&#x27;s judgment.</p>
<p>&quot;But…I don&#x27;t think I can take it if Rudy doesn&#x27;t remember me.&quot;</p>
<p>Ariel and Luke exchanged a look and smiled wryly.</p>
<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s think that part over now, shall we?&quot;</p>
<p>With these gentle words from Ariel, an impromptu strategy conference was convened on the spot.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>&quot;Perhaps it would be best to keep things simple. Why not just tell him you&#x27;re Sylphiette from Buena Village?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I think that&#x27;s inadvisable. If he really doesn&#x27;t remember her at all after all this time, the name might not ring a bell.&quot;</p>
<p>Luke and Ariel pondered each other&#x27;s words for a moment. To be sure, there was a good chance that Rudeus had forgotten Sylphie. It had been eight years since they parted, which was more than long enough to forget a childhood friend. At the very least, Sylphie hadn&#x27;t heard Rudeus mention her name even once in the last year. It was hard to imagine her name alone would suffice to jog his memory.</p>
<p>What could she do to make him remember her? That was the crucial question.</p>
<p>Ariel tried to put herself in Rudeus&#x27; shoes. She didn&#x27;t remember the names of all the attendants who&#x27;d served her eight years in the past, but there were a few she did recall. For example, Lilia, who&#x27;d left the court when Ariel was very young. Ariel couldn&#x27;t remember the woman&#x27;s face clearly, but she remembered the way she&#x27;d fought off an assassin to protect her.</p>
<p>&quot;Sylphie, what kind of memories do you have with him?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Memories?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes. People remember us by our skills and the memories we share. That&#x27;s the reason that nobles are constantly throwing parties to introduce themselves to each other. They memorize flowery speeches and practice complex dances in order to leave at least some impression in the memories of their peers. There are quite a lot of them, you see, so it&#x27;s impossible to remember every single one you meet.&quot;</p>
<p>Sylphie&#x27;s skills were certainly distinctive enough. There weren&#x27;t many people in the entire world who could cast spells silently, and none so young as Sylphie or Rudeus. But even with the benefit of that enormous hint, Rudeus hadn&#x27;t thought of her.</p>
<p>There were three reasons for this.</p>
<p>Firstly, Rudeus&#x27; tendency toward self-deprecation. He instinctively believed that anything he could do was easy enough for anyone else to imitate.</p>
<p>Secondly, he had since run into Ruijerd, Kishirika, Orsted, and Badigadi. His encounters with these overwhelmingly powerful individuals had left him with the impression that the world was full of people much stronger than he was. In his mind, it seemed reasonable for there to be plenty of silent spellcasters out there.</p>
<p>And finally, there was Ariel herself. Sylphie&#x27;s silent spellcasting might have seemed more unusual if she was only an ordinary student, but she was the guardian of a powerful princess. It made sense to Rudeus that any mage who served as a royal bodyguard would be highly capable.</p>
<p>&quot;Memories, huh? Uhm… I did tell you about how I used to be bullied, didn&#x27;t I?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes. You told me you were picked on rather ruthlessly about the color of your hair.&quot;</p>
<p>Incidentally, Sylphie had never revealed to Luke or the princess that her hair was originally green. She&#x27;d feared it might cause them to treat her with suspicion. It wasn&#x27;t that she didn&#x27;t trust them. The thought of admitting it was simply frightening, so she&#x27;d decided to pretend that her hair had been white all along. Once the lie was out there, it was hard to take back. And her hair showed no signs of changing back to its original color, so she didn&#x27;t really have to.</p>
<p>This was probably the ideal moment to reveal the truth she&#x27;d hidden from them until now… but the bullying she&#x27;d suffered in</p>
<p>childhood had left its mark on her mind, and she couldn&#x27;t bring herself to do so.</p>
<p>&quot;The first time I met Rudy was when he saved me from those bullies. It&#x27;s the strongest memory I have of him.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;…Hmm, I see.&quot;</p>
<p>Ariel thought it over. Could they arrange to have Sylphie attacked by a group of ruffians, giving Rudeus the chance to swoop in and save her?</p>
<p>There was a problem with that plan, unfortunately. Sylphie was a powerful mage. You wouldn&#x27;t know it to look at her now, but in real combat, she was decisive, quick, and deadly. Your average pack of thugs wouldn&#x27;t last five seconds against her. In all likelihood, Rudeus held his friend Fitz&#x27;s strength in some regard as well. Were there any potential attackers available that were skilled enough to put her in real danger?</p>
<p>…The answer was yes, actually.</p>
<p>Most of the adventuring clan Thunderbolt, known for its skill in combat, was currently staying in this city. For the right price, they could probably be convinced to pretend to assault Sylphie. However, rumor had it that they were on friendly terms with Rudeus. Someone claimed to have seen &quot;Quagmire Rudeus&quot; drinking tea with Soldat of the party Stepped Leader in a café recently. Elinalise Dragonroad and Cliff Grimoire had been there as well. Based on this fact, hiring Thunderbolt wasn&#x27;t a suitable option.</p>
<p>Choosing another random group of adventurers to play that role also didn&#x27;t strike Ariel as advisable. Rudeus probably had more acquaintances in that community than Ariel even suspected. Even if she tried to a find a group that didn&#x27;t know him, there was a good chance they&#x27;d have met somewhere before.</p>
<p>That might make things complicated. And messy. Someone might even end up getting hurt, and Ariel certainly didn&#x27;t want to risk that.</p>
<p>&quot;Do you have any other memories of him?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Uhhh… Oh, yes. One other thing comes to mind…&quot;</p>
<p>Sylphie&#x27;s face went red, and she paused for a moment before continuing. &quot;At first, Rudy thought I was a boy, you know? One day it started raining while we were outside practicing magic, so I came over to his house to take a bath. But then he, uhm, started tearing off my clothes…&quot;</p>
<p>Halfway through her story, Sylphie glanced over at Luke. He promptly covered his ears with his hands. Say what you will about the man, but he could take a hint.</p>
<p>&quot;Uhm, and then… h-he pulled down my panties… and saw my, uh, private parts. That was how he realized I was a girl…&quot; Sylphie went on to explain how Rudeus had been a little depressed for a while after that.</p>
<p>Ariel had already heard the story of what happened after this incident, actually. It seemed possible to her that these events had something to do with Rudeus&#x27; decision to keep his silence about Fitz&#x27;s gender. Even if he didn&#x27;t remember Sylphie clearly, he&#x27;d learned a lesson then that still stuck with him: you didn&#x27;t forcibly expose people&#x27;s secrets.</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s… quite a nice story,&quot; said Ariel, smiling. On the inside, however, she was thinking This is it. They&#x27;d just have to create an identical situation and make Rudeus undress Sylphie with his own hands. With excitement running high on both sides, she could hopefully overcome her anxiety and blurt out the truth.</p>
<p>&quot;All right. Let&#x27;s go with that.&quot; Ariel had made up her mind, and there wasn&#x27;t going to be any debate. &quot;Luke, take your hands off your ears. We&#x27;re going to discuss our plan now.&quot;</p>
<p>At this point, however, the princess remembered their secondbiggest problem: Sylphie&#x27;s penchant for self-sabotage. If they didn&#x27;t take some precautions, her cowardice would doom their plan to failure.</p>
<p>&quot;Before we get into the details, though, there&#x27;s one point I want to make sure we&#x27;re clear on.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;O-Okay…&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Sylphie, you told us that you want to be with Rudeus. But I&#x27;d like to know what that means to you, specifically.&quot;</p>
<p>Sylphie considered the question. What, specifically, did she want from Rudy? What did she want to do with him? She wanted to be at his side, at least. She&#x27;d been in love with him for quite some time, and those feelings had only grown since their reunion.</p>
<p>But she sometimes indulged in some very specific fantasizing, also. For example, she often daydreamed about what their life would be like after they got married.</p>
<p>In these fantasies, the house they lived in was the one Rudy&#x27;s family owned back in Buena Village, or at least one of the same size. The two of them shared the same bed, naturally. When she woke up every morning, Rudy was lying by her side. He greeted her with a &quot;good morning&quot; and a kiss, then got dressed and went off to do his morning training.</p>
<p>Heading downstairs, Sylphie made breakfast. This was one of her jobs around the household. It wasn&#x27;t anything too fancy, but Rudy always had a solid appetite, so she made plenty of food. By the time everything was ready, Rudy was back. He ate the food and said something like &quot;delicious as always&quot; once he finished. He didn&#x27;t talk while he was eating, though. Sylphie just watched him pack it in, serving second helpings when he wanted one.</p>
<p>Once breakfast was over, Rudy headed off to work. Sylphie handed him a lunch box and waved goodbye, then headed off to</p>
<p>meet up with Princess Ariel. They both had jobs, just like Rudy&#x27;s parents. She hadn&#x27;t come up with a specific job for Rudy, but it was just a fantasy, so that wasn&#x27;t too important.</p>
<p>When Sylphie finished her day at work and came back home, she ran into Rudy at the entrance. He smiled a little at the sight of her, brushed the snow off her shoulders, and pulled her in for a hug. Then they headed inside together and turned on the heating stove. Before too long, they had the bath ready. Once they cleaned themselves off and warmed up, it was time for dinner. While Sylphie worked on that, Rudy was making figurines by the stove or something.</p>
<p>Dinner was a little different from breakfast. Rudy was much more talkative, for one thing. He told her all about his day, and the things he&#x27;d seen at work. All of his stories were amazing… too amazing for her to think up in advance. She giggled at his jokes and felt impressed by his accomplishments.</p>
<p>Once the meal was over, they spent some quiet time together on the sofa by the heating stove. Sylphie snuggled against Rudy, and he wrapped his arm around her shoulders. Sometimes they talked; sometimes they didn&#x27;t even say a word. Before too long, they started gazing into each other&#x27;s eyes, and their faces drew closer. Their shadows would overlap as Rudy picked Sylphie up in his arms, turned off the stove, and carried her to the bedroom.</p>
<p>Rudy&#x27;s kind of a pervert sometimes, you know? He might say &quot;How many kids do you want?&quot; or something like that. But then I&#x27;ll say, &quot;As many as you want to give me, Rudy!&quot; He&#x27;d probably just laugh and say &quot;That might be too many,&quot; then start taking off my clothes… and then I laugh too, and say &quot;Better get started, then!&quot; Hee hee hee!</p>
<p>&quot;—Hee hee hee!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Ahem.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Gah!&quot; Snapped back to reality by a tactful throat-clearing from Ariel, Sylphie shut off her internal monologue, flushed bright red, and looked at the floor while fiddling with her ears.</p>
<p>&quot;Now then,&quot; said Ariel gently. &quot;Take that fantasy of yours and imagine another woman in your place.&quot;</p>
<p>Sylphie tried to picture Nanahoshi taking on the rule of Rudeus&#x27; wife. She imagined herself living in the house next door, watching them through the windows as they went about their day. But when Rudy and Nanahoshi noticed her, they smirked a little and shut the curtains…</p>
<p>&quot;You don&#x27;t like that idea, do you?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;N-No! Not one bit!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Very well then.&quot; With a firm nod, Ariel looked Sylphie right in the eyes. &quot;Whether this operation succeeds or fails is entirely dependent on your efforts, Sylphie.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;R-Right!&quot;</p>
<p>In case this wasn&#x27;t enough, Ariel chose to hammer the point home. &quot;I won&#x27;t allow you to chicken out again. Not this time. If you come back and tell me that you couldn&#x27;t find the courage to speak up when it counted, then I&#x27;ll never help you with this again. In fact, I&#x27;ll do worse than that. By my authority as Ariel Anemoi Asura, Second Princess of the Kingdom of Asura, I&#x27;ll forbid you to ever contact Rudeus Greyrat again.&quot;</p>
<p>Sylphie swallowed loudly. She understood, of course, that Ariel was only trying to push her forward. This was less a literal threat than an order to approach this very, very seriously.</p>
<p>Seeing the tension on Sylphie&#x27;s face, Ariel slowly said her final words on the matter. &quot;Give this everything you&#x27;ve got.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Uh… yes, ma&#x27;am!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Very good.&quot; Ariel nodded deeply one more time, and proceeded to outline her plan.</p>

<p>WE WASTED NO TIME putting our operation into action.</p>
<p>It was lunchtime on a school day, and I was on the first floor of the dining hall. The room was packed full of &quot;common&quot; students: exadventurers, beastfolk, demons, and all sorts of other people.</p>
<p>The noble-born students tended to mock this group ruthlessly at every chance they got. But most of their insults were based on nothing more than prejudice. Princess Ariel found that attitude absurd; she liked to point out that only four hundred years ago, some of the very tribes they mocked so freely had nearly driven humanity to total defeat.</p>
<p>Not that any of that was too relevant right now.</p>
<p>I spotted Rudy sitting at the very back table, chatting casually with a small group of friends. He was with Zanoba, King Badigadi, and Julie, who sat at the far end of their table holding a cup in both hands and shooting glances at the other three.</p>
<p>&quot;Do explain, Sir Badi. What are the most crucial qualities of a figurine, in your estimation?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;They must be cuter than the real thing! And more importantly, they must be sexy enough to excite all those who see them!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Ah yes, the erotic element! Your tastes are truly refined, your Majesty. Here, have another drink…&quot;</p>
<p>Badigadi was guzzling down large amounts of beer. His dark skin looked slightly flushed. Rudy and Zanoba watched with big smiles on their faces, regularly refilling their mugs. This was strange. This</p>
<p>dining hall didn&#x27;t even serve alcohol. Had they gone out to buy some beforehand?</p>
<p>&quot;Incidentally, Sir Badi, what would you think about me making a figure of Empress Kishirika? A very sexy one, of course.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That&#x27;s exactly the point, though. Once she&#x27;s back to normal, you won&#x27;t get to see the charming miniature version of her any longer! That&#x27;s why we need to preserve her current appearance for posterity.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I see! You may have a point there. The woman&#x27;s a bit careless at times, though, and she&#x27;s been known to get herself killed rather abruptly. I&#x27;d expect she&#x27;ll be back in her smaller form sooner or later.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;But surely the décor of your castle would be much improved by a display of Empress Kishirika at various ages!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You&#x27;re a human, boy. You won&#x27;t live long enough to see her at all her ages.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s the problem, all right. If we&#x27;re going to make that dream come true, I&#x27;ll have to pass on my figurine-making techniques to future generations. And that&#x27;s why I could really use your support, your Majesty! Ehehehe.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Bwahahaha! For all your power, you play the role of a wheedling salesman oddly well! I approve of your naked greed, boy. What is that you desire, then? Money? Men?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Oh, nothing like that. I was merely hoping you could put in a good word for me every now and then…&quot;</p>
<p>Rudy had his evil smile on again. It really did make him look like a total villain. He didn&#x27;t smile very often, but when he did, it tended</p>
<p>to turn out like that. That was one thing that hadn&#x27;t changed since I met him.</p>
<p>There&#x27;d been someone who smiled like that in the royal court as well—a man I knew as Minister Darius. He was our mortal enemy, and the one who eventually drove us out of the country. But his smile looked like Rudy&#x27;s, so I&#x27;d never flinched when he turned it on us. Maybe it was just something that came with being a clever person.</p>
<p>Rudy and Zanoba seemed really devoted to their hobby of using Earth magic to make little sculptures of people. It was hard for me to comment on the quality of their work, but at the very least, the figurines were really detailed and precise. When Rudy showed me a Red Wyrm sculpture they were working on, I&#x27;d been seriously impressed.</p>
<p>They were also training Julie, who&#x27;d proven to be a talented young dwarf, to help them out. And now they were trying to rope a Demon King into the business as well. It was obvious they were really serious about this project. I did want to join in and help them out, since I was a pretty good mage myself, but that wasn&#x27;t an option. I had to save my mana for protecting Princess Ariel.</p>
<p>&quot;Hello, Rudeus.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Oh! Hello there, Master Fitz.&quot; When I called out to him, Rudy looked up at me with a pleased expression. I&#x27;d been acting a little strangely around him recently, but it didn&#x27;t seem like he was wary of me or anything. Honestly, he could be kind of oblivious sometimes.</p>
<p>Still… it felt like proof that he trusted me completely. That did make me happy.</p>
<p>&quot;What can I do for you?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Uhm…&quot; I hesitated for a moment. It was kind of hard to broach the subject with Zanoba and the Demon King watching me. &quot;Er, would you mind stepping outside with me for a minute?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Not at all. Zanoba, can you handle the rest?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Of course, Master! Leave all the details to me.&quot;</p>
<p>Rudy and Zanoba sure were close these days. I couldn&#x27;t help feeling just a little jealous.</p>
