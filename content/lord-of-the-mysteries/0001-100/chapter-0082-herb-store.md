<p>Various colors surfaced as auras and entered Klein&#x27;s eyes. He casually studied Sir Deweyville&#x27;s condition.</p>
<p>He&#x27;s very healthy; there are almost no hidden problems... His emotional state is horrendous. Amid the dullness, there&#x27;s frailty... His mental state is frail? He has trouble sleeping well? But the purple aura at his head is completely ﬁne... Klein muttered silently to himself as Sir Deweyville walked off and left the library.</p>
<p>Retracting his gaze, Klein pinched his forehead and sighed inwardly.</p>
<p>Being a tycoon sure isn&#x27;t easy...</p>
<p>He didn&#x27;t pay much attention to the matter and returned his gaze onto the journal issues in front of him.</p>
<p>Klein didn&#x27;t find a lot of clues after reading each of them. He could only confirm a few things.</p>
<p>Firstly, there existed an ancient kingdom on the Hornacis mountain range, as well as its surroundings. The ancient kingdom&#x27;s history dated back to at least 1500 years. Secondly, their architectural style was primarily about being grand. They left behind all sorts of murals and from those murals, it could be deduced that they believed that the Evernight would protect the loved ones of the departed. Finally, in the ruins, there were symbols that represented the Evernight everywhere, but they were clearly different from the Evernight Sacred Emblem. &quot;If I had a chance, no— even if I have a chance, I&#x27;ll never go there!&quot; Klein muttered with clenched teeth. He vowed not to court death.</p>
<p>After tidying the journal articles and returning them back to their original spots, he put on his hat, lifted his cane, and left the Deweyville library.</p>

<p>Divination Club.</p>
<p>Bogda looked at the beautiful lady in charge of receiving guests and said, &quot;I&#x27;d like to have a divination.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But Mr. Moretti is not here today,&quot; Angelica answered with uncertainty.</p>
<p>Bogda fell silent for a moment as he paced a few steps and asked, &quot;When will Mr. Moretti be available?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No one knows. He has his own matters to deal with. From what I&#x27;ve seen, he usually comes here on Monday afternoons,&quot; Angelica said as she pondered over the matter. &quot;Alright.&quot; Bogda&#x27;s face darkened as he turned around, planning to leave.</p>
<p>&quot;Sir, you can choose other fortune-tellers as well. For example, you can choose Mr. Hanass Vincent who is famous in Tingen City,&quot; Angelica tried her best to prevent the loss of business.</p>
<p>Bogda stopped in his tracks and considered it for a moment before saying, &quot;No, I only trust Mr. Moretti. Well, can I wait here for a moment? Perhaps he might come after he&#x27;s done with his matters.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No problem,&quot; Angelica said with a warm smile.</p>
<p>Bogda went to the sofa and sat down. Sometimes he stroked his cane; at other times he looked out the window, clearly looking rather impatient.</p>
<p>Seconds turned to minutes. Just as Bogda&#x27;s mind was in a mess, unsure if he should continue waiting or leave, he heard the beautiful lady exclaim in pleasant surprise, &quot;Good afternoon, Mr. Moretti!&quot;</p>
<p>Klein saw the familiar Angelica and was just about to ask why it was always her. Did she not need to rest or take any days off?</p>
<p>However, he immediately took into consideration that he was a Seer, so it was not appropriate for him to ask such questions. Instead, he had to use the tone of a charlatan and say something like: &quot;How marvelous it is for fate to compel us to meet once again, Madam Angelica.&quot;</p>
<p>Uh, would this sound like I&#x27;m hitting on her? Klein&#x27;s mind whirled as he finally replied with a smile, &quot;Good afternoon, Madam Angelica.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;A customer wishes to hire you for a divination.&quot; Angelica pointed to Bogda who had hurriedly stood up from the sofa.</p>
<p>Someone actually requested for me? Klein took off his half top hat in pleasant surprise, pinching his glabella twice while doing so.</p>
<p>&quot;Good afternoon, Sir...&quot; He looked over when his voice suddenly came to a pause.</p>
<p>In his Spirit Vision, he saw the requester&#x27;s liver looking dim. It was nearly black in color. It was making the rest of his body unbalanced as his aura was thin in various places.</p>
<p>Klein deliberated over his words and he said in a serious expression, &quot;Sir, you should see a doctor and not seek a divination.&quot;</p>
<p>Bogda stood stunned on the spot as he immediately gave a pleasant look of surprise while muttering, &quot;How fascinating...&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Anna wasn&#x27;t lying to me...&quot;</p>
<p>...</p>

<p>&quot;Mr. Moretti, I&#x27;ve already seen a doctor and might have to undergo surgery. However, I&#x27;m frightened about the surgery. I would like to divine the outcome.&quot;</p>
<p>The surgery of this era is really fraught with danger... Although Emperor Roselle had given the impetus, this era still lacks most of the necessary technology... Klein didn&#x27;t reject his request and nodded slightly.</p>
<p>&quot;My divination fee is eight pence. Is that fine?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Eight pence?&quot; Bogda exclaimed in surprise. &quot;You&#x27;re only charging eight pence?&quot;</p>
<p>According to Anna&#x27;s description, and the performance Mr. Moretti had just shown me, I&#x27;m willing to pay at least a pound!</p>
<p>Haven&#x27;t you heard of small margins with large volume? Klein was embarrassed for a moment. After thinking for a few seconds, he calmly smiled and replied, &quot;It is enough just being blessed with the ability to receive revelations from the divine and catch a glimpse of fate. Therefore, we must maintain our humility and suppress our greed. Only by doing so, can we continue being bestowed with our gifts.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You are a true seer.&quot; Bogda held his chest and bowed, his tone filled with sincerity.</p>
<p>Upon receiving Bogda&#x27;s praise and trust, Klein&#x27;s spirituality seemed to relax. As for the description of his &quot;principles&quot;, it also gave him some new insight.</p>
<p>&quot;Miss. Angelica, is Topaz available?&quot; He turned to the beautiful lady beside him. Angelica heaved a sigh of relief for Bogda as she smiled sweetly.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Shall we use tarot cards for the divination?&quot; he inquired with a smile.</p>

<p>Influenced by his gentle and comforting tone, Bogda calmed down and followed the instructions by writing down the information and leaving his ring behind.</p>
<p>As he watched Bogda leave, Klein wrote a sentence on the piece of paper.</p>
<p>&quot;Outcome of Bogda Jones&#x27;s surgery.&quot;</p>
