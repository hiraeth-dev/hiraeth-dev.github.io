<p>Klein had a certain degree of curiosity towards the outcome of the exploration. He didn&#x27;t ask further as he walked past the captain&#x27;s cabin and walked down the stairwell.</p>
<p>After a few seconds, he felt his throat itch. He couldn&#x27;t help but raise his fist to his mouth and cough.</p>
<p>Klein wasn&#x27;t surprised by such a development, as it was inevitable. He had carried the Biological Poison Bottle for more than two hours last night. Only when it was late at night and his confirmation that Admiral of Stars and crew didn&#x27;t have any intention of attacking him did he move the mystical item into a black suitcase. Sadly, he ended up falling sick.</p>
<p>Of course, the amount of time he brought the Biological Poison Bottle with him wasn&#x27;t considered long. His body wasn&#x27;t in a weakened state, so his resulting illness wasn&#x27;t anything serious. He just felt his tonsils swell in pain.</p>
<p>Cattleya, who was walking slowly behind, saw this scene, but she didn&#x27;t find it a problem. Instead, she found it normal.</p>
<p>It was common knowledge for knowledgeable Beyonders that mystical items were bound to have negative side effects. Furthermore, they would learn from the seven orthodox Churches and categorize those with serious side effects which made them impossible to possess or use for prolonged time as Sealed Artifacts.</p>
<p>The reason why Cattleya had sold or exchanged many of her low-and mid-level mystical items was firstly because she wanted to better strengthen her survivability. Secondly, the various negative effects from multiple items were troubling. Often, one could avoid one but not the other. Some negative effects might even stack and synergize into something worse. Therefore, to most Beyonders, the disadvantages surpassed the advantages.</p>
<p>She discovered that Gehrman Sparrow had used mystical items, Beyonder weapons, and spirituality charms to arm himself to the teeth. While being amazed, she was already guessing at the type of negative effects he was facing. From what she saw today, it was a temporary simple illness.</p>
<p>With steady steps, Klein arrived on the deck and saw Frank Lee. He was still dressed in a white shirt and dungarees. His arm was stained with dirt as though he wasn&#x27;t afraid of the cold winds that blew down at him.</p>
<p>&quot;Good morning, Gehrman.&quot; Frank warmly waved his hand and said, &quot;Here, try my latest product. This is definitely the most welcomed item at sea!&quot;</p>
<p>As he said, he raised his other hand. In it was a wide and fat fish of an unknown breed.</p>
<p>No, I don&#x27;t want to know what &quot;monster&quot; you have cooked up this time... Klein paused in his footsteps as he looked at him with a cold expression.</p>
<p>Frank Lee didn&#x27;t discover any problems with his attitude. He pulled out a dagger from his waist and stabbed the fish before slicing a hole open.</p>
<p>Blood flowed out and accurately landed in a large beer cup on the deck. It didn&#x27;t emit any fishy smell. &quot;Smell that? It&#x27;s such an intoxicating alcoholic fragrance!&quot; Frank Lee half-closed his eyes as he fervently said, &quot;This is a fish who has red wine replacing its blood. As such, the wine contains lots of nutrients!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;...&quot;</p>
<p>Klein realized he was at a loss for words.</p>
<p>Frank looked at the surroundings sailors in excitement before saying to Gehrman Sparrow, &quot;Do you know what&#x27;s the most troubling matter out at sea? That&#x27;s when we run out of alcohol without being anywhere close to shore! As long as this kind of fish can rapidly reproduce and become the sea&#x27;s primary produce, then we won&#x27;t be lacking any alcohol no matter where we are. By the way, they can be categorized by species. Some will produce Lanti Proof, others Nepos, red wine, and beer. <p>Isn&#x27;t the most troubling matter the lack of water? Of course, all of you can use beer to replace water most of the time because it doesn&#x27;t easily spoil... Those poor ﬁshes... <p>&quot;Is Nina done with her preparations?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, she has already finished a bottle of Nepos!&quot; Frank pointed at a shadow formed by the sails.</p>
<p>The so-called preparations are to drink a bottle of Nepos, a specialty of Feysac? The Nepos which can light a ﬁre? Klein suddenly felt that Nina, the lady mentioned by the first mate, was likely someone with Feysac blood. &quot;Captain, I want a bottle of Sonia blood wine more!&quot; In the dark, a female figure slowly stood up and walked over.</p>
<p>She was more than 1.8 meters tall. Her blonde hair was casually tied up into a high pigtail. Her facial features weren&#x27;t anything outstanding, but she had the striking traits of someone from Feysac. Her skin was fair and her eyes were dull.</p>
<p>This lady named Nina wore a black tight suit made of fish skin. The top and bottom seemed to be a one-piece, fully accentuating her stunning figure.</p>
<p>Such a style was rather sexy to begin with. Nina&#x27;s breasts were also far above what was normal. It was obvious what the surrounding pirates were looking at.</p>
<p>Klein felt a little embarrassed and wished to move his gaze away. However, on second thought, Gehrman Sparrow was definitely not such an untried person. All he could do was empty his gaze and look straight at Nina&#x27;s face.</p>
<p>&quot;Gehrman, this is our boatswain, Nina! She&#x27;s also the navigator&#x27;s assistant. Haha, her Sequence&#x27;s name is Seafarer!&quot; Frank Lee still didn&#x27;t hold back in his introductions.</p>
<p>Apart from his research of crossbreeding, this Poison Expert is rather simple... I now recall that this lady named Nina has a bounty of 3,600 pounds. Her nickname is Seabed Murderer. <p>&quot;Good morning, Mr. Sparrow.</p>
