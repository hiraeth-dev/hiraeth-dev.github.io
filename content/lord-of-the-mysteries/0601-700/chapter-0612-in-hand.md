<p>The two street lamps on either side of the stone bridge silently illuminated the street, but they were unable to disperse the darkness beneath. Only the crimson moonlight from high above could barely illuminate the brown soil.</p>
<p>Hidden under the bridge, Fate Councilor Ricciardo remained silent for quite a while, making Klein suspect whether he had already passed away due to his heavy injuries. He guessed that a large reason as to why the luck augmentation had failed was because of this.</p>
<p>Just as Klein was about to ask a probing question to determine the situation, Ricciardo coughed and said in a deep, hoarse voice, &quot;You live up to the reputation of being a powerful adventurer capable of temporarily sealing the die...</p>
<p>&quot;What happened has exceeded my expectations. Ahem, I&#x27;m very curious as to why my luck augmentation on you had failed.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m very regretful that I can&#x27;t come out and meet you in person. Otherwise, I should be able to see what Beyonders from other pathways will find difficult to see, even if they possess relatively powerful Spirit Visions.</p>
<p>&quot;This is the uniqueness that the Fate pathway holds.&quot;</p>
<p>So the problem lies with me? That&#x27;s not the point. The point is that you need to reimburse me 700 pounds—No, 730 pounds... Klein cursed as he couldn&#x27;t help but recall the orphan, Ademisaul, who had the nickname of Monster in Tingen City. The young man who was equal to half a Sequence 9 had cried out after seeing Klein before falling to the ground. His eyes bled, and the fear he exhibited was as if he had seen a devil.</p>
<p>This is because of the uniqueness of the Wheel of Fortune&#x27;s path of the divine, so he saw something wrong with me. He saw something related to the gray fog? Unfortunately, this Fate Councilor is quite heavily injured and has a domain of bad luck around him. There&#x27;s no way for him to help inspect me... I should ask Snake of Fate Will Auceptin when I&#x27;m back. Perhaps he might know something. However, this pathway does have the bearing of a charlatan. He might not directly answer me and has a high chance of answering in vague terms... Klein maintained his expressionless attitude and said, &quot;Then, what do you plan on doing?&quot;</p>
<p>Ricciardo sighed and said, &quot;Get Darkwill here. I&#x27;ll augment him with good luck and get him to win the money at the casinos on your behalf.&quot;</p>
<p>Using the name of the inn Councilor Ricciardo gave, Klein quickly found the chubby Apothecary, Darkwill. Then, while carrying the suitcase and cane, Klein watched as he kept winning money at the major casinos. By midnight, he had already won 750 pounds.</p>
<p>After receiving the remaining 730 pounds, Klein realized a problem.</p>
<p>The reason for the luck augmentation&#x27;s failure was because of him!</p>
<p>Why is that so? Klein held close his bulging wallet and frowned as he thought silently to himself. He quickly thought of the thin, invisible grayish-white fog around him and realized the reason.</p>
<p>After advancing to Faceless, the gray fog has integrated with reality to a certain extent. Not only can it help me screen off ravings that aren&#x27;t too powerful, but it can also destroy any inﬂuence that changes my fate to a certain extent? Therefore, this councilor&#x27;s luck augmentation was ineffective?</p>
<p>According to this logic, bad luck to a certain extent shouldn&#x27;t affect me either.</p>
<p>Heh, what the heck? It doesn&#x27;t make me become some unlucky fool, but it also destroys my chance of being a lucky man or winner... Klein secretly shook his head and followed Darkwill and the owl back to the stone bridge. He informed Ricciardo of how to summon his messenger and to immediately send him news once he obtained any information on a mystical item with powerful offensive traits.</p>
<p>After doing all of this, Klein found an inn at the Oravi Island&#x27;s port city and got a simple room for himself.</p>

<p>Backlund. In the morning.</p>
<p>Emlyn White walked under his favorite gloomy skies, moving through the thick fog that couldn&#x27;t be compared to those in November or December as he once again arrived outside North Borough&#x27;s 7 Pinster Street.</p>
<p>After pulling the doorbell, he straightened his back as he lifted his chin and waited patiently.</p>
<p>When Leonard opened the door, he looked at the handsome but detestable face before making way and allowing the visitor to enter.</p>
<p>He was still dressed in his home wear, comprising of a white shirt and black trousers. However, he had an additional dark-colored vest draped over him.</p>
<p>&quot;Have you considered the matter?&quot; Leonard asked with a smile.</p>
<p>&quot;No problem.&quot; Since it wasn&#x27;t his money, Emlyn had no intention to haggle.</p>
<p>He even believed that with the attitude displayed by Leonard, there was a high chance of having the price raised if he tried.</p>
<p>Leonard gently nodded and smiled.</p>
<p>&quot;Very rich.&quot;</p>
<p>I can tell that you&#x27;re trying to lead me to say more... Emlyn silently scoffed and replied, &quot;That&#x27;s not something you need to concern yourself with.&quot;</p>
<p>With that said, he raised the suitcase in his hand and opened it, revealing 7,000 pounds in cash.</p>
<p>Leonard took out each stack of cash and thoroughly checked it carefully. Then, he pulled off the red glove he wore on his left hand and handed it to Emlyn.</p>
<p>&quot;This is the mystical item you require. It can change colors to disguise itself.</p>

<p>&quot;When the target is at Sequence 6, there is a chance of failure. The higher the Sequence, the higher the chance of failure.</p>

<p>He received Tinder and checked it carefully before taking out the stacks of cash from the suitcase and placing them on the coffee table.</p>
<p>Following that, he placed the glove into the suitcase and didn&#x27;t take it with him directly.</p>
<p>With the suitcase, Emlyn returned to the Harvest Church just like he did before. Once he entered the priest&#x27;s break room, he set up a sacrificial ritual.</p>
<p>The illusory and mysterious door opened as the red glove tore through the dark and deep void as it was sent directly above the gray fog.</p>
<p>At the moment the gray fog made contact with the glove, a tiny, distorted, terrifying face suddenly appeared.</p>
<p>It kept evaporating as it struggled to sustain itself.</p>
<p>Upon seeing this scene, Klein wasn&#x27;t surprised. After all, the Grandpa residing in Leonard&#x27;s body was an angel of the Marauder pathway. If Amon could do it, it was quite certain that &quot;He&quot; was capable of doing the same. For this, he had made sufficient preparations. <p>Picking up the Sea God Scepter, which had long been placed in front of him, Klein made the blue &quot;gems&quot; light up at the same time from a commanding position.</p>

As for what the dead Worm of Time could be used for, or when its spirituality would completely dissipate, he could only throw it into the junk pile due to his lack of the corresponding knowledge.</p>
