<p>&quot;If there really is a Fountain of Unaging, it would&#x27;ve been one of the main ingredients of a Demoness of Unaging, or it would be something that would be produced from their corpses. Therefore, any man who claims to have drunk from the Fountain of Unaging is lying.&quot;</p>

<p>Fountain of Unaging... Demoness of Unaging... It does match. So, the bloody text on that sailboat beside the ruin is to tell the world that the Fountain of Unaging is a scam? <p>She paused as though making a silent sigh. Then, she said, &quot;That&#x27;s because the Sequence 4 of his pathway is Demon.&quot;</p>
<p>Demon? Sounds deceitful... Creating a treasure scam to harm others, that does match the style of a demon... Klein was instantly enlightened.</p>

<p>&quot;According to the analysis of limited diary entries, Emperor Roselle suffered an extreme predicament in his later years. This forced him to come up with the idea of attempting something crazy.&quot;</p>
