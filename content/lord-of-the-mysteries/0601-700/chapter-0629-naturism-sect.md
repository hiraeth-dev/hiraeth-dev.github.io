<p>Amidst the holy beam of light, having already lost its support from the external power, the tree warts and flesh rapidly disintegrated. It was like snow meeting a red-hot metal ball.</p>
<p>Amidst an indescribable sizzling sound, Cynthia&#x27;s face winced as abnormal agony showed itself on her face.</p>
<p>She turned around with great difficulty, looking up and down, her expression turning horrified and confused. Finally, she realized that there was a problem with her.</p>
<p>&quot;Admiral...&quot; Her voice trembled as she looked ahead with fear and despair, but all she could find was an unfamiliar face.</p>
<p>Klein could&#x27;ve fed Creeping Hunger with Cynthia&#x27;s remnant flesh and Spirit Body, but at that moment, he sighed and said with a calm voice, &quot;Try not to make contact with strange artifacts or religions in the future.&quot;</p>
<p>From his point of view, the reason why Cynthia had turned into this monstrosity either had to do with a cult or some particularly dangerous artifact. She had connected with some existence which couldn&#x27;t be looked at directly, resulting in such a response.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s the Mother Tree of Desire, the Naturism Sect... &quot;Admiral, Admiral, I don&#x27;t want to die! I don&#x27;t want to die!</p>

He spread his arms again and said in a deep voice, &quot;Death is not the end. You will be redeemed when you are at god&#x27;s side.&quot;</p>
<p>Another pure beam of light landed as Cynthia&#x27;s beautiful, weeping face completely fixed in place.</p>
<p>Her eyes widened in despair, but there was the remnant hope of being sent to heaven after death.</p>
<p>Under the sun&#x27;s radiance, her brain and remnant flesh melted away at a discernible pace. By vanishing, it had obtained its final rite of cleansing.</p>
<p>Klein looked silently at this scene without any expression or movement.</p>
<p>After everything calmed down, he curled the corner of his lips and chuckled, finding it hard to hide his sorrow.</p>
<p>&quot;This is really a crazy and chaotic world...&quot;</p>
<p>He didn&#x27;t dare to channel Cynthia&#x27;s spirit after what had happened. The many oddities had made him abandon the thought of bringing Cynthia&#x27;s Spirit Body above the gray fog to &quot;inquire.&quot; Faced with the irremovable evil and the ever-worsening corruption, he could only choose to have Cynthia be cleansed. After all, in this world with true gods, there might exist a heaven after death.</p>
<p>As for Creeping Hunger&#x27;s backlash, Klein was temporarily not concerned. After spending a long time with it, this mystical item which had been sealed to a certain extent had adjusted to him. It didn&#x27;t need to be fed after every use. It had been restored to its original state. Once it was activated, it just needed to eat once in a twenty-four-hour period, regardless of how many times it was used. Of course, there was no need for it to be fed on the day it wasn&#x27;t used.</p>
<p>Therefore, Klein had plenty of time to seek out food for Creeping Hunger without harming the innocent.</p>
<p>If the worst comes to the worst, I can throw it above the gray fog... Klein surveyed his surroundings, and he realized that the bodyguards and the male and female attendants in the room had shaken off the influence of their magnified desires, and they had fallen unconscious. This included the maidservant who was strangling her companion in a bid to obtain her approval and praise, as well as blond secretary, Luan.</p>
<p>Indeed, once the problem is resolved at its root, everything will come to an end...</p>

<p>Yes, if it wasn&#x27;t because of these factors, as Cynthia&#x27;s primary target, my desire towards sex would deﬁnitely be magniﬁed. This wouldn&#x27;t just be me having a romantic dream... Klein habitually reached out with his right hand to cover his face as he changed once again back into Amyrius Rieveldt&#x27;s appearance.</p>
<p>As the lives of the others weren&#x27;t temporarily under threat, he carefully observed the room when his spiritual perception was suddenly triggered.</p>

<p>This is the dangerous item that caused Cynthia to mutate? As it had connected to the existence which couldn&#x27;t be looked at directly, it had completed its mission, allowing all its powers to be injected into Cynthia&#x27;s body?
