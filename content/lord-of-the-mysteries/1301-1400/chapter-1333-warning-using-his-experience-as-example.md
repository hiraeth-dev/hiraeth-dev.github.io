<p>All of a sudden, the muscles on Wendel&#x27;s back began to tense up as if he were about to explode.</p>
<p>He was shocked and doubtful as several guesses flashed through his mind.</p>
<p>The residents of Utopia are monsters in human skin. They usually look normal, but once they encounter blind spots in logic, they would show a side that is different from an ordinary person, ignoring the points that are obviously problematic?</p>
<p>Or perhaps that staff realized that I was lying and was unwilling to deal with me, so he pretended not to see me and let me go? But why?</p>
<p>Yes, carrying my luggage to the bathroom can be explained away as me being afraid of losing my luggage, but the entire platform is sheltered. There&#x27;s no need to take the umbrella. Besides, the rain had already stopped...</p>
<p>Wendel instinctively turned his gaze out of the window, only to see the sunlight shining on the platform he was at. One by one, passengers waited in order behind the safety line, completely different from the dark and gloomy vibes that Utopia gave off.</p>
<p>Phew... He exhaled and suddenly relaxed.</p>
<p>This isn&#x27;t Utopia... I&#x27;ve already left... Wendel murmured to himself as he wiped away the cold sweat that had seeped out from his forehead.</p>
<p>When he recalled his oversight earlier, it was as if he had fallen into a nightmare that he couldn&#x27;t wake up from no matter how hard he tried.</p>
<p>After a while, Wendel stood up and decided to smoke at the platform to ease his mood.</p>
<p>The tobacco comforted him greatly, allowing him to recall his past experiences in Utopia.</p>
<p>During this process, he gained inspiration from his encounter:</p>
<p>Maybe it&#x27;s because I sincerely helped Tracey, so that staff member intentionally ignored my problematic actions and let me go?</p>
<p>Compared to the entire population of Utopia being monsters hiding underneath human skin, Wendel was more willing to accept this explanation.</p>
<p>At this moment, from the corner of his eye, he saw the conductor talking to a group of people in a corner.</p>
<p>Wendel took a few steps forward, trying to hear what they were saying.</p>
<p>With the help of his hearing that transcended the normal limits of hearing, he vaguely heard the conversation from a distance that wouldn&#x27;t incur suspicion. &quot;Last night, the station... Utopia...&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Doesn&#x27;t exist... in the kingdom...&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Please keep it a secret...&quot;</p>
<p>Wendel&#x27;s brows twitched slightly. Based on the description of the document near his chest, he roughly understood what the train conductor was talking about.</p>
<p>They were saying that the kingdom didn&#x27;t have a station known as Utopia at all, and last night, the steam locomotive had gone &quot;missing&quot;!</p>
<p>At that moment, a strong sense of horror surged through Wendel&#x27;s heart again. He felt that it was his greatest blessing to be able to leave Utopia alive.</p>

<p>Alfred spent nearly a week before returning to Backlund from Eskelson Harbor.</p>
<p>This was because he had visited the family of his deceased comrades, his old friends, the elders who had returned to their fief for vacation, and some of his family&#x27;s business partners along the way.</p>
<p>&quot;This is even more tiring than participating in battle,&quot; Alfred grumbled to his father, Earl Hall. Earl Hall smiled and pointed at the staircase.</p>
<p>&quot;Return to your room and get some rest. We&#x27;ll talk in the study later.&quot;</p>
<p>He was quite satisfied with the mental state and progress of his second son.</p>
<p>Alfred surveyed the area and asked with a smile, &quot;Where&#x27;s the most dazzling jewel of Backlund?&quot;</p>
<p>He paused for a moment before adding, &quot;What about Hibbert?&quot;</p>
<p>Earl Hall laughed and said, &quot;Audrey went to her foundation and will only return in the afternoon. She kept complaining that you couldn&#x27;t provide her with a definite schedule, preventing her from knowing when you would arrive.</p>
<p>&quot;Hibbert is now a cabinet secretary. He&#x27;s very busy.&quot;</p>
<p>Alfred nodded and returned to his room to take a shower. He changed into a shirt, vest, and formal suit.</p>
<p>&quot;I prefer East Balam&#x27;s casualness.&quot; He looked into the mirror and smiled at his adjutant.</p>
<p>&quot;This outfit makes you look more like a noble,&quot; his adjutant said as he handed him the document in his hand. &quot;General, this is from MI9.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;MI9?&quot; Alfred thoughtfully destroyed the seal on the envelope. &quot;There&#x27;s a result regarding the investigation of Utopia that quickly?&quot;</p>
<p>Before he could finish his sentence, he pulled out the document and flipped through it.</p>
<p>During this process, Alfredo flipped through the pages slower and slower. In the end, he read it again from the first page.</p>
<p>The main content of this investigation was divided into two parts:</p>
<p>The first was regarding the MI9 member who had dispatched Alfred&#x27;s report. He had accidentally entered Utopia and witnessed a murder case. He managed to forcefully escape in the middle of the night and return to the steam locomotive. The second was that the railways in Desi Bay which led to Backlund didn&#x27;t have a stop named Utopia Station along the way, nor was there a harbor known as Utopia in the Berserk Sea. The subsequent investigators didn&#x27;t find any traces.</p>
<p>The two cases didn&#x27;t exceed the limits of Alfred&#x27;s imagination. What surprised him was the criminal involved in the murder.</p>
<p>Her name was Tracey. She was the owner of a hotel. She had received middle-class education and graduated from grammar school. After that, she became a mistress of a businessman. Recently, she was trying to free herself from this identity.</p>
<p>This was identical to the owner of the harbor hotel, Tracey, that Alfred had met. Every detail matched.</p>
<p>As a result, Alfred determined that the culprit behind the murder was Tracey, the beautiful woman who had received a certain degree of education, who was able to produce sad music in the middle of the night.</p>
<p>Is this her backstory? Alfred muttered to himself silently.</p>
<p>This made the residents of Utopia seem very realistic. It wasn&#x27;t what Alfred had expected—an illusion.</p>
<p>In other words, after the outsiders left, the inhabitants of Utopia continued to lead their own lives. They had their own love, hatred, pains, and sorrow. They had all sorts of experiences.</p>
<p>Apart from Utopia seeming to not exist in the real world, it was similar to any ordinary town in the Loen Kingdom.</p>
<p>Perhaps, Utopia is real. Everyone there is real. However, if one wants to enter the town, they have to be in the right place at the right time... Alfred nodded indiscernibly and put away the investigation report he had received from MI9.</p>
<p>To him, even if this matter ended here, he had no intention to investigate further.</p>
<p>One had to know that, in the Southern Continent, there were countless bizarre incidents and phenomena. If one was too curious, it would only bring him greater danger than he imagined.</p>

<p>Alfred tidied his blond hair, pushed the door open, and sat down.</p>

<p>&quot;No one would say such words to a man,&quot; Alfred replied without any reservations.</p>

<p>&quot;You should&#x27;ve experienced a lot of hardship.</p>

<p>&quot;Everyone undergoes a lot of pain in their lives,&quot; Alfred said with a sigh.</p>
<p>He used a Loen-styled euphemism.</p>

<p>Earl Hall didn&#x27;t continue on this matter and instead said, &quot;Your sister has also become a Beyonder.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Alfred, why are you looking for me?&quot; Audrey, who had changed into home clothes, led Susie and opened the door for her brother.</p>
