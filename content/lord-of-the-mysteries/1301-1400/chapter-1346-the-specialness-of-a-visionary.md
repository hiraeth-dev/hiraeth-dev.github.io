<p>Mysteries... The last time Klein heard this title was from the consciousness of the True Creator that had descended. The penultimate instance was from the ancient sun god from history, the City of Silver&#x27;s Creator.</p>
<p>In a sense, the two of them were equal:</p>
<p>The True Creator was equivalent to the ancient sun god &quot;evil spirit&quot; after retrieving Sasrir&#x27;s personality.</p>
<p>However, unlike before, Klein wasn&#x27;t flustered when he heard this manner of addressing him. Neither his heart trembled nor his teeth chattered.</p>
<p>At this moment, he felt as if he had finally seen a suspended rock fall to the ground.</p>
<p>Of course, he wasn&#x27;t that calm either. To a certain extent, surprise, shock, and disbelief were inevitable. It was because of the words &quot;Adam isn&#x27;t necessarily Adam&quot; from Ariehogg that he had already come up with many speculations and was mentally prepared.</p>
<p>We meet again. Mysteries... Adam is equivalent to the ancient sun god? Or is this the resurrection contingency of the ancient sun god? &quot;He&quot; knows of this, but &quot;He&quot; still wishes to revive &quot;His&quot; father, so that&#x27;s why Medici calls &quot;Him&quot; a zealot? Thoughts flashed through Klein&#x27;s mind as he gained a brand new understanding of certain matters. It was no wonder Adam never promoted teachings about &quot;Himself.&quot; &quot;He&quot; only spread what was left of the ancient sun god!</p>
<p>Klein originally believed that this was due to the special trait of a Visionary, and how &quot;He&quot; could use &quot;His&quot; Uniqueness to create a bunch of realistic believers as anchors. This way, &quot;He&quot; didn&#x27;t need to build a congregation, but from the looks of it, this was probably only one of the reasons.</p>
<p>After two seconds of silence, he looked into Adam&#x27;s pure eyes and said, &quot;You are the ancient sun god?&quot;</p>
<p>Adam maintained his warm smile and said in an illuminating tone, &quot;Don&#x27;t you think I&#x27;m the most unique among the eight Kings of Angels?</p>
<p>&quot;After reading the contents of the second Blasphemy Slate regarding the Great Old Ones, you should be able to clearly understand this point.&quot;</p>
<p>Indeed... Klein nodded, indicating that he could tell.</p>
<p>&quot;Of the five Kings of Angels related to omnipotence and omniscience, you are the only one with a Uniqueness.&quot;</p>
<p>According to the mysticism knowledge that Klein currently possessed, when the City of Silver&#x27;s Creator was still alive, the eight Kings of Angels were in the same state:</p>
<p>Dark Angel Sasrir digested two Sequence 1 characteristics of The Hanged Man pathway and controlled the first Blasphemy Slate, allowing &quot;Him&quot; to use a portion of the power of the Chaos Sea. Therefore, &quot;He&quot; was Heaven&#x27;s deputy, the left hand of God, a king among Kings of Angels.</p>
<p>White Angel Aucuses contained two Sequence 1 characteristics of The Sun pathway;</p>
<p>Wind Angel Leodero, Angel of Wisdom Herabergen, Angel of Fate Ouroboros also possessed two Sequence Beyonder characteristics of &quot;Their&quot; respective pathways.</p>
<p>Angel of Time Amon, Red Angel Medici were the ones with a Uniqueness of their own pathway and a corresponding Sequence 1 Beyonder characteristic.</p>
<p>Angel of Imagination Adam had the Spectator pathway&#x27;s Uniqueness and two Sequence 1 Beyonder characteristics.</p>
<p>This didn&#x27;t seem problematic at first glance, but it appeared strange on careful thought.</p>
<p>Angel of Time Amon and Red Angel Medici didn&#x27;t belong to the five pathways of omnipotence and omniscience, and there was no problem about the ancient sun god using different methods to give &quot;Them&quot; &quot;Their&quot; Uniqueness. However, Adam was different. Visionary belonged to one of the five pathways of omnipotence and omniscience, and the corresponding Uniqueness was one of the foundations needed by the ancient sun god to become a Great Old One. In other words, this was equivalent to one of the three Sequence Beyonder characteristics needed of a Sequence 0. How could it be randomly separated?</p>
<p>After all, Sasrir, as the deputy of Heaven, the left hand of God, didn&#x27;t obtain the Uniqueness of The Hanged Man pathway, much less Wind Angel Leodero, Angel of Wisdom Herabergen, and White Angel Aucuses. Klein originally believed that the ancient sun god had sensed the awakening of the Primordial One, so &quot;He&quot; had deliberately used the method of having children to separate one of the five Uniquenesses from &quot;Himself,&quot; taking the initiative to lower &quot;His&quot; level to resist the corruption and invasion.</p>
<p>This was probably the thoughts of other deities and Kings of Angels back then.</p>
<p>When he heard Ariehogg say &quot;Adam isn&#x27;t necessarily Adam,&quot; Klein felt that there might be a deeper secret hidden in this matter and came up with some guesses.</p>
<p>After witnessing the last few lines of the second Blasphemy Slate, he found it even stranger. This was because a Uniqueness was one of the foundations of becoming a Great Old One. If the sefirah was the foundation, then the corresponding Uniquenesses were weight-bearing columns. It would be deeply problematic no matter which one was lost. If the ancient sun god wanted to lower &quot;His&quot; level, the better choice would be to separate the Sequence 1 Beyonder characteristics. That would be relatively safer.</p>
<p>Dressed in a simple white robe and wearing a thick golden beard, Adam walked to Klein&#x27;s side and turned around. &quot;He&quot; looked at the Blasphemy Slate and said, &quot;A Visionary has something very special.</p>
<p>&quot;Normal Sequence 0s are made up of the Uniqueness and three Sequence 1 Beyonder characteristics. Missing any one of them prevents ‘Them&#x27; from being considered a true deity—they can only be considered a King of Angels. And after becoming a Sequence 0, a Visionary is able to split two Sequence 1 Beyonder characteristics and replace the corresponding characteristic via ‘Envisioning.&#x27; &quot;The object produced by ‘Envisioning&#x27; is real enough. It can work perfectly under the framework of the Uniqueness and a Sequence 1 Beyonder characteristic, allowing a Visionary&#x27;s level and strength to not decrease.</p>
<p>&quot;The two real separated Sequence 1 Beyonder characteristics can be combined with one&#x27;s other identity or a certain lifeform that&#x27;s ‘Envisioned.&#x27; It can make ‘Them&#x27; become an Author with complete freedom. When necessary, one can use a certain method or some mediums to transform &quot;Him&quot; into the true form.</p>
<p>&quot;And for The Omnipotent and Omniscient, ‘He&#x27; can ‘Create&#x27; a Uniqueness that borders between reality and illusory to maintain ‘His&#x27; state. As long as it doesn&#x27;t exceed the necessary limitations.&quot;</p>
<p>This is the relationship between Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt and &quot;His&quot; child, Dragon of Nightmare Alzuhod? I once thought that the Seer and Marauder pathways were the most bizarre. From the looks of it, they can&#x27;t compare to a Spectator... Sigh, be careful of the Spectator... Klein completely resolved the question on his mind. He also understood why Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt had arranged for 0-08 to meet Groselle&#x27;s Travels.</p>
<p>Once they met, 0-08&#x27;s quill, which originated from the Dragon of Nightmare Alzuhod, would awaken and become the Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt at a lower level. Then, this resurrected ancient god would use the negative persona sealed behind the bronze door to activate the Chaos Sea&#x27;s power, instantly reaching the level of the strongest King of Angels below that of true deities.</p>
<p>With such a foundation, &quot;He&quot; had the chance to compete with Adam for the position of Visionary and completely revive. It&#x27;s simpler, neater, and cleaner compared to the Black Emperor&#x27;s resurrection... Klein nodded slightly and said, &quot;The relationship between Dragon of Imagination and the Dragon of Nightmare?&quot;</p>
<p>Adam sat down beside Klein.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;However, the Dragon of Imagination had been corrupted, and ‘He&#x27; had characteristics of other pathways, putting ‘Him&#x27; in a poor condition. All ‘He&#x27; could do was separate an Author. Otherwise, ‘He&#x27; would have directly lost control.&quot;</p>
<p>Klein ignored Adam beside him and looked at the second Blasphemy Slate in front of him.</p>
<p>&quot;What&#x27;s the difference between this and a Marauder&#x27;s avatar?</p>
<p>&quot;If Amon can become an Error, ‘He&#x27; too can create at least two Sequence 1 avatars.&quot;</p>
<p>Adam leaned forward and simply explained, &quot;The avatar and the main body are still the same, but they are located in different areas.&quot;</p>
<p>Does that mean that the Sequence 1 that&#x27;s &quot;Envisioned&quot; is completely separated from the real body, and can be considered another person? Only by using some method or using some medium can one awaken? Klein asked thoughtfully, &quot;You awakened when you became a Visionary?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No.&quot; Adam looked straight ahead and replied with a gentle expression, &quot;When I perished, and ‘True&#x27; was born.&quot;</p>
<p>Klein asked in surprise, &quot;Was no one suspicious?&quot;</p>
<p>If the deities knew that the ancient sun god had long been &quot;resurrected&quot; in Adam, the history of the Fourth Epoch would most likely be to besiege the Solomon Empire, the Twilight Hermit Order, and the Amon family.</p>
<p>&quot;Apart from ‘True&#x27; knowing about this, perhaps only Evernight had ‘Her&#x27; suspicions,&quot; Adam said, &quot;His&quot; voice neither fast nor slow. &quot;I disguised myself as a zealot, a zealot wanting my father to awaken in my body. <p>Thinking up to this point, he lowered his head to look at the blood-stained wooden stake on his chest. He felt that he deserved more sympathy.</p>
<p>Adam said calmly, &quot;It was a necessary sacrifice.</p>

<p>&quot;You can also choose to leave this corpse cathedral, but Zaratul will be waiting for you at the entrance. In your present state, you can only become ‘His&#x27; marionette,&quot; Adam said without any emotion.</p>
