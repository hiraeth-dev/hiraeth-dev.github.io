<p>On the veranda covered by the autumn sun, Audrey wore a refreshing vacation straw hat and sat on a lawn chair as she leisurely flipped through a fashion magazine.</p>
<p>There were exquisite pastries and a cup of black tea made from tea leaves from her family&#x27;s manor on the round table beside her.</p>
<p>Suddenly, she looked up to the edge of the veranda and saw Gehrman Sparrow walking over in a black trench coat and silk top hat.</p>
<p>Audrey turned her head and glanced at her maid, Annie, and realized that she, like the servants around her, ignored the stranger who had appeared out of nowhere.</p>
<p>At the same time, Audrey noticed that the surroundings seemed to darken a little.</p>
<p>She stood up, lifting the corners of her skirt as she curtsied.</p>
<p>During this process, she had originally planned on seriously discussing the matter between the Psychology Alchemists and Mr. Wrath, but on second thought, she smiled again and said in that infectious and brisk tone, &quot;Mr. World, are you here to seek psychological counseling, or to deal with Mr. Wrath&#x27;s problem?&quot;</p>
<p>If it was just the former, Audrey believed that The World Gehrman Sparrow would let her meet him above the gray fog. That would be more convenient and faster. Therefore, this angel was definitely here under Mr. Fool&#x27;s instructions to complete a mission in the real world.</p>
<p>At the same time, she acutely sensed that Mr. World&#x27;s emotions were rather mixed. He was satisfied and happy, yet depressed and sad.</p>
<p>As Gehrman Sparrow&#x27;s main doctor, this situation wasn&#x27;t rare. It often meant that he needed a certain degree of counseling or comfort.</p>
<p>&quot;Both.&quot; Klein pulled a chair over and sat by the round table.</p>
<p>&quot;Do you want black tea or coffee?&quot; Although Audrey hadn&#x27;t taken the initiative to investigate Mr. World, she had met Dwayne Dantès many times before. They had attended meetings, banquets, and dances together, and under the keen observation of a Spectator, many details weren&#x27;t secrets.</p>
<p>For example, Audrey knew that when Dwayne Dantès was Gehrman Sparrow, he preferred food that was sweet with a little sourness. He liked to drink coffee with sugar without milk; for black tea, he loved having lemon slices; out of all the pastries, his first choice was the cream pastries. He loved foie gras, roasted chicken skin, and loved rib-eye steaks more than beef tenderloin. His love for Desi pie far exceeded bread.</p>
<p>In addition, Audrey was undoubtedly certain that the other party was a spice enthusiast. He had high expectations of condiments, and his preferences in this aspect were inclined towards that of Desi County.</p>
<p>Due to these details, Audrey believed that it would be very rude to not invite Mr. World to enjoy the pastries on the table. And the beverage of choice to match these snacks was undoubtedly either black tea or coffee.</p>
<p>&quot;Black tea,&quot; Klein answered after some thought.</p>
<p>Miss Justice&#x27;s relaxed and amiable attitude made him feel much more at ease. He felt that this state would be more beneficial for the subsequent &quot;Placating.&quot;</p>
<p>Since that was the case, it would definitely be better for him to enjoy the pastries while receiving psychological treatment.</p>
<p>&quot;Give me another cup of black tea and add a lemon slice,&quot; Audrey said to her maid, Annie.</p>
<p>Annie wasn&#x27;t surprised by the instructions, finding everything reasonable. She turned around and walked to the veranda&#x27;s entrance, giving instructions to the servants standing there.</p>
<p>Audrey wasn&#x27;t in a rush to begin the psychological treatment process, nor did she ask Mr. World why he was in a complicated mood. She smiled as she carefully introduced the local products of the manor. She made a comparison with Dwayne Dantès&#x27;s Maygur Manor and naturally informed him of the present situation regarding his butler and servants.</p>
<p>Some stayed in the manor and continued their previous work. With Maygur Manor&#x27;s production, it was perfectly fine sustaining them. There was even some surplus that could be converted to funds to repair the houses and be donated to charity organizations. Audrey only sent an accountant, an assistant butler, and a staff member from the foundation to do a check on them. Some felt that, without a true employer, they couldn&#x27;t play their role to their fullest potential. They felt ashamed of the salary they received and chose to resign and look for work. Some of them developed an interest in charity work and found a new calling to life and joined one of the foundations under Audrey&#x27;s name...</p>
<p>Klein listened silently, occasionally raising a question, as though he was chatting with a friend.</p>
<p>When the tea was served, he took a sip and nodded in satisfaction.</p>
<p>After watching him eat a piece of cream cake, Audrey&#x27;s green eyes darted around as she said with a smile, &quot;Food doesn&#x27;t seem to be necessary for an angel, right?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, it&#x27;s just a hobby,&quot; Klein answered simply before adding, &quot;It can be used to maintain one&#x27;s humanity.&quot;</p>
<p>Maintain one&#x27;s humanity... Audrey ruminated over the sentence. &quot;This is to resist the godhood within you? Your mental problems come under such a category?&quot;</p>
<p>She gently guided the topic to his psychological treatment.</p>
<p>Klein nodded slightly and said, &quot;That&#x27;s right.</p>
<p>&quot;You should&#x27;ve experienced it. Every Beyonder who has obtained godhood will definitely experience it in some way. The difference is in the quantity.&quot;</p>
