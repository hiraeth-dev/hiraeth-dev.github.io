<p>Queen Mystic Bernadette seemed to have a sense of foreboding when she saw Gehrman Sparrow&#x27;s arrival. Without any surprise, she put down the ancient scroll in her hand and cast her gaze at the visitor at the door.</p>
<p>Klein didn&#x27;t stand on ceremony and said directly, &quot;I wish to borrow the Magic Wishing Lamp for a day.&quot;</p>
<p>Bernadette nodded gently and raised &quot;Her&quot; hand to pick up the Magic Wishing Lamp beside &quot;Her.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Her&quot; invisible servant immediately held the unique lamp and brought it to Klein.</p>
<p>Klein took it and said in a deep voice, &quot;Thank you.&quot;</p>
<p>Bernadette didn&#x27;t make any requests. It was as though she wasn&#x27;t afraid that she would end up having accumulated an additional wish and dying a strange death.</p>
<p>She believed that Mr. Fool would be able to resolve this problem because the existence had displayed this ability the last time.</p>
<p>In the next second, Klein, who had transformed into Gehrman Sparrow, vanished from the Dawn and returned to Sefirah Castle. When he placed the Magic Wishing Lamp on the table in front of him, he immediately saw the candlewick light up, emitting a sticky light that formed a distorted, blurry golden figure.</p>
<p>&quot;You haven&#x27;t fulfilled your promise yet, so I won&#x27;t fulfill your wishes according to your will again,&quot; Genie said with a majestic and dignified voice. &quot;You should know that the current owner of this lamp is Roselle&#x27;s daughter. The first wish you made was to place ‘Her&#x27; accumulated wishes that could allow ‘Her&#x27; to suffer a backlash at the level Above the Sequences on you. And this time, I won&#x27;t permit you to transfer ‘Her&#x27; wishes onto yourself.&quot;</p>
<p>When Klein had used the Magic Wishing Lamp to escape, he hadn&#x27;t changed the ownership of this Grade 0 Sealed Artifact. It still belonged to Bernadette, and its wielder was still Queen Mystic.</p>
<p>Therefore, his wish would simultaneously be accumulated on himself and Bernadette.</p>
<p>At that time, his first wish was to transfer the two wishes that Bernadette had previously made under his name. This cleared Bernadette&#x27;s accumulated wishes, and he was burdened with two wishes.</p>
<p>But at the same time, this first wish was also accumulated. Therefore, after it was fulfilled, Bernadette&#x27;s count became 1, and Klein&#x27;s was 3.</p>
<p>Following that, Klein made a second wish. It would undoubtedly be counted towards both Bernadette and him.</p>
<p>Finally, Bernadette&#x27;s wish count was 2 without exceeding the critical point, while Klein reached 4. Without any surprises, he encountered the backlash from the rules that were at the level of an Outer Deity. He died on the spot in Adam&#x27;s corpse cathedral and successfully escaped.</p>
<p>Under the premise that Klein made another wish, it would make Bernadette&#x27;s wish count reach 3, causing her to unavoidably suffer something terrifying.</p>
<p>Of course, he could also mimic the first wish he did previously, transferring Bernadette&#x27;s wish count onto himself and spending one chance at &quot;revival.&quot; However, the Genie had already made it clear that &quot;He&quot; would distort any similar wishes, allowing the wish to be realized in a way that Klein didn&#x27;t wish to see.</p>
<p>Sigh, it&#x27;s also because I was in a rush the last time. And Roselle was affected by the Primordial Moon, so the wish we came up with was deﬁnitely ﬂawed... At that time, I should&#x27;ve added a description to make the ﬁrst wish only count under my name. Or perhaps, I should&#x27;ve added another wish between the ﬁrst and second wish to transfer the ownership of the Magic Wishing Lamp to me. After all, my death would result in the ownership being cleared, and the Magic Wishing Lamp would return to its wielder... As Klein sighed inwardly, he smiled at Genie.</p>
<p>&quot;You should be no stranger to ‘Tampering&#x27; and ‘Reassembly.&#x27;</p>
<p>&quot;With your level and current state as this lamp, I can now rely on Sefirah Castle to ‘Graft&#x27; its ownership from Bernadette onto me. Or perhaps, we can use a different method and directly use ‘Theft&#x27;?&quot;</p>
<p>Genie&#x27;s blurry golden figure fell silent. After a few seconds, &quot;He&quot; said, &quot;Although you aren&#x27;t as cunning as ‘Him,&#x27; you&#x27;re just as shameless.</p>
<p>&quot;However, this is of no use. After you accumulate your wishes and die, the ownership of this lamp will still return to Roselle&#x27;s daughter. And I will distort the rules and place your wish count onto ‘Her.&#x27;</p>
<p>&quot;Also, I said earlier that before you fulfill your promise, I won&#x27;t fulfill your wish normally again.&quot;</p>
<p>Klein maintained his smile.</p>
<p>&quot;I once heard a story regarding investors.</p>
<p>&quot;When a company is in trouble, the ones who are more willing to provide aid will definitely be the original investors. Although they have already paid a lot, if they don&#x27;t continue investing, they might not be able to recoup their investment and end up losing all their money.</p>
<p>&quot;Of course, if they could use other means to retrieve their own share, or exchange their investment for greater benefits, it would be another story.</p>
<p>&quot;Which type do you think you are?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;After you gain a deeper grasp of Sefirah Castle, you also seem to have obtained the eloquence of a Swindler. <p>&quot;Actually, this lamp has another use that can affect matters at Sequence 0.</p>

<p>After such a friendly exchange, Klein and Genie exchanged their views. The fact that both of them were connected by fate had allowed &quot;Them&quot; to come up with a preliminary agreement. Then, Klein placed the Magic Wishing Lamp into his pocket, prepared to use it at any time.</p>
<p>After completing all of this, he cast his gaze at the fragments of light related to the Hornacis mountain range in the fog of history.</p>
<p>He was almost done with his preparations. Next, he would create another avatar and steal the undigested Attendant of Mysteries Beyonder characteristic from himself. Then, he could face the ancestor of the Antigonus family.</p>
<p>And this might very well trigger a divine battle.</p>
