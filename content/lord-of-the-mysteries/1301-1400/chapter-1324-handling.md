<p>After staring at the deck below, Cattleya left the captain&#x27;s cabin and walked to the cabin&#x27;s entrance. She waited until Frank Lee carried a bucket of alcohol-free beer over.</p>
<p>&quot;Captain, do you want a cup?&quot; Frank raised the large bucket of beer with one hand.</p>
<p>Cattleya shook her head firmly and asked in a seemingly casual manner, &quot;Is this the will of the benevolent mother?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No,&quot; Frank said rather seriously. &quot;I just feel that drinking liquor that has been altered by a modified sedative isn&#x27;t good for the body. I hope that they&#x27;ll be able to accept this drink that has no alcohol inside, other than the smell of alcohol. Of course, this is only a preliminary result and it doesn&#x27;t involve liquor. After all, it can&#x27;t use distillation to enhance its taste.&quot;</p>
<p>Cattleya was momentarily at a loss for words as she nudged the heavy glasses on her nose bridge. After two seconds of silence, she said, &quot;Do you hear the benevolent mother&#x27;s voice or divine epiphanies?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No.&quot; Frank didn&#x27;t think much of it as he shook his head.</p>
<p>Phew... Cattleya secretly heaved a sigh of relief.</p>
<p>At that moment, Frank casually added, &quot;The benevolent mother&#x27;s will is in the ears of the wheat, in the milk of cows, in the growth of mushrooms, and in every corner of nature. One can feel it without the need to experience the divine epiphany.&quot; Cattleya&#x27;s gaze immediately shot towards Frank&#x27;s eyes. She didn&#x27;t find any signs of madness, only pureness.</p>
<p>Without another word, she nodded slightly and walked forward. She crossed Frank Lee and arrived on the deck.</p>
<p>After taking in the sea&#x27;s vista for a few minutes, Cattleya returned to the captain&#x27;s cabin. She unfolded a letter and wrote:</p>
<p>&quot;I wonder what&#x27;s your take regarding Frank&#x27;s actions? He claims that he doesn&#x27;t obtain any divine epiphanies, and believes that the benevolent mother&#x27;s will is hidden in every nook and cranny of nature.&quot;</p>
<p>This was a letter to Gehrman Sparrow. Cattleya believed that just by adding such a question, he would be able to understand what she was implying.</p>
<p>After folding the letter, she took out a gold coin and began to summon the terrifying messenger.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Ma&#x27;am Hermit wrote me a letter immediately after the Tarot Gathering ended... It shouldn&#x27;t be related to the Hidden Sage. It can&#x27;t be that fast... Klein was on a cruise ship, traveling on the Tussock River.</p>
<p>Wearing a black robe, he took the letter from Miss Messenger&#x27;s mouth and opened it. He finished reading all the content with a glance. This... Klein frowned slightly.</p>
<p>Perhaps it was because Frank had shown enough danger and &quot;madness&quot; in his usual behavior that he had actually neglected the possibility of this Druid being affected by the Mother Goddess of Depravity.</p>
<p>Frank had once been placed on trial by the Church of Earth Mother for a forbidden experiment, and then he was placed on a wanted list... If Earth Mother hadn&#x27;t been corrupted, and the Church of Earth&#x27;s operations were rather normal, then it means that they believe that Frank is problematic... However, if there&#x27;s concrete evidence, Frank, who went to the court, probably wouldn&#x27;t have lived... Klein&#x27;s mind raced as he attempted to find clues based on his understanding of Frank.</p>
<p>There&#x27;s no problem with the relevant actions. The prayer posture and his usual prayer gestures are different. It&#x27;s just like how when the Beyonders of the Church of Evernight pray, they won&#x27;t draw four stars on their chests at times. At most, they&#x27;ll just go through the ﬁnal motions...</p>
<p>Apart from this small problem, there&#x27;s something wrong with Frank in every aspect. He&#x27;s like a child raised by the Mother Goddess of Depravity... As these thoughts flashed through his mind, Klein had two contradictory guesses.</p>
<p>Either Frank&#x27;s corruption was very hidden, making it impossible for the Church of Earth Mother&#x27;s tribunal to be certain, or he wasn&#x27;t affected by the Mother Goddess of Depravity at all. It was purely because he had a mental problem that made him look like a Blessed of an evil god.</p>
<p>Regardless of which guess it was, Klein believed that the subsequent development wouldn&#x27;t be good. He remembered very clearly that Frank&#x27;s mushroom experiment had a breakthrough because he had been given an assistant who believed in the Primordial Moon. And the Primordial Moon was one of the manifestations of the Mother Goddess of Depravity.</p>
<p>That also meant that from that moment forth, regardless of whether Frank had any problems in the past, he could very well have entered the eyes of the Mother Goddess of Depravity.</p>
<p>This wasn&#x27;t a good thing.</p>
<p>Of course, I can&#x27;t eliminate the possibility that Frank is excessively crazy, causing the Mother Goddess of Depravity to just look away... Klein mumbled in amusement, hoping to make the best out of it.</p>
<p>This was nearly impossible. The horror and strength of a foreign god wasn&#x27;t something a demigod could understand.</p>
<p>Klein even suspected that if it wasn&#x27;t for the overly intense response that would destroy the anchors of the deities, allowing &quot;Them&quot; to attack each other in a frenzy, resulting in the Beyonder characteristics to gather and awaken the Oldest One, the Outer Deities might&#x27;ve already destroyed the sun and destroyed the ecosystem of the world. <p>These were things that had been placed above the gray fog for a long time and didn&#x27;t undergo any special sealing.</p>
