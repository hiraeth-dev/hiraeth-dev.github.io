<p>Susie was sitting by the door, waiting for Audrey to return.</p>
<p>At that moment, the golden retriever immediately went up to her as soon as she saw her approach. She even gave a rather talented showcase of her acting by barking and wagging her tail.</p>
<p>She didn&#x27;t ask on the spot; instead, she kept accompanying Audrey back to her bedroom before asking in concern, &quot;Is it over?&quot;</p>
<p>Audrey tersely acknowledged, indicating that there was no problem.</p>
<p>&quot;Is there anything I can help you with?&quot; Susie pressed.</p>
<p>&quot;Not yet.&quot; Audrey shook her head slightly.</p>
<p>Due to Susie&#x27;s concern, she instinctively recalled her previous experience, but to her surprise, she realized that she couldn&#x27;t recall the image and name of the Psychology Alchemists president. To recall even the information that she had learned from The World Gehrman Sparrow, she had to focus quite a bit.</p>
<p>To be frank, in front of Beyonders below the demigod level, she could do similar feats as a Manipulator. However, to influence a saint in the mind domain and make it impossible for them to sense it was almost impossible unless the other party was hypnotized, unconscious, or enslaved. That gentleman&#x27;s level is probably higher than I expected... Audrey controlled her emotions and calmly made a certain judgment.</p>
<p>She gave Susie a look and got the golden retriever to stand guard outside the door. In her mind, she outlined the character mask that represented Pride.</p>
<p>Just as she was thinking about this, she felt a slight fluctuation in the sea of collective subconscious around her.</p>
<p>With just a thought, Audrey reached out her left hand and grabbed it. She took out an abnormally cold, illusory grayish-white mask.</p>
<p>As long as I seriously think about it, will this Pride mask come to my side? Or should I say that it has already existed in my subconscious and has been following me? As Audrey carefully examined her mind island, she signaled with a thought that made the Pride mask vanish.</p>
<p>The ice-cold, illusory grayish-white mask quickly turned transparent and melted into the surrounding sea of collective subconscious.</p>
<p>Audrey still couldn&#x27;t determine where this Pride mask had gone. Why did it appear in such a timely manner each time?</p>
<p>This made her increasingly wary. She decided to pray to Mr. Fool and make the wish of sealing the mask.</p>
<p>Just as she was thinking of doing a few more experiments to obtain more knowledge from Pride, she saw the sea of collective subconscious begin to resonate without using her Manipulator Beyonder powers.</p>
<p>A beam of light came from afar as it grew larger and more obvious. Finally, it turned into an illusory letter.</p>
<p>This letter stopped in front of Audrey&#x27;s mind island as though it was searching for a path to complete the &quot;delivery.&quot;</p>
<p>Audrey naturally wouldn&#x27;t let any foreign object enter her mind world. She hurriedly reached out her left hand and touched the illusory letter.</p>
<p>Seeing that her fingers were about to touch the surface of the letter, Audrey suddenly stopped.</p>
<p>Her experience and intelligence told her at the same time:</p>
<p>Don&#x27;t touch anything in the mysticism world rashly. This was especially so for the mind domain. Otherwise, it would easily lead to corruption and produce mental problems!</p>
<p>With this in mind, Audrey produced a Virtual Persona and put on the black long-veiled glove, Hand of Horror.</p>
<p>After making the necessary preparations, she reached out to grab the illusory letter and watched as it peeled away its outer layer and flipped over one page at a time.</p>
<p>This was all the information related to the mind dragon in East Chester County. To use the sea of collective subconscious to transmit information... The higher-ups of the Spectator pathway are truly amazing... As a Manipulator, Audrey had been trying her best to maintain her initial yearning towards the mysticism world. It was a pure and innocent pursuit of &quot;dreams&quot; and &quot;magic.&quot;</p>
<p>This was one of the ways to prevent herself from losing herself in the &quot;Manipulating&quot; experience of things, and not be assimilated by the sea of collective subconscious.</p>
<p>After flipping through all the information, Audrey let the illusory letter melt into the sea of collective subconscious.</p>
<p>She was still in no hurry to pray to Mr. Fool. According to her previous arrangements, she went out to be busy over other things.</p>
<p>At dusk, before the banquet at home began, she found some time to quickly make a prayer in her bedroom and make a wish.</p>
<p>In the next second, Audrey saw the grayish-white Pride appear from the sea of collective subconscious in front of her. The illusory feeling slowly vanished as a tiny amount of metallic luster flashed.</p>
<p>For some reason, this persona mask had a certain substantial feel to it as though it was bordering between illusory and realism.</p>
<p>This meant that it was isolated from Audrey&#x27;s mind island in a physical sense.</p>
<p>Of course, it also lost the ability to return to the sea of collective subconscious.</p>
<p>Audrey held Pride and attempted to remove Lie that had turned into an emerald necklace, overlapping the two together.</p>
<p>Just as she had expected, the half-illusory, half-real Pride embedded itself into Lie, turning into a blob of patterns that roughly looked like a human face.</p>
<p>In the future, I&#x27;ll use this method to carry it with me. I won&#x27;t allow the persona mask to have any contact with my mind or body until I need to use it... Audrey&#x27;s mind raced as she sincerely thanked Mr. Fool.</p>
<p>She then left for the banquet hall.</p>
<p>Along the way, she met her father, Earl Hall.</p>
<p>&quot;Good news.&quot; Earl Hall laughed.</p>
<p>Audrey didn&#x27;t hide her surprise.</p>
<p>&quot;Alfred&#x27;s coming back?&quot;</p>
<p>This was her other brother.</p>
<p>&quot;You actually guessed it?&quot; Earl Hall said in surprise, &quot;In the next half of the year, he will return to Backlund as a general.&quot; Half a year... Susie is already a Dreamwalker... Audrey thought for a moment and asked, &quot;Father, when are we returning to East Chester County?&quot;</p>
<p>As the war had just ended and there were many things in the kingdom that needed to be done, the nobles didn&#x27;t return to their respective fiefs during New Year&#x27;s. They remained in Backlund all this time. It was already the end of February.</p>
<p>Earl Hall nodded and said, &quot;About April.&quot;</p>
