<p>Chapter 1319: Life­Preserving Incantation</p>

<p>&quot;It comes from the Holy Bible of the Third Epoch&#x27;s Creator. It involves the highest mystery of the mind domain, and it has a close relationship with something of the Psychology Alchemists.</p>

<p>Derlau didn&#x27;t explain and instead said, &quot;As a councilor, you should be in charge of a Grade 1 Sealed Artifact. <p>&quot;As long as you have the chance to resist an accident, then you can directly enter this mind city to avoid the enemy and obtain the item.</p>

<p>You said &quot;Adam&quot; twice. That entity is probably already watching. No, &quot;He&quot; might have been watching from the very beginning... Audrey&#x27;s heart almost beat faster when she heard that. &quot;Then how do we enter without your invitation?&quot; Mr. Envy asked as he nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;There will be some effects that need attention. But I believe that you are all experts in the mind domain. You have the ability to resolve problems in this aspect,&quot; Derlau said frankly.</p>
<p>Audrey was a little worried that these seven persona masks had something to do with Adam, but she didn&#x27;t dare think about it in the mind city. She forced herself to rein in her thoughts and replied to what Derlau had said before, &quot;I can accept the two safekeeping methods of the Grade 1 Sealed Artifact. <p>Audrey subconsciously wanted to stand up and lead the other councilors into bowing and bidding farewell. However, she quickly came to her senses and continued sitting there, being the penultimate person to stand up.</p>
<p>Before leaving, she asked curiously, &quot;Mr. President, does this city have a name?&quot;</p>
