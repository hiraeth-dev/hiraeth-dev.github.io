<p>Deep in the Forsaken Land of the Gods, on the peak of a mountain stood a gigantic cross that bordered the realm of reality and illusion.</p>
<p>There was a blurry figure hanging there. Ancient wooden stakes dyed with fresh blood that didn&#x27;t drip down passed through &quot;His&quot; body, nailing &quot;Him&quot; to the cross.</p>

<p>Adam, whose face was half-covered by a pale blond beard, walked over and stopped in front of the huge cross. &quot;He&quot; raised &quot;His&quot; head and silently looked at the hanging figure.</p>
<p>&quot;He&quot; held Arrodes in one hand and held the second Blasphemy Slate in the other. &quot;His&quot; eyes were limpid and his expression was calm.</p>

<p>In the light, a sticky but illusory black liquid surged out. A boundless sea that seemed to contain all colors appeared. It looked like it was at arm&#x27;s length but couldn&#x27;t affect reality.</p>
