<p>The nearly frozen silence lasted for nearly ten seconds before the Abraham family members uniformly cast their gaze at Fors.</p>
<p>After sensing the mixed emotions of wariness, guardedness, and fear, Fors took the initiative to take a few steps to the side and warned, &quot;Be careful of the negative effects.&quot;</p>
<p>Even if the Beyonder characteristics hadn&#x27;t fused with the surroundings and formed Sealed Artifacts, they contained certain negative effects. However, most of the time, it would only have effects from direct contact. Of course, the Beyonder characteristics here were all High-Sequence Beyonder characteristics. No one could be certain if they would actively expand their area of influence.</p>
<p>Seeing that Fors didn&#x27;t show any obvious greed, Dorian nodded and said, &quot;When you finish digesting the Secrets Sorcerer potion, you can consider advancing to Sequence 3 Wanderer. I&#x27;ll give you the potion formula and prepare the corresponding ingredients for you. Of course, at this level, the higher the Sequence, the greater the danger you face. This is an objective situation. It doesn&#x27;t change because of your personal will and arrangements. When the time comes, you can decide whether you wish to advance or not.&quot;</p>
<p>He said these words because he cared for his student, and on the other hand, he wanted to assure her that whatever the Abraham family possessed was equivalent to her possessing it. No one would treat her as an outsider and deliberately make things difficult for her on the matter of raising her Sequence.</p>
<p>This could effectively eliminate the heat brought by greed. And after obtaining so many High-Sequence Beyonder characteristics, Vilos and the other members of the Abraham family weren&#x27;t unwilling to part with a Wanderer Beyonder characteristic. They even believed that using it to exchange for &quot;peace&quot; was absolutely worth it. After all, there was only one demigod here—Fors.</p>
<p>Without the time to bring out their Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts, and because of the ineffectiveness of the seals, the Abraham family members didn&#x27;t dare take out most of the Grade 1 Sealed Artifacts. Under such a situation, Fors had the ability to finish all of them off.</p>
<p>Faced with her teacher&#x27;s promise, Fors tersely acknowledged.</p>
<p>&quot;Was the ‘door&#x27; to the concealed space opened?</p>
<p>She thought that the High-Sequence Beyonder characteristics in front of her were all from the Abraham family&#x27;s treasure vault and had been thrown into the real world due to the recent abnormality. However, she did feel that there were too many of them.</p>
<p>&quot;No.&quot; Dorian slowly shook his head, looking confused.</p>
<p>The other Abraham family members remained silent, equally confused.</p>
<p>No one would believe that treasure would fall from the skies unless it happened before their eyes!</p>
<p>&quot;Should we pray to Mr. Fool?&quot; Fors tried giving a suggestion. Dorian, who had a vague guess, immediately looked at the other Abrahams. He saw the ones who had changed faiths to Mr. Fool nod in agreement. Those who hadn&#x27;t changed faiths were clearly hesitant and eager to object.</p>
<p>After some consideration, Dorian composed himself and said, &quot;Gather the ones with negative area-of-effect traits while using the correct method. Prevent them from combining with the surroundings.</p>

<p>Following that, they seized the opportunity to identify the characteristics and attempt to gather them.</p>
<p>After some of the members had obtained a certain amount, Dorian finally retreated to Fors&#x27;s side and bowed his head to pray to Mr. Fool.</p>
<p>Soon, a grayish-white fog and an ancient palace deep in the fog appeared in front of him. A high and solemn voice resounded in his ears:</p>
<p>&quot;These are the relics of Mr. Door.</p>
<p>&quot;Your bloodline curse has been completely removed.&quot;</p>
<p>Relics of Mr. Door... Relics... Dorian ruminated over the word and opened his eyes, casting his gaze at the High-Sequence Beyonder characteristics.</p>
<p>He looked on silently, his vision gradually blurring.</p>

<p>Alfred and the others, who had just figured out the source of the explosion and the &quot;enemy attack&quot; shout, saw the doors and windows opening at the same time as they crashed into the walls.</p>
<p>During this process, several pieces of glass shattered.</p>

<p>&quot;At the same time, send a telegram to the archbishop of East Chester diocese, and request for assistance.&quot;</p>

<p>He had plenty of time to do the former after daybreak. But if anything happened to the latter, it would be impossible to make up for it. <p>&quot;Sit down and get some rest.&quot;</p>
