<p>Blue Mountain Island, within a primitive forest.</p>
<p>Worried that official Beyonders would discover the ritual outside the city, Verdu Abraham, who had finished making preparations, used a Sealed Artifact to &quot;Teleport&quot; away from Bayam and arrived in someplace that was nearly uninhabited.</p>
<p>Rubbing the pain in his ribs, Verdu took off his classic robe and placed it aside.</p>
<p>Following that, he set up an altar, lit the candles, and burnt the correct essential oils, extracts, herbal powder, etc.</p>
<p>After finishing the setup, Verdu took two steps back and lowered his voice to chant in ancient Hermes, &quot;The great God of War;</p>
<p>&quot;The symbol of iron and blood;</p>
<p>&quot;The ruler of chaos and strife...&quot;</p>
<p>A howling wind echoed in the dark forest.</p>
<p>Amidst the rustling of the branches and leaves, two of the candles on the altar suddenly emitted crackling sounds.</p>
<p>The corresponding candle flames rose from the size of a &quot;sapling&quot; to a &quot;towering tree.&quot; At the same time, the color of the candlelight changed from orange to bright white.</p>
<p>The wind that passed through the forest became even more intense. Two fiery pillars of fire intertwined together, twisting into a blurry and huge figure.</p>
<p>At that moment, Verdu Abraham felt an indescribable gaze cast from high above and onto his body.</p>
<p>He hurriedly bowed his head and said, &quot;Great God of War. Your lowly believer would like to pray for your help.&quot;</p>
<p>As he spoke, he kept adjusting his attitude and tried his best to remain calm.</p>
<p>He knew from his family&#x27;s books that the most important thing to take note of when praying to the God of War was to &quot;not be angry.&quot;</p>
<p>The massive figure formed from a blinding white flame constantly spewed out flames as it used a language that Verdu could understand, but had no idea what it was.</p>
<p>&quot;Lowly mortal, a god is not used to satisfy your wishes.</p>
<p>&quot;Speak, say your prayers, and I&#x27;ll decide whether to help you in the end.&quot;</p>

Sacrifice a Bizarro Sorcerer, a Secrets Sorcerer, and a Parasite. You should already know about it,&quot; the flaming giant said in a deep and dignified manner.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s the same as the answer given by Dorian&#x27;s student... Verdu sighed and said,</p>
<p>&quot;Great God of War, what must I pay to request you to complete this ritual for me?&quot;</p>
<p>The flaming giant looked down at the Astrologer and said, &quot;The corresponding price is not something you can afford. Your pale and puny soul isn&#x27;t even qualified to be embers.&quot;</p>
<p>Just as Verdu was disappointed and abnormally depressed, the flaming giant continued, &quot;But Mr. Door can.</p>
<p>&quot;Besides, I&#x27;m in a good mood today.</p>
<p>&quot;Sacrifice a portion of your blood to me as the mark of the contract. I will request the corresponding price from Mr. Door.&quot;</p>
<p>That&#x27;s possible? Verdu subconsciously had his suspicions, but after some thought, he felt that there was nothing wrong with it.</p>
<p>Indeed, only his ancestor, Bethel Abraham, had the right to trade with a secret existence like the God of War!</p>
<p>As for whether Mr. Door was willing to proceed with the deal, Verdu had never considered it. From his point of view, anyone who was in an exiled and sealed state would undoubtedly want to escape, even if they had to pay a huge price! &quot;Yes, great God of War.&quot; After pondering for a moment, Verdu agreed.</p>
<p>He then changed the ritual and added the sacrifice and bestowment parts of a ritual. He then used a metal dagger to pierce through his arm and let out dark red blood.</p>
<p>When his blood turned into red &quot;pearls&quot; and passed through the Door of Sacrifice and Bestowment, it became extremely dark, as though there were countless monsters lurking in it.</p>
<p>In the next second, an object was spat out from behind the illusory door.</p>
<p>It was a semi-transparent meat blob with slippery tentacles. On the meat blob, twisted maggots crawled out one after another.</p>
<p>Upon seeing the object, Verdu&#x27;s head turned dizzy as though large amounts of gunk had been injected into it.</p>
<p>At this moment, a flame landed, enveloping his body, turning his vision red.</p>
<p>With this layer of red light, Verdu no longer suffered any anomalies when looking at the blob of meat.</p>
<p>Right on the heels of that, another object was spat out of the Door of Sacrifice and Bestowment.</p>
<p>It was a bird with a deformed head. Every feather shimmered with a faint stellar radiance. Strands of insect-like brilliance drilled into its body and occasionally emanated out. Worm of Stars... Is this a monster with a Secrets Sorcerer&#x27;s Beyonder characteristic? The previous one corresponds to a Bizarro Sorcerer... Indeed, Secrets Sorcerer and Bizarro Sorcerer are only names of potions. They aren&#x27;t just referring to humans. It&#x27;s the same with the title of demigods. Unless the term half-man is added to the title... Verdu has a deep understanding of his pathway, allowing him to understand the current situation.</p>
<p>And this meant that the ritual to help Mr. Door escape could be done by sacrificing demigod monsters of the Seer, Apprentice, and Marauder pathways. Furthermore, there was no need for Verdu to do it himself. The great God of War had already prepared everything.</p>
<p>This made it difficult for Verdu to suppress the joy in his heart. He looked at the Door of Sacrifice and Bestowment light with great anticipation, waiting for the third sacrifice to be spat out.</p>
<p>In just a second, an object flew out from the darkness behind the door and landed on the altar.</p>
<p>It was an unconscious crow. Of course, it only looked like a crow. Its right eye had a ring of black, and its feathers were almost transparent. On it were rings.</p>
<p>A monster corresponding to Parasite... Verdu was delighted as he sincerely praised the great God of War.</p>
