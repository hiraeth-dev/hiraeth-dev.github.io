<p>So that&#x27;s how it is... It&#x27;s no wonder Mrs. Aulisa didn&#x27;t wish to inform Mr. Lawrence of her and her husband&#x27;s death... Now that I think about it, the Abraham family is really pitiful and sad... Fors muttered a few sentences to herself before rummaging and taking out Sights in the Spirit World from a pile of ordinary books, in preparation to copy the first and second chapters.</p>
<p>For this, she entered Cogitation and adjusted her breathing in order to be in her optimal condition. Otherwise, just copying the contents of Sights in the Spirit World would bring her mania and confusion.</p>
<p>The words clearly didn&#x27;t have any spirituality, but once they were combined together to describe those ridiculous scenes, it was as if they had the ability to affect her emotions... I need to take a ﬁve-minute break after every ﬁve minutes of copying it. I don&#x27;t want to lose control because of this... <p>In the Berg household in the City of Silver.</p>
<p>Derrick woke up as a flash of lightning which illuminated everything flashed in his eyes. He looked around, but he didn&#x27;t feel like he was being monitored.</p>
<p>But The Hanged Man&#x27;s precise description of the prior events, through reasoning and his deep confidence, convinced him that his conjecture was correct and that there had to be one or more Beyonders with particular powers that were secretly monitoring him at that very moment.</p>
<p>Besides, Mr. Fool didn&#x27;t deny it... Derrick got out of bed and stretched his body like he usually did.</p>
<p>During this process, the analysis and suggestions, that The Hanged Man had given him, rapidly surfaced in his mind.</p>
<p>&quot;Up till now, no one has come looking for you or has chosen to throw you into the dungeons. <p>Above the gray fog, within the towering, ancient palace.</p>
<p>Klein was staring idly at the stone pillars that supported the dome, feeling that the building he had willed resembled an Athens temple.</p>
<p>Is it because of my own subconscious? his thoughts wandered.</p>
<p>At that moment, the dark red star corresponding to The Sun began to emit light, and it quickly dispersed the ripples to form an illusory and mysterious door. Above the gray fog, a small amount of power was stirred up as it flowed like water.</p>
<p>Upon hearing the stacked pleas, Klein lowered his head and looked at the All-Black Eye placed in front of him. Let&#x27;s hope Little Sun doesn&#x27;t lose this. Otherwise, Mr. World would have to be gloriously sacriﬁced...</p>
<p>It was precisely because he had the All-Black Eye that Klein, who wasn&#x27;t a Marionettist, could secretly control The World as a fake person, allowing him to act as if he was made of flesh and blood.</p>
<p>Once the item was lost, he would have to let The World die to prevent the &quot;magic&quot; from getting exposed.</p>
<p>While sighing, Klein emanated his spirituality, stirring the power of the fog into flowing towards the illusory door, forcefully pushing it open slightly and stabilizing the transmission channel.</p>
<p>Soon after, he placed the All-Black Eye into an iron box and threw it into the gap of the mysterious door. He casually said, &quot;Do not touch the item inside.&quot;</p>
<p>After doing all this, he didn&#x27;t stay any longer, because he knew that Little Sun wouldn&#x27;t be able to find an opportunity to take action that quickly, and he was unsure when Miss Magician would be able to finish copying the two chapters of Sights in the Spirit World.</p>
<p>Relaxing his spirituality, Klein&#x27;s figure disappeared from the space above the gray fog.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>In the Berg household in the City of Silver. As soon as Derrick saw an iron-black box on the altar, he heard Mr. Fool&#x27;s familiar voice: &quot;Do not touch the item inside.&quot;</p>
<p>Do not touch... Derrick made a note of this reminder and decided to carry it out to a tee.</p>
<p>After thanking The Fool and Mr. World and ending the bestowment ritual, he opened the box with extraordinary care and examined the contents.</p>
<p>It was a completely black eye without a pupil. Just looking at it threw his mind into chaos, and his thoughts became sluggish. Faint, indistinct, and seemingly crazy ravings sounded in his ears.</p>
<p>After a sudden shiver ran through his body, Derrick closed the lid of the iron box, picked up a silver dagger, and completely sealed the iron box with a wall of spirituality.</p>
<p>Then, he put the iron box into a secret pocket on the inside of his clothes, fixed the Axe of Hurricane in place and left the room, heading straight for the training field.</p>
<p>The exploratory team members were still under the disguised quarantine.</p>
<p>However, Derrick had no plans to take action today. He carefully followed Mr. Hanged Man&#x27;s instructions and planned to observe the situation first and patiently wait for an opportunity. Of course, if there was a good opportunity, he would have to act decisively.</p>
<p>Upon entering the training field, Derrick circled around the exploratory team&#x27;s area and saw that they were gathered in groups of two or three, whispering to each other, but once they noticed that someone was watching, they would immediately stop and stand there in silence. At times, they would take a few steps, like zombies that had just crawled out of the darkness.</p>
<p>What would they do if I removed the wall of spirituality now and allow them to sense the feelings emanated by Mr. World&#x27;s item? Will they turn their heads at the same time to look at me? Derrick imagined the scene of everyone turning their heads and looking at him with cold and emotionless eyes, and he felt a baffling, indescribable fear.</p>
<p>He drew a breath and told himself to be patient, to wait, and not to panic.</p>

<p>When certain existences were described in detail, even if it was written in text form, it would still affect the reader&#x27;s thoughts and psyche!</p>
<p>Amongst them, the scariest were records related to true gods.</p>
<p>A book explaining a particular god in detail would cause every reader to go crazy or even distort their ideologies. As for Beyonders, there was a high chance of them losing control!</p>

<p>In the first chapter, he described the lights in the spirit world. He believed that the seven rays of pure light of different colors contained the limitless knowledge of different domains, and that no matter where the travelers were in the spirit world, they would be able to see them blanketing higher elevations. What was even more surprising was that these seven rays of light were alive! They were like Spirit Bodies that lingered there!</p>
<p>In the field of mysticism, many of the secret deed rituals were actually aimed at the seven pure lights, and through their secret connections, they allowed one to gain a certain amount of knowledge. Therefore, many mysticism experts called the seven pure lights &quot;master&quot; or &quot;teacher.&quot;</p>
<p>In response to this, the seven pure lights seemed very happy. They had even pulled quite a few spirit world creatures to form a secret organization that specialized in teaching people—the Great White Brotherhood!</p>
