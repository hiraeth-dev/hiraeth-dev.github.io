<p>Black Emperor? The Hanged Man, who was casually listening to Miss Justice, suddenly raised his head. His gaze suddenly became sharp and piercing.</p>
<p>He had just learned that the card in Mr. Fool&#x27;s hand was one of the Cards of Blasphemy created by Emperor Roselle; the name of the card was Black Emperor!</p>
<p>Does this have to do with Mr. Fool? Alger quickly glanced at the end of the long bronze table, before immediately lowering his head.</p>
<p>If it was really done by Mr. Fool&#x27;s Blessed, this deﬁnitely isn&#x27;t a simple matter... Alger listened attentively to Miss Justice&#x27;s subsequent descriptions.</p>
<p>Fors, who had long since learned of this from Xio and had some predictions about it, also gave The Fool a glance. She wanted to see the truth from the concealed entity&#x27;s reaction, but The Fool remained as calm as ever in the thick gray fog.</p>
<p>Audrey, who wasn&#x27;t able to glean anything either, paused for a moment, then continued, &quot;The victim is a tycoon, Capim. Many rumors point to him being the biggest human trafficker in Backlund. <p>&quot;When Capim&#x27;s corpse was discovered, it was covered with a deck of tarot cards. His face was covered with the cards, Judgment and The Emperor.&quot; She didn&#x27;t mention the safe as she found it unimportant.</p>
<p>Covered with tarot cards? Is that the symbol for when our Tarot Club takes action? Wait a minute, a human trafﬁcker? Alger sharply noticed a phrase.</p>
<p>He turned to the end of the long bronze table and humbly asked, &quot;Mr. Fool, is Capim related to the disappearance of the colonial slaves?&quot;</p>
<p>The latter was a mission had been assigned to him by the Church of Storms, and he had formerly targeted a suspect named Baelen and had asked Backlund residents—Justice, The Magician, and The World—to keep an eye out for him.</p>
<p>As a result, upon hearing the word &quot;human trafficker,&quot; Alger intuitively believed that it had something to do with the disappearance of many tribes on the Southern Continent and the flight of many slaves from many islands in the Sonia Sea.</p>
<p>He felt that only a matter such as this, which seemed to hold some secrets, could interest Mr. Fool.</p>
<p>Of course, it cannot be ruled out that it was an independent act by Mr. Fool&#x27;s Blessed. He couldn&#x27;t possibly be waiting for everything to be arranged like a manipulated puppet... Alger thought.</p>
<p>Capim has something to do with the recent disappearance of the colonial slaves? On second thought, that&#x27;s not a possibility that can be eliminated. There were four Beyonders in his villa, and that in itself isn&#x27;t normal in the ﬁrst place... Even if a tycoon like him were to hire Beyonder bodyguards, then he would only be able to get a Sequence 7 and two Sequences 8s or 9s at most. <p>However, he wasn&#x27;t sure about his theory, and he could only disregard The Hanged Man&#x27;s question. He chuckled and said, &quot;My Blessed made some minor contributions in this matter.&quot;</p>
<p>As expected! Audrey&#x27;s eyes shone brightly as she cheered in her heart.</p>
<p>As expected... It&#x27;s the ﬁrst time that I encountered anything to do with the Tarot Club in real life... Fors was half happy and half wistful.</p>
<p>Alger believed that Mr. Fool&#x27;s words were an indirect confirmation of his conjecture.</p>
<p>Capim was deﬁnitely not a simple human trafﬁcker. Otherwise, he wouldn&#x27;t have caused a hidden existence&#x27;s Blessed to take action. Therefore, there was a strong correlation between the human trafﬁcking and the disappearance of the slaves, both were matters that weren&#x27;t simple.</p>
<p>Without waiting for Miss Justice to speak, Klein lightly tapped his finger on the edge of the ancient long table and leisurely added, &quot;He obtained two Beyonder characteristics from this matter and wishes to sell them as quickly as possible. <p>Sheriff, Interrogator... Aren&#x27;t those from the Arbiter pathway which is controlled by the Augustus family? Only members of the royal family, ancient nobles, and the military could possibly acquire them... Capim had one of them backing him? <p>Audrey&#x27;s thoughts were similar to his. She had never thought that the Beyonders at Capim&#x27;s villa would be from the Arbiter pathway.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s impossible that the faction in control of the corresponding formulas would collude with a human trafﬁcker like Capim... Audrey pursed her lips slightly, puzzled and confused.</p>

<p>That&#x27;s not what I expected. Shouldn&#x27;t you directly ask me how much it is? <p>&quot;Mr. Fool, may I answer the question? The corresponding payment shall be paid directly to you.&quot;</p>
