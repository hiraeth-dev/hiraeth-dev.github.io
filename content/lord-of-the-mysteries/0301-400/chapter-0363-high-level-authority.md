<p>The quivering above the gray fog became obvious. Little dots of pure light intertwined and swept toward Klein, blending with his spirituality.</p>
<p>The Sunbird-shaped dark golden brooch began to glow with a dazzling brilliance. It grew brighter and denser, and in an instant, drop after drop of a translucent golden liquid began to condense.</p>
<p>The liquid quickly merged together and formed a human figure that was as tall as Klein. It was a golden figure, a sacred figure!</p>
<p>It does work... Sun Holy Water that&#x27;s created from a higher level of power is closer to a God&#x27;s bestowment! Klein&#x27;s heart was filled with joy. <p>As for the divine figure condensed by the golden Sun Holy Water, it wasn&#x27;t a result of the Sun Brooch, but simply a manifestation of Klein&#x27;s subconscious thoughts. Reaching this step, he had already allowed the golden figure to move forward. Through the illusory crimson star, it merged with Little Sun through the secret deed ritual as it dispelled the &quot;evil spirit&quot; hidden inside his body, as well as giving him a certain amount of knowledge and a miraculous Soul Body experience.</p>

<p>Amon was attempting to use the connection to rip open the boundary and extend his hand into the gray fog!</p>
<p>Whoosh!</p>

<p>Klein&#x27;s pupils shrank. He no longer hesitated, and he picked up the Black Emperor card.</p>
<p>As soon as he held this item in his hand, he immediately felt that his spirituality was no longer subdued by the power of the mysterious space.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the golden figure became abnormally tall, and on its back, it grew pairs of enormous pitch-black wings. There were a total of twelve pairs!</p>
<p>On each pair of wings, there were dark and bright feathers engraved with many mysterious symbols.</p>
<p>The contrast between gold and black was stark, and the huge figure, driven by Klein, spread its wings to cover the vast dome of the towering palace. <p>The light and shadows intersected, and a fierce wind blew in all directions. The palm that was so shriveled that it didn&#x27;t have any flesh, to the point of only having skin and bones left, shrank backward uncontrollably, but it also firmly refused to retreat.</p>
