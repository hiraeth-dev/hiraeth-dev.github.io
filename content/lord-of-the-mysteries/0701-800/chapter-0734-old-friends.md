<p>You actually know me? This means that Mr. Isengard Stanton often mentions me as a friend, or does it mean that the Church of the God of Knowledge and Wisdom knows that I was embroiled in the Great Smog of Backlund? Klein smiled as he nodded in an unperturbed manner.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, I&#x27;m Sherlock Moriarty.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mr. Stanton has been worried about you all this time. He was afraid that you met with trouble. He can now be at peace.&quot;</p>

<p>Klein wasn&#x27;t used to such a custom, so he forced himself to reciprocate it and smile.</p>
<p>&quot;Mr. Stanton, this isn&#x27;t something a believer of Wisdom would do.&quot; The bishops and priests of the God of Knowledge and Wisdom had their pride, and they seldom gave greeting hugs.</p>
<p>But in fact, apart from the boorish Feysac Empire and the liberal Intis Kingdom, such a manner of etiquette was rare in other countries and regions. It only happened among very familiar friends.</p>
<p>Isengard took two steps back and chuckled.</p>
<p>&quot;No, Sherlock. We&#x27;re never stingy with respect and friendliness towards intelligent friends.</p>
<p>&quot;In my heart, you&#x27;re one of the top five detectives in all of Backlund.&quot;</p>
<p>I like that! Klein smiled inwardly as he retorted in jest, &quot;So you&#x27;re one of the top three detectives?&quot;</p>
<p>To be praised as having true wisdom by a Sequence 7 believer of the God of Knowledge and Wisdom was really delighting.</p>
<p>&quot;I wish that you share the same thoughts as myself,&quot; Isengard skillfully and gently replied. Then, he invited him to the living room and to the sofa.</p>
<p>He leaned into a reclining chair and picked up his pipe. With a deep breath, he exhaled.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m very happy that nothing bad happened to you. You seem especially fine, both in body and mind. &quot;How was it? Was Desi Bay fun?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;In fact, I didn&#x27;t go to Desi Bay. I ended up going to Constant. Heh heh, I was previously embroiled in some trouble in Backlund, so I could only find a place to hide.&quot;</p>
<p>Sherlock Moriarty was a gentleman from Midseashire who had a slight accent. It was a very normal choice to return to his hometown after causing trouble. Constant was Midseashire&#x27;s capital.</p>
<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Isengard replied heavily.</p>
<p>He didn&#x27;t inquire about the trouble which Sherlock had involved himself in. Instead, he said with a smile, &quot;In short, welcome back to Backlund. Come to me if you need any help.&quot;</p>
<p>Klein didn&#x27;t stand on ceremony as he immediately said, &quot;The purpose of my visit was first because it&#x27;s really has been a while since we last met, and second, I wish that you can sell my shares in the Backlund Bike Company on my behalf. <p>In order to act as a mysterious tycoon and to repay Miss Messenger with the 10,000 gold coins, not only did he plan on selling items he had little use for, but he also planned on letting go of the last 10% of his shares in the Backlund Bike Company. After all, Sherlock Moriarty wasn&#x27;t able to appear in a legitimate fashion for a long period of time. &quot;Are you really going to sell it?&quot; Isengard stroked his pipe and said, &quot;Although I&#x27;ve never been a businessman, I can tell that the bike is a product that&#x27;s of great value and something that can be promoted on a large scale. <p>&quot;That&#x27;s why a buyer will be very willing to raise the price significantly because of this expected value.&quot; Klein chuckled. &quot;I believe the people who can tell the value of the bike and its future aren&#x27;t in the minority. <p>&quot;The pricing of shares isn&#x27;t about the present, but about its future...&quot; Isengard softly repeated Klein&#x27;s words, and after a moment he sincerely sighed. &quot;Sherlock, perhaps you should be involved in the business world. <p>&quot;To dare to take risks is equivalent to chivalry in business. Oh well, I admit that I&#x27;ve recently been in dire need of large sums of cash,&quot; Klein replied with a smile.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;ve convinced me. &quot;I will specially hire a lawyer and accountant to confirm the market value of Backlund Bike Company.
