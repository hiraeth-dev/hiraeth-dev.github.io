<p>&quot;How would that be possible? No one would dare to claim it on his behalf!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s right—unless they wish to bear the wrath of the Church of Storms or selling out Gehrman Sparrow!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Haha, wouldn&#x27;t you be enjoying yourself in the Red Theater for half a year first?&quot;</p>

<p>... As the adventurers conversed, they began imagining their lives after obtaining 42,000 pounds. They even had a conflict of opinions and began arguing with flushed faces.</p>
<p>No way... Are they implying that Gehrman has ﬁnished off Admiral of Blood? No, although that madman always had the intention of doing so, he lacks the required support that he needs. He needs to work with Captain... Anderson Hood? Danitz stood up, pressed down his cap, and kept his head down. <p>What did that madman do? He really ﬁnished off Admiral of Blood? It&#x27;s only been a few months, and his strength has risen to such a level? Furthermore, the papers didn&#x27;t even mention Anderson Hood... Danitz was alarmed and thankful that he wisely chose to submit in front of Gehrman Sparrow. <p>No, no. Back then, my death wouldn&#x27;t have been published... Man, Gehrman Sparrow is really a member of a cult... As he thought, Danitz suddenly froze like a statue.</p>
<p>That was because he was apparently, probably, likely a member of that cult...</p>
<p>Haha, the Church and military often likes to exaggerate. Yes, it&#x27;s a secret organization, not a cult! Danitz consoled himself before having the feeling that the organization backing Gehrman Sparrow was surprisingly mysterious and abnormally powerful.</p>
<p>The successful hunting of Senor, one of the Seven Pirate Admirals, was evidence!</p>
<p>Phew... Danitz exhaled as he apprehensively praised The Fool inwardly, expressing his desires to handle matters seriously.</p>

<p>Thankfully, Elland had never hidden anything from the beginning. He had reported to his superiors that Gehrman Sparrow was of unknown origins, but that he was friendly to the military. <p>As for Oz Kent, there weren&#x27;t any problems at all. He had followed regular protocol when claiming the bounty for Gehrman Sparrow.</p>
