<p>&quot;Yes, it&#x27;s rumored to be hidden somewhere in the Berserk Sea...&quot;</p>

<p>Hazel was clearly interested in these as she responded to Klein in a rare instance. She would even occasionally ask other questions, making the dance between them less awkward. Without realizing it, their dance came to an end.</p>
<p>Klein skillfully ended the topic and switched to asking, &quot;Do you plan on returning to where you were, or do you plan on heading over to get some food?&quot;</p>
<p>After a dance, the gentleman had to abide by the lady&#x27;s wishes and send her to where she wished to go. It didn&#x27;t have to be where she originally stood. Hazel opened her mouth as though she wished to ask further, but she ultimately didn&#x27;t speak further. She nodded her head in a reserved manner, &quot;Where I was.&quot;</p>
<p>Hehe, she clearly misses the stories at sea... She&#x27;s just a big brat. As long as you grasp her temper and ﬁnd what interests her, she&#x27;s actually not difﬁcult to interact with... <p>Compared to the dance and entertaining others, food is the true essence of a gathering... As Klein thought, he worked hard at trying to make his appearance while eating appear elegant enough.</p>
<p>At this moment, he saw Ma&#x27;am Mary walk over and fork a piece of foie gras soaked in red wine onto her plate.</p>

<p>&quot;How may I address you? <p>Klein laughed and replied, &quot;I&#x27;m a merchant who just returned from Desi Bay, Dwayne Dantès. I live on this street. &quot;Ma&#x27;am, do I have the honor of knowing your name?&quot;</p>

<p>Mary Schott. She has taken up her original last name? Right, she has already divorced... Klein silently thought to himself and said with a smile, &quot;I know of this company. <p>Heh heh, her attitude towards Sherlock Moriarty and Dwayne Dantès is very different...
