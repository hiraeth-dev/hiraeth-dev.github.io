<p>Inside the ancient palace standing above the gray fog, Alger wasn&#x27;t in a rush to mention the matter regarding Crazy Captain Connors Viktor. He turned his head to look at The Sun.</p>
<p>&quot;Are you still in Afternoon Town?&quot;</p>
<p>Derrick nodded honestly.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, we took a very long time to establish a camp. Later, with small teams, we wiped out the remnant monsters, one street at a time, in search of any ancient ruins that might still exist. Although this process is slow, it&#x27;s sufficiently safe.&quot;</p>
<p>His answer was extremely detailed, and it gave the impression that Mr. Hanged Man could ask more questions if he wished to know more.</p>
<p>Audrey listened with interest as she raised her hand slightly.</p>
<p>&quot;With all of you staying in Afternoon Town for so long, what do you rely on for food? Is there any Black-Faced Grass nearby?&quot;</p>
<p>She had long heard from Little Sun that the City of Silver relied on the Black-Faced Grass that they grew around the city as their source of sustenance.</p>
<p>&quot;We carried a portion of powdered Black-Faced Grass, but the main source of food comes from hunting those monsters. Once the Beyonder characteristic is expelled, a large proportion of them can be eaten once we skin them and roast them with fire. However, there&#x27;s a certain degree of corruption in the meat, which will result in psychological problems. We can&#x27;t continuously consume the meat, and we will need to spread out the intervals,&quot; Derrick replied in a serious manner to Miss Justice&#x27;s question.</p>
<p>Audrey recalled the few monsters in the depths of the darkness mentioned by Little Sun, and having terrifying vibes about the dark and eerie area around the City of Silver, she couldn&#x27;t help but press further, &quot;Wouldn&#x27;t that be very disgusting?&quot;</p>
<p>Fors, who was attentively listening to the other members&#x27; conversation, was inspired by this question. She deliberately didn&#x27;t look at Emlyn as she asked Derrick with piqued curiosity, &quot;I remember you mentioning that monsters in the depths of the darkness include Vampires. <p>Emlyn, who heard the question, forgot to correct her by informing her that Sanguine weren&#x27;t Vampires. His expression turned livid as he felt an inexplicable feeling of indescribable horror.</p>
<p>Derrick was silent for two seconds before saying, &quot;There are some monsters that are indeed disgusting—very disgusting. Just like the Vampires as mentioned by Miss Magician, but we have no choice. As long as it&#x27;s edible, we will eat it.&quot;</p>
<p>His voice gradually turned heavy, as though he once again deeply felt the tragedy that was the curse which enveloped the City of Silver.</p>
<p>The palace that looked like a giant&#x27;s residence fell silent once again. Even Emlyn, who wanted to retort Derrick, didn&#x27;t say a word. All he did was curl his lips and retract his arm.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, The World broke the silence with a chuckle.</p>
<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s return to the sea. There have been certain matters that require your attention. Crazy Captain Connors Viktor&#x27;s Single-eyed Skull drifted to a nearby harbor after a storm. The masts were snapped, and there were charred marks. The entire crew, including Connors Viktor, died. No one survived.</p>
<p>Justice and company didn&#x27;t know who Crazy Captain was. They weren&#x27;t too interested in the news, other than being curious as to what caused this destructive naval tragedy.</p>
<p>On the other hand, Alger shared completely different thoughts from them.</p>
<p>There must be a tremendous secret if The World is paying attention to the matter regarding Crazy Captain! It&#x27;s no wonder His Eminence Kottman issued such an important command to get the Mandated Punisher and captains to investigate the situation! Alger&#x27;s eyes darted about slightly as he said after some thought, &quot;In Bayam, the Church of Storms is actively investigating the people and matters surrounding Crazy Captain Connors Viktor.&quot;</p>
<p>Heh, there&#x27;s no need for me to commission it... Klein chuckled inwardly and got The World to directly ask, &quot;What clues does the Church of Storms possess?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I have no idea. I&#x27;ll try my best to understand the situation.&quot; Alger frankly shook his head.</p>
<p>He believed that The World understood what he was implying— that he wasn&#x27;t exactly sure, but he would sound out what the Church knew.</p>
<p>Audrey, who was listening out of curiosity, realized that the matter regarding Crazy Captain wasn&#x27;t simple. Hence, she asked probingly, &quot;Mr. World, isn&#x27;t this common infighting among pirates?&quot;</p>

<p>Behind the Great Smog of Backlund and the disappearances, there might be a particular faction from the royal family. It also included some military personnel. And Miss Justice would lean towards the nobles and royal family both her emotions and from her standpoint. I would subtly change the direction in which her standpoint and emotions are directed towards. For this, I can bear the burden that some of the information regarding The World is leaked... Klein thought for a moment and made The World say with a relaxed, but mocking tone, &quot;Crazy Captain is related to the disappearance of the colonial slaves which Mr. Hanged Man was previously investigating.</p>
<p>&quot;The Demoness Sect takes the people who are tricked or abducted and hands them over to Vice Admiral Ailment Tracy, and they have them ship the people over to the Northern Continent&#x27;s East Sea&#x27;s coast. This pirate is intricately tied with many human traffickers and slave merchants in Loen. &quot;Back when I informed Mr. Hanged Man of the news in January, someone witnessed Baelen, who was involved in the disappearance cases, with a MI9 member who is loyal to the royal family.</p>
<p>&quot;Also, Backlund&#x27;s largest human trafficker, Capim, had guards from the Arbiter pathway.</p>
<p>&quot;With so many matters compounded together, do you think that Crazy Captain&#x27;s death is simply a result of pirate infighting?</p>
