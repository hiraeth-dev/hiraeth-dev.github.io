<p>How enviable it is for Miss Justice to always maintain a cheerful mood. I wish I could be like that... Listening to her lively greetings, Klein couldn&#x27;t help but sigh wistfully.</p>
<p>He then recalled the time when she had taken out a thousand pounds so easily, and he realized that it would be very difficult if he wanted to maintain his cheerful emotions like Miss Justice.</p>
<p>The Sun, Derrick Berg, was a young man that cared a lot about his reputation. He immediately replied, &quot;I have obtained the Telepathist formula.&quot;</p>
<p>For the past week, he had been settling the inheritance that his parents had left him. Besides the property, furniture, and a few sentimental items, the rest of the valuable items were brought to the black market in the City of Silver in exchange for the Telepathist formula and the Bard potion&#x27;s ingredients. His meals were now rationed.</p>
<p>However, he believed that the situation wouldn&#x27;t be for long. When he passes the combat examination, he would then join the team that cleansed the Things of the Dark in the outskirts of the city and be paid a decent amount.</p>
<p>When I become stronger, I&#x27;ll apply to become a member of the elite squad, to explore the depths of the dark and ﬁnd a way to remove the curse... Derrick thought with hope as he looked towards The Fool who was engulfed in the fog.</p>
<p>He noticed that last time after Miss Justice made a request to Mr. Fool, she was able to produce a page of the unknown Roselle diary out of thin air!</p>
<p>Although Derrick didn&#x27;t quite understand what had happened, he felt that it&#x27;d be better if he watched Mr. Fool.</p>
<p>&quot;First, recall the formula in your head. Then, grab the pen by your side and instill it with the strong desire to express your thoughts.&quot; Klein casually leaned back in his high chair.</p>
<p>As The Sun was from the City of Silver, which might be the Forsaken Land of God, the pen that instantiated before him wasn&#x27;t a fountain pen, but a quill instead.</p>
<p>Of course, there still wasn&#x27;t any ink.</p>
<p>Derrick didn&#x27;t dare to doubt what The Fool said, so he immediately grabbed the quill that suddenly appeared by his hand.</p>
<p>He followed The Fool&#x27;s instruction, and as expected, he saw the Telepathist potion formula appear on the brown goatskin parchment before him within seconds.</p>
<p>After looking through it twice, Derrick silently pushed the promised item towards Miss Justice.</p>
<p>Audrey was overjoyed and eager, but she took the parchment gracefully. She glanced at the page, and the words that Klein had translated came into sight.</p>
<p>&quot;Main ingredients: Phantom Netherdrake&#x27;s complete pituitary gland, 10 ml of Half Specter Rabbit spinal fluid. &quot;Supplementary ingredients...&quot;</p>
<p>Main ingredients that I&#x27;ve never heard of... Hmm, I don&#x27;t know enough. Audrey, who had been trying to learn more about the different types and names of Beyonder ingredients from Fors and Xio, seemed to fret in thought.</p>
<p>During such moments, she would completely forget how a Spectator should behave.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Audrey heard a light rapping sound. She quickly looked towards the seat of honor at the long bronze table subconsciously.</p>
<p>She was surprised to see Mr. Fool rapping at the edge of the table with his right index finger while he gestured to her with a nod.</p>
<p>What&#x27;s going on? Audrey was confused as her eyes wore a vacant look.</p>
<p>Just when she was about to ask, the corner of her eyes suddenly saw some changes on the Telepathist formula. There were remarks next to some of the ingredients:</p>
<p>&quot;Main ingredients, Phantom Netherdrake&#x27;s complete pituitary gland (also known as Rainbow Salamander), 10 ml of Half Specter Rabbit (also known as Farsman Rabbit) spinal fluid.</p>
<p>&quot;Supplementary ingredients...&quot;</p>
<p>I know all of these! Audrey was stunned at first, then there was an intense surge of delight from the bottom of her heart. &quot;Thank you, Mr. Fool. You&#x27;re really very knowledgeable.&quot; She looked towards the seat of honor as she thanked and sincerely complimented him.</p>
<p>The Hanged Man Alger didn&#x27;t know what had happened, but he felt extreme contempt towards what Justice had said.</p>
<p>How could you describe a godlike ﬁgure with the word &quot;knowledgeable&quot;?</p>
<p>His existence alone is equivalent to knowledge itself to a certain extent!</p>
<p>Klein accepted Miss Justice&#x27;s compliments without any misgivings because this wasn&#x27;t something he could&#x27;ve done just because he had chanced upon the Psychology Alchemists&#x27; Telepathist formula.</p>
<p>After he pulled The Sun into the Tarot Club, he had been taking precautions against such problems by taking into consideration The Sun&#x27;s special circumstances of being from the City of Silver. He had been constantly studying ancient terminology. Therefore, even if Daxter Guderian hadn&#x27;t managed to get the formula in time, he could&#x27;ve made the notations easily. Through prior divination and comparison, he had made certain that both Telepathist formulas were accurate.</p>
<p>This is why we say, &quot;Preparedness averts peril...&quot; Klein thought smugly.</p>
<p>Audrey looked at the Telepathist formula a few times and then reined in her gaze unwillingly. She then personally expressed Roselle&#x27;s diary onto a page. &quot;You deserve this.&quot; She put down her pen and looked towards the fog-engulfed Fool. &quot;In addition to this, I&#x27;ll give your Blessed another 300. Is 300 pounds okay?&quot;</p>
<p>She sounded a little guilty because the three pages of Roselle&#x27;s diary only cost her twenty pounds, while the Sheriff formula at Sequence 8 required 450 pounds.</p>
<p>In other words, from the perspective of simple math, she had to pay another 430 pounds on top of the three pages of the diary.</p>
<p>However, Audrey felt that it was thanks to her luck that the seller didn&#x27;t know the value of Roselle&#x27;s diary. It allowed her to buy it at a low price.</p>
<p>Emperor Roselle&#x27;s diary costs at least ﬁfty pounds per page! Audrey held her fist and encouraged herself.</p>
<p>300 pounds? Until today, I&#x27;ve only seen that much money at Sir Deweyville&#x27;s place... Klein sighed and pretended to not be interested in money as he nodded and said, &quot;A reasonable deal.</p>
<p>&quot;This is my Blessed&#x27;s information.&quot;</p>
<p>He avoided speaking of terms like &quot;Backlund Bank&quot; and &quot;anonymous account&quot; verbally through The Fool&#x27;s mouth as they damaged his image. He made them appear on the parchment before her.</p>
<p>Klein had taken time to visit Backlund Bank&#x27;s Tingen branch last Wednesday while investigating the houses with red chimneys. He had disguised himself and opened an anonymous bank account. The account only required one to memorize his account number and the corresponding password in order to withdraw cash from any Backlund Bank branch.</p>
<p>If one found that it wasn&#x27;t secure enough, he could also request to add in a signature and thumbprint verification. But that would be more troublesome.</p>
<p>In order to keep his identity secret, Klein left it with a password.</p>
<p>The password is written in ancient Hermes: &quot;The Fool that doesn&#x27;t belong to this era, the mysterious ruler above the gray fog; the King of Yellow and Black who wields good luck.&quot;</p>
<p>As ancient Hermes itself can be used for rituals and prayers, anyone who dares to copy the password would be reciting my name. Then, I&#x27;ll immediately receive a signal, and can simply ﬁnd out who&#x27;s the one trying to steal my wealth from the world above the gray fog! <p>The only downfall was that it would slightly expose the existence of The Fool, but the risk was within an acceptable range.</p>
<p>Audrey pushed the diary page to Mr. Fool as she took the parchment with the information of The Fool&#x27;s Blessed. Recorded on it, was the Backlund Bank and a bunch of numbers that formed the anonymous bank account.</p>
<p>I wonder if Mr. Fool&#x27;s Blessed is a male or female, and which Sequence he or she is... Hmm, he must be very powerful, at least not weaker than Vice Admiral Hurricane Qilangos... <p>&quot;It doesn&#x27;t have to be so troublesome.&quot; Just then, she heard The Fool&#x27;s low yet gentle voice. &quot;When you get home, recite my name and you&#x27;ll be able to write down the information directly.&quot;</p>

<p>That works too? From Mr. Fool&#x27;s words, he appears very conﬁdent. He lives up to his status of a godlike ﬁgure if he can even do this... Audrey was stunned at first before coming to the realization that everything seemed to make logical sense.</p>

<p>The &quot;acting method&quot; is taught in general education classes... Audrey stole a glance at The Hanged Man and realized that he was looking back at her. The two of them suddenly fell silent.</p>
<p>The origin of The Sun is indeed mysterious. I wonder where Mr. Fool pulled him into the Tarot Club from... The more I think about it, the more I revere him... Audrey settled down and looked at The Fool who didn&#x27;t look visibly surprised in any way.</p>

<p>As expected... We&#x27;re using an ingenious method to go around obstacles and completely break down the remnant spirits in the potion, without submitting to it...
