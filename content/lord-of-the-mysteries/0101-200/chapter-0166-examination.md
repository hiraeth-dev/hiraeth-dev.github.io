<p>There was a cold breeze blowing from the basement, providing a hint of relief for Klein&#x27;s tense emotions.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s ﬁnally here.</p>
<p>Once I pass this stage, I won&#x27;t have to worry about being examined like this for at least half a year...</p>
<p>Once I advance to Sequence 8 and become a so-called &quot;Clown,&quot; I&#x27;ll possess actual combat strength. With the aid of divination and my Flaring Sun Charms for backup, I&#x27;ll have a chance of surviving even more relatively dangerous situations...</p>
<p>Since I was waiting for the Holy Cathedral&#x27;s examination, I haven&#x27;t even dared to withdraw the three hundred pounds that Miss Justice transferred to the anonymous account. Just in case they audit my ﬁnancial situation and ﬁnd out that I&#x27;m in possession of a large sum of money from an unknown source...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Just as Klein&#x27;s thoughts flashed through his mind uncontrollably, Dunn Smith smoothed his sleeve and said in a low voice, &quot;The person in charge of the examination is one of the nine high-ranking deacons of the Nighthawks, Crestet Cesimir. The Holy Cathedral attaches great importance to you.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;A high-ranking deacon?&quot; Klein blurted in surprise. In general terms, the thirteen archbishops and nine high-ranking deacons made up the upper echelons of the church. It was said that there was no lack of High-Sequence Beyonders among them!</p>
<p>The twenty-two ladies and gentlemen were all equal in terms of their rankings. They only followed the orders of the Evernight Goddess, and they were only answerable to the Pope.</p>
<p>Dunn took a whiff of the cold wind from the basement before nodding faintly.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, he&#x27;s a high-ranking deacon. But you don&#x27;t have to be nervous. Crestet is only a Sequence 5 and has yet to enter a demigod state. So, you don&#x27;t have to be too afraid or reverent.</p>
<p>&quot;Oh, his title in the Beyonder world is the ‘Goddess&#x27;s Sword.&#x27; As he possesses a holy item, his combat strength is similar to a newly advanced Sequence 4 Beyonder.</p>
<p>&quot;I just chatted with him. He was very friendly.&quot;</p>
<p>If I read between the lines, Captain is telling me that he only said what was necessary. He doesn&#x27;t want me to be nervous and go according to the plan... Klein nodded thoughtfully and asked, &quot;Where should I met the high-ranking deacon?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The alchemy room where we concoct potions,&quot; Dunn replied simply, as a hint of gloominess flashed across his face.</p>
<p>The alchemy room where we concoct potions? The laboratory where Old Neil made my Seer potion? Klein slowly let out a breath and returned to the Nighthawks recreation room and took his outerwear from the clothes rack.</p>
<p>He put on the black trench coat, placed his hands into his pockets, and walked down the winding stairs that connected to the basement. Then, he took a left turn at the cross junction.</p>
<p>Very quickly, Klein saw a secret door under the light of the elegant gas lamps that lined the walls. He saw that the long tables in the room had been moved aside to open up a large space in the center of the room.</p>
<p>There were two classic high back chairs facing each other with less than a meter in-between them.</p>
<p>There was a man in his thirties wearing a black trench coat and a white shirt seated on the chair that was facing the door.</p>
<p>His golden-brown hair was cut very short, and his blackish-green eyes were as dark as a forest on a moonless night. The collars of his shirt and trench coat were put up, and his entire chin was hidden within the shadows.</p>
<p>&quot;Hello, Reverend Cesimir.&quot; Klein bowed.</p>
<p>Crestet Cesimir had his right leg crossed over his left as he leisurely leaned back into his chair. He smiled and replied, &quot;Hello, Klein. You may sit over there.&quot;</p>
<p>He pointed at the high back chair opposite him.</p>
<p>By the side of his leg was a suitcase made of silver. It was about the size of a violin case. It can carry a sword with an appropriate length... Klein walked forward and sat at his appointed seat.</p>
<p>Crestet rested his right index finger on his upper lip as he thought for a few seconds.</p>
<p>&quot;I plan to first examine how well you&#x27;ve mastered your potion. That&#x27;s not a problem, right?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Not at all.&quot; Klein shook his head with utmost confidence.</p>
<p>&quot;Very confident.&quot; Crestet smiled but maintained his previous posture. All he did was intently watch Klein.</p>
<p>Klein suddenly felt the light from the surrounding gas lamps vanish, as though they were swallowed by the rich darkness.</p>
<p>He suddenly became exhausted, as though his biological clock had struck the time for sleep.</p>
<p>But, his mind was extremely tense, making it impossible to relax. It was just like when he was unable to sleep peacefully due to over-exhaustion.</p>
<p>The silent &quot;night&quot; filled his surroundings as Klein heard the noise of dripping water from a tap that wasn&#x27;t closed properly. Then, he heard the conversations in the Blackthorn Security Company and the movement of the wind blowing through the stairway.</p>
<p>Besides that, he didn&#x27;t see anything that he shouldn&#x27;t see, nor did he hear any noises that he shouldn&#x27;t be able to hear. &quot;Excellent.&quot; Crestet&#x27;s hypnotic voice dispersed the darkness, and the light from the gas lamps inside and outside the alchemy room came into Klein&#x27;s sight again.</p>
<p>Klein suddenly shook off his exhaustion and returned to his previous energetic self.</p>
<p>He affected me without me realizing it... Is that what a Sequence 5 Beyonder is capable of? This is the horror of a high-ranking deacon? He recalled what had happened and felt a little frightened.</p>
<p>Crestet Cesimir clasped his hands and put them on his knees. He bent down slightly, and his lips were blocked by his collar.</p>
<p>&quot;You passed the test. You achieved a level beyond outstanding in the mastery of your potion.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll need to observe to see if there are any hidden dangers in your mind, to make sure that the potion&#x27;s remaining spirit hasn&#x27;t changed your character subconsciously or left some problems behind.</p>
<p>&quot;You have three minutes to prepare.&quot;</p>
<p>Klein immediately nodded and said, &quot;Alright.&quot;</p>
<p>He secretly took a breath and allowed himself to enter Cogitation to remove various negative thoughts.</p>
<p>Crestet didn&#x27;t speak again. He took out a silver pocket watch from the inner pocket of his black trench coat and flipped it open.</p>
<p>Then, he attentively watched the second hand move.</p>
<p>Three minutes later, Crestet closed his pocket watch and said with a smile, &quot;I&#x27;ll begin singing.&quot;</p>
<p>Singing? Klein wore a look of confusion.</p>
<p>Before Klein could reply, Crestet started humming a lovely melody.</p>
<p>The melody reverberated in the alchemy room and gradually lost its harmony and went out of tune.</p>
<p>Squeak! Scratch! Zing! Klein heard the noise akin to the scratching of blackboards with nails, the sound of bubble wrap rubbing against each other, electric drills drilling, and various other annoying noises.</p>
<p>The noises intensified and turned more and more chaotic. They made him want to vent his frustrations and cause destruction.</p>
<p>But Klein, who frequently experienced the mad ravings and terrifying screams, restrained his urges very quickly.</p>
<p>He displayed annoyance, tension, frustration, and insecurity at appropriate times.</p>
<p>Being in too perfect of a state would end up being a problem! It was unknown when Crestet Cesimir had stopped singing. The noises in the alchemy room disappeared and room the was awash with tranquility and silence.</p>
<p>Silence sure is great! Klein exclaimed in his head.</p>
<p>&quot;Very good, excellent. There are no latent problems in your soul. Of course, if you wanted to beat me up or stuff my mouth with something, that&#x27;s only normal.&quot; Crestet&#x27;s mouth was blocked by his collar so Klein could only determine his emotions through his tone.</p>
<p>&quot;No, I wouldn&#x27;t dare,&quot; Klein admitted honestly.</p>
<p>Crestet smiled and said, &quot;Congratulations, you have passed all the tests. Now it&#x27;s time for the question and answer session.&quot;</p>
<p>His green eyes suddenly darkened. His gaze was deep, as though he could see through flesh, and looked directly at the spirit.</p>
<p>&quot;Go ahead,&quot; Klein replied, sitting straight.</p>
<p>Crestet maintained his earlier posture and casually asked, &quot;You said that your experience in the Divination Club allowed you to quickly master the potion?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; Klein answered frankly but didn&#x27;t describe further.</p>
<p>Crestet nodded slightly and said, &quot;And you said that your inspiration came from the maxim of the Mystery Pryers and also Daly&#x27;s example?&quot; &quot;Yes.&quot; Klein confirmed this first before explaining in detail, &quot;I found out from one of my teammates who was a Mystery Pryer that those who abide by the maxim of the Mystery Pryers have a lower probability of losing control than normal. After that, I heard that Madam Daly once said that she wanted to be a real Spirit Medium and that she is a genius that leveled up to Sequence 7 within two years.</p>
<p>&quot;After noticing both situations, I thought I could give it a try, I attempted to be a real Seer and outlined some principles for a Seer. The outcome was better than I expected. I mastered the potion very quickly. Reverend Cesimir, I&#x27;m not sure if you have had a similar experience. When I fully mastered the potion, there was a very special, very magical feeling...&quot; Klein described his experience as if he only vaguely understood the &quot;acting method.&quot;</p>
<p>The man he had been when he was on earth would&#x27;ve been nervous and embarrassed to speak so many half-lies before such a powerful Nighthawk. But ever since he transmigrated to the current world, he had lied so much that he was used to it. He could do it flawlessly.</p>
<p>The darkness in Crestet&#x27;s eyes disappeared, and his gaze returned to normal. He smiled and said, &quot;Don&#x27;t worry, it&#x27;s not an illusion.&quot;</p>
<p>From his answer, Klein couldn&#x27;t see any doubt or scrutiny, so he felt at ease.</p>
<p>&quot;Dunn endorsed your experience. I believe that you really are a genius, with a logical mind and sharp senses,&quot; Crestet complimented. He then asked, &quot;Did you share your experience with your teammates?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Of course,&quot; Klein admitted frankly. &quot;I hope that I can help them lower the risk of losing control. We&#x27;re teammates, comrades that face danger together. I don&#x27;t have any reason to hide the truth. But for the same reason, I didn&#x27;t tell the clerks.&quot;</p>
<p>Crestet uncrossed his right leg and sat up straight. His thin lips were exposed from the shadow of his collar.</p>
<p>He lifted the corner of his lips and said, &quot;Although you haven&#x27;t even been with the Nighthawks for two months, I believe that your understanding of partners is much better than many others.</p>
<p>&quot;Hmm, I plan to share more information with you, but according to the Holy Cathedral&#x27;s rules, you have to swear to the Goddess that you won&#x27;t reveal the contents of our conversation to anyone that doesn&#x27;t know about this.</p>
<p>&quot;That should be fine, right?&quot;</p>
<p>I passed the test? Klein was delighted. He nodded without hesitation.</p>
<p>&quot;No problem!&quot;</p>
<p>Although I won&#x27;t be able to teach others the &quot;acting method,&quot; I can let Miss Justice and Mr. Hanged Man do so indirectly!</p>
