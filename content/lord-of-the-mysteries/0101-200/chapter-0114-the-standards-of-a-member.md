<p>No crude oil? It couldn&#x27;t be found for some reason, or there really wasn&#x27;t any available?</p>
<p>From the period Emperor Roselle was assassinated till this day, about a hundred and fifty years have passed, and there are still no traces of crude oil...</p>
<p>Klein&#x27;s pupils constricted as his hand quivered while holding the diary.</p>
<p>No crude oil not only meant that the future of the internal combustion engine became uncertain, it would also lead to a state of stagnation in the chemical industry. In other words, Earth&#x27;s modern industrial age would never transpire here!</p>
<p>In short, the development of this world was uncertain to Klein.</p>
<p>Although he couldn&#x27;t invent things, he had assumed that he was still at an advantage because he knew a bit of everything and could foresee the direction of technological development. When he saved enough money, he could make a risky investment on an industry that he thought showed promise. Furthermore, he wouldn&#x27;t put all his eggs in one basket.</p>
<p>Klein thought that it was only a matter of time until he could own enormous wealth. By then, he would hire the so-called white gloves as representatives to establish international charity foundations. On the surface, they would provide relief to the poor. In reality, they would actually be establishing and funding a revolt, in order to fight against the higher strata of society and enhance the living standards of the people in the lower class. If he were to find a method of returning to Earth, he would segregate his property. A third to Benson, a third to Melissa, and a third for his foundation.</p>
<p>However, it was a pity that his perfect vision of the future was instantly half-shattered.</p>
<p>Luckily, there&#x27;s still electricity and magnetism in this world. The telegram is a successful example, I should mainly invest in this in the future... Klein settled down and read down row by row.</p>
<p>&quot;21st December. I&#x27;m no longer thinking about crude oil anymore. Upgrading my Sequence level is what matters!</p>
<p>&quot;22nd December. The filthy environment in Richeux Borough is unacceptable. If I hadn&#x27;t visited incognito, I might&#x27;ve never known that it still looks the same as when I was young. I want to gather all my ministers and formulate a ‘Capital Sewer and Public Toilet Enhancement Plan&#x27;. Hmm, I have to rectify the people&#x27;s bad habits. Let them boil hot water for consumption, wash their hands and faces frequently, don&#x27;t litter, don&#x27;t pee and poop anywhere, use condoms if possible... Haha, I thought of what to name this campaign: the Patriotic Health Campaign!</p>
<p>&quot;Hence, the invention of the condom has to be brought forward. There&#x27;s also masks, paper cups, and others. Yes, even the most primitive version would do. Give it a try. I have to thank this world for they still have rubber trees.</p>
<p>&quot;23rd December. Perhaps I should consider that suggestion. Keep a back door for myself outside of the Church of the God of Craftsmanship. For example, I could join that ancient and mysterious organization which influences the world from the shadows?&quot; Klein then suddenly realized that there was nothing else at the bottom. His emotions were indescribable.</p>
<p>Emperor Roselle, what was the name of the ancient and mysterious organization that was inﬂuencing the world from the shadows? Do I know it?</p>
<p>How could you stop here? Why didn&#x27;t you write more?</p>
<p>It&#x27;s just like when I used to read novels. When I read till the end and realized that the author ended up dropping the novel...</p>
<p>And Patriotic Health Campaign? The Emperor sure knows how to have fun...</p>
<p>The contents of the diary should&#x27;ve been written after he became the Consul of the Intis Republic. He might already have called himself the Emperor Caesar.</p>
<p>I have to read some books when I get back and ﬂip through some historical texts of other countries. I have to see which year the ‘Capital Sewer and Public Toilet Enhancement Plan&#x27; took place.</p>
<p>After his nearly twenty seconds of silence, Klein reined back his thoughts and let the diary in his hands vanish into thin air.</p>
<p>&quot;You can start your discussion now.&quot;</p>
<p>Audrey let out a breath of relief and adjusted her state to become a Spectator. She smiled faintly and said, &quot;I&#x27;d like to know if there are any Sequence potion named Arbiter, or a kind of Beyonder that can go through wooden doors or make locks ineffective?&quot; I know about this... Enveloped in the grayish-white fog, Klein was going to reply, but The Hanged Man answered first.</p>
<p>&quot;I need you to help me investigate something in return for the answer.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What is it?&quot; Audrey asked with interest as well as with puzzlement.</p>
<p>Alger glanced towards The Fool and said, &quot;I&#x27;d like to know if the King has the intention of taking revenge on the Feysac Empire and launching a new war on the East coast of Balam within this year or before June of next year.&quot;</p>
<p>The Tarot Club was currently using the Loen language, which was confirmed by the trio&#x27;s accents at the first Gathering. Hence, Alger knew that Miss Justice was a noble in the Loen Kingdom while he also believed that Miss Justice knew that he was a Loen.</p>
<p>As for The Fool, Alger believed that His behavior as a Loen was merely a disguise, a disguise that would ease the discussion.</p>
<p>Ever since the ritualistic magic, Alger started using ‘Him&#x27; to address The Fool politely.</p>
<p>Audrey recalled everything that she heard from various social events. She nodded confidently and said, &quot;No problem, but I would need sufficient time to be certain.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I can wait.&quot; Alger smiled and said, &quot;With Mr. Fool as a witness, I believe you wouldn&#x27;t go back on your promise.&quot; Audrey looked towards the quiet yet mysterious Fool engulfed in gray fog as the corner of her mouth curved upwards.</p>
<p>&quot;But I think the value of this information is worth more than both questions put together.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;When you confirm the answer, I&#x27;ll provide compensation depending on the situation,&quot; Alger replied with an answer he prepared beforehand.</p>
<p>Miss Justice, Mr. Hanged Man, do you need virtual currency to determine value? Klein smiled and leaned backwards while he looked at the two people before him.</p>
<p>Audrey relaxed and cheered for herself in her mind.</p>
<p>Well done! Audrey, you learned how to negotiate! She was so excited that she nearly broke out of her Spectator state. She quickly thought of something and asked, &quot;Oh right, Mr. Hanged Man, did you receive the one thousand pounds?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m sorry, I&#x27;m still sailing. I have yet to return to land.&quot; Alger wasn&#x27;t willing to bring it up. He answered her original question, &quot;The Beyonder that can go through wooden doors and foil locks would probably be Sequence Apprentice. The secret organization, Theosophy Order, has its formula. However, don&#x27;t ignore the possibility that it was obtained through other channels, such as an ancient tomb of the Fourth Epoch.&quot;</p>
<p>The Theosophy Order, the secret organization that has countless ties with the Demoness Sect... Klein rubbed his chin with his finger leisurely. Seeing that Mr. Fool didn&#x27;t refute what was said, Audrey couldn&#x27;t help but sigh.</p>
<p>&quot;If I had found the formula for Apprentice before, I might not have opted for Spectator.&quot;</p>
<p>The performance was simply outstanding!</p>
<p>Alger didn&#x27;t bother with Miss Justice&#x27;s remark but continued his explanation, &quot;There is also a Sequence potion that is entitled Arbiter. I think you should be familiar with it, because it is the Sequence pathway that the Augustus and Feynapotter Kingdom&#x27;s Castiya family has. Of course, the low Sequence formulas were used as rewards in ancient times. Some nobles might have received it before.&quot;</p>
<p>The Augustus family was a royal family of the Loen Kingdom while the Castiya family was a royal family in the Feynapotter Kingdom.</p>
<p>It turns out that the Augustus family are all Arbiters... Audrey was enlightened and felt that it cleared up her suspicion.</p>
<p>She sighed and thought, It&#x27;s no wonder I&#x27;ve always gone along with their arrangements, always uncomfortable, always willing to admit defeat, like I&#x27;m never myself when I&#x27;m before them! I thought it was because I was timid...</p>
<p>&quot;The Arbiter has a convincing charm and considerable authority, as well as outstanding combat ability that can deal with the unexpected,&quot; Alger described the situation simply.</p>
<p>Audrey nodded slowly and leaned backwards. She then spoke elegantly, &quot;I have no more questions.&quot;</p>
<p>Alger thought and looked towards the seat of honor at the long bronze table.</p>
<p>&quot;Honorable Mr. Fool, I&#x27;d like to ask if the True Creator&#x27;s Holy Residence that the Aurora Order advocates is the legendary Forsaken Land of God?&quot;</p>
<p>Forsaken Land of God? I have only seen that term once in Roselle&#x27;s diary... It might be in the secret dockets of the Nighthawks, but it isn&#x27;t something I can know of currently... How do you want me to answer? Klein nearly twitched the corner of his lips.</p>
<p>He considered it for a while then he replied in a calm tone, &quot;This is not something you should know now.&quot;</p>
<p>Alger felt his heart tighten, and he immediately lowered his head and replied, &quot;Please forgive me for overstepping my boundaries.&quot;</p>
<p>Audrey wanted to ask about the Forsaken Land of God but she also gave up the thought when she heard that.</p>
<p>In the lofty divine hall above the gray fog, silence suddenly filled the air.</p>
<p>At that moment, Audrey felt that she should say something.</p>
<p>&quot;Mr. Fool, if—and I&#x27;m saying if—I have the opportunity to join another organization, such as the Psychology Alchemists, is it permitted?&quot; Klein maintained his posture of leaning backward as he said with a chuckle, &quot;That is no problem. My requirement is that the existence of the Tarot Club is not to be exposed.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;If you become a member of another organization, the materials and information you can use for exchange will also increase.&quot;</p>
<p>After saying that, he suddenly recalled that he was also a member of another organization. He was a real Nighthawk while The Hanged Man was most likely related to the Church of Storms.</p>
<p>Would my Tarot Club be the so-called Rebels Alliance? Traitor Gathering? Klein was drowned in deep thoughts.</p>
<p>&quot;I understand now.&quot; Audrey was excited but she immediately thought of a question, &quot;Mr. Fool, if I found a suitable gentleman or lady for this gathering, could I guide them to join? How do I do that?&quot;</p>
<p>Alger thought and asked, &quot;Mr. Fool, what is the requirement to be a member of this gathering? How do we determine?&quot;</p>
<p>Ambitious, ethical, cultured, disciplined... Four words popped into Klein&#x27;s head instantly.</p>
<p>He maintained his silence for a few seconds and only spoke when Justice and Hanged Man appeared a little uneasy.</p>
<p>&quot;You can inform me here of people who you find suitable. I will decide if they will join us. Before that, you can&#x27;t give any hint that would cause the secret of the Tarot Club&#x27;s existence to be exposed. You must remember, to non-Gathering members...&quot; Klein paused and said in a heavy voice, &quot;You must not speak my name without my permission.&quot;</p>
