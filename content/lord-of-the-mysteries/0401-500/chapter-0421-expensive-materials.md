<p>The front door to Patrick Jason&#x27;s house suddenly burst open, and a group of Nighthawks in black tweed coats jogged inside.</p>
<p>They wore vigilant and alert expressions, as though they were facing an extremely terrifying enemy.</p>
<p>Tap! Tap! Tap! A person decked out in full silver armor walked in.</p>
<p>The armor exuded a feeling that made it seem like it was abnormally heavy. Various details about it adhered to an ancient style, and from its left shoulder, all the way diagonally to the abdomen area, it was stained with a splash of dark red blood that appeared impossible to remove. Coupled with the sputtering red spots in other places, it created a very queer and beautiful scene, as if it was a unique and magnificent decoration.</p>
<p>Soul Assurer Soest took out his pocket watch and gave it a look.</p>
<p>&quot;Change.&quot;</p>
<p>The silver armor stopped, lifting its visor to reveal the wearer. It was a handsome man with black hair and green eyes.</p>
<p>&quot;Leonard, hot water has been prepared in the master bedroom&#x27;s bathroom on the second floor. Don&#x27;t delay any longer; otherwise, you&#x27;ll only be able to return to the embrace of the Goddess,&quot; Soest exhorted him.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, Captain Soest.&quot; Leonard Mitchell, with the help of the rest of the Nighthawks, removed his heavy blood-stained silver armor.</p>
<p>With red gloves on, he didn&#x27;t say anything, nor did he hesitate as he rushed to the second floor and found the bathtub still billowing with white steam.</p>
<p>Leonard quickly stripped off his clothes and lied down in the hot water without even exposing his nose.</p>
<p>His skin quickly turned red like a cooked lobster, and strange, scar-like silver lines gradually started to appear on the surface of his skin.</p>
<p>Those silver lines were like pure blade beams which constantly spread outwards and fused with the hot water.</p>
<p>In less than ten seconds, the steam disappeared and a thin layer of transparent ice formed on the surface of the hot water!</p>
<p>Only when all the silver lines dispersed did Leonard sit up, panting heavily.</p>
<p>He cocked his head slightly as if he was listening to something. Then, he said with a lowered voice, &quot;Old Man, do you know the origins of 1-42?&quot;</p>
<p>An elderly voice rang out in his mind.</p>
<p>&quot;You are getting more and more impolite.</p>
<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know where that strange armor comes from.&quot; Without waiting for Leonard to ask further, he let out a chuckle.</p>
<p>&quot;But I think I recognize the owner of the blood.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Who is it?&quot; Leonard asked curiously.</p>
<p>The slightly-aged voice said in a low, deep voice, &quot;An ancient god before the Cataclysm.&quot;</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>South of the Bridge, Rose Street, Harvest Church.</p>
<p>As soon as Klein stepped into the quiet prayer hall, he saw Father Utravsky and Vampire Emlyn White, one in the front and one in the back, sitting on chairs of different heights. Their hands were placed in front of their mouths and noses, with their fingers clasped together and palms empty.</p>
<p>This was the unique prayer position of the Church of Earth Mother.</p>
<p>Right now, Emlyn White&#x27;s expression was gentle and calm, without any trace of the arrogance and vexation which he had before.</p>
<p>Klein slightly moved the corner of his mouth as he silently drew a triangular Sacred Emblem on his chest.</p>
<p>He took a seat at random, waited until the prayer was over, and then walked over to Emlyn White. He said with a smile, &quot;You are especially pious today.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What?&quot; Emlyn snapped to his senses as he muttered to himself while his expression was pale. &quot;What have I done, what have I done...&quot;</p>
<p>His voice trailed off, as though recalling what he had just done.</p>
<p>&quot;Maybe it&#x27;s not a bad thing.&quot; Klein consoled the vampire in an utterly unconvincing manner before sitting down next to him.</p>
<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t want to hear others say something like that. Although I feel my resistance is weakening...&quot; Emlyn wore a livid expression and he said with a tone filled with despondence, &quot;But I don&#x27;t want to betray the moon!&quot;</p>
<p>Klein didn&#x27;t continue with the topic that depressed the vampire as he casually asked, &quot;Do you Sanguines worship the Primordial Moon, or a particular deity that represents the moon? Or perhaps, the two of them can be considered the same?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;All of them.&quot; Emlyn slightly raised his chin, &quot;To a pure-blooded Sanguine, we obviously believe in the deity that represents the moon. of course, it is the god who represents the moon. <p>&quot;A human becoming a Sanguine?&quot; Klein wasn&#x27;t surprised that Emlyn White was able to say the name of an ancient god from the Second Epoch. Instead, he was more concerned about the tidbit about humans becoming Sanguine. Is this the Vampire Sequence that Mr. Azik had mentioned? he thought.</p>
<p>Emlyn said with a slightly complicated expression, &quot;Yes, there are two types. One is transformed from a bestowment from a powerful Sanguine, and the other is transformed from consuming a corresponding potion. The latter is our most hated enemy.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Why?&quot; Klein vaguely guessed at the answer.</p>
<p>Emlyn gritted his teeth and replied, &quot;The main ingredient of their potion is our blood essence.&quot;</p>
<p>As expected... Klein turned his head and sized up Emlyn a few times.</p>
<p>The gaze left Emlyn feeling a little nervous as he snorted.</p>
