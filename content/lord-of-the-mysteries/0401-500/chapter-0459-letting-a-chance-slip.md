<p>... Monday morning. Cherwood Borough, 15 Minsk Street.</p>
<p>Klein squatted in front of the toilet, holding a brush and carefully cleaning the dirt inside it.</p>
<p>According to his schedule, after completing his &quot;visits&quot; on Saturday and Sunday, he decided to take a day off and wait until tomorrow to report back to Prince Edessak about the final results and hand over the assignment. But during what should&#x27;ve been his relaxation time, he found the house a little too messy and dirty.</p>
<p>It was only when he saw this that he remembered that the cleaning of the house had been done twice a week through the temporary employment of the landlady&#x27;s maid next door.</p>
<p>As the Sammers had gone on holiday to Seville City in Desi Bay, one of the maids had gone along with them, while another had returned to the countryside after receiving a year-end bonus. 15 Minsk Street, where he lived, hadn&#x27;t been cleaned by anyone for quite some time.</p>
<p>Klein had planned to put up with it for two days since he was &quot;leaving&quot; Backlund, but in his free time, he didn&#x27;t dare to take the time to head to the Quelaag Club to have fun. He was afraid of infuriating Prince Edessak, so he could only stay home. Hence, seeing the things that irked him, he changed into his old clothes and began spring cleaning before the new year.</p>
<p>He went on to scrub the toilet, wash the bathtub, wipe the windows, mop the floors, clean his equipment, and wash clothes... Klein busied himself from eight to eleven before barely completing his desired cleanup.</p>
<p>Of course, he only did it perfunctorily without that much attention to detail.</p>
<p>Sometimes, renting too big a house isn&#x27;t a good thing... Klein washed his hands and wiped his face with a towel.</p>
<p>Walking out of the bathroom and looking at the clean and tidy living room and dining room, and watching the sunlight pierce through the clouds and shine through the transparent glass window, showering golden spots in his house, he had a strange sense of accomplishment. He felt a lot happier.</p>
<p>Time to reward myself for lunch. I&#x27;ll ﬁnd a good restaurant... Klein returned to the second floor and changed his clothes.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s almost the new year, and there are still people coming to entrust me with missions?&quot; As he rose and walked to the door, Klein made up his mind to decline.</p>
<p>Although he only had 34 pounds left in cash, in order to get out of the royal strife, he had to &quot;go on vacation&quot; to the south as soon as possible. He could no longer accept assignments.</p>
<p>To his surprise, the visitor wasn&#x27;t a stranger, but the old butler of Prince Edessak.</p>
<p>The old butler was wearing a well-cut tuxedo. <p>After enduring for a while, he realized that he couldn&#x27;t hold it in any longer and apologetically said to the old butler, &quot;I&#x27;m very sorry. I&#x27;ll need to use the bathroom first. My stomach isn&#x27;t feeling well.&quot;</p>
