<p>Just as Reinette Tinekerr&#x27;s figure appeared in Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar&#x27;s pupils, &quot;She&quot; left the window to the heart and began expanding back to &quot;Her&quot; original size.</p>
<p>This meant that &quot;Her&quot; Wraith&#x27;s possession attempt had failed.</p>
<p>The Dark Demonic Wolf&#x27;s body rapidly shrunk and thinned as &quot;He&quot; madly absorbed the light around &quot;Him,&quot; becoming a translucent black velvet curtain.</p>
<p>This was an item that Klein had seen via dream divination. It was ejected from the grayish-white fog and had landed in the hands of the Dark Demonic Wolf. It was suspected to contain the Sequence 1 Attendant of Mysteries Beyonder characteristic of the Seer pathway.</p>
<p>At this moment, Dark Demonic Wolf Kotor had borrowed some of the powers of the &quot;curtain&quot; to dodge Reinette Tinekerr&#x27;s Wraith&#x27;s possession at the critical moment via some unknown means, effectively avoiding the subsequent series of control.</p>
<p>The &quot;curtain&quot; was stowed away after &quot;He&quot; used it, allowing the Dark Demonic Wolf with &quot;His&quot; dark, short fur to appear in a spot that was originally empty.</p>
<p>Reinette Tinekerr wasn&#x27;t surprised by the failure. Taking advantage of this opportunity, a golden-haired, red-eyed head in &quot;Her&quot; hand spat out a rectangular, diamond-like charm.</p>
<p>The other three heads used ancient Hermes, Jotun, and Elvish to say a word:</p>
<p>&quot;Yesterday!&quot;</p>
<p>The rectangular diamond-like charm was instantly engulfed in transparent flames as it fused with the void.</p>
<p>Reinette Tinekerr&#x27;s body swelled as the four blonde, red-eyed heads flew up and landed on &quot;Her&quot; empty neck, stacked upon one another.</p>
<p>In the blink of an eye, Miss Messenger had transformed into a huge doll that could almost break through the Dark Demonic Wolf&#x27;s castle.</p>
<p>&quot;She&quot; was wearing a black gothic dress with countless mysterious symbols and sinister vines. &quot;Her&quot; eyes were blood-red and &quot;She&quot; exuded an aura that no human should possess.</p>
<p>Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar didn&#x27;t attempt to stop Reinette Tinekerr from borrowing strength from &quot;Her&quot; past, nor did &quot;He&quot; immediately show &quot;His&quot; complete Mythical Creature form. With eight feet on the ground, &quot;He&quot; raised &quot;His&quot; neck and let out a roar.</p>
<p>This roar seemed to be of a higher level, containing words similar to Jotun. It made the &quot;curtain&quot; float up and instantly expand, blanketing the ancient castle from top to bottom.</p>
<p>The &quot;curtain&quot; quickly turned transparent and fused with the building. It was as though it had never appeared or had any effect on it. However, Klein&#x27;s spiritual intuition told him that this place was already isolated from the outside world. If he wanted to leave, he had to first break the invisible barrier.</p>
<p>In other words, that &quot;curtain&quot; made the Dark Demonic Wolf&#x27;s castle become an independent &quot;kingdom.&quot;</p>
<p>This was the embryonic form of a divine kingdom!</p>
<p>Klein, who had just failed and planned on continuing to summon angels to help him, had a spark of inspiration. He instantly changed his mind, reaching into the void and dragging out another him.</p>
<p>It was Gehrman Sparrow, one who was holding a historical projection of the Staff of the Stars.</p>
<p>Right on the heels of that, Klein controlled his historical projection to turn him into his marionette and transfer some Worms of Spirit over.</p>
<p>While he was busy with these matters, Reinette Tinekerr had already recovered to &quot;Her&quot; peak condition. &quot;Her&quot; bright red eyes reflected the eight-legged demonic wolf.</p>
<p>With a faint flash, the Dark Demonic Wolf turned into a white goat.</p>
<p>However, outside the dark hall, a giant holding a broom suddenly trembled and transformed into a demonic wolf with a tuft of gray hair on its forehead.</p>
<p>At the moment when the curse was cast on &quot;Him,&quot; a Miracle Invoker, &quot;He&quot; had swapped places with &quot;His&quot; marionette! &quot;He&quot; opened &quot;His&quot; mouth once again and let out a roar.</p>
<p>This roar also contained words that mostly resembled the source of Jotun, describing a beautiful wish:</p>
<p>&quot;I wish for all godhood here to dissipate!&quot;</p>
<p>In the next second, as the God of Wishes, the Dark Demonic Wolf granted &quot;His&quot; wish. Klein immediately felt his godhood being repressed. The Beyonder powers that stemmed from Scholar of Yore and Bizarro Sorcerer could no longer be used!</p>
<p>Fortunately, the historical projection was maintained with spirituality. As long as the summoning was successful, it wouldn&#x27;t be dispelled by the Dark Demonic Wolf&#x27;s wish. Similarly, as Klein had already turned his historical projection into a marionette and transferred a number of Worms of Spirit over, he could still swap locations with his projection.</p>
<p>Apart from him, Reinette Tinekerr and Dark Demonic Wolf were also affected by the wish that had been granted. Their Beyonder powers above Sequence 5 had vanished into thin air, making it difficult to use them.</p>
<p>Of course, be it the demonic wolf marionette that had turned into a goat, or the gigantic doll-like Reinette Tinekerr, there was no change in &quot;Their&quot; form. What existed was reasonable, so it couldn&#x27;t be eliminated by the &quot;Power of Wishes.&quot;</p>
<p>After making this wish, the Dark Demonic Wolf arched &quot;His&quot; back and bent &quot;His&quot; body slightly. It looked like &quot;He&quot; was about to engage in a battle with Klein and Reinette Tinekerr. At that moment, &quot;His&quot; mountain-like body was about the size of the castle-like doll. &quot;He&quot; looked down coldly at Klein, capable of crushing him with a single swipe of &quot;His&quot; claw.</p>
<p>In addition, the castle had many marionettes rushing over. They consisted of giants, elves, vampires, humans, and deformed monsters.</p>
<p>Only a number of them were at Sequence 5, but now, they had Kotar&#x27;s Worms of Spirit in their bodies. In other words, in this special environment, all of them were at Sequence 5.</p>
<p>After the dissipation of godhood, in this independent &quot;kingdom,&quot; Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar&#x27;s advantage became obvious.</p>
<p>More than a hundred Sequence 5s were besieging two Sequence 5s!</p>
<p>Furthermore, the Dark Demonic Wolf was itself a mutant. Even if &quot;He&quot; couldn&#x27;t reveal any godhood, &quot;His&quot; massive size and terrifying strength made &quot;Him&quot; adept at combat. &quot;He&quot; wasn&#x27;t on the same level as a weak human like Marionettist Klein.</p>
<p>At this moment, the body of Reinette Tinekerr, who was dressed in a black gothic dress that twined with sinister vines, suddenly turned incorporeal. First, &quot;She&quot; reflected on a floor-to-ceiling window, then it jumped into the pitch-black pupils of the Dark Demonic Wolf.</p>
<p>&quot;She&quot; had once again attempted Wraith&#x27;s possession.</p>
<p>However, Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar swapped locations with another marionette in a timely fashion, preventing Reinette Tinekerr from successfully &quot;possessing&quot; &quot;His&quot; body.</p>
<p>Reinette Tinekerr wasn&#x27;t discouraged as &quot;She&quot; continued using mediums such as glass windows, chandeliers, and eyeballs to &quot;jump&quot; through the different marionettes in pursuit of the real Dark Demonic Wolf.</p>
<p>As for the Dark Demonic Wolf, &quot;He&quot; relied on &quot;His&quot; numerous marionettes, and had the advantage of copious numbers of Worms of Spirit. &quot;He&quot; swapped between different marionettes without any pause to avoid being forcibly possessed by the Ancient Bane.</p>
<p>Amidst the two angels&#x27; silent conflict, a large number of the Dark Demonic Wolf&#x27;s marionettes had surrounded the two Kleins.</p>
<p>Beyonder powers like a vampire&#x27;s &quot;Abyss Shackles,&quot; a giant&#x27;s &quot;Hurricane of Light,&quot; an elf&#x27;s &quot;Wind Binding,&quot; and a human&#x27;s &quot;Psychic Piercing&quot; and &quot;Holy Light Summoning&quot; inundated their targets. They controlled, weakened, attacked, and purified Klein, acting with great rapport. Klein was only able to dodge a portion of the attacks before being &quot;drowned&quot; by the attacks.</p>
<p>Plasma exploded as the light blasted out. Klein&#x27;s Staff of the Stars-holding figure quickly outlined itself to the side.</p>
<p>At that critical moment, he had exchanged spots with his marionette in time. He imagined his &quot;Traveling&quot; powers and had used the Staff of the Stars to complete the teleportation.</p>
<p>In this special scenario, this was essentially a release of &quot;Recorded&quot; powers. After dodging this round of attacks, Klein discovered that a marionette was controlling his Spirit Body Threads before he could catch his breath.</p>
<p>At the same time, a human marionette reached out his right hand under the influence of the Dark Demonic Wolf and aimed at the Miracle Invoker&#x27;s actual body.</p>
<p>In the next second, the Dark Demonic Wolf and another marionette switched positions. As for the marionette, it was &quot;possessed&quot; by Reinette Tinekerr.</p>
<p>This way, the human marionette&#x27;s right hand was targeted at Reinette Tinekerr.</p>
<p>The marionette&#x27;s palm clenched tightly as his wrist spun half a circle, stealing away the target&#x27;s subsequent thoughts.</p>
<p>Right on the heels of that, he jumped and pounced on an ally.</p>
<p>Reinette Tinekerr was momentarily rooted to the ground.</p>
<p>When the Dark Demonic Wolf saw this, &quot;He&quot; immediately got several marionettes to spread open their arms and summon pure pillars of cleansing flames to surround them.</p>
<p>In the holy light, Wraith Reinette Tinekerr&#x27;s body first began to melt. Following that, using &quot;Mirror Jump,&quot; &quot;She&quot; moved to a glass window situated high above the castle to avoid the subsequent purification.</p>
<p>In just a few seconds, &quot;She&quot; and Klein inevitably fell into a perilous situation.</p>
<p>The restricted environment, being on &quot;His&quot; home ground, and &quot;His&quot; trait as the God of Wishes had magnified Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar advantage to the extreme.</p>
<p>If it were any other Scholar of Yore, there was only one problem he needed to consider—how to rely on Reinette Tinekerr to escape, but Klein had his trump card.</p>
<p>Just as he pulled back his Spirit Body Threads, he didn&#x27;t hesitate to stir the powers of Sefirah Castle.</p>
<p>This trait of his wasn&#x27;t suppressed by the Dark Demonic Wolf&#x27;s &quot;wish.&quot;</p>
<p>This was something even the Forsaken Land of the Gods couldn&#x27;t screen!</p>
<p>In midair, grayish-white fog appeared. The ancient palace above the spirit world appeared faint.</p>
<p>Its appearance brought with it slight tremors. A certain power followed the connection and shook the demonic wolf&#x27;s castle, causing the translucent black velvet &quot;curtain&quot; to jolt out of place a little, allowing the independent &quot;kingdom&quot; to intersect with reality.</p>
<p>Seizing this opportunity, Klein opened his mouth and shouted out a name in Jotun:</p>
<p>&quot;Leodero!&quot; Boom! Boom! Boom!</p>
<p>Thousands of thick silver lightning bolts descended from the sky, smiting Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar&#x27;s castle. The area transformed into a lightning forest that emitted a strong destructive aura.</p>
