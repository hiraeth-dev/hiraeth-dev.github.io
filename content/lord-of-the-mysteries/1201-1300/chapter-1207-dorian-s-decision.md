<p>Backlund, Cherwood Borough. 22 Hope Street, Hat Trick Inn.</p>
<p>With broad shoulders and thick arms, Dorian Gray Abraham unknowingly paced back and forth in the room, waiting for his student, Fors, to visit.</p>
<p>No matter how composed and mentally prepared he was, he couldn&#x27;t help but feel nervous and uneasy when he eventually needed to face the answer.</p>
<p>After an unknown period of time, there was a series of knocks on the door.</p>
<p>Dorian listened to the rhythm for a few seconds before taking a deep breath. He walked to the door, twisted the handle, and pulled it back.</p>
<p>Outside the door was the brown-haired, curly-haired Fors, who was wearing a dark-colored long dress and a pair of tinted glasses.</p>
<p>Dorian habitually looked behind Fors, and after confirming that no one was looking in their direction, he made way for his student to pass.</p>
<p>At the same time, he glanced at Fors&#x27;s hands and realized that his student wasn&#x27;t carrying any luggage.</p>
<p>Dorian retracted his gaze and walked to the middle of the room. He found a seat and sat down before pointing to the sofa opposite him.</p>
<p>&quot;Have a seat.&quot;</p>
<p>Fors cautiously lifted the ends of her skirt slightly, sat down, and greeted, &quot;Good morning, Teacher.&quot;</p>
<p>Dorian didn&#x27;t go straight to the point. After some thought, he said, &quot;Did the lot of you really kill Botis?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot; Fors took out an exquisite, long cigar case from her pocket and opened it to show Dorian its interior.</p>
<p>It was a pair of dark-black eyes that were frozen with indescribable horror, as though they had seen something extremely terrifying before they died.</p>
<p>Dorian had originally been mentally prepared. It was just like receiving the terrifying head that would cause nightmares the previous time, one that was put together one bloody fragment at a time. He never expected his student to not carry any luggage and to only take out a ladies&#x27; cigar case.</p>
<p>This made him believe that it was a relic that could prove Botis&#x27;s identity, but the truth was beyond his expectations once again.</p>
<p>This was still Botis&#x27;s corpse, but there was even less than what Lewis Wien left behind!</p>
<p>Only a pair of eyes are left... The spiritual intuition of an Astrologer convinced Dorian that the eyeball belonged to Botis. Seeing that the teacher had fallen silent, Fors subconsciously explained, &quot;His body has completely collapsed and dissipated. Only this pair of eyeballs is still intact.&quot;</p>
<p>She paused for a moment before saying, &quot;These eyeballs contain the remnants of the terror and contamination of the Box of the Great Old Ones before Botis&#x27;s passing. It&#x27;s a very strong cursed item, so I didn&#x27;t send it directly to you. It would cause terrible things to happen to the postman, and he might even die unknowingly.&quot;</p>
<p>The corruption of the Box of the Great Old Ones... Dorian nodded in enlightenment and sighed with a smile.</p>
<p>&quot;Did he eventually die under the hands of the Box of the Great Old Ones?</p>
<p>&quot;This is really fate...&quot;</p>
<p>The first item that Botis had stolen after his betrayal from leading the Aurora Order over was the Box of the Great Old Ones.</p>
<p>Fors had heard Ma&#x27;am Hermit&#x27;s and Miss Justice&#x27;s description of the general situation at the private gathering where they distributed the spoils of war. She learned how gorgeous and dangerous the battle she had missed was. She thought for a moment and said, &quot;You could say that...</p>
<p>&quot;However, before he was contaminated by the Box of the Great Old Ones, he had already begun losing control.&quot;</p>
<p>Dorian wasn&#x27;t surprised as he said to his student, &quot;Keep it. This is a spoil of war you deserve.&quot; After Fors closed the cigar case and placed it back into her pocket, Dorian leaned forward, clasped his hands, and touched his nose.</p>
<p>&quot;Botis was one of the most talented Apprentices I have ever seen. Who knew that he would end up like this...&quot;</p>
<p>Having said that, Dorian let out a long sigh as though he was recalling and confessing something.</p>
<p>Fors didn&#x27;t know much about the details of what had happened back then, so she didn&#x27;t dare to speak recklessly. She could only remain silent and wait for her teacher to recover from his mixed emotions.</p>
<p>Ten seconds later, Dorian straightened his body and asked, &quot;How did you digest the Scribe potion?&quot;</p>
<p>This wasn&#x27;t only to show concern for his student, but also to accumulate experience to provide some guidance to the other members of the family.</p>
<p>Fors&#x27;s expression immediately turned complicated as though she was recalling something she didn&#x27;t wish to recall.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s mainly because someone had provided me with help. On the one hand, I ‘Recorded&#x27; a lot of unique or high-level abilities. On the other hand, I was brought to many places in the past few months where I ‘Recorded&#x27; different cultures and beautiful scenery...&quot;</p>
<p>Dorian fell silent for a moment before nodding. &quot;This isn&#x27;t easy to imitate...&quot;</p>
<p>He then asked, &quot;Gehrman Sparrow?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot; Fors gave an affirmative response.</p>
<p>Dorian fell silent again. A few seconds later, he said, &quot;What kind of deal does he want to make?</p>
<p>&quot;Or rather, what does he want?&quot;</p>
<p>Fors focused her attention and answered in an embarrassed manner, &quot;He wants the potion formula of a Planeswalker, and he plans on using the Box of the Great Old Ones to exchange for one of the two Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts in your family&#x27;s hands.&quot;</p>
<p>This price was definitely a generous offer. Fors had originally thought that Mr. World was going to use a promise to exchange for the Planeswalker potion formula and a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact, but she never expected him to throw in the Box of the Great Old Ones.</p>
<p>Of course, it was definitely of value to allow the Abraham family to be free of the curse. However, a promise was forever a promise that might not be fulfilled.</p>
<p>Dorian wasn&#x27;t surprised by Gehrman Sparrow&#x27;s request for a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact. He had long been mentally prepared, and instead, he felt that the conditions were beyond his imagination. After all, the Abraham family didn&#x27;t have many things that a demigod could covet at this point in time. He frowned slightly and said, &quot;Why does he want the potion formula of a Planeswalker?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know,&quot; Fors answered frankly.</p>
<p>Dorian stood up and started pacing.</p>
<p>Suddenly, he stopped and looked at Fors.</p>
<p>&quot;I need to meet him and have a chat before I can make a decision.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Alright.&quot; Fors agreed without any hesitation.</p>
<p>Dorian heaved a sigh of relief and prepared to send his student away before quickly taking out the medicine for his consumption.</p>
<p>He had arranged to meet at this time because he had taken into consideration that his curse would happen at any moment. If anything were to happen, the enemy wouldn&#x27;t be able to channel his spirit in time.</p>
<p>However, after Fors got up, she didn&#x27;t walk to the door. She stood on the spot and grabbed at the void.</p>
<p>Her arm suddenly sank, and she quickly pulled out a figure wearing a black trench coat and silk top hat.</p>
<p>This figure had black hair and brown eyes. His facial features were cut and cold. It was none other than Gehrman Sparrow. Gehrman Sparrow&#x27;s eyes moved slightly as he quickly recovered from his sluggish look and became no different from a real person.</p>
<p>&quot;Teacher, he&#x27;s here.&quot; Fors introduced seriously, &quot;He&#x27;s Gehrman Sparrow.&quot;</p>
<p>This action left Dorian Gray Abraham slightly agape. He forgot to close his mouth and didn&#x27;t respond for a moment.</p>
<p>Although he came from an ancient family clan and knew many secrets, there were many things he couldn&#x27;t imagine even if he read the description due to him being only a Sequence 7.</p>
<p>After moving his consciousness over, Klein reached into his clothes and took out a golden pocket watch.</p>
<p>Pa! He opened his pocket watch and took a look. Without any emotions, he said to Dorian, &quot;You have three minutes.&quot;</p>
<p>...It&#x27;s exactly as the rumors say. He&#x27;s cold, arrogant, and crazy... Dorian didn&#x27;t dare to waste any time and directly said, &quot;Give me a reason to believe in your promise.&quot;</p>
<p>As he closed his pocket watch, Klein placed it back into his inner pocket and said, &quot;In truth, I&#x27;m already aware of the ritual that allows Mr. Door to return.&quot;</p>
<p>Dorian&#x27;s eyes lit up. Just as he was about to ask, he heard Gehrman Sparrow calmly add, &quot;But I don&#x27;t plan to do that.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Why?&quot; Dorian and Fors were puzzled, but one dared to ask while the other didn&#x27;t.</p>

<p>When it came to understanding the cosmos, the Abraham family was definitely ranked first outside the true deities and angels. Klein believed that they must&#x27;ve left behind some hints and hidden records.</p>
<p>Dorian nodded solemnly and said, &quot;Yes.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I suspect that Mr. Door has been corrupted by the cosmos,&quot; Klein explained simply. &quot;As for my promise, it can be fulfilled right away. However, you might not be willing to do so. Furthermore, it doesn&#x27;t completely resolve the curse.&quot;</p>

<p>Klein&#x27;s expression immediately turned serious.</p>
<p>&quot;You and your family members will change their faith to my Lord. That way, when the full moon or Blood Moon happens, you will be blessed and no longer suffer from the curse.&quot;</p>
<p>After completely digesting the Scholar of Yore potion, there was no need for him to pull a person above the gray fog to avoid Mr. Door&#x27;s ravings. He could directly use &quot;Angel&#x27;s Embrace&quot; to resolve the problem. The only thing he needed to worry about now was that there might be too many members of the Abraham family. <p>Klein suppressed his shame and said solemnly, &quot;The great Mr. Fool.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The Fool... Are you from the Antigonus family?&quot; Dorian suddenly made some connections.</p>
<p>Klein shook his head and rejected his guess.</p>
<p>Dorian fell silent again, but considering that there were only three minutes left, he hurriedly asked, &quot;If we believe in that entity, we can avoid the troubles brought about by the curse?&quot;</p>
<p>As an ancient family member, he knew very well how dangerous it was to believe in an unknown existence. He was afraid that he would resolve Mr. Door&#x27;s ravings, but bring about another curse.</p>
<p>Klein answered frankly, &quot;This is just a temporary solution. I will find a better solution for you.&quot;</p>

<p>He planned on using himself as an experiment to see if the method worked. Furthermore, he didn&#x27;t plan on telling the other family members.
