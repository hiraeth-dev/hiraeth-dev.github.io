<p>It&#x27;s not Dark Angel Sasrir... The projection of Enuni has been eroded by the power that surged out from the Giant King&#x27;s residence, becoming a monster that I can&#x27;t understand at the moment. Furthermore, it looks like it can exist for a long period of time... Perhaps there&#x27;s a bit of Sasrir&#x27;s will involved in this... With his left hand covering his face, Klein allowed the Worms of Spirit to burrow through the gaps in his fingers freely.</p>
<p>What he couldn&#x27;t understand the most was something else. He had clearly opened the palace where the Dark Angel lay slumber, but the deities and Kings of Angels showed no reaction.</p>
<p>According to Klein&#x27;s imagination, when the surging &quot;sea&quot; gushed out, be it the True Creator, the God of Knowledge and Wisdom, Blasphemer Amon, or Amon&#x27;s brother who ultimately hid behind the scenes, &quot;They&quot; should&#x27;ve immediately descended or appeared to confirm Dark Angel Sasrir&#x27;s present state or snatch whatever corresponding items. To his surprise, the entire Giant King&#x27;s Court remained completely silent. No external powers exerted any influence.</p>
<p>Could it be that &quot;They&quot; are still waiting for me to enter the Giant King&#x27;s residence and to come into contact with that deputy of Heaven? Hmm, the True Creator said to meet Sasrir... My Spirit Body and physical body haven&#x27;t recovered from the corrosion, which means that the True Creator&#x27;s goal has yet to be achieved... Haha... As Klein&#x27;s thoughts raced, he realized that he was unable to control his emotions, be it anger, sadness, worry, or depression. He couldn&#x27;t help but pull the corners of his mouth up and let out a scoff.</p>
<p>The only thing he was glad about was that this didn&#x27;t affect his brain. He could still think and use all sorts of Beyonder powers, but sometimes, his madness would suddenly be aggravated, turning him rash and aggressive.</p>
<p>I wonder if the demigod of the mind has any way to treat this situation. In any case, the Sealed Artifacts or Beyonder characteristics that I can summon can&#x27;t do it... This is a result of an incomplete spirit. If I don&#x27;t resolve my severed shadow, I probably won&#x27;t be able to completely recover. However, perhaps there are methods that can allow me to temporarily return to normal. For example, create a virtual persona to make up for the incompleteness? Unfortunately, I can&#x27;t enter Seﬁrah Castle... Klein&#x27;s thoughts were in a mess as the scenes that surfaced in his mind gradually vanished.</p>
<p>He then picked up the half top hat that had dropped to the ground and wore it. Then, through the huge glass window formed from ground orange gems, he observed the changes in the Giant King&#x27;s Court.</p>
<p>Unlike before, an illusory sun appeared high in the sky, allowing the magnificent building complex situated in the frozen sunset to enjoy the sun at noon.</p>
<p>The troll statues that stood guard in the various palaces seemed to be draped with a cloak weaved out of shadows.</p>
<p>Enuni, who had a pair of black, illusory wings on his back, and the shadow that originally belonged to Klein jumped over the railing and glided towards him.</p>
<p>Klein&#x27;s gaze froze as he subconsciously took a few steps forward, intending to fight the enemy.</p>
<p>But very quickly, he snapped back to his senses and checked his current state. He quickly retreated to the ancient &quot;elevator,&quot; pulled the switch, and landed back in the Barren Tunnel. Then, with &quot;Blink,&quot; he appeared near the Waning Forest and ran all the way to the edge of the Giant King&#x27;s Court.</p>
<p>His intuition told him that in his current state, he had no means of defeating the mutated &quot;Enuni&quot; and his separated shadow.</p>
<p>Of course, he had the confidence if it was just the latter.</p>
<p>When he arrived at the edge of the radiant sunlight, he turned and saw that Enuni and his shadow hadn&#x27;t chased after him. It was as though there was a limit to their range of activity.</p>
<p>There&#x27;s no way to leave the Giant King&#x27;s residence, or should I say, be too far away from Dark Angel Sasrir? Just as this thought flashed through his mind, Klein saw the illusionary &quot;sun&quot; atop leap from the center to the west. The dusk&#x27;s colors once again enveloped the divine kingdom.</p>
<p>Klein carefully observed all of this and was in no hurry to leave the Giant King&#x27;s Court.</p>
<p>Not long after, the illusory &quot;sun&quot; plunged into the palace where Dark Angel Sasrir slumbered. Darkness became the ruler of the Giant King&#x27;s Court.</p>
<p>The darkness was different from the one found on a normal night—there was no moon or starlight, only blurry shadows.</p>
<p>Standing in this darkness, Klein had a nagging feeling that someone was clinging to him from behind. However, he clearly knew that it was an illusion and didn&#x27;t turn around recklessly. A few minutes later, the illusory &quot;sun&quot; rose, and the light of dawn dispersed the darkness.</p>
<p>The power that surged out from the Dark Angel&#x27;s chamber has changed the Giant King&#x27;s Court despite it being an ancient divine kingdom... It really is a power that&#x27;s close to that of a true deity. At the very least, it controls a &quot;Uniqueness,&quot; but which pathway&#x27;s Uniqueness would it be?</p>
<p>The generated changes include &quot;Blazing Sun,&quot; &quot;Darkness,&quot; &quot;Degeneration,&quot; and &quot;Mutation.&quot; This is a little contradictory. It doesn&#x27;t seem like the result of a single Uniqueness... Besides, the Uniqueness of the Sun pathway is deﬁnitely with the Eternal Blazing Sun... The &quot;Shadow&quot; and &quot;Degeneration&quot; is brought about by &quot;Darkness&quot; which is undoubtedly with the True Creator... When I opened the door, I saw an illusory, pitch-black, viscous ocean... It&#x27;s related to the Chaos Sea? Klein shook his head as he continued observing. This continued on as he waited for the City of Silver&#x27;s expedition team to rendezvous with him.</p>

<p>The howling winds swept through the fog, bringing a sense of clarity from time to time. However, it was soon filled with surging whiteness.</p>
<p>Audrey&#x27;s eyes suddenly turned golden, allowing her to see even further. As she carefully treaded, the fog in front of her suddenly became much thinner.</p>
<p>At the same time, her Beyonder powers suffered a retrogression.</p>
<p>The weakening of mysticism... The concepts and information previously mentioned by Mr. World flashed across her mind.</p>

<p>Multiple salvos from Backlund fired ahead, causing the shells to leave trails of red, blue, silver, or black in their wake as they bombarded the enemy&#x27;s base.</p>
<p>However, in the next second, these shells encountered invisible barriers and exploded in midair, making the transparent &quot;wall&quot; tremble.</p>
<p>At this moment, a rather blurry figure appeared in the distant, thick fog. &quot;He&quot; was in human form, but he was more than ten meters tall. His torso and waist each had two arms extending out as he emitted a rich darkness.</p>
<p>For some reason, as the figure that seemed to arise from a legend appeared, the Feysac and Intis allied force&#x27;s base stirred.</p>

<p>At this moment, radiant sunlight shone on them, allowing them to see light and bringing them courage.</p>
<p>However, there were still a small number of soldiers who were unable to control themselves. They kept shouting &quot;mommy&quot; or a particular woman&#x27;s name in their bids to escape from the battlefield.</p>
<p>Bang! Bang! Bang!</p>

<p>They landed on the ground, turning motionless after slightly twitching. No one cared what their names were, nor did anyone know of their past.</p>
