<p>Boom! Boom!</p>
<p>In an underground shelter in Backlund, Audrey, who was dressed in hunting attire, listened to the distant explosions.</p>
<p>When she turned around, she happened to see Melissa looking at her in confusion.</p>
<p>The young girl, who had just reached adulthood, asked in a dreamy tone, &quot;Miss Audrey, will the war end if we&#x27;re completely defeated? Will we no longer have to worry about bombardments, raids, and having insufficient food?&quot;</p>
<p>Audrey looked at her deeply and said, &quot;But if that happens, you will have to change your faith.&quot;</p>
<p>Melissa hesitated, not knowing how to respond. At this moment, a commoner curled up against the wall blurted out, &quot;I believe in the God of Steam and Machinery! Even if Feysac and Intis win, I don&#x27;t need to change my faith!&quot;</p>
<p>When that happens, life would return to its original warm and peaceful state!</p>
<p>These words stirred the commoners who were hiding in the shelter. They whispered to each other and discussed possible developments. There were no lack of Evernight believers.</p>
<p>To most people, faith wasn&#x27;t that important compared to life. After all, a true deity would eventually still protect them.</p>
<p>The police officers who maintained the order of the shelter didn&#x27;t stop the commotion from spreading. They watched on coldly, some even having a hint of anticipation.</p>
<p>However, the defeated will deﬁnitely suffer something far crueler than you can imagine. It&#x27;s not something that can be summarized with just a change of faith... Be it a lesson from history or her conclusion deduced from the human psyche, all of them made Audrey more pessimistic than everyone present.</p>
<p>She looked around and could not help but sigh inwardly.</p>
<p>&quot;The Goddess&#x27;s anchor is already greatly shaken... If not for the grain support from before, it might have completely collapsed...&quot;</p>
<p>As for what this situation meant, Audrey knew very well in her heart. She closed her eyes, tilted her head slightly, and muttered to herself silently, &quot;The battle of gods is about to begin...&quot;</p>
<p>The final outcome was about to appear.</p>
<p>After nodding at Melissa, Audrey turned and left the area, arriving at the entrance of the shelter.</p>
<p>The golden retriever, Susie, was seated there, looking like a qualified guard.</p>
<p>&quot;You... don&#x27;t seem like you plan to return?&quot; Susie&#x27;s nose twitched as she asked with a suppressed voice. Audrey had hidden herself in this shelter from the beginning of the siege that began today; therefore, she hadn&#x27;t had the time to return to her own residence in Empress Borough. As the battle had decreased in intensity slightly, Earl Hall had already sent two people to urge her to return, so that she could head for a sanctuary for nobles.</p>
<p>Audrey shook her head and said with a faint smile, &quot;I have to do what I need to do.&quot;</p>
<p>Without waiting for Susie&#x27;s reply, she smiled and said, &quot;Stay here on my behalf and secretly placate them. Don&#x27;t let any commotions happen here. If they want to pat you, let them do so.&quot;</p>
<p>Susie hesitated for two seconds before saying, &quot;Alright.&quot;</p>
<p>Audrey didn&#x27;t say anything else. She left the shelter, completely ignored by the troop of soldiers guarding the shelter.</p>
<p>The sky outside was dark, and there were many buildings that had collapsed. They burned with flames that were about to be extinguished. The streets were empty, with no carriages or pedestrians.</p>
<p>This was completely different from what Audrey remembered of Backlund.</p>
<p>Backlund was originally blue, yellow, and beige. It was lively, bustling, and full of vitality. But now, it was gray, black, and scarlet. It was in shambles, disorder, and somewhat silent.</p>
<p>Looking left and right, Audrey identified her bearings while in her hunting attire, and walked towards the city borders.</p>
<p>What she wanted to do was simple:</p>
<p>Join the war and do her best to help Loen not collapse before the end of the battle of gods.</p>
<p>If the winner of the battle of gods was the opposing side, she would use various methods, such as &quot;Cue,&quot; &quot;Hypnosis,&quot; &quot;Mental Plague,&quot; and other means, to prevent the soldiers, officers, and Beyonders from venting their emotions, as well as reduce the damage brought by the war.</p>
<p>Amidst the flickering flames, Audrey quickly passed through them and ran into the distance.</p>

<p>Rorsted Archipelago, City of Generosity, Bayam. In a room at the top of the Cathedral of Waves.</p>

<p>Heh, I had already considered the fact that fellows from the Sailor pathway are more irritable, making them incapable of telling the difference between a joke and sarcasm. Once their anger erupts, they don&#x27;t even consider the overall situation. Otherwise, I wouldn&#x27;t even need to speak like this... Dogsh*t! <p>The Rorsted Archipelago, along with the surrounding seas, was shrouded by thick fog. It became indistinct and surreal.</p>
<p>A seabird flew past and attempted to land on the dock, but it failed to pass through it, unable to set foot.</p>
<p>The commoners in the archipelago continued living normally, aside from noticing the brewing storm.</p>
<p>This... Jahn Kottman&#x27;s dark blue pupils dilated significantly. He subconsciously turned his head to look at the Sea God&#x27;s Blessed, Blazing Danitz.</p>
<p>Danitz&#x27;s mouth gaped slightly as he forgot to close it. His shock was not lesser than the shock Sea King experienced.</p>

I will immediately convene a meeting with the key figures in Bayam to discuss this matter. I will give you an answer in an hour.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I will wait patiently.&quot;</p>
