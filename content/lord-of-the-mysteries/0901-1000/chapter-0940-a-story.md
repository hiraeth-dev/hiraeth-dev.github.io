<p>After leaving the local Nighthawks&#x27;s base which was disguised as a private detective office, Leonard glanced at Daly Simone who had suggested to team up with him.</p>
<p>&quot;Where do we begin? Any suggestions?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This is when a gentleman&#x27;s decisiveness and style should be showcased.&quot;</p>
<p>Leonard looked at his left hand which was wearing a red glove. He deliberated and said, &quot;If we begin by following Captain Soest&#x27;s arrangements, that might allow us to find some clues, but it&#x27;s not necessarily useful. <p>&quot;Why do you say that?&quot; Daly wasn&#x27;t joking as her expression wore a rare, solemn look.</p>

<p>Daly recalled the information regarding Ince Zangwill and nodded.</p>
<p>&quot;Soest had also mentioned this problem in the beginning. However, he didn&#x27;t verbalize it as clearly as you did. It&#x27;s still in the stage of backward inference from a result of failures.</p>
<p>&quot;Therefore, Her Excellency Goddess&#x27;s Eye has gotten us to investigate the situation of the nearby district&#x27;s Hunter pathway Beyonders and ingredients, and pretending as though nothing is confirmed. <p>Leonard turned his body and spoke as he walked:</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s probably the case, but I suspect that it&#x27;s unlikely for 0-08 to not know of it...&quot;</p>
<p>Daly followed diagonally behind as she said in thought, &quot;I believe there&#x27;s still a chance for success. Don&#x27;t forget that one of the honorific names of the Goddess is the Mother of Concealment.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That can counter 0-08? The Church has a high-ranking member or Sealed Artifact that can counter 0-08? Daly nodded very slightly as her expression turned soft.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, her pupils contracted as she blurted out, &quot;Do you think our discussion has caught the attention of 0-08?&quot;</p>
<p>Leonard&#x27;s expression fell, but he didn&#x27;t dare confirm anything nor shake his head. He and Daly looked at each other, looking back and forth as they fell silent for a moment.</p>

<p>&quot;...After leaving Bansy Harbor, Sauron Einhorn Medici, who had obtained a certain object, was no longer only stubborn or only capable of instinctively conspiring. <p>&quot;To an evil spirit of the Red Priest pathway, such a promise cannot be guaranteed to be effective, but Ince Zangwill no longer had any other choice.</p>
<p>&quot;From his point of view, this series of matters had many coincidences, but fundamentally, this was inevitable. At the very least, Sauron Einhorn Medici was many times better at creating stories than him.</p>
<p>&quot;...After confirming the destination to be the Southern Continent&#x27;s East Balam, Ince Zangwill boarded a ship to the Berserk Sea... <p>&quot;...Ince Zangwill&#x27;s purchase of additional Bard and Warrior pathway Beyonder ingredients was very reasonable. <p>&quot;...After numerous provocations, the Iron and Blood Cross Order&#x27;s Tony Down finally locked onto Ince Zangwill and began pursuing him. <p>&quot;This was a little coincidental, but it was nothing surprising. <p>&quot;Likewise, he was overly confident, causing him to neglect the possibility that Ince Zangwill was also on the ship. This didn&#x27;t match his intuition as a hunter, but in this world, anyone could make mistakes!</p>
<p>&quot;When Ince Zangwill disembarked on Waypoint Island, he sensed someone observing him from a first-class cabin, but he didn&#x27;t mind it. This was exactly the effect he wanted. It was the best development if someone among the passengers happened to know him! Yes, happened to...</p>
<p>&quot;... It was neither too early or too late. Before Soest&#x27;s Red Gloves took action, Ince Zangwill met with Hand of White Palenque Taciblius of the Numinous Episcopate&#x27;s Artificial Death faction, hoping to obtain their help in exorcising the evil spirit...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;...The development of things became a little odd. Before having a sufficient amount of clues, Leonard Mitchell and Daly Simone of the Soest&#x27;s Red Gloves team seemed to come to a conclusion. It apparently came from a letter sent by Reinette Tinekerr...</p>
<p>&quot;Where did the problem lie? Ince Zangwill was rather puzzled by this. From his point of view, unless one could directly capture Palenque Taciblius or other key personnel of the Numinous Episcopate, no one could come up with such a conclusion so quickly.</p>
<p>&quot;This made his preparations a little hasty, but thankfully, this was an outcome he wanted.</p>
<p>&quot;...The Red Gloves team led by Soest discovered Ince Zangwill&#x27;s abnormalities from the feedback from various telegrams. Daly Simone used this opportunity to mention the assumption of an evil spirit&#x27;s possession and had received a unanimous agreement.</p>
<p>&quot;She claimed it to be a reasonable theory, but she was already in the know. She had learned it from Leonard Mitchell, who has a Parasite, and Leonard Mitchell&#x27;s information source came from a letter sent by Reinette Tinekerr. Who could be the one who mailed it?</p>

<p>&quot;Of course, before beginning this operation, Ince Zangwill had to first satisfy Sauron Einhorn Medici&#x27;s thirst-a thirst for Mid-and High-Sequence Beyonder characteristics of the Hunter pathway. <p>&quot;When the evil spirit&#x27;s undying nature, Red Angel&#x27;s level, and the uniqueness of a Gatekeeper combined, Ince Zangwill possessed the relatively High Sequence ability of ‘Spirit World Traversal&#x27; of the Death pathway. <p>&quot;After satisfying Sauron Einhorn Medici, Ince Zangwill decided to first kill Daly Simone and Leonard Mitchell and pretend to vaguely sense the Church of Evernight&#x27;s trap. He would forcefully counterattack, only to lose his rationality and head for the trap.</p>
<p>&quot;He didn&#x27;t plan on leaving any unstable elements. All avengers that survived Tingen had to die!&quot; The notebook flipped to an empty spot as a pale-white hand grabbed a quill and added the words: &quot;Today, everything will go smoothly.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You aren&#x27;t done with your investigations?&quot; Anderson asked in boredom as he walked beside Danitz.</p>

<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t be nervous.&quot;</p>

<p>This figure had apparently been there all this time, but he had always been ignored.</p>
<p>He wore a strangely simple white robe. He had a pale gold beard that covered half his face. His light-colored eyes were clear like a child&#x27;s. He had a genial and reserved look. Looking at the quill in Ince Zangwill&#x27;s hand, this middle-aged man who was dressed like an ordinary priest half-closed his eyes, raised his right hand, and gestured four points before him.</p>
