<p>In a Tingen suburb, there was a house with a green lawn.</p>
<p>The house had a garden that had begun to wither at the beginning of September. Attached to the house was a crimson chimney.</p>
<p>There was a desk next to the window in the bedroom of that house. An ordinary notebook was spread open on the table.</p>
<p>A slightly pale hand flipped the notebook to the very first page, then it quickly flipped to the back.</p>
<p>As the paper flipped, the rows of words appeared faintly.</p>
<p>Regence, a member from the Secret Order, sold the Antigonus family&#x27;s notebook as an ordinary ancient book by accident due to the inﬂuence both of weariness and illusions. It was a coincidence that made logical sense.</p>
<p>Affected by the calling of the Antigonus family&#x27;s bloodline, the notebook secretly affects its owners, one after the other. After repeated changes in ownership, it came to Tingen. It fell into the hands of the members of the Aurora Order—Sirius Arapis and Hanass Vincent.</p>
<p>After they ﬂipped through the content that temporarily appeared in the notebook and copied the corresponding potion formulas, Sirius and Hanass were worried that the Secret Order, who were good at divination, would track them down. After discussing it, they decided to avoid the risk by selling it to another person. They didn&#x27;t wait for Mr. Z&#x27;s reply; perhaps because he stayed at Enmat Harbor.</p>
<p>Through Sirius&#x27;s introduction, Hanass got to know Welch McGovern from the Khoy University&#x27;s Department of History. Then, Hanass sold the Antigonus family&#x27;s notebook to McGovern as an ordinary ancient book.</p>
<p>Subsequently, Sirius was attracted by the treasure that was said to exist on the main peak of the Hornacis mountain range. He started to visit the Deweyville Library and research the mountain. He didn&#x27;t think that there would be any problems, so he left his real address and name. That aligned with his personality.</p>
<p>During this process, he acquainted himself with Lanevus, who was reading up on information about iron mines to set up a scam.</p>
<p>Lanevus&#x27;s hidden lunacy and swindling ability were well appreciated by Sirius. He decided to nurture him into a member of the Aurora Order. Of course, before that, an investigation was inevitable.</p>
<p>Sirius covertly informed Lanevus about the evil ritual of praying to the True Creator to deliver &quot;His&quot; spawn into this world. But he knew that the possibility of Lanevus succeeding was very low, because the difﬁculty level of the ritual was very high, and the requirements were very harsh. However, the latter expressed his strong interest in the matter, tempted by the possibility of obtaining a deity&#x27;s approval. He planned to complete the ritual simultaneously as he carried out the plans of establishing his steelworks company.</p>
<p>The cunning Lanevus could tell that there was something ﬁshy about Sirius Arapis. But for his personal goal, he didn&#x27;t expose him. He visited Hood Eugen in the asylum once again. They had known each other long ago, and they knew about their respective situations.</p>
<p>After a dark divination, the Antigonus family&#x27;s notebook was awakened fully. Welch and his classmate died. The lucky survivor, Klein Moretti, sent it to Ray Bieber&#x27;s house under the inﬂuence of the notebook. It was a destined ending.</p>

<p>In the Nighthawks&#x27; investigation of Welch&#x27;s case that followed, he met Dunn Smith and joined the Nighthawks.</p>

<p>When they were looking for their ﬁnal hideout, they chanced upon the room opposite Ray Bieber&#x27;s apartment.</p>

However, he was in a semi-insane state, and he couldn&#x27;t make the best and safest choice. He abandoned his dead mother, but he continued remaining in Tingen City. He only found a more elusive spot to carry out his digestion ritual. It was truly pathetic.
