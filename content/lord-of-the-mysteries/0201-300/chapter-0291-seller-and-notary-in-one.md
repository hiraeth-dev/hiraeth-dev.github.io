<p>&quot;Yes, I really don&#x27;t have the potion formula for Wind-blessed, nor will I have the means to obtain it anytime soon.</p>

<p>&quot;So, are you interested? The spinal fluid of a Dark Patterned Black Panther is worth between 500 and 700 pounds and the Spring of the Elves marrow crystals is between 300 and 400 pounds. <p>Well, there are also latent risks. I would be at a huge disadvantage if Mr. Hanged Man suddenly dies... With such an expensive deal at hand, Klein couldn&#x27;t help but feel a little apprehensive.</p>
<p>The incomplete formula of a Sequence 4 potion? An incomplete formula that leads to the path of a demigod? This is something that you can&#x27;t even buy with money! Audrey&#x27;s eyes lit up. <p>Mr. World actually has a Sequence 4 potion formula. Even if it&#x27;s incomplete, it&#x27;s still very shocking... No wonder Mr. Fool pulled him into the Tarot Club! <p>Miss Justice&#x27;s question was just what Klein wanted. He made The World reply in a husky voice, &quot;I was planning to say it anyway.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s the potion formula to Sequence 4 of the Sun pathway, Unshadowed.&quot; The Sun pathway? Derrick instantly looked towards Mr. World, who sat at other end of the long bronze table, his eyes filled with sudden eagerness and longing.</p>
<p>However, he maintained his silence because he knew that he was still far from the threshold of a demigod. He had to spend his limited resources to strengthen himself first.</p>
<p>Only caring about the near future was the way the City of Silver operated. This was because, to its residents, there might not be any future at any moment.</p>

<p>She felt like she had learned something important again.</p>

<p>This was completely different from the script he had imagined!</p>

<p>&quot;No, I really have no need for it.&quot; Alger shook his head. No need for it... He really has no need for it...
