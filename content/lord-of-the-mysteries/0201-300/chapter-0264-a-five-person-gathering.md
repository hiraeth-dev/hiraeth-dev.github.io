<p>Chapter 264: A Five­Person Gathering</p>
<p>If &quot;The Fool&quot; signified the beginning of the tarot club, then &quot;The World&quot; signified the end, a perfect ending of a cycle and sublimation. Klein had named his smurf &quot;The World&quot; because he hoped that it would lead to a beautiful beginning and end.</p>
<p>In the future, I&#x27;ll be able to make The World make requests or do things that wouldn&#x27;t be convenient for me. This will greatly reduce the possibility that my image as Mr. Fool will collapse. With an alt, no—a smurf truly is what makes life complete! Klein sighed in relief and waved The World away.</p>
<p>Following that, he spread out his spirituality, wrapped himself, and stimulated a descent into the gray fog and returned to the real world.</p>
<p>He had left Rosago&#x27;s All-Black Eye above the gray fog. After all, he couldn&#x27;t usually use the &quot;item&quot; anyway, and he had to constantly worry about having it discovered by others or losing it.</p>
<p>After putting away the summoning ritual materials, Klein looked up towards the crimson moonlight that passed through the curtains, filled with anticipation for tomorrow&#x27;s gathering.</p>
<p>When it came to understanding the aristocracy, he believed that Miss Justice would definitely surpass 99% of the people of Loen.</p>
<p>And with his smurf, The World, he could directly ask her about the Pound family without worrying about affecting his image as a The Fool! Of course, this had to be done in a more tactful manner. After all, Detective Sherlock Moriarty was also looking for information on the Pound family, and only with enough information would he be able to perform a divination above the gray fog.</p>
<p>Phew... Klein thought hard for a while before he relaxed and fell asleep.</p>
<p>After entering the gray fog, it was as though the remnants of the evil spirit&#x27;s aura had completely disappeared. Klein slept until dawn, but he didn&#x27;t see the sun because of the thin fog outside.</p>
<p>He continued as planned, heading to the rest of the public libraries in Backlund, but he no longer queried the librarians nor did he mention the Pound family. He only leafed through the materials concerning the nobility by himself.</p>
<p>At twenty minutes to three in the afternoon, Klein entered the mysterious space above the gray fog ahead of time.</p>
<p>Inside the vast and ancient palace, he sat down at the seat belonging to The Fool. He conjured the fake World on the opposite side of the mottled bronze table as he familiarized himself with operating The World.</p>
<p>After a few minutes, Klein took out his pocket watch and glanced at it, sending a message to the crimson star that symbolized The Sun to prepare for the gathering.</p>
<p>While he waited, he played with the All-Black Eye and &quot;fitted&quot; it with a silver chain. Then, he wound it around his right wrist and covered it with his sleeve. <p>At the same time, through his gray fog-augmented Spirit Vision, he discovered that the surface color of The Sun&#x27;s Astral Projection had become a little purer, but it had yet to reach the standards of Sequence 8.
