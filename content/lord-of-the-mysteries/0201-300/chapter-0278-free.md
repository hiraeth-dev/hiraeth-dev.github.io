<p>After dinner, in the billiard room of the Bravehearts Bar.</p>

<p>&quot;Maric isn&#x27;t here.</p>

<p>&quot;There&#x27;s no need.&quot;</p>

<p>She was still wearing her black Gothic regal dress and matching soft hat. Her face was as pale as ever, and her blonde hair and delicate features accentuated each other.</p>
<p>&quot;Good evening, Madam.&quot; Klein slightly bent his back and bowed.</p>
<p>&quot;Good evening, Ma&#x27;am. Sharron.&quot; Kaspars did the same.</p>
<p>So her name is Sharron... Klein thoughtfully waited for her to speak.</p>
<p>The lady named Sharron looked at Kaspars and said, &quot;Maric won&#x27;t be coming back here again.</p>
<p>&quot;If you need him for anything, leave a message according to the third method.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, Ma&#x27;am. Sharron.&quot; Kaspars, who had clearly seen a lot of things in his life, seemed to have an instinctive fear of Miss Bodyguard. Upon hearing this, Klein interrupted, &quot;If, I mean if—I wanted to get Maric&#x27;s help, how should I get in touch with him?&quot;</p>
