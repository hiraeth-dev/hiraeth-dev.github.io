<p>Klein&#x27;s lips curled up in a slight nod in response to Miss Justice&#x27;s pleasant greeting.</p>
<p>He was just about to introduce The Hermit to everyone, and everyone to The Hermit when he noticed that Miss Justice didn&#x27;t stop. Her gaze shifted to the other side as she greeted, &quot;Good afternoon, Mr. Hanged Man.&quot;</p>
<p>This time, she didn&#x27;t follow the order of the tarot cards but followed the order in which the person joined the Tarot Club.</p>
<p>After this round of greetings, The Hermit will essentially get to know everyone, and there&#x27;s no need for The Fool to introduce her... Miss Justice is obviously doing it on purpose. She&#x27;s really considerate. As long as a Beyonder of the Spectator domain gets serious, they act differently for matters like this... Klein was surprised for a moment before he praised her silently.</p>
<p>At the same time, Alger responded to Audrey&#x27;s greeting while cautiously sizing up the new member.</p>
<p>Female. It&#x27;s barely possible to tell that she&#x27;s wearing a black gown with many patterns on it... Her hair is black and her eyes are a little purple... Alger managed to find certain traits in her blurred, distorted image.</p>
<p>Suddenly, his heart sank as he recalled the scene he saw a few months ago at the pirate convention.</p>
<p>Back then, scattered starlight had magically formed a long bridge between the huge sailboat and the palace in the depths of the island. Admiral of Stars Cattleya, who was wearing a black classical robe, had walked in midair, carrying a celestial globe and a short scepter by her waist.</p>
<p>And the celestial globe resembled the strange weather bottle that Alger had previously received. The latter had coincidentally shattered after he had been pulled into the gathering by Mr. Fool.</p>
<p>Could it be her? She looks somewhat similar based on her attire... But the Admiral of Stars wears glasses, while she doesn&#x27;t... It cannot be conﬁrmed. It&#x27;ll require further observation and evidence. Unfortunately, I only saw her from afar and know little...</p>
<p>Regardless, I should be careful. When exchanging information, I should try my best to avoid divulging details about my actual situation... The World is Mr. Fool&#x27;s Blessed; letting him know of my identity is totally different compared to the others—no, the other members...</p>
<p>After the matter regarding the &quot;Sea God,&quot; Mr. Fool has recovered a considerable amount of strength. He&#x27;s even able to pull in a powerhouse like a pirate admiral?</p>
<p>Thoughts flashed through Alger&#x27;s mind as he slightly fidgeted in his seat.</p>
<p>Mr. Hanged Man&#x27;s body language tells me that he is alert and guarded... Does he know the female newcomer, or has he guessed something? How intriguing... Audrey&#x27;s gaze swept across without leaving a trace and landed on Derrick.</p>
<p>At this moment, Cattleya was also carefully and seriously examining the members of the Tarot Club in the order they were greeted in.</p>
<p>The mysterious purple color in her eyes slowly flowed in an indiscernible fashion. Although she couldn&#x27;t truly see through the blurry concealment of the fog, she could still make out the details of their clothing.</p>
<p>The Hanged Man, a man in his prime, in his thirties, and has messy hair like seaweed with a rare deep blue color. This is a common mutation seen in the Sailor pathway... He&#x27;s wearing a robe with the patterns of storms and waves... Someone from the Church of Storms? Cattleya&#x27;s pupils contracted. She didn&#x27;t show any abnormalities as she continued to observe the rest of the members.</p>
<p>The Sun, a young male, perhaps still underage. His attire is rare and has a simple ancient sense of beauty. It&#x27;s very tight, making it suitable for combat. Is he a Beyonder in this aspect?</p>
<p>The World, neither an old man nor a young man. He has a gloomy temperament and wears a hooded robe. Eh, he has a feeling as though he&#x27;s not a living person. Is this something similar to Senor or Steel Maveti? Or some other Sequence I&#x27;m unaware of...</p>
<p>The Magician, a young woman, wears a common Loen-styled dress. Apart from having a trait of languidness, she doesn&#x27;t have any other characteristics. This kind of person is the easiest to ignore, so she needs to have more attention paid to her.</p>
<p>The Moon, a young man. He sits very casually. Black hair, red eyes, and wears an Earth Mother priest robe... There seems to be only one possibility for such a combination—a vampire...</p>
<p>As expected, the members of Mr. Fool&#x27;s Tarot Club aren&#x27;t that simple. Just a preliminary judgment involves two Churches... Cattleya withdrew her gaze and met Audrey&#x27;s gaze.</p>
<p>Sensing the other party&#x27;s intention to ask, she nodded lightly.</p>
<p>&quot;The Hermit.&quot;</p>
<p>Cattleya had spoken in ancient Feysac, deliberately avoiding the more familiar languages of Intis and Loen, as she had done so back when she was communicating with Mr. Fool.</p>
<p>And she had noticed one thing. Mr. Fool had previously told her that the time the gathering was being held was at 3 p.m. on Monday, Backlund time.</p>
<p>Does this mean that the members of this gathering are currently predominantly made up of people from Loen? Cattleya made her own judgment.</p>
<p>&quot;Justice.&quot; Audrey smiled faintly in return. &quot;Good afternoon, Ma&#x27;am Hermit.&quot;</p>
<p>She had noticed what The Hermit had been up to and had summarized the person&#x27;s traits in her heart.</p>
<p>She&#x27;s a very cautious woman who has rich experience in the Beyonder world. She&#x27;s quiet but not introverted, and she&#x27;s very conﬁdent in her observation skills or certain abilities related to her eyes. Furthermore, her clothing style and the short scepter hanging at her waist indicates that her Sequence pathway leans towards mystery. This requires conﬁrmation. Perhaps it&#x27;s just a hobby. At the same time, Cattleya also managed to clearly make out Miss Justice&#x27;s clothing.</p>
<p>She wears two hanging earrings, and the earrings are small and exquisite, inlaid with very high quality emeralds which accentuate her emerald-green eyes. They&#x27;re obviously worth a lot...</p>
<p>The necklace around her neck looks unique. It&#x27;s lined with diamonds that are quite difﬁcult to count, but it&#x27;s nothing ostentatious...</p>
<p>Her milky-white dress is simple yet beautiful. It&#x27;s tailored to be elegant and beﬁtting of her status, like the work of a stylish designer...</p>
<p>As she went through the details, Cattleya couldn&#x27;t help but close her eyes.</p>
<p>A 17-18-year-old girl... Very rich... The way she moves and her etiquette makes her highly suspect to be a noble with high status, a Loen noble? Cattleya didn&#x27;t know why she sighed in her heart.</p>
<p>After a round of greetings from Audrey, there was no need for Klein to introduce the original members of the Tarot Club to The Hermit or The Hermit to the rest.</p>
