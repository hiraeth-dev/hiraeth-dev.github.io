<p>Symeem Island, the island at the farthest end of the Rorsted Archipelago. From Bayam, it&#x27;ll take four to ﬁve hours to get there by liner, and there are only two trips a day, at 9 a.m. and 10 a.m., respectively. Wendt&#x27;s parents are long dead and he has no relatives. The only person involved is a girl he just can&#x27;t forget, Raine. <p>He tried to recall the novels he had read and the movies and TV dramas he had watched, hoping for the most perfect solution.</p>
<p>Soon, he had a general idea of what to do, stabilizing his mood in the process. His thoughts began to drift towards the matter regarding Steel Maveti.</p>
<p>I hope Miss Magician will get a radio transceiver as soon as possible... Klein sighed silently and got onto a rental carriage.</p>
<p>...</p>

<p>He introduced three types, with detailed addresses and approximate prices. The most expensive is just twelve pounds. Fors nodded slightly, feeling that this wasn&#x27;t a very important business transaction.</p>
<p>She suddenly felt her self-esteem was a little inflated. Perhaps because she had seen so many hundreds and thousands of pounds transactions in the Tarot Club, she no longer paid much notice to anything in the range of ten pounds.</p>

Yes, I&#x27;ll just add postage, my transportation costs, and the price of the materials expended for the ritual to the original price... <p>I wonder when I&#x27;ll receive the Spirit Eater&#x27;s stomach pouch from The Sun... Fors couldn&#x27;t wait to advance. ...</p>
<p>In the Berg household in the City of Silver.</p>

<p>Humans who walked inside were exceptionally small, and they revered the owner of this scene from the bottom of their hearts.</p>
<p>... Backlund, Hillston Borough, Waymandy&#x27;s house.</p>
<p>After listening to an explanation, Emlyn White combed his hair with his hand and intentionally asked, &quot;Lord Baron, I forgot where I heard it from, but there was a very famous city named the City of Silver during the Second Epoch or in the early half of the Third Epoch.&quot;</p>

<p>His black hair was neatly combed back, and he was wearing a dark red cotton shirt with a brown pipe in his hand. As he enjoyed the warmth of the fireplace, he said thoughtfully, &quot;No, at least in my memory, there&#x27;s no City of Silver before the Cataclysm.&quot;</p>
<p>Before Emlyn could even revel in his joy, Waymandy went on to say to himself, &quot;But there was a Kingdom of Silver which was originally ruled by the Giant King&#x27;s Court and later came under its jurisdiction.&quot;</p>
<p>Kingdom of Silver? Emlyn White thought for a while and said, &quot;Lord Baron, is there anything more specific?&quot;</p>
<p>Waymandy looked up at him, smiled, and said reminiscently, &quot;The Kingdom of Silver had a rather special position in the Giant King&#x27;s Court. They didn&#x27;t directly believe in the Giant King, Aurmir, and instead, they believed in the Giant King&#x27;s Court&#x27;s queen, Omebella.&quot;</p>
<p>... City of Generosity, Bayam, dock area. Acid Lemon Street, Wind of Azure Inn.</p>
<p>Klein stood at the corner of the street, undid the topaz pendant on his left wrist, and divined to ensure that there was no danger ahead.</p>
<p>With that in mind, he strolled back to the inn, went up to the third floor, and opened the door of the luxurious suite.</p>
<p>He was slightly surprised to find Blazing Danitz already back, slumped in his reclining chair, drinking.</p>
<p>After a moment of consideration, Klein calmly asked, &quot;What time is it?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Isn&#x27;t there a clock?&quot; Danitz muttered.</p>
<p>He looked at the wall opposite him and read out the time.</p>
<p>&quot;3:40 p.m....&quot;</p>
<p>Before he finished his sentence, Danitz suddenly came to his senses, sat up straight, and laughed dryly.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ve already gone to the places I can get information from and asked the people I can ask. There&#x27;s no need to stay outside any longer. It will increase the risk of exposure and affect your hunting plan!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What problems could there be? Although I&#x27;m mainly an adventurer who hunts treasure, I&#x27;m also a qualified part-time pirate.&quot; Danitz felt that his abilities had been insulted.</p>
<p>After being swept by Gehrman Sparrow with a cold glance, he smiled and gave a brief overview of the places he went, the people he met, and the news he had heard from the morning and afternoon.</p>

Is he some powerhouse of higher standing in the Church of Storms who&#x27;s disguised as a pirate, or someone who has some sort of relationship with the heretics in Bansy Harbor? Hmm... <p>&quot;You know him? That guy isn&#x27;t simple!&quot; Danitz sighed. As expected... Heh heh, I really didn&#x27;t expect to meet him so easily... Klein didn&#x27;t respond; instead, he leaned forward and said, &quot;Continue.&quot;</p>
