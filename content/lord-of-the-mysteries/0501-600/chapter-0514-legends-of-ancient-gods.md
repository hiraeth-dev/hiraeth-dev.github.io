<p>Above the endless gray fog, the majestic palace stood still.</p>
<p>Just as Emlyn White, who had arrived here once again, regained his vision, he saw Miss Justice opposite him looking at the end of the mottled long table before greeting cheerfully, &quot;Good afternoon, Mr. Fool~&quot;</p>
<p>Audrey greeted everyone, one person at a time, starting with The Fool and ending with The World.</p>
<p>This order wasn&#x27;t according to the seating arrangement, but by the order of the tarot cards—The Fool was the beginning that contained all possibilities, and The World was the end that represented perfect sublimation.</p>
<p>This was a little obsessive-compulsive disorder on Miss Audrey&#x27;s part, a mysticism enthusiast. It started to appear with the increasing number of Tarot Club members.</p>
<p>She really is a carefree girl of stately birth... Emlyn deliberately passed a deep judgment and politely responded to the greeting.</p>
<p>During this process, he caught sight of The Sun through the corner of his eye. He couldn&#x27;t help but think of the recent days when he had been studying the detailed history of the Sanguine at the home of Baron Waymandy.</p>
<p>There are a lot of details that I don&#x27;t know about. There are indeed differences between professional researchers and amateur enthusiasts, but from this week onwards, I, Emlyn White, would be a professional researcher... Baron Waymandy has never mentioned anything related to the City of Silver, so I can&#x27;t ask him directly. As the secret messiah of the Sanguine who is burdened with secrets, I have to be careful in such matters... Attending class for half a day and going to the cathedral for half a day before accompanying the dolls at night seems to be a pretty good life... Emlyn&#x27;s thoughts gradually dispersed.</p>
<p>Suddenly, he frowned and realized a very important question.</p>
<p>Why do I still go to the Harvest Church every day?</p>
<p>Yes, as a noble Sanguine, I must put an end to the things I start. There have been a lot of patients recently. Sigh, having your reputation spread isn&#x27;t a good thing... Emlyn&#x27;s brow gradually relaxed into a softer frown.</p>
<p>He saw that Miss Justice had completed her greetings. He raised his hand to knock on the table and looked at her.</p>
<p>&quot;The things you need...&quot;</p>
<p>Audrey pressed her right hand down and made a gesture to stop him before saying with a faint smile, &quot;We&#x27;ll talk about this during the transaction period.</p>
<p>&quot;This is Mr. Fool&#x27;s reading time.&quot;</p>
<p>Based on Mr. Vampire&#x27;s tone, there must be at least one of the fruits of the Tree of Elders and the blood of a Mirror Dragon... Audrey, you&#x27;re going to be a Psychiatrist soon! You&#x27;re about to become a Mid-Sequence Beyonder! Audrey&#x27;s mood became increasingly cheerful, the corners of her eyes filled with smiles. She looked towards the figure amidst the thick fog and said with an uplifted tone towards the end, &quot;Honorable Mr. Fool, I&#x27;ve gotten another three Roselle diary pages~&quot;</p>
<p>Just short of four pages... She pursed her lips while in thought.</p>
<p>With Miss Justice setting an example, Fors also said, &quot;Mr. Fool, I also have three new pages of Roselle&#x27;s diary.&quot;</p>
<p>She was also full of anticipation for this Tarot Gathering because her second Apprentice potion had been digested much faster than expected. The dormant dangers had already been completely eliminated the day before yesterday.</p>

<p>He really is a good child who doesn&#x27;t need anyone to remind him... Klein, who had a rich harvest, sighed with emotion.</p>
<p>Seriously, he hasn&#x27;t improved at all... Even though this will lower the difﬁculty of me obtaining beneﬁts, it also conceals the risk of me losing the resources from the City of Silver... Exposure is a problem that requires constant vigilance... Alger shook his head indiscernibly.</p>
<p>Roselle&#x27;s diary and the legend of the ancient gods soon appeared in the palm of Klein&#x27;s hand. He skimmed through it quickly and found that two of the six pages in the diary were repetitions of the past. For example, one of Miss Justice&#x27;s contributed pages matched a page which The Hanged Man had handed over last year.</p>
<p>That&#x27;s inevitable unless I can really teach them Simpliﬁed Chinese... Klein sighed and willed the two diary pages to the back.</p>
<p>The remaining four pages didn&#x27;t contain very valuable information. Most of them belonged to the period when Roselle had lost his ideals and was obsessed with money, and the text was filled with sentences such as &quot;What did I invent today,&quot; &quot;What will I invent tomorrow,&quot; &quot;How many gold coins can it be sold for,&quot; or &quot;how much investment I received.&quot; It nearly made Klein lose control of his expression, wishing to smack these diary pages in Roselle&#x27;s face.</p>
<p>At this moment, Audrey keenly discovered that the Black Emperor card had returned to the side of Mr. Fool ‘s hand and was placed face down.</p>
<p>So it really was bestowed to his Blessed! Was it to help him complete a mission? Hmm... or is there another possibility, that this is a new Card of Blasphemy, and not the original one! Audrey guessed with interest.</p>
<p>After flipping through the diary pages, Klein resisted the urge to take a deep breath to calm his heart, and he looked at the legends of the ancient god provided by Little Sun.</p>
<p>It was quite a rough description. It divided the eight ancient gods of the Second Epoch into three camps. The Giant King, Aurmir; the Elf King, Soniathrym; and the Vampire Ancestor, Lilith, were allies who fought against the Dragon of Imagination, Ankewelt; the Phoenix Ancestor, Gregrace, and the Mutant King, Kvastir. As for the Devil Monarch, Farbauti and the Annihilation Demonic Wolf, Flegrea, they were the ones who wanted to subvert all order and corrupt all living beings.</p>
<p>In the records of the City of Silver, the eight ancient gods were described as violent, evil, cruel, and terrifying. Even the Ancestor Vampire, Lilith, who superficially looked the most normal and was described as the Embodiment of Beauty, had a disgusting and ferocious side. For instance, she would manifest into a mountain range of flesh. On top of it grew all kinds of reproductive organs as they spewed out thick black fog while constantly producing all sorts of strange creatures. All powerhouses who approached &quot;Her&quot; would be overwhelmed by the desire to mate and reproduce, becoming a walking organ.</p>
<p>And all living beings who saw this scene—other than the other ancient gods—would lose their minds without exception or mutate on the spot, while others became crazy. The records of the City of Silver originated from the ancient texts of the Giant King&#x27;s Court. Of course, that was what they claimed.</p>
<p>I cannot eliminate the possibility that this is to discredit an ally... This aspect is rather similar to the loss of control of the Primordial Moon who the Sanguine pray to... Is this one of the traits when one follows the Moon pathway to the end? At this thought, Klein almost raised his head to sweep his glance at Emlyn White.</p>
<p>In the legends of the City of Silver, the Vampire Ancestor, Lilith, really did control the crimson moon. <p>This is the power of a deity... I wonder what the difference is between the ancient gods and the current true gods. Why do all of them look like evil gods... Or could it be that there was a change midway, from the one recorded in the City of Silver to the one depicted in the legends of the Sanguine? Klein looked away and took note of the details.</p>
