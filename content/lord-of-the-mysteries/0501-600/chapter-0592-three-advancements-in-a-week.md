<p>What has &quot;me receiving the mission the day before yesterday&quot; have to do with when I ﬁnd him? <p>&quot;But I have no way of confirming it...&quot; Darkwill said in hesitation.</p>
<p>&quot;This is because it involves an important Sealed Artifact of the Life School of Thought,&quot; Klein said directly. Darkwill immediately jumped in fright as he took two steps back and looked around warily, afraid that someone had heard what Klein had just said.</p>
<p>Can this sort of thing be said out on the street? He knows that we&#x27;re from the Life School of Thought, and he knows that Old Man is involved in an important Sealed Artifact... I only learned of that from my last communication with him... Darkwill gradually began to believe the adventurer in front of him.</p>
<p>Klein swept a glance at the owl who swooped down and landed on the chubby Apothecary&#x27;s shoulder.</p>
<p>&quot;You can make your payment.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I can&#x27;t confirm it. I can&#x27;t just believe what you say, right...&quot; Darkwill said stubbornly.</p>
<p>Then, he saw the eyes of the adventurer in front of him turn abnormally cold.</p>
<p>He trembled and hurriedly said, &quot;Alright. I&#x27;ll barely count it as you completing the mission.&quot;</p>
<p>Unwillingly, he took out a wad of cash from a secret pocket in his witchdoctor robes and handed Klein 100 pounds in change.</p>
<p>Although he was a Beyonder, the physical enhancements he obtained from his job as an Apothecary and Beast Tamer wasn&#x27;t enough to play a role in direct combat. He wasn&#x27;t necessarily able to defeat an ordinary person with a revolver. As for the Beyonder pet he reared, it was also a fellow without any combat powers. Faced with an experienced and resourceful adventurer who was also likely a Beyonder, both he and the owl together wasn&#x27;t necessarily his match. All he could do was act as he said.</p>
<p>It wasn&#x27;t easy saving up that amount of money... he lamented inwardly.</p>
<p>It was indeed profitable being an Apothecary, even more so when out in the colonies. There were pirates, sailors, and adventurers. There were all sorts of people, and the official organizations were more relaxed on enforcing the rules. He could sell certain banned medicine without anyone coming to knock at his door. For the past two months, just the customers from the Red Theater was enough to earn him more than what he previously earned in a year.</p>
<p>However, as a Beyonder, his expenses were huge as well. The savings he had had primarily been expended when advancing to Beast Tamer. Later, he reared a pet and, with great difficulty, bought the potion formula and ingredients for it. He also purchased defensive charms for himself, leaving him only a few hundred pounds in savings.</p>
<p>Klein received the cash and, while counting it, verified their authenticity before putting it in his pocket and leaving.</p>
<p>After seeing Klein&#x27;s figure disappear from the alley, Darkwill&#x27;s expression sank.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s too dangerous here... Too dangerous... I have to leave as quickly as possible! As he mumbled, he returned inside his herb store. Once inside, he dragged a brown suitcase and quickly stuffed all sorts of clothes and cash which had been folded neatly into it.</p>
<p>Finally, he poured out the notes and coins of different denominations from the cash register, stuffed them into his pocket, and took the fake identification documents he had spent a fortune on. With the brown suitcase, he walked out of his folk herb store.</p>
<p>Looking back at the various medicinal ingredients he hadn&#x27;t finished selling yet, Darkwill&#x27;s chubby face clearly twitched.</p>
<p>He took a breath and locked the door while resisting his aching heart. He went straight for the harbor by getting a rental carriage, heading for Bayam&#x27;s liner ticketing company.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s too dangerous, way too dangerous. Old Man has been caught... He&#x27;s been caught... he sat in the carriage and repeated himself silently as he trembled.</p>
<p>In this state, he finally arrived at the ticketing company, and after paying for the carriage, he rushed into the lobby and queued up for the liner to East Balam.</p>
<p>Phew... Phew... Darkwill kept taking a few deep breaths as he closely followed the customer ahead of him and shifted forward.</p>
<p>&quot;Get me the first ship out of here,&quot; he emphasized to himself.</p>
<p>As he sporadically inched forward, Darkwill finally calmed down.</p>
<p>His expression occasionally turned twisted before quickly calming down. This repeated numerous times.</p>
<p>When there was only one customer left in front of him, Darkwill paused on the spot.</p>
<p>Dumbass! You dumbass! he cursed himself and immediately turned around, carried his suitcase, and walked out the ticketing lobby with his identification documents.</p>
