<p>What happened? The sudden change made the inexperienced Hazel momentarily lost as to how to react. She stood there in dazed silence for a few seconds before pushing open the door and rushing in.</p>
<p>When she arrived beside the charred rat, the existence that claimed to be a demigod rolled in a fit, speaking with a calm tone, &quot;You forgot to close the door.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Ah...&quot; Hazel first wore a blank look before realizing that she had failed to close the door like she usually did because of her eagerness to access the situation. Doing so prevented the maids from peeping in.</p>
<p>With the rat appearing fine, Hazel pursed her lips and turned around, walking back to the door.</p>
