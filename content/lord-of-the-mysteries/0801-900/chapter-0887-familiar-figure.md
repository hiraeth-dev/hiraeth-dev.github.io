<p>&quot;I can&#x27;t do anything about it either. I&#x27;m still in my mother&#x27;s womb!</p>

<p>Following that, he gave his suggestion:</p>
<p>&quot;Perhaps we can do this, you can summon my messenger when you&#x27;re born to write to me.&quot;</p>

<p>Klein&#x27;s eyes darted about for a while before saying, &quot;That works.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;His name is Azik Eggers.&quot; As Mr. Azik hadn&#x27;t replied yet, Klein couldn&#x27;t help but feel worried. He heard a dragged out and distant breathing, making it impossible for him to interpret what the dream meant.</p>
