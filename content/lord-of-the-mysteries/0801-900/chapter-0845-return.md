<p>It really is the spirit world... Klein was delighted as he inwardly muttered to himself.</p>
<p>Without needing further confirmation, his spirituality and spiritual perception had told him that this was the real spirit world!</p>
<p>And this meant that he had returned to the real world, the place with all kinds of delicacies.</p>
<p>I nearly cried tears of joy... Klein inwardly made a self-deprecating comment as he considered where he was supposed to head to next.</p>
<p>From the position of the stars, moon, and sun, it&#x27;s still morning. If there&#x27;s no time difference between the real world and the foggy town, it&#x27;s at most 7:30 a.m., or maybe earlier. At this point, the servants must&#x27;ve discovered the disappearance of their mate, and they would deﬁnitely inform the priests and bishops.</p>
<p>Even if they have received the corresponding training and would report to their superiors according to the protocol, they will have to ﬁrst eliminate the possibility that someone was skiving away or having a stomachache. They would need more than ten minutes before conﬁrming this and begin taking the necessary actions.</p>
<p>And after the report to their superiors, the priests and bishops wouldn&#x27;t be able to instantly tell the severity of the matter. They would only believe that the servant had escaped, and they wouldn&#x27;t quickly connect this matter to the Keepers. After they do a divination or investigation to ﬁgure out the truth, it would be twenty to thirty minutes later.</p>
<p>That also means that they likely haven&#x27;t begun their search in the surrounding area to ﬁnd the intruder. The identity of Dwayne Dantès hasn&#x27;t been exposed yet.</p>
<p>Hmm, if the Bizarro Sorcerer potion formula given by Zaratul is real, then the identity of Dwayne Dantès still has many uses. If I can still keep the identity going, I should try harder to not abandon it.</p>
<p>Besides, the only things that I&#x27;m wearing, which are real, are my glove and underwear. If I head elsewhere, I&#x27;ll be a pervert...</p>
<p>Klein already had a choice he was leaning towards. He took out the iron cigar case, which appeared to be inside his pocket, but was actually lodged under a rubber band. He opened it and took out the gold coin inside.</p>
<p>&quot;Returning to 160 Böklund Street is dangerous,&quot; Klein muttered seven times, and with a flick, he saw it slowly spin in the spirit world, wobbling up and down, left and right, before landing on his palm.</p>
<p>This time, it was tails, indicating a negative response!</p>
<p>Klein nodded indiscernibly and immediately traveled towards Backlund.</p>
<p>After three stops, his figure finally appeared in his room at 160 Böklund Street. The curtains were drawn tightly and it was dim. It was very suitable for sleeping. And on the bed, Dwayne Dantès was lying on the bed facing up. His hands were grabbing the ends of the blanket near his neck.</p>
<p>From the looks of it, the investigation hasn&#x27;t reached me... This appearance is ridiculous... Klein secretly heaved a sigh of relief and saw &quot;Dwayne Dantès&quot; dissipate and turn into a palm-sized mirror.</p>
<p>Water ripples appeared on the mirror&#x27;s surface as silver light bloomed and turned into Loenese text:</p>
<p>&quot;Exalted Great Master, did you encounter something? Your loyal and humble servant, Arrodes, failed to sense you before!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;It wasn&#x27;t anything important,&quot; Klein answered perfunctorily.</p>
<p>This made him confirm that Arrodes was unable to pry into the secrets that happened in the foggy town. After all, this fellow was able to present the environment where the rampaging Zaratul was.</p>
<p>It really is related to the authorities of a deity? Klein&#x27;s mind stirred as he asked, &quot;Did anyone look for me?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No, no one came to disturb you.&quot; The silver on the mirror&#x27;s surface changed and produced new words.</p>
<p>Klein felt truly relieved as he said to Arrodes, &quot;You may leave. I&#x27;ll summon you again if needed.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Alright, Master. Goodbye, Master~&quot; Like before, Arrodes produced a drawing of a hand waving on the mirror. After the aqueous light vanished, the mirror was restored to normal. Klein walked to the bedside and pulled out the pajamas underneath and wore it.</p>
<p>Then, he took out candles and other items before entering the bathroom. He set up a sacrificial ritual, preparing to send Creeping Hunger, Azik&#x27;s copper whistle, the iron cigar case, and the various mysticism materials above the gray fog to avoid any investigations he might face.</p>
<p>After doing all of this, Klein didn&#x27;t delay and sat at The Fool&#x27;s seat. He conjured the Bizarro Sorcerer&#x27;s potion formula in front of him.</p>
<p>&quot;Sequence 4: Bizarro Sorcerer.</p>
<p>&quot;Main ingredient: Bizarro Bane&#x27;s main eye, the true soul body of a Spirit World Plunderer.</p>
<p>&quot;Supplementary ingredients: 200 ml of a Bizarro Bane&#x27;s blood, 30 grams of a Spirit World Plunderer, 10 grams of Red-hair Birch bark. One segment of golden grapevines, fingernail-sized Self-made Rubber Mask.</p>
<p>&quot;Advancement ritual: Relying on one&#x27;s strength and strategy, orchestrate a grand performance before many spectators to kill a Beyonder creature at the level of a demigod. Then, at the end of the performance, consume the potion.&quot;</p>
<p>Klein didn&#x27;t consider what this ritual actually meant. Beneath the potion formula, he wrote a corresponding statement.</p>
<p>Following that, he summoned a spirit pendulum from the junk pile. Holding it in his left hand, he began a divination.</p>
<p>Before long, Klein opened his eyes and saw the dangling topaz rotating clockwise.</p>
<p>This meant that the Bizarro Sorcerer potion formula he had was genuine!</p>
<p>Phew... Klein exhaled as he took the opportunity to determine what kind of changes Creeping Hunger had experienced, as well as what the various negative side effects were.</p>
<p>After a dream divination and several direct attempts, he figured out the exact situation in less than two minutes.</p>
<p>Creeping Hunger could now Graze seven souls. During each Grazing process, it could obtain two or three Beyonder powers. One of them can be chosen by the wearer.</p>

<p>Following that, he washed his face and brushed his teeth to make Dwayne Dantès look sharp.</p>
