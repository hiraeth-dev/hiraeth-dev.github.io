<p>Upon gaining recognition, Derrick was taken aback for two seconds. His shame lessened as he said, somewhat embarrassed, &quot;I will take further steps to understand this matter.&quot;</p>
<p>I will work hard to make two sources of information, no—a friend before the next Tarot Gathering... Derrick quickly made a target for himself.</p>
<p>Upon seeing this, Klein made The World hoarsely say, &quot;If it involves the domain of Death, you can seek my advice.&quot;</p>
<p>And I can seek Mr. Azik&#x27;s advice... he silently added.</p>
<p>As for Frank Lee&#x27;s new mushrooms, he had no plans on transferring them to Little Sun, as they were still incomplete. The &quot;fruits&quot; that were eventually produced were filled with poison and madness.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you, Mr. World,&quot; Derrick answered gratefully.</p>
<p>After a short exchange regarding other matters, the Tarot Club entered the &quot;learning&quot; segment until it ended.</p>
<p>Returning to the real world, Klein immediately wanted to resolve the problem of the Marauder demigod, but the plan he conceived of was met with an obstacle at the first step.</p>
<p>He had no idea where to find Leonard Mitchell and the grandpa inside his body! Saint Samuel Cathedral? Leonard is most probably underground, but I have no way of entering... He only prays in the cathedral once or twice a week, and he doesn&#x27;t do it at a ﬁxed time. I can&#x27;t be heading there thrice a day all week just to meet him, right? What kind of crappy plot is this? Is this what&#x27;s called &quot;a stake-out&quot;? Even if I really did it, it might not be effective. As a Red Glove, he might&#x27;ve left Backlund... As Klein lampooned, he felt a deep sense of regret. He regretted being too focused on the sophistry and in fooling Leonard Mitchell; thus, forgetting to ask for his contact method.</p>
<p>I should&#x27;ve said to Leonard, &quot;I will inform Klein Moretti about his identity being exposed. If he has anything he wishes to say, I will pass it on for him.&quot; That way, I&#x27;ll be able to establish a private method of contact... Klein exhaled slowly. All he could do was use his final solution.</p>
<p>That was to ask the magic mirror!</p>
<p>Drawing the symbols that implied &quot;concealment&quot; and &quot;mystery prying,&quot; Klein cast his gaze at the full-body mirror. He saw aqueous light ripple, producing white Loenese text:</p>
<p>&quot;Exalted Great Master, your puny, loyal and humble, terrified servant, Arrodes, is here to answer your summoning.</p>
<p>&quot;Before answering any questions, I want to say:</p>
<p>&quot;I was wrong! I was wrong!&quot;</p>
<p>Klein pricked up his brows and asked, &quot;Why are you suddenly admitting to a mistake?&quot; On the mirror, the white Loenese words warped and turned into new words:</p>
<p>&quot;In short, I was wrong...&quot;</p>
<p>After a series of ellipses, the white words trembled into shape.</p>
<p>&quot;Recently, there have been many people trying to find out about you, and they have learned of the reputation of your present identity...&quot;</p>
<p>So, Dwayne Dantès&#x27;s amorous preference of liking anyone beautiful has spread, so much so that even Miss Justice knows of it? Well, that&#x27;s good. I used the explanation that it&#x27;s a shared identity. More than one Blessed plays as Dwayne Dantès, so having a myriad of preferences can be explained... Hehe, look at this mirror. It&#x27;s scared white... Klein was somewhat enlightened as he secretly laughed before saying, &quot;It&#x27;s your turn to ask.&quot;</p>
<p>The full-body mirror&#x27;s words remained white as it formed new words:</p>
<p>&quot;Will you forgive me?</p>
<p>&quot;No, I mean, are you willing to watch my subsequent performance?&quot;</p>
<p>This attitude... Klein tsked inwardly and said with a sullen expression, &quot;Work hard.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, Great Master!&quot; The mirror surface&#x27;s ghastly white words bloomed with silver light. &quot;Since you have summoned me, do you have a question to ask me?&quot;</p>
<p>Klein nodded.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.</p>
<p>&quot;Where will Leonard Mitchell be living in the next few days?&quot;</p>
<p>The silver marks distorted quickly, forming new words:</p>
<p>&quot;7 Pinster Street.&quot;</p>
<p>Beneath the words, the aqueous light rippled, forming a scene:</p>
<p>It was a terrace house numbered Unit 7. There was a black-haired, green-eyed youth just about to get his keys.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s the same old place. There hasn&#x27;t been any change... If I were to pay a visit directly, it will sully Leonard&#x27;s impression of Dwayne Dantès. It will be quite a step down... Get Emlyn White to go? Leonard has probably ﬁgured out that the vampire and Sherlock Moriarty, who is also Klein Moretti, have ties... The current problem is that it&#x27;s hard to determine that grandpa&#x27;s stance... I have no way of conﬁrming &quot;His&quot; true motives. Giving &quot;Him&quot; a big gift based on his present state might not be appropriate. Perhaps it will bring extreme danger to Leonard... As it doesn&#x27;t involve myself, doing a divination above the gray fog won&#x27;t be effective... Thoughts arose in Klein&#x27;s mind as he changed his plans.</p>
<p>Compared to directly informing the grandpa in Leonard&#x27;s body about the Marauder demigod, using Pallez Zoroast&#x27;s or Amon&#x27;s name to warn the target to force him to leave the area was a milder method that led to fewer repercussions!</p>
<p>Of course, the premise is that I don&#x27;t expose myself... Klein pondered for a few seconds and asked again, &quot;Where is the demigod beside Hazel Macht hiding?&quot;</p>
<p>The mirror&#x27;s surface had aqueous light ripple out as the scene changed.</p>
<p>On a thick carpet with beautiful embroidery, there was a small leather sofa. On the surface of the single-seater was a white, furry cushion. In the middle of it was a gray rat. Compared to its kind, its eyes were closer to dark red.</p>
<p>Rat... That Marauder demigod has parasitized a rat? And he&#x27;s sleeping inside Hazel&#x27;s room in broad daylight? He got himself what looks like a very expensive cushion... He had to transform into this because I foiled his plans? Klein was surprised before he felt a little amused.</p>
<p>The scene fixed as silver lines surfaced:</p>
<p>&quot;Great Master, what other instructions do you have for me?&quot;</p>
<p>Very sharp... Klein tersely answered and said, &quot;Use the mirror in the room to warn that demigod.</p>
<p>&quot;Tell him that all around this street there is an angel from the Marauder pathway with ill intentions plying it. Furthermore, Blasphemer Amon might come at any time.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Alright, Master. I&#x27;ll do it immediately!&quot; The words on the mirror sparkled.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>In Hazel&#x27;s room, the gray rat felt his spiritual perception stirred as he hurriedly stood up and cast his gaze on the full-body mirror in the room.</p>
<p>On the surface of the mirror, words that seemed to be written with fresh blood that had yet to coagulate appeared.</p>
<p>&quot;Leave this area!&quot;</p>
<p>The gray rat&#x27;s gaze froze for a second as it fell silent for a moment.</p>
<p>&quot;Why?&quot;</p>
<p>The blood seemed to flow as the words spread out and formed new words:</p>
<p>&quot;The surrounding area has an angel from the Marauder pathway in urgent need of replenishment plying it. This pathway is the nemesis of all High-Sequence Beyonders. Blasphemer Amon is rushing over.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m warning you because I do not wish for ‘Them&#x27; to benefit.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ve already answered one question of yours. Based on the principle of reciprocity, it&#x27;s my turn to ask.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;After you hurriedly parasitized a rat, you should be influenced by the body&#x27;s construct and hormones. Now, which entity will make you have the desire to mate:</p>

<p>&quot;Please answer.&quot;</p>

<p>This strength wasn&#x27;t anything for it to fear when it was in its optimal state, but now, everything it had accumulated had been drained. The latest action was to infuse a dream to the Beyonder bodyguard whom Dwayne Dantès had hired.</p>
