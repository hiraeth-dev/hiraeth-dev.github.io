<p>Looking at the young man with bright red eyes, I snap to my senses. The first thought I have is:</p>
<p>What a waste of a handsome face!</p>
<p>After two seconds, I become somewhat worried.</p>
<p>Is this guy mentally challenged, or just simply mental?</p>
<p>Is he the type of person who easily loses his way to the point that a missing report pamphlet is handed out the very next day?</p>
<p>No, he answered me very ﬂuently just now. He looks ﬁne on the surface... Perhaps he&#x27;s just bad with directions? Woah! How can someone like this be allowed outside the house on their own? I hesitate for a moment before asking, &quot;Do you have your family&#x27;s number? Do you need to give them a call?&quot;</p>
<p>The man&#x27;s expression instantly turns extremely ugly, giving off the feeling that he wants to bury his head in the ground.</p>
<p>&quot;There&#x27;s no need!&quot; he answers firmly. &quot;It was just a mistake. A mistake. I wasn&#x27;t focused, as I was thinking about other problems.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;There&#x27;s no need to explain,&quot; I immediately respond. Trying to explain it just shows your attempts to hide things... I add inwardly.</p>
<p>Following that, I stand up, turn around, and point at the end of the road.</p>
<p>&quot;Follow this path to the crossroads, then turn right, understand? Turn right.&quot;</p>
<p>I move my right arm as I face the same direction as him.</p>
<p>The man gloomily says, &quot;I&#x27;m not a juvenile.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll concentrate this time. There definitely won&#x27;t be a problem!&quot;</p>
<p>I ignore his emphasis and instead remind him, &quot;If you encounter any more accidents, you can look for the police.&quot;</p>
<p>I originally wanted to mischievously say &quot;Mr. Policeman,&quot; but since he has already said that he isn&#x27;t a juvenile, I&#x27;ll just forget about it.</p>
<p>However, is this guy living in some stage play? His choice of words are quite literal!</p>
<p>Juvenile?</p>
<p>Using the term &quot;primary school student&quot; would be more down-to-earth! The man falls silent for two seconds before taking a deep breath.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You&#x27;re welcome.&quot; I suppress my urge to make a joke.</p>
<p>After watching this guy walk far down the road, I sit back down and enjoy the barbecue.</p>
<p>I have to say that meat kebabs have to have fat in order to be delicious. Otherwise, they need to be marinated. Otherwise, it would definitely be dry, woody, and tasteless.</p>
<p>In this aspect, chicken skin is definitely the type of food that can take up the challenge. In my heart, it&#x27;s better than pork belly.</p>
<p>However, the chicken skin&#x27;s quality is hard to guarantee. Some barbecue stalls use the kind that were frozen for god knows how long. As for the one I chose, I can be quite certain of its standards.</p>
<p>I bite down on a piece of condiment-infused chicken skin that&#x27;s been coated with grease. Combined with a mouthful of pineapple and the chilling taste of beer, I find this summer evening simply wonderful in a way that cannot be described.</p>
<p>This pineapple beer isn&#x27;t bad. It&#x27;s refreshing and quenches my thirst... I&#x27;m completely engrossed in the food to the point that I even neglect my phone.</p>
<p>Meat, chives, eggplant, and an iced beverage. As I take in this assortment of delicacies, the food in front of me decreases. Phew, nice... I rub my belly in satisfaction.</p>
<p>In the next second, I start to have regrets.</p>
<p>No, I have to control myself! What if I get fat!?</p>
<p>In the near future, I can only indulge myself once a month—no, once a week.</p>
<p>While I&#x27;m in a dilemma, I look up and see a figure.</p>
<p>He&#x27;s wearing a three-piece attire that isn&#x27;t suited to proper nightlife. He has two bright red eyes.</p>
<p>Almost at the same time, the figure seems to sense my gaze. He turns his head and casts his gaze at me.</p>
<p>Me: &quot;...&quot;</p>
<p>Him: &quot;...&quot;</p>
<p>Why the f**k are you back?</p>
<p>Don&#x27;t tell me that you got lost?</p>
<p>The man stands by the roadside, neither advancing nor retreating. He seems to be embroiled in an intense inner struggle. Uh... I look around, but I don&#x27;t see any patrolling policemen.</p>
<p>I hesitate for a moment before standing up and walking towards the man.</p>
<p>I&#x27;ve already paid for the barbecue when the kebabs were delivered, so the lady boss doesn&#x27;t stop me from leaving.</p>
<p>&quot;Why don&#x27;t I take you there?&quot; I don&#x27;t mention the fact that he&#x27;s lost.</p>
<p>As a person, one has to know when to joke and when not to.</p>
<p>The man&#x27;s expression changes. After a few seconds, he whispers, &quot;Alright.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s go.&quot; I turn and lead.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you...&quot; The wind nearly scatters his voice from behind.</p>
<p>As I walk along the road to the crossroads, I casually chat.</p>
<p>&quot;Which country are you from? You speak pretty good Mandarin.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m a minority.&quot; The man hesitates for a moment before replying.</p>
<p>&quot;Oh, no wonder. Which ethnic group are you from? How should I address you?&quot; I&#x27;m enlightened. The man walks to my side and falls silent for a moment before saying,</p>
<p>&quot;My surname is Bai . My name is Ailin.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s a nice name, just a little feminine,&quot; I tease.</p>
<p>To be honest, a superficial joke like this definitely doesn&#x27;t match the sarcasm in my heart. I&#x27;ve already given Bai Ailin a feminine nickname in my mind:</p>
<p>Lin Lin, or Lyn Lyn.</p>
<p>Bai Ailin ignores my joke and walks forward before turning left.</p>
<p>&quot;Wait. Wrong. That&#x27;s wrong. Over here.&quot; I quickly correct him.</p>
<p>You really are a primary school student!</p>
<p>Bai Ailin immediately turns around and follows me to take the right turn.</p>
<p>I don&#x27;t look at his face, and I don&#x27;t say a word. I give him time to calm down, lest he gets angry.</p>
<p>To be honest, who gave him the courage to come out in the middle of the night to look for a hospital with such a terrible sense of direction? Couldn&#x27;t he just get a taxi?</p>
<p>Or let his parents accompany him!</p>
<p>After turning right, it&#x27;s only a fifty to sixty-meter walk. The hospital appears before us.</p>
<p>&quot;This hospital isn&#x27;t big. This is the inpatient building. If you go straight ahead, you&#x27;ll see the Emergency Department and the outpatient building. Do you need me to bring you there?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No need, I&#x27;m just going to the inpatient building.&quot; Bai Ailin is visibly relieved. &quot;Thank you.&quot;</p>
<p>As he speaks, he takes out a simple brass key from his pocket.</p>
<p>Strange... People actually use a key like that in this day and age? In actuality, it just appears like that on the surface. It&#x27;s essentially a smart key? I don&#x27;t probe further and wave.</p>
<p>&quot;Goodbye.&quot;</p>
<p>After walking for a few meters, I look back worriedly, only to find Bai Ailin gone.</p>
<p>I can no longer see him on this road. It&#x27;s as if he vanished into thin air!</p>
<p>The hospital&#x27;s side door didn&#x27;t open either! Could he have fallen into the sewers? I look over and realize that the manhole cover is fine.</p>
<p>Perhaps he used the key to open the door and enter... Heh heh, the old man guarding the side door must be asleep, right? I shake my head and stop thinking about this.</p>
<p>I head all the way home. I find myself feeling a little thirsty thanks to the barbecue, so I take out a cup from my room, open the fridge, and pour the cold water that has been chilled the entire day into the cup.</p>
<p>This is a habit of mine during the summer. Before I head out in the morning, I boil a pot of water, pour it into the large container, and place it in the fridge. I can then drink it after work.</p>
<p>Gulp! Gulp! Gulp... I drink two glasses in a row and finally feel fine.</p>
<p>After pouring another glass, I saunter back to my bedroom and sit at the computer table.</p>
<p>As I sweep my gaze, I suddenly sense something amiss.</p>
<p>Eh...</p>
<p>The black notebook I bought this afternoon is laying open.</p>
<p>I remember that before I went out for barbecue, it was clearly closed. Besides, I didn&#x27;t open the windows for fear of mosquitoes! 1. Literally &quot;White&quot; in Chinese Back.</p>
