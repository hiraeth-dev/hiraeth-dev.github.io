<p>After finding the project team and gaining an understanding of the situation, I spend quite a bit of time organizing the information. I also leave the company at three in the afternoon, preparing myself to head towards the city&#x27;s police station to visit the staff in charge.</p>
<p>After leaving my company&#x27;s building, I take out my phone, open my ride-sharing app, and submit my destination.</p>
<p>Since it&#x27;s not the evening rush hour, I get a driver in just a few seconds.</p>
<p>He&#x27;s in the vicinity, one minute... I glance at the notification and am very pleased that I don&#x27;t need to wait too long.</p>
<p>One minute passes quickly, but as I look left and right, I don&#x27;t see the license plate that I remember.</p>
<p>Suspecting that I&#x27;ve misremembered, I hurriedly unlock my phone to confirm the situation.</p>
<p>At this moment, I heard the chiming of a bicycle bell.</p>
<p>Uh... I subconsciously look up and see a green-orange bicycle.</p>
<p>Sitting on the bicycle is a young man wearing a dark baseball cap and a thin black coat. Ring! Ring! Ring!</p>
<p>The bicycle stops in front of me as the man extends his right foot to support the bicycle against the ground.</p>
<p>His forehead is wide and his face is thin. His short black hair is slightly curled, and his eyes are darker than an ordinary person&#x27;s—close to pure black.</p>
<p>His biggest characteristic was that, like a cosplayer, he wore a crystal monocle on his right eye.</p>
<p>Holding the bicycle&#x27;s handle with one hand, the man adjusts his monocle with the other. He smiles at me and says, &quot;You were the one who requested a ride?&quot;</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>I did, but I requested a car, not a bicycle...</p>
<p>I open my mouth, unsure if I should answer.</p>
<p>At that moment, I even underwent existential questions: Who I am. Where am I? Where am I going?</p>
<p>&quot;You&#x27;re going to the police station, right?&quot; The young man with the monocle didn&#x27;t care that I didn&#x27;t respond as he asked with a smile.</p>
<p>...No way? Is there really an option for getting a bicycle? And I selected it somehow? I slowly nod. At this moment, there are only two clear thoughts in my mind:</p>
<p>Should I take a photo or video to post on my Moments?</p>
<p>Should I call the customer service to complain?</p>
<p>At that moment, the young man wearing the monocle points to the side of the building and says, &quot;Wait another minute, my car is parked there.&quot;</p>
<p>As he speaks, he takes out his phone and waves it.</p>
<p>&quot;Huh?&quot; I still don&#x27;t understand what&#x27;s happening.</p>
<p>The young man presses the edge of his monocle and smiles.</p>
<p>&quot;I did something in the nearby alley just now. I couldn&#x27;t drive through, and the distance to walk here was a little far, so I rented a shared bike.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m sorry, please wait a minute or two.&quot;</p>
<p>Since the other party has already said so and I&#x27;m not in a rush. I politely reply, &quot;It&#x27;s fine.&quot;</p>
<p>The young man immediately puts away his phone and rode his bicycle to the side of the building.</p>
<p>Soon, a white car drives over and stops in front of me. &quot;Alright, you can board it now.&quot; The car window rolls down, revealing the face with the monocle.</p>
<p>Just as I&#x27;m about to open the car door, I suddenly realize a problem:</p>
<p>The car displayed on the app is black!</p>
<p>Uh... I&#x27;m just about to walk to the back to look at the car plate when the driver waves his phone at me across the passenger seat.</p>
<p>&quot;I have two cars.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m driving this car today, but I bound my account to the other one.</p>
<p>&quot;Look, my phone is right.&quot;</p>
<p>With that, he calls me.</p>
<p>Seeing that the number displayed isn&#x27;t a problem, and considering how I&#x27;ve encountered similar situations before, I&#x27;m relieved. I open the car door and get in.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s broad daylight in a bustling city. What is there to be afraid of?</p>
<p>However, the reviewing and verifications undertaken by the platform are really problematic. When I get into the car, the driver with a monocle looks ahead as he picks up a cigarette box and hands it back.</p>
<p>&quot;Want a cigarette?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Isn&#x27;t smoking banned?&quot; I blurt.</p>
<p>&quot;As long as I don&#x27;t mind it.&quot; The thin driver smiles through the rearview mirror.</p>
<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t smoke.&quot; I instinctively shake my head.</p>
<p>The driver holds the steering wheel with one hand, not really paying attention to the situation on the road.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s good that you don&#x27;t smoke. I don&#x27;t smoke either. Smoking causes one to have a ghastly appearance.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Then why do you have cigarettes...&quot; I follow up quickly.</p>
<p>As the car drives down the road, the driver uses his empty hand to touch the monocle on his right eye.</p>
<p>&quot;There will always be some friends you need to meet despite you being unwilling to meet.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s true.&quot; I nod.</p>
<p>At this moment, I recall Vice President Wu from the company next door. Although he smokes, his appearance is definitely not ghastly. However, there&#x27;s no need to say it out loud. I&#x27;m not someone who gets into debates with others in a professional manner.</p>
<p>&quot;You don&#x27;t look like a rideshare driver.&quot; I glance at the obviously expensive cigarettes, and glance around the luxurious interior of the car.</p>
<p>&quot;Heh heh,&quot; the driver replies with a smile. &quot;Is my performance that obvious?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You don&#x27;t look like you make a living from ridesharing. You look like you come from a rich family.&quot; I give a random reason.</p>
<p>Either way, I can&#x27;t tell.</p>
<p>&quot;Heh heh, being born with a silver spoon doesn&#x27;t bar me from making a living from ridesharing. It allows me to experience a completely different life from my past, and being able to see all kinds of unique people is a form of entertainment. Don&#x27;t you find it interesting?&quot; the driver says with a smile.</p>
<p>How philosophical... I nod and casually say, &quot;That may be the case, but I think you occasionally only take a few ride orders and are usually busy with other things.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, I went to your building to investigate.&quot; The driver looks at the rearview mirror again, and his smile makes me feel a little strange.</p>
<p>&quot;Investigate...&quot; I&#x27;m at a loss. &quot;It&#x27;s about an evil cult. They like to use vending machines to harm others.&quot; The corner of the driver&#x27;s lips curls up.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>If it wasn&#x27;t for the fact that I already knew about it, I would&#x27;ve lost my composure!</p>
<p>&quot;You&#x27;re a private detective? Do you know a detective named Sherlock Moriarty? This might be his nickname.&quot; I suddenly have an idea.</p>
<p>The driver raises his hand to pinch the edge of his monocle and chuckles.</p>
<p>&quot;Of course. We&#x27;re old friends.&quot;</p>
<p>I don&#x27;t know if it&#x27;s my imagination, but I feel like he emphasized the words &quot;old friend.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;D-did you, or any of you find anything?&quot; I try my best to act like a bystander.</p>
<p>The driver half-turns his head to glance at me and says with an obvious smile, &quot;Guess.&quot;</p>
<p>That&#x27;s such a punchable smile... I rein in my emotions.</p>
<p>&quot;I guess so.&quot; &quot;Then it&#x27;s as you say,&quot; the driver says with an expression of &quot;I know a lot, but I won&#x27;t tell you.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;...&quot; As I think about what to say, he suddenly straightens his back and looks ahead.</p>
<p>Then, he pushes the monocle on his right eye.</p>
<p>Suddenly, countless fragments appear in my mind.</p>
<p>Overlapping roads, tunnels, mixed colors, different signboards, pedestrians, cars that swept backwards rapidly...</p>
<p>They simultaneously explode in my mind at the same time, making me feel like I&#x27;m suffering from motion sickness.</p>
<p>&quot;We&#x27;re here.&quot; The driver&#x27;s voice enters my ears the next second.</p>
<p>I get out of the car in a daze and squat down at the side of the road, feeling like I might vomit at any moment.</p>
<p>I recover after a while, but the ridesharing car has already left.</p>
<p>Thinking back, I suddenly shudder.</p>
