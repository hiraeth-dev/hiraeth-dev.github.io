<p>Stole two sedan cars in a row... This is probably a crime syndicate. How can one person drive two cars? I can&#x27;t help but mutter inwardly.</p>
<p>This is the cultivation I&#x27;ve gone through as a fan of courtroom dramas and detective series, as well as me being a loyal reader of crime deduction novels.</p>
<p>Of course, I don&#x27;t say it out loud. I believe that people with standard intelligence can come to a similar conclusion.</p>
<p>When the guard on duty calls to confirm my identity, I take out my phone and check the time.</p>
<p>There&#x27;s more than enough time... I let out a sigh of relief as I unhurriedly circle around the pond in the middle of the courtyard and enter the building facing the main entrance.</p>
<p>After I run through the possible exchange in my mind, I knock on the door of the target&#x27;s office three minutes early.</p>
<p>&quot;Please come in.&quot; A mellow voice sounds from inside.</p>
<p>&quot;O-Officer Deng...&quot; As soon as I push open the door and enter, I realize that the officer sitting behind the desk is the one who had come to check on my residence permit!</p>
<p>I always imagined that he&#x27;s a police officer at the police station, but I never expected him to have an office at the Municipal Public Security Bureau. He even has the room to himself!</p>
<p>&quot;You are?&quot; Officer Deng raises his hand to comb his receding hairline and asks with a puzzled look, &quot;Do we know each other?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Last night, you checked on my residence permit and reminded me of a mysterious cult. Later, we met again in Lawson. I even reported a woman suspected to be a member of the cult.&quot; I begin to suspect that the person in front of me is Officer Deng&#x27;s biological brother, but I still give the details.</p>
<p>Officer Deng is enlightened.</p>
<p>&quot;Zhou-Zhou Mingrui, right?</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m face-blind, and I don&#x27;t have a good memory. It was night, so I couldn&#x27;t see clearly.&quot;</p>
<p>I don&#x27;t need you to tell me that your memory isn&#x27;t good or how you have face-blindness. I can tell... I politely smile.</p>
<p>&quot;Today, I&#x27;m representing the Intis Group to take over the previous project.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, we agreed to meet at four.&quot; Officer Deng points at the chair opposite his desk.</p>
<p>I immediately approach it and plan on placing the black notebook that I just bought on the table. I will then flip it to the middle—I can&#x27;t let Officer Deng notice that I just bought it and have never written on it. Officer Deng glances at the notebook and frowns slightly.</p>
<p>Following that, he picks up the phone and dials an internal department number.</p>
<p>&quot;Hey, the Intis Group is here. Come over for a meeting.&quot;</p>
<p>After a simple briefing, he puts down the phone and smiles at me.</p>
<p>&quot;This is an international collaboration project. We can&#x27;t decide on our own. Yes, the other party&#x27;s representative will be here soon.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m aware of that.&quot; I&#x27;ve read the relevant documents in the morning and know what&#x27;s going on.</p>
<p>Of course, even if I&#x27;m lazy and didn&#x27;t read it, I&#x27;ll still have given such an answer. I can&#x27;t let the partner I&#x27;m working with find out that I&#x27;m skiving and not doing my job well.</p>
<p>Not long after I sit down, I hear Officer Deng&#x27;s office door open.</p>
<p>I stand up politely and look back. I see an old man in a long black robe.</p>
<p>His hair is white, and the wrinkles at the edge of his eyes and mouth are deeply recessed. His dark red eyes appear slightly turbid.</p>
<p>A red moon pendant hangs on his chest. He looks like a priest from a cathedral.</p>
<p>A foreign priest... I nod with a smile, fully displaying my courtesy.</p>
<p>For some reason, I find this old man a little familiar, just like the first time I met Officer Deng.</p>
<p>Strange... Recently, I&#x27;ve been feeling sad for some inexplicable reason. Is this the after-effect of the Assassin beverage? I silently inhale and control my emotions.</p>
<p>At that moment, Officer Deng introduces, &quot;Old Neil, an Interpol representative, a clergyman.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Hello, Mr. Neil.&quot; I quickly greet him.</p>
<p>Old Neil doesn&#x27;t speak. He sizes me up and chuckles.</p>
<p>&quot;Lad, have you been troubled by ghosts recently?&quot;</p>
<p>...You can tell? I&#x27;m shocked. I don&#x27;t know if I should admit it or hide it from a stranger.</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t lie. My eyes are very sharp.&quot; Old Neil smiles and points at his eyes.</p>
<p>&quot;A little...&quot; I mutter. &quot;Haha.&quot; Old Neil immediately laughs. &quot;You&#x27;re still too young. You confessed the moment I called you out. Actually, I&#x27;m not that sure. Yes, I&#x27;m a priest. I have the ability to expel wraiths and shadows. Do you need my help?&quot;</p>
<p>...Is it okay to discuss such feudal superstitions in front of a police ofﬁcer? I open my mouth, speechless.</p>
<p>Old Neil walks to Officer Deng&#x27;s table and very naturally picks up the coffee can on it.</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t worry. I don&#x27;t charge much. If you&#x27;re still a believer of the Goddess, I can even do it for free.&quot;</p>
<p>I wasn&#x27;t a believer of the Goddess in the past, but I am now! At this moment, my heart is palpitating.</p>
<p>I hesitate for two seconds before sighing.</p>
<p>&quot;But, I&#x27;ve already found a helper.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Who is it? There aren&#x27;t many people with such abilities. Most of them are cheats.&quot; Old Neil skillfully brews his coffee and asks casually.</p>
<p>&quot;...Daly, do you know her?&quot; I ask hesitantly.</p>
<p>&quot;Daly... Ah, then that&#x27;s fine.&quot; Old Neil retracts his smile and asks, &quot;Are you the representative of the Intis Group? Let&#x27;s begin.&quot;</p>
<p>At the same time, Officer Deng shakes his head with a smile, looking helpless.</p>
<p>&quot;You know Madam Daly?&quot; I ask.</p>
<p>Old Neil sighs and points at Officer Deng.</p>
<p>&quot;His fiancée.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Oh, oh...&quot; Everyone knows each other! I can&#x27;t help but sigh.</p>
<p>This boosts my confidence in Madam Daly.</p>
<p>I stop chatting and begin communicating with Officer Deng and Old Neil about the project, taking note of their requests.</p>
<p>An hour passes.</p>
<p>&quot;Yawn.&quot; Old Neil stretches his back. &quot;It&#x27;s finally over. These old bones of mine really can&#x27;t withstand the torment of such meetings. I no longer have the energy.&quot;</p>
<p>With that said, he stands up and smiles at me and Officer Deng.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s time for me to retire.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#x27;s time for me to leave. I&#x27;ll go to a faraway village. I&#x27;ll grow some flowers, feed the chickens, and play the piano for my wife. I don&#x27;t have to worry about things here anymore.&quot; Officer Deng stands up as well. As he sends Old Neil out, he smiles and says, &quot;Let&#x27;s go together.&quot;</p>
<p>As I watch them leave, I suddenly feel speechless.</p>
