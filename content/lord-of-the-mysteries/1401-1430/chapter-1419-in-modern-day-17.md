<p>It&#x27;s not even 5:30, and I can get off work normally again! I&#x27;ve already composed myself by the time when I walk out of the police station, feeling happy that I don&#x27;t have to work overtime today.</p>
<p>Before I left in the afternoon, I communicated with the administration department and was told that I didn&#x27;t need to return to the company. After all, we didn&#x27;t know how long the meeting would take.</p>
<p>What if Officer Deng got me to stay for dinner, sing some karaoke, and enjoy a spa, right?</p>
<p>Although the probability of this happening is low, even if it wouldn&#x27;t happen to me, I still have to be prepared.</p>
<p>The distance from the old estate where my rented apartment is to the police station is shorter than it is from my company, but I still chose to take public transport, because the company doesn&#x27;t reimburse the transportation fees for returning home.</p>
<p>Metro line 3,600 meters... Bus 72, 150 meters... I take out my phone and search for a route home.</p>
<p>After careful consideration, I choose to take the bus.</p>
<p>The weather is hot in the summer, so it&#x27;s best if I can minimize the steps taken. As I wait at the bus stop for the bus, I can&#x27;t help but doubt my life.</p>
<p>I&#x27;m already a real, powerful, and extraordinary assassin. Why do I have to live like a slave of society?</p>
<p>But I can&#x27;t get any money without work.</p>
<p>Assassins also need to save money to buy a house, food, and clothes!</p>
<p>Sigh, at times like this, it should be like a game. Give me an assassin manager and help me gather missions. After confirming how evil my target is, I&#x27;ll shave my head and flash through the crowd. No, being bald is too ugly. Forget it...</p>
<p>What can I do?</p>
<p>Record videos of extreme sports?</p>
<p>I still have to practice and learn. I&#x27;ve never paid attention to it before...</p>
<p>Actually, I can consider joining the police academy. As an assassin, I&#x27;m capable of capturing thieves and scammers. I won&#x27;t disappoint with my abilities. The only problem is that I&#x27;ve heard that a policeman&#x27;s income isn&#x27;t too high.</p>
<p>Perhaps it&#x27;s better after the probation period?</p>
<p>As I seriously consider the future, I board the bus. I hold the handle and notebook with one hand, and I pull out my phone with the other.</p>
<p>I&#x27;m a refined person. I definitely won&#x27;t blast out any sounds in public. I don&#x27;t have wireless earphones, so I can only browse Weibo and HUPU, an online sports community website.</p>
<p>As the bus drives, it suddenly stops. The driver&#x27;s voice sounds from the speaker.</p>
<p>&quot;Get off the bus. There&#x27;s a malfunction. Wait for the next one.&quot;</p>
<p>The passengers curse as they get out of the bus. I&#x27;m among them as I sigh at my bad luck today.</p>
<p>There&#x27;s only one stop left. There&#x27;s no need to wait. I&#x27;ll just walk back. I open the map app and confirm my current location.</p>
<p>If I knew this would happen, I might as well have taken the subway!</p>
<p>I follow the GPS—walking straight, taking a bend, crossing the street—to the old estate I live in.</p>
<p>At this moment, I see a five or six-year-old girl jump out from the roadside, and an SUV is speeding over.</p>
<p>&quot;...&quot; I&#x27;m stunned at first, but then I realize that I&#x27;m an assassin, an expert that surpasses ordinary people.</p>
<p>Without any hesitation, I run towards the girl. Seeing that I might not make it in time, I exert strength into my legs and kick out, relying on my assassin&#x27;s ability to unleash all of my strength in one strike to pounce forward.</p>
<p>During this process, I feel like a sharp arrow that had been shot from a bow.</p>
<p>In the blink of an eye, I lunge for the little girl and hug her.</p>
<p>Then, I straighten my back and quickly adjust my body structure, making myself as light as a feather.</p>
<p>The SUV finally stops. I take a few steps to the side and steady myself.</p>
<p>To be honest, I was hit, but because I made myself seemingly weightless, I didn&#x27;t suffer any damage.</p>
<p>&quot;Are you alright?&quot; The SUV driver gets out of the car and frantically asks.</p>
<p>His face is drenched in sweat.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m fine. It was a close one.&quot; I put down the dazed little girl and pat her shoulder.</p>
<p>She has long, jet-black hair which makes her qualified to be a model in a shampoo commercial.</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s good, that&#x27;s good.&quot; The SUV driver is relieved to see that I didn&#x27;t dirty or injure myself. At this moment, a middle-aged man rushes out from the side of the road.</p>
<p>When he sees that the little girl is fine, he&#x27;s obviously relieved.</p>
<p>His skin is bronze in color and he has soft facial features. He has a pair of eyes that are closer in color to brown. They appear to have been through a lot.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you, thank you. Are you okay?&quot; he asks, concerned.</p>
<p>&quot;No, my luck isn&#x27;t bad. I just happened to avoid the collision,&quot; I reply with a smile, inexplicably in a good mood.</p>
<p>I&#x27;ve finally used the ability belonging to an assassin.</p>
<p>The SUV driver points at his license plate.</p>
<p>&quot;Contact me if you have any problems. I still have something to do, so I&#x27;ll be leaving first.&quot;</p>
<p>The middle-aged man nods before saying to me,</p>
<p>&quot;Why don&#x27;t you come to my shop to have a drink to calm yourself down?&quot;</p>
<p>For some reason, I feel a sense of closeness with him. And since it&#x27;s free, I don&#x27;t reject him. &quot;Okay.&quot;</p>
<p>As I speak, I pick up the black notebook.</p>
<p>After leaving the road and passing through the non-motorcar lane, I see this gentleman&#x27;s shop.</p>
<p>This is a themed bookstore. It&#x27;s renovated to look like a coffee shop. The other door leads to a nearby park with a wooden swing hanging on it.</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t wander around in the future. It&#x27;s very dangerous. Come on, thank Uncle.&quot; The bookstore owner pulls the little girl over.</p>
<p>&quot;Call me Brother. Don&#x27;t make me sound so old,&quot; I joke.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you, Brother.&quot; The little girl follows his instructions.</p>
<p>Without waiting for my reply, she cleverly says, &quot;Then my father is your elder!&quot;</p>
<p>Damn, did I just get taken advantage of... I can only smile helplessly.</p>
<p>The bookstore owner immediately asks, &quot;What would you like to drink?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;A cold beverage would do.&quot; I&#x27;m not a picky eater. &quot;Okay.&quot; The bookstore owner points at the surrounding bookshelves and says, &quot;You can also pick a book to read.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Okay.&quot; As he mixes the cold beverage, I curiously walk to the bookshelf to see what&#x27;s on it.</p>
<p>Groselle&#x27;s Travels</p>
<p>Book of Secrets</p>
<p>Roselle and his 100 Mistresses</p>
<p>The Self-Cultivation of Evil Gods</p>
<p>How to Make the Believers Offer Money</p>
<p>What kind of books are these? I&#x27;m stunned as I read them. Finally, I choose the seemingly normal Groselle&#x27;s Travels.</p>
<p>Just as I find a seat and sit down, the bookstore owner walks over with a glass.</p>
<p>The drink is brownish-red in color, and there&#x27;s quite a few pieces of ice in it. After drinking it in one gulp, it&#x27;s slightly sour and sweet. It&#x27;s cold and very refreshing. Great for the summer.</p>
<p>&quot;Not bad,&quot; I praise.</p>
<p>The boss doesn&#x27;t say anything else and returns to the cashier. I then leisurely lean back in my chair and flip through Groselle&#x27;s Travels. From time to time, I take a mouthful of the cold beverage, feeling very pleased.</p>
<p>After a while, the girl runs to the back door and shouts, &quot;Daddy, Daddy, I want to sit on the swing.&quot;</p>
<p>The bookstore owner sighs and walks over with a smile.</p>
<p>I smile and take another sip of my drink. I can&#x27;t help but sigh.</p>
<p>An afternoon like this is really nice...</p>
