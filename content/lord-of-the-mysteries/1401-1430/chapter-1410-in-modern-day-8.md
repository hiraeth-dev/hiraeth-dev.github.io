<p>As an international metropolitan city, there are definitely countless beauties dressed fashionably out on the streets. Even if I don&#x27;t pay attention to them, I would often find women that catch my attention.</p>
<p>But even so, this person that&#x27;s inside Lawson is a rare gem. I can&#x27;t help but steal another look after seeing her.</p>
<p>Her brown-dyed hair cascades down like a waterfall, and two crystal-like accessories hang from her earlobes. Her eyes sparkle, looking as clear and beautiful as the eyes of deers in forests. She has plenty of charisma.</p>
<p>Out of courtesy and habitual shyness, I tear my eyes away from the girl and walk past her to the area where the fridge is.</p>
<p>During this process, I can&#x27;t help but turn around to take a few more looks, but I suppress my actions to avoid offending her.</p>
<p>Hmm, which ice cream should I buy? I gaze at the transparent sliding door of the coolbox and fall into deep thought.</p>
<p>Recently, the more popular ones are &quot;Fat Crabs,&quot; &quot;Iron Cauldron Stew&quot;...</p>
<p>Taking into account my successful &quot;weight loss&quot; and my need to prevent any rebounds, as well as my tight financial situation at the end of the month, I ultimately chose &quot;Fat Crabs.&quot; Then, I go to the neighboring room to get a bottle of chilled CR and a packet of cucumber-flavored potato chips.</p>
<p>All three combined form my supper for the night.</p>
<p>If it was the beginning of the month, I definitely would&#x27;ve bought more. I&#x27;ll keep them at home and eat them when there&#x27;s a need. I don&#x27;t have to go downstairs every time, but there&#x27;s no need to be so carefree with my expenses now.</p>
<p>CEO Huang had once said: &quot;Don&#x27;t let trivialities take up your most important liquidity.&quot;</p>
<p>Without any hesitation, I take my snacks and walk to the counter to stand in line to settle the bill.</p>
<p>At this moment, I suddenly smell a faint fragrance.</p>
<p>In the next second, I turn my head and see the gorgeous beauty walk past two rows of shelves. She smiles and says with her eyes shimmering, &quot;Can I add you on WeChat?&quot;</p>
<p>?</p>
<p>Me? I arch my arms a little and express my puzzlement in my speech and pose.</p>
<p>I&#x27;ve never had girls ask me for my contact details all my life!</p>
<p>&quot;That&#x27;s right.&quot; The beauty nods happily and says with a smile, &quot;Your looks and temperament matches my taste. I want to get to know you.&quot;</p>
<p>If she were to praise me for being handsome or good-looking, I definitely wouldn&#x27;t have believed her. However, it&#x27;s not impossible that my looks and temperament matches her tastes.</p>
<p>Maybe she likes my type, right?</p>
<p>For a moment, I understand what it means to be overwhelmed and flattered.</p>
<p>In the next second, I become more wary.</p>
<p>Could it be some multi-level marketing scheme?</p>
<p>Or some honey trap?</p>
<p>As an aficionado of legal shows, a fan of the Legal Report of many years, I am instinctively wary of good things falling into my lap.</p>
<p>As I hesitate, the eyes of that beautiful woman standing in front of me fogs up. It makes me feel ashamed and guilty.</p>
<p>Perhaps it&#x27;s not some multi-level marketing or honey trap... Even if it is, she&#x27;s just adding me on WeChat. It won&#x27;t be a problem if I pay more attention in the future... I think for a moment and hurriedly say, &quot;Alright.&quot;</p>
<p>I turn around and place the bottle of CR on the counter before taking out my phone from my pocket. However, when I look at that girl again, she has already disappeared.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s not as if I can&#x27;t see her. I can still see instances of her back appearing through the cracks of the shelves.</p>
<p>...What about appreciating my looks and temperament... How did you have a change of heart so quickly? Just as this thought flashes through my mind, I heard a chime!</p>
<p>The door of the convenience store opens automatically, and a figure walks in.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s Officer Deng who had checked on my residence permit in the evening, but he has already changed into casual wear.</p>
<p>He walks to the front desk and tugs at the bottom of his black jacket&#x27;s bottom. He says to the staff, &quot;A cup of coffee.&quot;</p>
<p>As he speaks, Officer Deng turns to look at me.</p>
<p>&quot;A-are you footing your bill?&quot;</p>
<p>Before he can finish, he pinches his forehead.</p>
<p>&quot;You are, you are Zhou, Zhou...&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Zhou Mingrui, Officer Deng. We just met this evening,&quot; I reply with a smile. At that moment, from the corner of my eye, I catch the woman leaving the convenience store.</p>
<p>She gave up striking up a conversation with me and ran off in a hurry because she saw Ofﬁcer Deng? Is she really involved in multi-level marketing or pulling a honey trap?? Feeling regretful and puzzled, I hand over the items in my hand to the shop assistant and say without minding, &quot;Give Officer Deng his coffee first.&quot;</p>
<p>The shop assistant spends some time making the coffee before giving it to Officer Deng.</p>
<p>He gives me a friendly smile and heads out with the cup in hand.</p>
<p>After taking a few steps, he stops and says with a self-deprecating smile, &quot;I forgot to remind you that the cult will use all kinds of means to approach the target. Be careful.&quot;</p>
<p>Cult... Suddenly, I am aware of a possibility:</p>
<p>Could that beauty from before be from that cult?</p>
<p>She hit on me because I drank the Assassin beverage and became their target?</p>
<p>Th-this method really makes it impossible to guard against! They actually used a honey trap! I&#x27;m alarmed. With Officer Deng about to turn around and leave the convenience store, I rush forward and say to him, &quot;Just now, a woman wanted to add me on WeChat.&quot; Officer Deng doesn&#x27;t laugh at me. He asks very formally, &quot;What does she look like?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Very beautiful, more beautiful than many celebrities...&quot; I give a simple description and conclude, &quot;I don&#x27;t think I have the charm to make a beauty hit on me.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Not bad. Many victims only get deceived because they feel that they are very charming,&quot; says Officer Deng with a smile. &quot;We will track and investigate this. As for you, you should just try your best. Try not to speak to strangers as depicted in television dramas. Speaking is fine, just don&#x27;t establish more connections.&quot;</p>
<p>My heart warms as I nod vigorously.</p>
<p>&quot;Alright.&quot;</p>
<p>After seeing Officer Deng leave, I return to the counter to settle the bill. I take the snacks and walk to the entrance of my residential compound.</p>
<p>Along the way, after passing the barbecue stall, the strong fragrance inundates my nostrils again. It tempts me to sit down and order a pile of food.</p>
<p>I sweep a glance, and I see a group of gangsters.</p>
<p>They&#x27;re sitting on simple plastic stools at the barbecue stall, waiting for the food to be cooked.</p>
<p>Their hair is dyed in all sorts of colors, and it&#x27;s obvious that they&#x27;re punks following the scene subculture. If it were in the past, I definitely would&#x27;ve upped my pace and widened my distance to avoid any unnecessary conflicts.</p>
<p>But now, I&#x27;m looking forward to them making things difficult for me. I want them to have a good taste of an assassin&#x27;s prowess.</p>
<p>Just then, one of the hoodlums glances at me and stands up abruptly.</p>
<p>Holding a stack of paper, he jogs towards me.</p>
<p>I&#x27;m thrilled and begin to imagine what pose I should put on later.</p>
<p>The charred yellow-eyebrowed hoodlum quickly comes in front of me and says, &quot;Hi there, perhaps you would be interested in Dream Tutoring Classes?&quot;</p>
<p>?</p>
<p>Dream Tutoring Classes?</p>
<p>This development is completely different from what I expected!</p>
<p>As he speaks, the hoodlum hands a flyer to me.</p>
<p>&quot;Our Dream Tutoring Classes covers many topics. Even if you&#x27;re already working, you can learn things from the courses to improve yourselves and even switch careers.&quot; I blankly receive the flyer and subconsciously ask, &quot;Are you a tutor?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, I&#x27;m a foreign language teacher.&quot; The hoodlum smiles arrogantly. &quot;My surname is Da.&quot;</p>
<p>Before he can finish his sentence, a few of the hoodlums shout, &quot;Dani, the food is ready. It&#x27;s time to dig in!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;**!&quot; The hoodlum in front of me turns around and flips his middle finger before running back.</p>
<p>I shake my head in disappointment and wrap my snacks in the flier before entering my compound and return to my rented apartment building.</p>
<p>As this place is old, there are a few lamps along the stairs that are out. Some of the floors are dark. I have to take out my cell phones to turn on the flashlight function before I can see where I&#x27;m placing my feet clearly.</p>
<p>The surroundings are very quiet. My phone suddenly flashes a few times, turning extremely dim.</p>
<p>At the same time, I feel a cold wind blow past me.</p>
