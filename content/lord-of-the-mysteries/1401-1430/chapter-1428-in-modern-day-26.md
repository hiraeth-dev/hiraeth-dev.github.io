<p>In a daze, I open my eyes.</p>
<p>Holy sh*t, when did I fall asleep? I have no recollection at all!</p>
<p>I glance around blankly and find my colleagues working in their respective cubicles.</p>
<p>I only remember that I was getting ready to listen to a song, and here I am? Did I black out? I didn&#x27;t drink... Ouch, my head hurts, I&#x27;m so dizzy, I want to vomit... At this moment, I feel like I&#x27;ve just woken up from a hangover.</p>
<p>Of course, this is just a feeling, as I&#x27;ve never been drunk.</p>
<p>Raising my hands to rub my temples, I glance at the music player and find that it has stopped playing.</p>
<p>Advance! Advance! ...Yes, I was listening to this song! I can&#x27;t remember its melody at all... Puzzled, I click on the song&#x27;s reviews again.</p>
<p>&quot;Nice!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;An unprecedented experience.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Excellent pleasure.&quot; ...</p>
<p>Because I&#x27;ve read the reviews on the first few pages, I only take a few casual glances and scroll to the end:</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you so much for this song! It cured my chronic insomnia.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The purest and most punk death rock!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;This song made me faint. This isn&#x27;t a description, but literally what happened!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I can&#x27;t imagine how humans can make such sounds...&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;If you&#x27;re a man, try lasting 30 seconds!&quot;</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>This... This song is a little problematic... I won&#x27;t listen to this so-called &quot;death rock&quot; again... I grit my teeth for a few seconds before deciding to add another review:</p>
<p>&quot;An unprecedented form of music! It transcends human understanding!&quot;</p>
<p>I have to let others experience it too. I can&#x27;t be selfish!</p>
<p>After getting a preliminary explanation for the mysterious blackout, I take off my headphones and prepare for work.</p>
<p>However, my head is dizzy and my forehead is throbbing in pain. From time to time, I feel disgusted.</p>
<p>This is a lot worse than the heatstroke I previously suffered from. Even the office&#x27;s powerful air-conditioning is unable to ease my discomfort.</p>
<p>No, I have to go to the hospital for a checkup. I feel like I&#x27;m going to crash... I try to calm myself down, but I still can&#x27;t hold on.</p>
<p>Luckily, CEO Huang has always been generous. Every employee has two days of paid medical leave without a doctor&#x27;s note.</p>
<p>I quickly write a request for time off and head to Director Ai&#x27;s office to ask for his signature.</p>
<p>Perhaps it&#x27;s because my complexion is really bad, Director Ai doesn&#x27;t make things difficult for me. Out of concern, he even asks me if I need someone to send me to the hospital.</p>
<p>&quot;There&#x27;s no need. It&#x27;s not that serious.&quot; I still care about my face. I would never ask for help if I can solve it myself.</p>
<p>&quot;Okay, go back and have a good rest. If there&#x27;s anything urgent in the afternoon, I will arrange someone else to do it.&quot; Director Ai doesn&#x27;t insist.</p>
<p>Out of the building, I get a rideshare without hesitation. I don&#x27;t put on a strong front by taking the bus. I&#x27;ve always been willing to spend money when the time calls for it.</p>
<p>&quot;Mushu Hospital.&quot; Very soon, the car arrives. I board it and first verify my destination.</p>
<p>Mushu Hospital is one of the better public hospitals in the surrounding areas, but it&#x27;s not one of the best. Hence, there are fewer patients and there wouldn&#x27;t be a long line for the Emergency Department.</p>
<p>The hospital that Bai Ailin went to last night is at the district level. Although I haven&#x27;t been there before, I have a nagging feeling that it&#x27;s very reliable.</p>
<p>&quot;Okay,&quot; the driver answers simply and starts the car.</p>
<p>Time ticks by as I gradually feel alive. My head isn&#x27;t that dizzy anymore, and I no longer have cold sweat. The throbbing pain completely disappears.</p>
<p>Holy f*ck... Did I choose a hospital that&#x27;s too far away? I&#x27;m scared that I&#x27;ll be alright by the time I make it to the hospital... I can&#x27;t help but have a strange thought.</p>
<p>As it turns out, this isn&#x27;t wishful thinking. When the driver arrives at my destination, I&#x27;m completely awake and alive. I even feel a little hungry.</p>
<p>There&#x27;s no need to go to the Emergency Department... After a round of inspection, I&#x27;m certain I&#x27;m in a good condition. Thus, my only option is to enjoy this spare time for myself. After all, I&#x27;ve already taken time off, and there&#x27;s no way to cancel it.</p>
<p>Return home to play games? Find a movie to watch? Ah, right. I still have to register at the Dream Tutoring Classes. I can&#x27;t not do anything after being paid. I happen to be free right now anyway. I pick up my phone, look for the picture of the address of the tutoring center that I took, call another rideshare, and head there.</p>
<p>&quot;Hello, I&#x27;d like to know more about your tutoring class.&quot; I don&#x27;t directly say that I&#x27;m going to sign up while facing the male receptionist.</p>

<p>I&#x27;m not discriminating against men, but I feel that women are more patient and gentle.</p>
