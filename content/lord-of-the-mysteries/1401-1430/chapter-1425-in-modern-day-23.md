<p>&quot;These dried mushrooms don&#x27;t look... very delicious.&quot; Rozanne looks at the bag of dried mushrooms in my hand seriously for a few seconds before replying hesitantly.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s obvious that she&#x27;s a little embarrassed to say that the mushroom looks dry and black—it causes alarms to go off, and it can hardly whet up an appetite. After all, I&#x27;d offered it out of kind intentions.</p>
<p>Hehe... I take this opportunity to change the topic.</p>
<p>&quot;What&#x27;s your drink called?&quot;</p>
<p>Rozanne immediately turns around and picks up the beverage bottle on the table. She says with a smile, &quot;This is it! The design looks cool, right?&quot;</p>
<p>The beverage bottle has a streamlined design with a footprint theme. The name is:</p>
<p>&quot;Reporter.&quot;</p>
<p>That&#x27;s different from mine... I politely compliment, &quot;Looks good.&quot;</p>
<p>Rozanne twists the cap that has been opened a long time ago as if she&#x27;s preparing to take a sip. I instinctively want to stop her, but after opening my mouth, I realize that I don&#x27;t have a reason.</p>
<p>Furthermore, a third of the Reporter beverage is gone. This means that Rozanne has already drank it. There&#x27;s no point in stopping her now.</p>
<p>Gulp... Rozanne lowers the bottle and twists the cap back on.</p>
<p>&quot;It doesn&#x27;t taste bad. Try it.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Okay, okay,&quot; I say perfunctorily.</p>
<p>Rozanne doesn&#x27;t urge me any further. Instead, she points at the remaining beverage bottles in the plastic bag.</p>
<p>&quot;You can also switch bottles.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;No need, there&#x27;s no need,&quot; I reply without hesitation.</p>
<p>Either way, I don&#x27;t intend to drink it. It doesn&#x27;t matter if I switch it or not.</p>
<p>After two seconds of deliberation, I look at Rozanne and probe, &quot;How do you feel after drinking it? Any special sensations?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Special sensations? Do you think this is a drug? Erm, it&#x27;s moderately sweet and refreshing. The fragrance will definitely be better after chilling it.&quot; Rozanne says, a little pissed. She doesn&#x27;t look like she&#x27;s lying... This means that she probably didn&#x27;t obtain superpowers from the Reporter beverage... I subconsciously survey my surroundings and realize that my fellow colleagues have more or less drunk some of Rozanne&#x27;s beverages, but none of them show any signs of abnormality.</p>
<p>They don&#x27;t even appear dazed or confused.</p>
<p>Could it be that only the Assassin beverage I picked last time has a special effect? I wonder if that&#x27;s considered being lucky or not... I quickly control my doubts and wave the Instigator beverage in my hand.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll need to get down to work.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Go ahead. Don&#x27;t let me stop you.&quot; Rozanne waves her hand casually.</p>
<p>Returning to my seat, I sit down. I look at the Instigator beverage in front of me and fall into deep thought.</p>
<p>After about twenty seconds, I use my large mug meant for drinking water. This is something I prepare for taking pleasant gulps of water. I open the cap of the Instigator beverage and pour the liquid inside.</p>
<p>Although I don&#x27;t plan on drinking this beverage, I&#x27;m curious about what the liquid inside looks like, and whether it contains some special powers.</p>
<p>Splash. I pour all of the Instigator beverage into my mug. That&#x27;s too little, isn&#x27;t it? That deﬁnitely isn&#x27;t 500 milliliters! I can&#x27;t help but look down on this beverage&#x27;s manufacturer.</p>
<p>My mug isn&#x27;t small, but it usually can&#x27;t hold the entirety of a 550-ml bottle of mineral water.</p>
<p>In other words, while there appeared to be quite a bit of &quot;Instigator&quot; in it, the manufacturer had actually cut corners!</p>
<p>Hmm, it&#x27;s dark blue. It&#x27;s clear, and it&#x27;s quite enticing... I carefully observe the liquid in the mug and gather some preliminary information.</p>
<p>Other than that, the drink is no different from anything else, at least not on the surface.</p>
<p>After some thought, I decide to throw something into the mug to see if there will be any changes.</p>
<p>What do I throw? I look around when I suddenly get a bout of inspiration.</p>
<p>Those dried mushrooms!</p>
<p>Anyway, this isn&#x27;t something I plan on eating, so I can use it for experiments.</p>
<p>I really am a genius!</p>
<p>Without any hesitation, I tear open the packaging and throw the black dried mushrooms into the dark blue liquid, watching them stretch out and expand slightly.</p>
<p>There aren&#x27;t any changes. I&#x27;m starting to think that I&#x27;m overthinking matters.</p>
<p>Suddenly, messages come from my work&#x27;s chat group. I start to get busy.</p>
<p>About fifteen minutes later, I finish my work and raise my head, preparing to stretch my neck.</p>
<p>Suddenly, I see a mushroom!</p>
<p>A white and tender mushroom with red star dots all over it!</p>
<p>...Where did this mushroom come from? I glance over and discover that it&#x27;s growing in my mug.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s about the size of a palm. Its skin, no—its surface is so tender and supple. On the cap, there are mushrooms of the same breed, just like its hands and feet.</p>
<p>What the hell is this? I&#x27;m confused.</p>
<p>At that moment, the mushroom sways and the cap moves upwards as though it has lifted its head.</p>
<p>The two slightly larger red stars that look like eyes meet my gaze. Just like that, I look at it, and it seems to be looking at me.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, the mushroom reaches out its many &quot;hands,&quot; and using the edge of the mug as leverage, it jumps out.</p>
<p>In the next second, it jumps to the floor and wildly runs towards the office door.</p>
<p>F*ck... I&#x27;m still confused.</p>
<p>At the same time, I discover a problem: All of the Instigator beverage in the mug is gone.</p>
<p>Almost subconsciously, I stand up and chase after the mushroom.</p>
<p>I suspect that it&#x27;s a product from the fusion of the Instigator beverage and the dried mushrooms!</p>
<p>I have to resolve this accident I caused, as soon as possible!</p>
<p>The mushroom is &quot;short&quot; and extremely fast. It passes through the office without being obstructed, and wasn&#x27;t even discovered. On the other hand, I&#x27;m stopped by my colleagues several times and have no choice but to slow down.</p>
<p>So, although the mushroom&#x27;s footsteps are obviously smaller than mine, it still escapes from the company.</p>
<p>I-it charged into the company next door! I subconsciously follow.</p>
<p>At this moment, the security guard next door stops me.</p>
<p>&quot;What are you here for?&quot; the tall and strong security guard asks.</p>
<p>My mind races as I come up with an excuse:</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m here for Vice President Wu.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Vice President Wu?&quot; The security officer doesn&#x27;t believe me. &quot;I&#x27;ll ask the front desk.&quot;</p>
<p>While he&#x27;s walking to the front desk, I approach the door and gaze inside.</p>
<p>I&#x27;m shocked by what I&#x27;m seeing.</p>
<p>The employees of the company next door are either wearing black hooded robes or wearing strange costumes. They&#x27;re dancing with something similar to an &quot;Employees&#x27; Dance for Business.&quot;</p>
<p>A cosplay convention? A masquerade ball? It&#x27;s only nine in the morning... I&#x27;m confused again, forgetting to sneak inside.</p>
<p>&quot;What&#x27;s your boss doing?&quot; I ask when the security guard returns.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s so strange! The security officer shakes his head.</p>
<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know either.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m just a worker.&quot;</p>
<p>Tsk, the moment I hear that, it&#x27;s obvious that he&#x27;s trying to draw a clear line between him and them... I press, &quot;Is your boss inside too?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, that one over there who isn&#x27;t wearing anything,&quot; the security guard replies nonchalantly.</p>
<p>I focus my gaze, and the corner of my mouth twitches involuntarily.</p>
<p>Pervert!</p>
