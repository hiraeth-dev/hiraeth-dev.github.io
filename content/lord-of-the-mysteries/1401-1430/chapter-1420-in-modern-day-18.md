<p>Having had half a day to spare, I stay in the bookstore until it&#x27;s almost seven.</p>
<p>During this time, I can&#x27;t refuse the owner&#x27;s repeated offers. I reluctantly take a small slice of lemon cake, a set of pasta with minced meat, and two grilled sausages that had been battered and fried with spicy chicken powder.</p>
<p>I don&#x27;t need dinner... I rub my belly and decide to take a walk home.</p>
<p>This helps with digestion.</p>
<p>The only problem is that the weather is hot and stuffy. If I walk in this weather for a while, I&#x27;ll definitely sweat.</p>
<p>However, ever since I entered society, I&#x27;ve always had the habit of taking a shower everyday. And today, I&#x27;m also preparing to wash some clothes, so I&#x27;m not too averse to sweating.</p>
<p>To my surprise, perhaps because of the Assassin beverage, I managed to passively lose weight. I don&#x27;t even have a tummy. Furthermore, I&#x27;m good at using the shadows to hide myself. When I return to my rented apartment, I&#x27;ve only sweated a fifth of what I expected.</p>
<p>That medium lady, Madam Daly, will be here at nine. There&#x27;s still about two hours left. With more than enough time, I decide to use the fan after my shower, and to play some games. As for using the air conditioner, the weather isn&#x27;t too hot. Besides, after becoming an Assassin, I feel that I have a strong tolerance for heat and stuffy conditions.</p>
<p>To be honest, this cannot be considered unbearable. It&#x27;s just that I don&#x27;t really feel that hot and stuffy.</p>
<p>When I enter the bathroom, I glance at the mirror and suddenly think of a question:</p>
<p>That female ghost was introduced by Detective Sherlock Moriarty to protect me and investigate that mysterious cult. She hasn&#x27;t shown any intention of harming me. Should I remind her that I&#x27;ve found a medium, so she should hide in advance?</p>
<p>She doesn&#x27;t have any ill intentions. She&#x27;s very friendly. Besides, my reason for dealing with that mysterious cult is reasonably convincing... She must&#x27;ve sensed Madam Daly&#x27;s power last night. Even if I don&#x27;t tell her, she should be able to guess what will happen next... I think for a moment and make up my mind.</p>
<p>However, a new problem rears its head:</p>
<p>How should I warn her?</p>
<p>I don&#x27;t even know where she is or how I should contact her!</p>
<p>Looking around, I can&#x27;t find any clues. It seems like I&#x27;m the only person in the room.</p>
<p>Uh, yes, there&#x27;s only one person, but who knows if there are any ghosts. Perhaps she&#x27;s hiding somewhere and I can just talk to the air? It&#x27;s rather silly talking to myself... I recall last night and cast my gaze back at the mirror.</p>
<p>Looking at my reflection in the mirror, I whisper, &quot;I&#x27;ve hired a medium. She&#x27;ll be here at nine.&quot;</p>
<p>As soon as I finish speaking, the mirror in front of me turns dark, as if the surrounding light had been sucked in.</p>
<p>The pale female ghost wearing a small bonnet and a retro dress appears in the mirror and nods gently.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you.&quot;</p>
<p>In a second, her image disappears and the mirror returns to normal.</p>
<p>I heave a sigh of relief and hang up my clothes before I start to take a shower.</p>
<p>As I shower, I suddenly freeze.</p>
<p>Since the female ghost has already left, why am I spending so much money to hire a medium?</p>
<p>The female ghost clearly has no ill intentions, and she&#x27;s even secretly protecting me. Why would I spend so much money to hire a medium?</p>
<p>That&#x27;s 20,000 yuan, with a maximum of 50,000! Wouldn&#x27;t that be a waste of money?</p>
<p>While taking pity on my wallet, I feel the need to think of a way to cancel the contract.</p>
<p>Anyways, it&#x27;s still early. As long as Madam Daly doesn&#x27;t live in the suburbs, she probably hasn&#x27;t set off yet.</p>
<p>But I can&#x27;t be sure that the female ghost doesn&#x27;t have any ill intentions... In my hesitation, I wipe myself dry and put on my clothes.</p>
<p>After careful consideration, I decided to use the strategy of delaying things to hold back on the exorcism assignment.</p>
<p>During this process, if I discover something amiss, I&#x27;ll immediately get Madam Daly to come over!</p>
<p>Sigh, it&#x27;s really embarrassing to break a contract... I return to my room, pick up my phone, and send a message to SleepingWithCorpses.</p>
<p>&quot;Madam Daly, the ghost that is haunting me seems to have left.&quot;</p>
<p>This is the truth, but I didn&#x27;t explain why.</p>
<p>&quot;Indeed...&quot; After more than ten seconds, Madam Daly replies, &quot;Could it be that the restrictions I did last night scared her away? Or maybe you were too much of a pervert and scared her away. Didn&#x27;t I ask you to watch ‘A Chinese Ghost Story&#x27; and learn how to interact with female ghosts?&quot; I feel that it&#x27;s more likely that ‘A Chinese Ghost Story&#x27; will make the female ghost shy... I didn&#x27;t dare reply as I cautiously say,</p>
<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know.</p>
<p>&quot;How about this? Don&#x27;t come over today. I&#x27;ll keep an eye out for a few more days. If the female ghost really doesn&#x27;t appear again, then forget it. If there&#x27;s any problem, I&#x27;ll let you know again. How about that?&quot;</p>
<p>After some hesitation, I add, &quot;I can reimburse you for your preparations. No, I mean pay a reasonable fee.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s do this for now. I thought I could catch a female ghost and play with it.&quot; Madam Daly sends an emoji.</p>
<p>Hearing this, I relax. I feel like I&#x27;ve earned a huge sum of money.</p>
<p>After settling this matter, I happily play games.</p>
<p>At eleven o&#x27;clock, I decided to go downstairs to buy some delicious food to reward myself as I didn&#x27;t spend any money for dinner and didn&#x27;t have to pay for the exorcism.</p>
<p>&quot;Eggplants, I have to have roasted eggplants...&quot; I mutter as I rush out of the estate.</p>
<p>Before I can choose the barbecue stall, I see those punks again.</p>
<p>Among them, the hooligan nicknamed &quot;Dani&quot; is seriously handing out flyers. His dyed, charred-yellow hair, eyebrows, and his introduction of the description of tutoring class form a strong contrast.</p>
<p>This tutoring class is interesting... I glance around and see an acquaintance sitting at the barbecue stall on the right.</p>
<p>Miss Bernie Huang!</p>
<p>How is a rich young lady like her eating at a roadside barbecue stall? Does CEO Huang know? I casually size up Miss Huang&#x27;s companion.</p>
<p>If it was a guy, hehe, that would be amazing gossip!</p>
<p>Uh, a girl wearing black-framed glasses. She&#x27;s not too old, about the same age as Miss Huang... I feel full of regret and was about to retract my gaze.</p>
<p>At this moment, Miss Huang raises her head and looks over.</p>
<p>At this moment, our eyes meet.</p>
<p>She immediately stands up and walks towards me.</p>
<p>This is a little awkward... However, there&#x27;s nothing to feel awkward about. I live nearby, so what&#x27;s wrong with coming out to eat some barbecue? I stand my ground and wait for Miss Huang and her friend to come over.</p>
<p>Miss Huang stops in front of me and tersely acknowledges my presence. &quot;I have something I need your help with.&quot;</p>
<p>The girl in black-framed glasses beside her asks curiously, &quot;Father, who is he?&quot;</p>
<p>Pfft... What kind of address is that? The relationship between youngsters nowadays is so complicated... I&#x27;m actually at a loss as to how to respond. I can only force the corners of my mouth to curl up as I express my doubts with a smile.</p>
<p>&quot;We had a bet in our dorm. Whoever lost had to address the winner as father,&quot; Miss Bernie Huang explains.</p>
<p>She points at the girl beside her.</p>
<p>&quot;Her family runs a tutoring class.</p>
<p>&quot;I would like you to register for the Dream Tutoring Classes and sit in for their classes.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll pay for it. I&#x27;ll also pay you a fee for the work you do later.&quot;</p>
<p>Register for the tutoring class... I can&#x27;t help but turn my head to look at the hooligans who are seriously handing out flyers.</p>
