<p>As the cold wind blows, the flashlight on my phone dims. I shiver and my hair stands on end.</p>
<p>To be honest, after becoming an Assassin, even if there isn&#x27;t any light, I can still see the surroundings clearly without worrying about what&#x27;s on the ground.</p>
<p>But there&#x27;s no conflict between me being able to see in the darkness and my need for a flashlight.</p>
<p>Who isn&#x27;t afraid of the dark?</p>
<p>At that moment, my heart beats faster, pounding like a drum.</p>
<p>Almost at the same time, I sense someone watching me. The cold wind is the breath of a monster lurking deep in the darkness.</p>
<p>Recalling what happened on the bus previously, I pretended not to notice anything amiss and look down at my phone.</p>
<p>It&#x27;s broken again?</p>
<p>I had previously encountered the sudden experience of my phone&#x27;s flashlight dimming, but I was using a crappy phone. At this moment, the &quot;experienced&quot; me habitually blamed it on the phone. After &quot;checking&quot; the phone and making the lights turn on again, I continue forward without anything happening.</p>
<p>The eerie wind is still lingering in the corridor. The light flickers in brightness, digging up the fear hidden at the bottom of my heart.</p>
<p>As I walk, I seriously consider what powers an Assassin has against ghosts.</p>
<p>The answer is:</p>
<p>Nothing.</p>
<p>Since I have nothing, I don&#x27;t look forward to slaying evil or dealing with the possible existence of a ghost as if dealing with gangsters.</p>
<p>One step, two steps, three steps. I calmly walk up the flight of stairs and arrive at the top floor.</p>
<p>The lights in the building are working fine as they shine down with their yellow light.</p>
<p>Everything returns to normal.</p>
<p>Following that, the cold gust of wind stops. The light stops flickering. I successfully return to the sixth floor and stop at the entrance of my rented apartment.</p>
<p>Phew, there&#x27;s actually a ghost present here! I have to move out as soon as possible! This thought subconsciously flashes across my mind.</p>
<p>I don&#x27;t feel any shame. This is an instinctual response that anyone can have!</p>
<p>However, if I quietly moved away, Sia Tas and her boyfriend would deﬁnitely not know what&#x27;s happening here. They&#x27;ll continue living here. Who knows whether the devil might pester them... Also, there are twelve units in this building. It&#x27;s impossible for me to inform every household, right? Also, how do I say it? Ghosts? Who would believe me? I hold my key, momentarily held in a difficult spot.</p>
<p>Quickly hire a capable Daoist priest or monk to exorcise the ghost? But I don&#x27;t know any. Before today, I didn&#x27;t even know that there are supernatural powers in this world. It was all fantasy... Yes, the Tarot Club&#x27;s The Star knows that the mysterious cult provides all kinds of superpowers, which means he has a certain level of understanding in such matters... Will he know a few masters who are good at busting ghosts? Phew, I&#x27;ll give it a try. If there&#x27;s nothing I can do, I&#x27;ll make an anonymous call to inform the police. I&#x27;ll post a ﬂyer that mentions how this place is haunted at every household&#x27;s door, No, not ﬂyer, a bulletin... I quickly come up with a plan.</p>
<p>As for the outcome, I don&#x27;t have any demands. After all, I don&#x27;t have any experience in this area. Besides, I should prioritize protecting myself.</p>
<p>Opening the door, I enter the room and return to my room. I think for a while before logging on to QQ and entering a private chat with The Star.</p>
<p>Uh, The Hanged Man and The Hermit suspect that he&#x27;s a member of cyber police. If I were to directly look for him, he will deﬁnitely be able to ﬁnd my IP address and lock onto my identity... Just as I&#x27;m about to type the message, I remember the warning from two other members of the Tarot Club.</p>
<p>After tinkering for a while, I use a proxy and use my alt, The World, to say to The Star:</p>
<p>&quot; Are you there? I have something to ask you.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;...Don&#x27;t use that emoji. It scares me.&quot; The Star replies quickly.</p>
<p>&quot;Why?&quot; I ask.</p>
<p>The Star says:</p>
<p>&quot; This emoji has become popular recently. It&#x27;s very strange.</p>
<p>&quot;Furthermore, something tragic happened to a family last week. Everyone in that family wore a monocle and died in different places. The only survivor was a girl who went mad from the trauma. She repeatedly tells people not to go near her.</p>
<p>&quot;I suspect that those who use this emoji will be targeted by an unknown existence. They will automatically put on a monocle and commit suicide or kill each other.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t scare me .&quot; I reply, unsure if I should believe him.</p>
<p>&quot;Haha, it&#x27;s just a joke. The monocle in this emoji is on a different eye from the tragedy. Furthermore, there are thousands of people using this emoji. Which unknown existence can keep an eye on everyone?&quot; The Star switches to asking, &quot;Why are you looking for me?&quot;</p>
<p>I directly say:</p>
<p>&quot;My area is haunted. Do you know anyone who&#x27;s good at ghostbusting?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m very good at that.&quot; The Star replies without hesitation.</p>
<p>Then wouldn&#x27;t I be exposed? I quickly send an emoji:</p>
<p>&quot; I won&#x27;t trouble you for the time being. You seem very busy.</p>
<p>&quot;Do you have anyone else that you could introduce to me?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes, a lady, a medium. She&#x27;s skilled in techniques from overseas, but I can guarantee that it&#x27;s effective. There&#x27;s another foreigner, a priest. He&#x27;s also good at setting up exorcism rituals.&quot; The Star replies.</p>
<p>&quot;They&#x27;re all foreigners? You don&#x27;t have Daoist priests or monks to introduce?&quot; I&#x27;m more trusting towards local ones.</p>
<p>The Star replies:</p>
<p>&quot;I only know foreign ones!&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Okay. Who&#x27;s better?&quot; I had wanted to ask who&#x27;s cheaper, but considering how someone cheap might not do the job, I don&#x27;t dare to skimp on that.</p>
<p>The Star immediately says:</p>
<p>&quot;Ma&#x27;am Da.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll give you her QQ number. Add her directly and contact her.</p>
<p>&quot;XXXXXXXXXXXXX.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Okay, thank you.&quot; I quickly copy the QQ number and paste it into the search box.</p>
<p>Ma&#x27;m Da&#x27;s QQ name is &quot;SleepingWithCorpses,&quot; and adding her requires one to answer a verification question: &quot;How old are you?&quot;</p>
<p>How old? This question is strange... Do I need to use a countersign? I&#x27;m puzzled for a moment. I take a screenshot and send it to The Star.</p>
<p>&quot;What&#x27;s the answer?&quot;</p>
<p>The Star replies:</p>
<p>&quot;Just answer that you&#x27;re an adult.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What&#x27;s the meaning of this? She doesn&#x27;t entertain underaged clients?&quot; I&#x27;m confused.</p>
<p>&quot;No. She just wants to determine the extent she can go in the conversation.&quot; The Star answered simply, and then used an image to supplement it: Open to suggestions, ready to be suggestive.</p>
<p>&quot;...&quot; For some reason, I&#x27;m at a loss on how to respond. I fill up the verification according to The Star&#x27;s answer and fill in &quot;Adult.&quot;</p>
<p>Da doesn&#x27;t seem online. I didn&#x27;t get added even after a few minutes. After thanking The Star, I use snacks to console myself.</p>
<p>After eating and drinking, I head to the bathroom outside to wash up and prepare to head to bed.</p>
<p>Ma&#x27;am Da still hasn&#x27;t added me.</p>
<p>I switch on the bathroom light and turn on the tap. I reach for the towel.</p>
<p>Turning around, I suddenly see a figure in the mirror.</p>
<p>That figure is blurry. Vaguely, I see it wearing a small hat. It&#x27;s a woman in a black dress.</p>
<p>Clearly, this isn&#x27;t my projection!</p>
<p>It can&#x27;t be Sia Tas, who wears pajamas as home! Instantly, my mind explodes and I hurriedly retreat.</p>
<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t be afraid,&quot; the woman in the mirror says in an ethereal voice. &quot;Someone hired me to protect you.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Who?&quot; I ask subconsciously.</p>
<p>The figure in the mirror replies calmly, &quot;A detective named Sherlock Moriarty.&quot;</p>
