<p>Even though I&#x27;m separated from them at a distance, with all the weirdly dressed people in the way, I still see the naked man bound in dark red ropes.</p>
<p>As for whether he&#x27;s wearing something over the lower half of his body, or what he&#x27;s wearing, I can&#x27;t see it because of the table, chairs, and other things that are in the way.</p>
<p>In short, the impression that guy gives me is:</p>
<p>A pervert!</p>
<p>And he might even be into S&amp;M.</p>
<p>Of course, it&#x27;s unclear whether he&#x27;s a real pervert or just cosplaying.</p>
<p>&quot;Alright, you can go in now,&quot; the security guard says casually.</p>
<p>&quot;Huh?&quot; I doubt my ears.</p>
<p>I had lied about being here for Vice President Wu in my rush to give an excuse. I didn&#x27;t expect to get permission at all. My original plan was to sneak in while the security guard went to inquire at the front desk, and catch that &quot;mushroom man.&quot; However, the large-scale cosplay scene inside gave me a shock, causing me to temporarily fall into a dazed state of questioning myself: &quot;Who am I,&quot; &quot;Where am I from,&quot; and &quot;What am I doing?&quot; I didn&#x27;t have the chance to take advantage of this opening. &quot;Vice President Wu has given you permission to enter. Why? Do you not understand what I&#x27;m saying?&quot; The security guard senses my confusion.</p>
<p>I&#x27;m even more surprised.</p>
<p>&quot;V-Vice President Wu personally said to allow me entry?&quot;</p>
<p>This...</p>
<p>Is he trying to pull me into his marketing team?</p>
<p>The security officer glances at me.</p>
<p>&quot;You aren&#x27;t very familiar with Vice President Wu?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;ve only met him a few times,&quot; I quickly emphasize.</p>
<p>The security guard turns to look at the company&#x27;s interior. Seeing that Vice President Wu wasn&#x27;t nearby, he says, &quot;Vice President Wu doesn&#x27;t like to talk. He doesn&#x27;t answer questions or give instructions.</p>
<p>&quot;Later, we figured out the rules. Whatever he doesn&#x27;t object to on the spot is him giving tacit approval for.&quot;</p>
<p>Is that so... But it could also be because of other reasons. I once knew a friend who wasn&#x27;t smart and had a slow reaction. He only understood the ﬁrst joke when someone was beginning the second joke. Perhaps Vice President Wu is such a person. He doesn&#x27;t answer the question because he&#x27;s still digesting the information. He needs more than ten minutes to think about the answer... I don&#x27;t argue with the security guard and just mutter inwardly.</p>
<p>Since I have an opportunity like this, I obviously wouldn&#x27;t care if Vice President Wu really acquiesced or if the Creator had given him looks but forgot to give him intelligence. I immediately walk past the security guard and enter the neighboring company.</p>
<p>I carefully circle the edge of the room which is in a noisy din with all kinds of crazy dancing happening, searching for the mushroom man I created by mistake.</p>
<p>Skull decorations... Blood-colored juice... painting made of leather... This... Not only is the perverted boss next door a multi-level marketing person, but he also believes in some strange cult? As I scan my surroundings, my heart beats faster as my urge to escape increases.</p>
<p>If I wasn&#x27;t already an Assassin and was able to do a Leap of Faith and be in possession of extraordinary powers, I definitely wouldn&#x27;t have dared to stay any longer.</p>
<p>As I walk, I see a long table filled with breakfast.</p>
<p>At this moment, a man in a long black robe with a hood suddenly turns his head and looks at me.</p>
<p>&quot;Who are you?&quot; he asks hesitantly.</p>
<p>I reply without any scruples, &quot;I&#x27;m cosplaying an employee!&quot;</p>
<p>Just because all of you are cosplaying cultists or all kinds of strange figures, does that forbid me from doing a serious role?</p>
<p>&quot;Employee...&quot; The man repeats my words in confusion.</p>
<p>He doesn&#x27;t seem to have a high IQ... That&#x27;s true. If I&#x27;m doing a MLM business, I&#x27;ll deﬁnitely like this kind of member more. They&#x27;re easy to deceive! I take this opportunity to walk past the black-robed man and to the long table.</p>
<p>As I sweep my gaze, joy suddenly surges in my heart.</p>
<p>I see that &quot;mushroom man&quot;!</p>
<p>It&#x27;s lying in a pile of roasted mushrooms, wrapping itself with a layer of sauce, pretending to be ordinary, normal food.</p>
<p>However, its seductive aura betrays it.</p>
<p>Even though there&#x27;s a lot of food on the long table, it still stands out.</p>
<p>It doesn&#x27;t seem to have a high IQ as well... I pretend not to notice as I slowly approach.</p>
<p>Then, I extend my hand toward the mushroom man.</p>
<p>At this moment, a fork stabs the &quot;mushroom man&quot; and lifts it up before I can get to it. The &quot;mushroom man&quot; attempts to struggle, but to no avail. It&#x27;s stuffed into the mouth of a &quot;beautiful-looking&quot; man with a fork and is chewed.</p>
<p>Poor thing. As it had no mouth, it was eaten by the other party before it could even scream.</p>
<p>Me: &quot;...&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What? What&#x27;s wrong?&quot; the man with the androgynous looks asks in a muffled voice while eating the mushroom.</p>
<p>&quot;Are you alright?&quot; I ask hesitantly.</p>
<p>Gulp... The beautiful man swallows the last mouthful of mushroom.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m good.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Really? You&#x27;re fine?&quot; I ask subconsciously.</p>
<p>I have a nagging feeling that a strange creature like the &quot;mushroom man&quot; wouldn&#x27;t die so easily.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m fine.&quot; The beautiful man shakes his head. &quot;You are?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m with Vice President Wu. How may I address you?&quot; I carefully observe the other party&#x27;s condition. The beautiful man&#x27;s expression sinks.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m Mr. A.&quot;</p>
<p>I stare at him for a few seconds before smiling.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#x27;m heading to Vice President Wu&#x27;s side.&quot;</p>
<p>Without any hesitation, I maintain my smile and quickly leave the long table and walk out the door.</p>
<p>After leaving the neighboring company, I turn to the stairwell and hold in my expressions. I take out my phone.</p>
<p>&quot;Hello, is this the police? I found the site of a large-scale pyramid scheme!&quot;</p>
